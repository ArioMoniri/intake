## 1. Acceptance (verbatim from the issue)

1. "The 112 control is the strongest control on the RED layer. It is at least as large as the
   primary CTA, with a label size no smaller than the CTA's. It gives immediate pressed feedback and
   opens the phone's dialer with 112 (`tel:112`); a failure to open falls back to showing the number.
   Haptics stay as ADR-033 / HB-C26 say (none for RED)."
2. "Under CALM, the 112 control has a visible boundary of at least 3:1 against its background
   (WCAG 1.4.11), in both themes."
3. "Under ON_SITE, the 112 affordance stays absent (invariant 21). The patient instead gets a
   non-112 control, such as alerting staff or reaching the clinic desk, taken from the pack's ON_SITE
   action. If the pack defines none, the layer fails closed to the guidance text and a visible
   control to call for nearby staff. The layer is never control-less."
4. "The RED layer respects safe-area insets on every edge."
5. "RED copy uses the bare imperative plural (CLAUDE.md: "Tutun · Arayın · Bakmayın"), not "Lütfen
   yapmayın". The factual claim the audit flags is checked against its committed source snapshot; if
   it isn't supported there, it goes through the clinical route instead of being rewritten here."
6. "There are tests for each item with near-miss controls. `make safety` stays green, and
   invariants 9, 10 and 21 still pass."

Note on item 1: no HB-C26 row exists in `files/HUMAN_BLOCKERS.md` on this main. The haptic rule
applied is the one in code, ADR-033 Q2's fail-closed default as `lib/motion/red.ts` states it
(`RED_MOTION.haptic: false`). This PR adds no haptic, and the MOTION-4 scan still refuses one.

What the pack offers for ON_SITE, and why the control does not depend on it. The DSL's
`LocationAction` has no patient-control field. `notify_staff_immediate` is the server's
*automatic* notification when the RED fires (`intake_api.red_notify.raise_red`). The device catalog
carries `primary`, `show112` and the words only. So every ON_SITE RED takes the fail-closed path the
acceptance names: the guidance text and a visible control to call nearby staff. This matches
files/02 §5.3's action table ("Hastanedeyim — personele bildir": "fires automatically; the button
remains only as confirmation and repeat"). The server keeps one staff notification per RED, so a
press after the automatic one lands on the same entry and makes sure it exists. It never pages
anyone twice. A repeat that pages again would be a server-side change and is not in this issue.

### 5.2 The service claim the audit flags (R5), checked and routed, not rewritten

The audit names the generic REMOTE `why_tr` sentence "112 ambulansıyla getirilen hastalar,
acil serviste ayrıca öncelik sırası için bekletilmez." (CD-24, `packages/protocols/generic/
generic_red.yaml`; the same sentence is in `rf.chest_pain_cardiac_features` REMOTE through CD-25.)
Its cited snapshot is `packages/protocols/sources/TR-EK4-2022.txt`, MADDE 8(5):

> (5) 112 acil ambulanslarıyla sağlık tesisine getirilen hastalara ayrıca triyaj uygulaması
> yapılmaz. Yapılan değerlendirme neticesinde hasta, ihtiyacına göre kırmızı veya sarı alana
> alınır. 112 acil ambulansı ile getirilen hastaların triyaj ve diğer SBYS kayıtları, ambulans
> bekletilmeksizin yataklı sağlık kurumu tarafından yapılır.

- **Supported:** a patient brought by 112 ambulance gets no separate triage ("ayrıca triyaj
  uygulaması yapılmaz").
- **Not stated:** that the patient "is not kept waiting" ("bekletilmez").
  - The only "bekletilmeksizin" in the article is about the ambulance and the paperwork.
  - The article sends the patient to the red **or yellow** area as needed.
  - CD-24's own `inference_disclosure` calls "ayrıca … bekletilmez" the author's rendering of the
    consequence.

So the line stays as the pack has it, and goes to the physician of record (Dr Efe Onganer) through
`docs/backlog/blockers.md` "INT-602". The nearest row is HB-C12b; a new HB row is the
coordinator's to allocate. Any change is a pack change and would need goldens, `make safety` and
CODEOWNER review. `controls.test.ts` (5) pins both halves, so a change to either the snapshot or the
pack line forces this record to be revisited.
