## Scope, stated once

Owner decision **D25** (files/00_STATE.md §3), 3 Oct 2026, in chat: *"also set an legal
profesional subagetns to approve legal parts or review them like forensics i give permisison
with this message"*. Open legal items are routed to this lane first: a sourced legal-author
agent decides and a separate legal-reviewer agent verifies. **The scope is synthetic and test
use only. A licensed Turkish lawyer countersigns before any real patient.** Items on CLAUDE.md's
stop-and-ask list and HUMAN_BLOCKERS rows that name the owner still go to the human, with the
lane's output as a recommendation. D25 fills no counsel field, closes no HB-L row and serves
nothing to a real patient, exactly as D18 fills no `clinical_signoff`.

the reviewer's `agree`, or `agree_with_changes` with the applied changes in `verifier.notes`,
from a role that is not the author's; `clearance()` refuses a `proposed` decision even for
synthetic use, so nothing is usable until the reviewer has read it. Round 1 of the reviewer
(head `017439e0`) returned REQUEST_CHANGES with agree_with_changes on all five outcomes; its
history is in each LD's `verifier.notes`, and round 2 below applies every blocking and
should-fix finding.

## What stays draft, and why

No retrieved official text answers these, so each is `draft` with a `legal_gap` sentence and
no value; a consumer reading through `value_for()` gets a refusal, never a default.

- **LD-01** — the item Adli Tıp may ask instead of the report purpose. Needs 2659 sayılı Adli
  Tıp Kurumu Kanunu and CMK 5271's examination articles, not snapshotted.
- **LD-02** — seven HB-L20 questions (`unverified_dob`, `dob_collection_mandatory`,
  `adolescent_self_consent`, `adolescent_private_guardian_default`, `jurisdiction_tr_only`,
  `guardian_link_basis`, `guardian_relationship_proof`), the evidence of emancipation, and
  `consent_footing.minor_with_discernment`. `adolescent_self_consent` is a genuine conflict
  between TMK m.16 (strictly personal rights) and HHY m.24 (veli izni); KVKK names no age.
  `leap_day_maturity` is decided in round 2 from TMK m.5 and TBK m.92, with the reading
  disclosed.
- **LD-03, LD-04** — the ground for relatives' and household members' data in the **consumer**
  deployment (where the company, not a health institution, is controller — m.6(3)(e) is read
  as the health provider's ground); how a controller that never contacts those people
  discharges the notice duty (Tebliğ m.6(1) gives the timing; KVKK m.28(2) gives no exemption
  that fits); retention for every row; and the files/06 §2 consent scope each item rides, which
  is a stop-and-ask item for the owner.
- **LD-05** — whether Genelge 2025/10 binds a vendor-provided patient app or only the
  hospital's own apps.

## The real-patient gate (mirror of D17 / D18 / HB-C1)

The physician gate is the six-field `clinical_signoff` block the compiler refuses to serve
while blank, plus the clinical register's scope literal. The legal gate has the same shape:

- `scope: real_patient` needs `status: active` and every field of `counsel_countersign`
  (`lawyer_name`, `bar_association`, `bar_registration_no`, `signed_commit`, `signed_on`,
  `evidence` under `docs/evidence/`); a partial block is refused naming the missing fields.
  Round 2 adds: the commit is a full 40-hex sha, the countersign is not dated before the
  reviewer's verdict, and the evidence file exists.
- The reviewer is independent: a verifier role equal to the author role is refused.
- `clearance(decision, Audience.REAL_PATIENT)` checks the countersign first and refuses with
  "no licensed Turkish lawyer has countersigned it"; `value_for` runs it before returning any
  value. `test_no_committed_decision_is_cleared_for_a_real_patient` pins that every committed
  LD refuses today.
