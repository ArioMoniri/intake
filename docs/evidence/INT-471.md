## Scope, stated once

Owner decision **D25** (files/00_STATE.md §3), 3 Oct 2026, in chat: *"also set an legal
profesional subagetns to approve legal parts or review them like forensics i give permisison
with this message"*. Open legal items are decided by a sourced legal-author agent and verified
by a separate legal-reviewer agent. **The scope is synthetic and test use only. A licensed
Turkish lawyer countersigns before any real patient.** Legal questions go to this lane, never
to the owner. D25 fills no counsel field, closes no HB-L row and serves nothing to a real
patient, exactly as D18 fills no `clinical_signoff`.

## Acceptance (verbatim from docs/backlog/issues.yaml)

- D25 in files/00_STATE.md §3 records the policy with the owner's quote, the synthetic/test
  scope and the real-patient countersign gate, and that legal questions go to this lane, never

  retrieved_on; no source means draft with a legal_gap, never an invented answer; each carries
  the independent legal reviewer's verdict (near-miss: an unsourced or unverified decision
  fails; a real-patient decision without a licensed lawyer's countersign fails)
- first decisions LD-01..LD-05: the Adli Tıp report purpose; minors and proxy answers (HB-L20)
  in synthetic scope; KVKK inventory rows for family history, relatives' health, consanguinity
  and the hearing-voices item; occupational items (sick leave, tenure, household TB contact);
  the WCAG 2.2.1 classification of the RED no-answer wait
- docs/legal/ texts keep their hash binding and strings.yaml stays verbatim

## Process

| Role | Agent | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Legal author — Turkish health, data-protection, family and accessibility law | INT-471 legal-author worker | the loader, the snapshots, LD-01..LD-05, D25 |
| Independent legal reviewer | spawned by the orchestrator (not by the author) | verdicts, relayed into each LD's `verifier` block |

Every LD is committed `proposed` with verdict `pending`. The loader refuses `active` without
the reviewer's `agree` or `agree_with_changes`, and `clearance()` refuses a `proposed` decision
even for synthetic use, so nothing is usable until the reviewer has read it.

## What stays draft, and why

No retrieved official text answers these, so each is `draft` with a `legal_gap` sentence and
no value; a consumer reading through `value_for()` gets a refusal, never a default.

- **LD-01** — the item Adli Tıp may ask instead of the report purpose. Needs 2659 sayılı Adli
  Tıp Kurumu Kanunu and CMK 5271's examination articles, not snapshotted.
- **LD-02** — eight HB-L20 questions (`unverified_dob`, `dob_collection_mandatory`,
  `adolescent_self_consent`, `leap_day_maturity`, `adolescent_private_guardian_default`,
  `jurisdiction_tr_only`, `guardian_link_basis`, `guardian_relationship_proof`) and the
  evidence of emancipation. `adolescent_self_consent` is a genuine conflict between TMK m.16
  (strictly personal rights) and HHY m.24 (veli izni); KVKK names no age.
- **LD-03, LD-04** — the ground for relatives' and household members' data in the **consumer**
  deployment (where the company, not a health institution, is controller — m.6(3)(e) is read
  as the health provider's ground); how a controller that never contacts those people
  discharges the notice duty (Tebliğ m.6(1) gives the timing; KVKK m.28(2) gives no exemption
  that fits); and retention for every row.
- **LD-05** — whether Genelge 2025/10 binds a vendor-provided patient app or only the
  hospital's own apps.

## The real-patient gate (mirror of D17 / D18 / HB-C1)

The physician gate is the six-field `clinical_signoff` block the compiler refuses to serve
while blank, plus the clinical register's scope literal. The legal gate has the same shape:

- `scope: real_patient` needs `status: active` and every field of `counsel_countersign`
  (`lawyer_name`, `bar_association`, `bar_registration_no`, `signed_commit`, `signed_on`,
  `evidence` under `docs/evidence/`); a partial block is refused naming the missing fields.
- `clearance(decision, Audience.REAL_PATIENT)` checks the countersign first and refuses with
  "no licensed Turkish lawyer has countersigned it"; `value_for` runs it before returning any
  value. `test_no_committed_decision_is_cleared_for_a_real_patient` pins that every committed
  LD refuses today.

## Findings for other lanes (not built around)

1. **PR #796 (INT-465):** `apps/mobile/src/lib/red/store.ts` `answerLocation` returns early
   once the wait has resolved the question to `noAnswer`, so a late answer is ignored. Under
   LD-05 that closure is not essential and SC 2.2.1 (BK13) cannot be reported as supported for
   the timer until the question stays answerable. What a late "evet" then shows is CD-24's
   `on_yes` branch — the clinical lane's call.
2. **Adli Tıp at a private-hospital tenant:** Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 52(5) bars
   referring a person to a private provider for an adli rapor. LD-01 cites it only for
   referrals; whether a forensic pre-visit intake belongs in a private tenant at all is open.
3. **Consumer deployment:** LD-03 and LD-04 leave relatives' and household members' data with no
   decided ground when the company is controller; those items must not be served there.
