The navigation rail, CTA bar and welcome card use system Liquid Glass on iOS 26, with a blur or solid fallback, and Reduce Transparency forces solid, failing closed. A layered icon proposal is in `docs/design/apple-native/icon/`. No new dependencies. The full research, spec and before/after evidence are in `docs/design/apple-native/{research,spec,evidence}.md`.

The failing runs and the planted-defect mutants are recorded in `docs/design/apple-native/evidence.md`.
