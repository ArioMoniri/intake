# INT-372: Visit-card chevron clears long department names

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** mobile · **GitHub:** #606
**Slice:** S5 of INT-354 (#560), int-demo `01e224a2`. See `docs/evidence/INT-354.md` (commit
table row 19).

## Problem
On `main` the visit card's "›" was painted at the card's right edge (`position: "absolute"`,
`right: spacing.lg`, `top: "50%"`) while the card's text spanned the full width. A long
Turkish department name such as "Kadın Hastalıkları ve Doğum" wrapped straight under the
arrow on Vizitlerim. The `top: "50%"` offset also put the glyph's top edge, not its centre, on
the card's midline.

## What int-demo `01e224a2` did, and why it is adapted
The commit wraps the text in a body with `paddingRight: spacing.lg` (24 pt) and moves the
glyph into an absolute full-height column, so it centres. That fixes the overlap at the
default size. Measured in the shipped title face (Newsreader 500, `fonts.display`, read with
the INT-295 reader in `e2e/fontMetrics.ts`, an upper bound with no kerning), it fails the
acceptance's own bound:

- At the largest step (2.0) the title is 48 pt. On a 320 pt screen the card's content box is
  320 − 2·20 (screen) − 2·1 (border) − 2·16 (large-text card padding) = **246 pt**.
- "Hastalıkları" needs **245.2 pt**. It fits a card with no chevron by 0.8 pt. With the
  commit's 24 pt inset it has 222 pt and breaks as "Hastalıkla / rı". That is the mid-word
  break 00 §2 exists to prevent, and the one `Themed.tsx`'s Card comment records.

So the port keeps the commit's intent (the text wraps before the arrow, and the arrow centres
against a card of any height) and changes the mechanism:

- `apps/mobile/src/lib/visitCardLayout.ts` (new, pure): `CHEVRON_SIZE` (14), `CHEVRON_GAP`
  (`spacing.sm`), and `chevronPlacement(fontScale)`. It returns `beside` below the
  large-text scale and `dateLine` from it up. The threshold is `isLargeText`, the same one at
  which the Card drops to `md` padding, so the card has one idea of "large".
- `apps/mobile/src/components/VisitCard.tsx`: the card is a row. The text column has
  `flex: 1`. Under `beside`, the chevron takes a full-height column of its own
  (`justifyContent: "center"`, `marginLeft: CHEVRON_GAP`), so its width comes out of the
  text's measure instead of being painted over it. Under `dateLine` (large text), the chevron
  ends the date line and the department name keeps the card's whole measure. The component
  has no absolute positioning left.

No copy, no i18n key and no new data field changed. The hero card (Bugün) has no chevron and
renders as before.

- `apps/mobile/src/lib/visitCardLayout.test.ts`: `beside` at every app step below
  `LARGE_TEXT_SCALE`; `dateLine` at and above it, including an OS value past the clamp (3.1);
  the switch agrees with `isLargeText` at 0.9, 1.0, 1.3, 1.59, 1.6, 1.61 and 2.0; the glyph
  and gap values.
- `apps/mobile/e2e/visitCard.spec.ts`:
  - Wiring: `VisitCard.tsx` code (comments stripped) has no `position: "absolute"` and no
    `top: "50%"`. It calls `chevronPlacement(`, renders `size={CHEVRON_SIZE}`, and has a
    flexing text column, a `marginLeft: CHEVRON_GAP` column and a row date line.
  - Geometry: the card this file measures is the one `Themed.tsx` draws (border 1, `lg`
    padding, `cardTight` `md` at `isLargeText`, screen padding `spacing.screen`).
  - Measured in Newsreader 500, over every word of every `SEED_BRANCHES` name plus
    "Kadın Hastalıkları ve Doğum":
    - At 2.0 on 320 pt, the long name needs more than one line and every one of its words
      fits the line, so it wraps at a space.
    - At every app step from the large-text scale up, and at 3.1, on 320 and 375 pt, the
      chevron costs the name nothing: no word that fits a chevron-less card is broken.
    - Below the large-text scale, on 375 pt (the narrowest supported iPhone), no word is
      broken at any app step. On 320 pt the broken set must equal a recorded residual (below).
    - At 2.0 on 320 pt, every tr/en weekday and month name and the `visits.noDate` words fit
      the date line beside the chevron (Inter 400, caption).

### Residual, recorded (the owner decides)
The column costs 22 pt. At the one app step below large text, 1.3 (title 31 pt), on a 320 pt
screen, three subspecialty words fit a chevron-less card (230 pt) but not the column's 208:
"Gastroenteroloji" 221.1, "Endokrinolojisi" 210.0 and "Hidroklimatoloji" 227.2 pt.
They now break inside the word, where `main` hid a letter under the arrow. No supported
iPhone width is affected. The spec pins the set in `COLUMN_RESIDUAL`, which may only shrink.
Choosing the date-line form at every scale, a one-line change to `chevronPlacement`, removes
it. That is a design call, so it is left to the owner.
