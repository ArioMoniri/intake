# INT-497 — voice experience: natural, seamless, no delay, a live feel

GitHub #848 · Sec-Row `voice-runtime` · phase P1 · 16 Oct 2026

The owner's order of 16 Oct 2026 asks for voices that are natural, emotional
and seamless, with no delay, nothing robotic, and a conversation that feels
live. It sets two targets:

- first agent audio after the patient stops talking: under 800 ms at p50 and
  under 1.2 s at p95;
- no audible gap between consecutive clips.

The order says to measure first, then pull the levers.

Every figure below is a measurement of a named corpus, on a named machine, on
16 Oct 2026. None of them is a property of the product (invariant 20).

## 5. The end-of-turn detector: delay against cut-offs

The traces come from `make voice-experience`. Each one is the E04 voice's own
50 ms level envelope over a quiet room (−58 dBFS) and a noisy room
(−42 dBFS). There are five kinds:

- the opening word or two of a clip, as a short answer;
- a whole clip;
- a whole clip answered to a free-text question;
- each of the two above with a hesitation of 300, 500 or 650 ms inside.

The true end of speech is recorded for every trace. Every trace runs against
a fresh policy, which is the strictest case: a session that has already been
cut off once waits longer.

A flat wait chosen without the question, over long utterances and long
hesitations (238 traces, first sweep):

| wait (ms) | 450 | 550 | 650 | 700 | 750 | 800 | **850** | 1000 | 1200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| cut-offs | 43.7 % | 29.0 % | 18.1 % | 10.9 % | 6.3 % | 3.4 % | **1.3 %** | 1.3 % | 0.8 % |
| delay p50 (ms) | 380 | 480 | 580 | 630 | 680 | 730 | **780** | 930 | 1130 |

Short answers, by the short wait. The cut-off column is the number of
answers with a hesitation of that length that were cut, out of those
replayed:

| short wait (ms) | delay p50 | cut, 500 ms hesitation | cut, 650 ms hesitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 | 440 | 20/40 | 37/38 |
| 550 | 490 | 5/40 | 37/38 |
| **600** | **540** | **2/40** | 36/38 |
| 650 | 590 | 2/40 | 17/38 |
| 700 | 640 | 1/40 | 3/38 |

**What shipped: 600 ms after a short answer, 850 ms after a long one or any
answer to a free-text question.** It widens by 250 ms for the session after
a premature end, up to 1.6 s. The final replay (596 traces):

| kind | n | delay p50 / p95 (ms) | cut-offs |
|---|---|---|---|
| short | 120 | 540 / 570 | 0 |
| long | 120 | 780 / 850 | 1 |
| narrative | 120 | 780 / 850 | 0 |
| long + 300/500/650 ms hesitation | 118 | 770–790 / 790–960 | 6 |
| short + 300/500 ms hesitation | 80 | 780–790 / 800–830 | 2 |
| short + 650 ms hesitation | 38 | — | 36 |

The last row is the trade the owner's two targets force. A first word
followed by a pause longer than the short wait is cut the first time it
happens. The patient's next words then barge in on the question, and the
session's wait widens past that pause. Nothing is lost: ask-ahead
transcribes the words already said and extracts them against every open
question. The tap still ends a turn.

**What the replay cannot say.** The stand-in speech is TTS. Spontaneous
Turkish speech has more and longer pauses, and Q9's clinical speech set
does not exist yet. The device session will show the live cut-off rate,
because each premature end produces a barge-in inside `CUT_OFF_WINDOW_MS`.
The iOS meter is averagePower. Android's expo-audio meter is peak-based and
was not replayed.

## The live feel

- **Listening.** The listening pulse is unchanged. The lens orb is #842
  (INT-491), which is not merged and so not wired here.
- **Thinking.** "Soru hazırlanıyor…" still shows until a clip is audibly
  playing. It now shows for less time.
- **No dead silence over 1.5 s.** A question whose clip is not ready within
  `HOLDING_AFTER_MS` (120 ms) is fronted by a ready acknowledgement from the
  deck. That line:
  - is never the line the previous turn ended on, and never the last holding
    line;
  - is never said around RED, inside an instrument, before a sensitive
    question, after a barge-in or before a repair;
  - is shown in the transcript.

  When no acknowledgement is ready, the question text is on screen, as
  before.
- **Barge-in.**
  - The detector is unchanged.
  - A voice barge-in inside `CUT_OFF_WINDOW_MS` of an automatic end now also
    widens the session's wait.
  - A tap on the speaking bubble within `LATE_TAP_GRACE_MS` (1.2 s) of an
    automatic end is read as the "Bitirdim" the patient was already reaching
    for, not as an interruption.
- **Context-aware lead-ins.** These are INT-414's, unchanged: deck lines
  only, and the question is said verbatim.
