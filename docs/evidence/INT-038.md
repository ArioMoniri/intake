## Decision notes (with sources)

- **Stale = absent + audited.** A live grant whose `text_hash` no longer equals the
  current docs/legal hash refuses like an absence, after appending `consent.stale`
  to the audit chain; plain absence appends nothing (tests/api pins "a refused read
  is not a read"). Re-granting under the new text restores access — re-prompt on
  change, test-pinned both directions.
- **Unbound grants (pre-INT-210 dev seeds).** With no canonical text on disk the
  catalog cannot bind; the fail-closed edge is the GRANT path (grant_consent refuses
  without a text), while already-recorded seed rows are honoured until the text
  lands — at which instant they go stale and force re-prompt, because adding the
  text IS the version bump. Rule 2 of HUMAN_BLOCKERS.md: mechanism built, gate
  fails closed, content stays empty.
- **Cascade graph.** `core` → every other purpose + live shares (a withdrawn service
  consent leaves nothing to ride on); `voice` → `lane:affect` (files/06 §2.1: affect
  infers from the SOUND of the voice); `lane:linguistic` deliberately independent of
  voice (files/06 §2.1: text-only); `share:*` withdrawal revokes live Share rows
  (files/06 §2.3: revoke delivered Doctor Links).
- **≤ 1 min proof design.** The synchronous pass cascades at the withdrawal instant
  (same transaction — test-pinned, including the guard refusing immediately after).
  The sweep bounds the crash window: fixed 30 s grid, next tick strictly after the
  withdrawal, plus a 25 s pass budget ⇒ worst case 55 s < 60 s, asserted as a
  schedule property over arbitrary withdrawal offsets AND exercised end-to-end with
  a fake clock against a deliberately half-finished withdrawal. The sweep spares
  grants and shares that post-date the withdrawal instant (re-grants are new
  consents; test-pinned).
- **Default-on purposes.** files/06 §2's exact parentheticals are encoded as data:
  `learning:personal` (basis: service performance, on by default, inspectable) and
  `learning:service_improvement` (de-identified aggregates, opt-out) evaluate ON with
  no rows, and a withdrawal materialises an opt-out row (version marker `opt-out` —
  no açık rıza text governs a non-consent basis, but an unprovable opt-out is no
  opt-out). Everything consent-based is OFF until granted.
- **Guard shape.** Enforced scopes are checked for the requesting patient before the
  endpoint body; `conditional` scopes are declared for the scan and enforced inside
  the endpoint where the condition lives (role, record, send-vs-draft) — both slots
  are registry-validated at import time, and the scan covers their union.
