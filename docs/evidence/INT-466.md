# INT-466: the chat stage — ADR-039 and the live dialogue on it

GitHub #795. Decision: ADR-039 (Accepted, 3 Oct 2026; owner, in chat: "yes to all"), recorded as `files/00_STATE.md` §3 D23.
Glass placement follows Apple's own guidance, summarised with sources in `docs/design/liquid-glass-research.md`.

## The ask

The owner, 3 Oct 2026: the chat still looked old. A bold redesign was prototyped on a dev route (commit `ae1fc689`, not for merge), and the owner approved its six items ("yes to all"):

1. the orb moves;
2. an ambient gradient;
3. Liquid Glass controls;
4. interactive glass;
5. larger radii;
6. a stage palette.

The coordinator asked for this PR to put the real chat on the new parts, wired to the live session. RED, haptics, the screen reader, verbatim text and `siz` were to stay unchanged, and the dev route was to be deleted.

The owner then asked for more Liquid Glass:

- glass answer cards;
- a glass panel behind the question;
- GlassEffectContainer merging;
- a native top area;
- an orb lens and a tilt-following highlight.

The owner also asked for Apple's guidance to be read first, and followed where it disagrees with that list.

## Where Apple's guidance overrides the list

Apple says Liquid Glass belongs to the control layer that floats above content, not to the content itself. So:

- the answer cards stay solid, filled with the accent when chosen;
- the question has no panel;
- the messages are solid.

Everything else on the list is done:

- the native bar, its scroll-edge effect and a label-only bar item;
- one `GlassContainer` that holds the turn actions and the composer;
- tint only on the primary action;
- the orb's clear lens.

The tilt highlight is not done. `expo-sensors` would bring a motion-permission string, which is a store artefact for legal review, and the simulator has no gyroscope to test it on. ADR-039 records this under "Not done".

## Defects found on the simulator, each fixed test-first or by a guard

- **The orb did not animate in the live chat.** Its loop started while it was thinking, and SwiftUI does not animate to a new period once the value has moved. The fix keys the body on its loop periods and starts it from the host's layout callback, with no timer (BK13, MOTION-7). A 4.8 s recording shows the motion.
- **The top edge fade stopped at the status bar.** The SwiftUI host now ignores the safe area. This is decoration only, recorded in `a11y.spec` `LANDSCAPE_SAFE`.
- **The bar item outlived the dialogue.** It persisted into the typed steps after the handoff. An unmount cleanup now resets the options.
- **The bar item's icon hid the count.** The item is now a label only.

## Liquid Glass and screen-capture protection (open, for the owner and security)

In fresh sessions, the glass effect did not draw on the clinical screens: neither the stage's own panes nor the native bar's item. Changing the container, interactivity, draw timing, remounting and rebooting the simulator did not change this.

The suspected cause is INT-131's `useClinicalScreenPrivacy` (expo-screen-capture). It moves the key window's layer into a secure text-field layer, and the system glass may not sample through it. This is not verified: testing it would need the protection switched off, and that was refused and not pursued.

The protection is unchanged. Each glass pane draws over a veil floor (55 % white in light, 55 % `#2C2C2E` in dark), so the labels hold the contrast above whether or not the glass samples. Whether glass is wanted on screens that keep capture protection is a decision for the owner and security. This PR does not take it.

**Not verifiable on the simulator.** The simulator has no Taptic Engine, so the haptics are proved by the INT-462 tests and guards and are unchanged here. The orb's numbers are proved by `orb.test.ts`, not timed. Any device timing goes here later, with a device and a date.
