## What was NOT authored, stated before anything else

**No clinical content, and not one scripted question rewritten.** The pack's
question strings are untouched byte for byte — `git diff` on
`apps/mobile/src/lib/i18n/tr.ts` adds three keys and changes none — so
invariant 10 is not engaged at all by the change of form. That turned out to
be possible because the titles were **already open interrogatives**:
*"Nefes darlığınız ne zaman oluyor?"*, *"Ağrınızı nasıl tarif edersiniz?"*,
*"Nasıl başladı?"* are all answerable in a sentence. What was closed was the
**interaction** — the agent read the answer list aloud, and the screen offered
nothing but cards.

Nothing here decides what an answer means. No threshold, no cut-off, no
red-flag level, no age band, no dose. That is the physician of record's
(INT-043 → INT-044..051, **HB-C1**).

## 1. Which kinds convert, and which must not

| kind | converts? | why |
|---|---|---|
| `single` / `chips` authored anamnesis dimension | **yes** | the option list is a *coding* of an answer the patient can say; extraction already maps an utterance onto exactly those ids |
| `single` / `chips` history and base-layer step | **yes**, same reason | |
| `text` | already open | it *is* the patient's own words; there is no option list for an open ask to be the alternative to, so it carries no ask style |
| `measure` (boy, kilo) | **no — type-level** | invariant 22: no numeral reaches any TTS model, and there is no free numeric text entry on the clinical critical path. There is no open form of a guarded stepper |
| any step whose `delivery` is `text_first` or `text_only` | **no — type-level** | files/13 §13 puts substance use, sexual and reproductive history and pregnancy/LMP in `text_first`, and screening in `text_only`, *so that the answer does not travel on the voice channel*. An open ask makes speaking the primary act, which is the harm that section exists to prevent. §13 is also explicit that reading it aloud is not a mitigation |
| **an instrument item** | **no — type-level** | invariant 10 spends instrument items verbatim from `text_tr`; CLAUDE.md keeps *"exact validated Turkish item semantics"*; files/13 §4 states the consequence in one line — *"Instruments verbatim, explicit option selection … An LLM inferring a PHQ-9 score from narrative is not the PHQ-9."* An open-ended PHQ-9 item is not a PHQ-9 item |
| an enumerated condition **checklist** | **no — declared per question** | files/13 §4 quotes Bent et al., *Ann Intern Med* 2006 (randomised, N = 214): a 53-item checklist elicited **238 adverse events in 77 %** of patients, an open-ended question **11 in 14 %**, p < 0.001. Opening a checklist is a *measured* loss of yield, not a style choice |
| a step whose scripted question **names the list** | **no — declared per question** | *"Sigara kullanımınızı hangisi en iyi anlatıyor?"* has no open form without new scripted text, and rewriting a scripted question is a pack change with a CODEOWNER (invariant 10), not a delivery change |

The last two are **authoring decisions, not type refusals**, and the file says
so. They are pinned by exact equality in `openAsk.test.ts` with a reason
string per member, so a new closed question is visible in review — the same
discipline `PROSE_REGISTER_FINDINGS` and `no_placeholders_allow.txt` carry.

The closed set, in full: `substance`, `pain_score` (both also refused by the
type), `pmh_conditions`, `fh_{mother,father,brother,sister}_conditions`,
`smoking`, `disability`. **Nine of eighty-nine choice steps** (101 steps in
all, of which ten are `text` and two are `measure`). The other eighty are
open.

### The three refusals are in the type, not in a reviewer's memory

`IntakeQuestion` was one interface with everything optional. It is now a union
of four arms, and each refusal is an arm that has no inhabitant:

- the `open` arm declares `instrument?: never` → `{ ask: "open", instrument }`
  does not typecheck;
- the `open` arm declares `delivery?: "voice"` → `text_first` / `text_only`
  cannot be open;
- the `measure` arm declares `ask?: never` → a stepper has no ask style at all.

The controls are three `@ts-expect-error` assignments in `openAsk.test.ts`,
each paired with a **positive control** — the same object with the offending
field removed, which must compile. `@ts-expect-error` is a real assertion
here: if a refused shape ever became constructible, `tsc --noEmit` fails on
the unused directive, and `pnpm -r typecheck` is inside `make check`.

`InstrumentItemRef` has **no shipped instance** and a test asserts that. The
authored pack is HB-C1 and two of the four instruments are licence-BLOCKED
(HB-L2, HB-L3). The mechanism is wired and fails closed and the content is
empty, which is what CLAUDE.md asks of a blocked dependency.

## 2. How a free-text answer still lands on a standardised slot value

It does not land by a new mechanism. It lands by the one that already exists,
and the change is that the option list stops competing with it.

`lib/extract.ts` sends the pack to the extractor as
`{id, kind, options: string[] | null}` and **re-validates every response
against it**: an option id the pack does not declare is dropped, a
single-select candidate carrying more than one value is dropped rather than
guessed, and a `text_only` step never reaches the extractor at all. The result
is a *suggestion*, which reaches the patient as the spoken confirm turn or a
panel row, and only the patient's confirm commits it (invariant 1 posture,
INT-235/239). That is INT-083's boundary at the dev tier and **no second path
was added** — in particular the typed lane resolves nothing locally, because
a local synonym matcher would be exactly that second path.

So the standardised value space is never traded away for the open form.
`openAsk.test.ts` asserts every open question still carries at least two
options, and `speech.test.ts` asserts the confirmation read-back is byte-for-
byte what it was.

## 3. What actually changed, per surface

**Spoken.** `questionAsideTr` returned the option enumeration whenever the
list fit the Cowan bound of four (files/13 §5.3). The photographed step has
**exactly four**, so the agent said *"Seçenekler: hareket ya da eforla,
dinlenirken de oluyor, …"* — a multiple-choice question delivered by voice,
however open its wording. Now an open question gets the authored invitation
`dialogue.openInvite` instead, **at every list length**, and a closed one
keeps today's enumeration unchanged. The turn's shape is untouched: one aside
replaced another, so it is still ≤ 2 sentences with one question
(files/03 §4.2), and the chat bubble renders the same aside the clip speaks
(INT-261).

**Typed** — the lane the photograph came from. An open step now leads with a
free-text answer field (with the dev `VoiceNote` mic beside it, as the `text`
steps already have), then `intake.open.orChoose`, then the same cards or
chips. The pack's own control hint travels down with the control it describes
— *"Birden fazla seçebilirsiniz"* above a text box is the INT-261 defect
again, one surface over. A single-select step grows a CTA it never had,
because without one an answer given only in words could not be committed.

**The note.** `Answer` gains `words`. Open-ended asking buys nothing if the
sentence evaporates on the way to the clinician — the note would read exactly
as it did with a tapped card. The dialogue already **had** these words (the
candidate's `quote`, rendered on screen as provenance) and was dropping them
at the commit; now they travel with the answer, and the typed open field
collects the same thing. They print quoted, after the coded labels:

```
Nefes darlığının zamanı: Hareket ya da eforla, “merdiven çıkarken nefesim daralıyor”
```

## 4. Branching and mode depth are preserved — the mechanism, not just the claim

`words` is **never in `values`**, and `askIfMet` reads `values` and nothing
else. That is what keeps a free-text answer from becoming a route around the
rules engine (invariant 1): there is no string a patient can say that opens a
gate, because gates read declared option ids and extraction can only produce
declared option ids. `openAsk.test.ts` asserts both directions — words alone
leave `breath_timing` unasked, the same words plus `dyspnoea` open it.

The consequence is stated rather than hidden: an answer given only in words
opens no follow-up. That is not a new rule, it is files/13 §9.2's Kleene
reading already in the code — an unresolved gating answer is UNKNOWN and
UNKNOWN does not open a gate — and it is the same thing that happens today
when extraction returns nothing for a question. The patient is not nagged for
it either: `missingQuestions` counts words as an answer, because telling
someone who just described their breathlessness that the question is still
missing is asking them to say it again in the one form they declined.

INT-282's depth is untouched and pinned: the gate set is asserted to still
resolve to declared option ids of the gating question, and
`packForVisit("Kardiyoloji", detaylı)` is asserted to be a strict superset of
the Hızlı pack. `history.test.ts` (36) and `baseLayer.test.ts` (55) pass
unchanged.

## 5. What a patient sees when extraction cannot resolve their answer

Nothing is invented, and nothing they said is thrown away.

- **In the conversation:** the turn already advanced (ask-ahead), no candidate
  ever lands, and at `settling` the question falls to the manual steps —
  today's INT-235 hand-over, unchanged.
- **On the typed step it lands on:** the open field, the same standardised
  options underneath, and their earlier words are **not** poured into the box
  — a suggestion is not something the patient typed, and the quote is already
  on screen above as provenance.
- **If they answer in words again and still tap nothing:** the answer is
  committed with `values: []` and their sentence, the note prints the sentence
  quoted on its own line under the question's label, and the review shows the
  question as answered. A clinician reads what the patient said. Nobody —
  neither a model nor this code — picks a coded value on their behalf.

## 8. What is deliberately left for a pack owner

- **`pain_present` is not marked `text_only`** while its block comment says
  every step in the block is. It is `voice` today and therefore open; that is
  a pre-existing INT-282 inconsistency and changing a delivery class is a
  files/13 §13 decision, not a delivery-form one.
- **`text_first` is not honoured by the dev dialogue at all**: `spokenPack`
  drops only `text_only`, so `substance` is still spoken. INT-286 makes it
  `closed` (and the type would refuse anything else), which is as far as this
  issue's scope reaches; making `text_first` mean text-first in the runtime is
  its own issue.
- **The counterbalanced option order** files/13 §5.4 asks for (forward and
  reverse, never randomised) is still not implemented for the closed steps.
  Untouched here.
