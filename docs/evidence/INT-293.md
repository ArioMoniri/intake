### Why the app muted its own voice — measured, not guessed

`selectTtsLane` walks every (tier, host) pair in order, and `clipDeadlineMs` bounds the whole
clip. Replaying the real walk against the live daemons:

```
voxcpm2   http://127.0.0.1:8805        over_budget    1721 ms
voxcpm2   http://172.20.10.3:8805      unhealthy      1505 ms
freya     http://127.0.0.1:8803        ok              798 ms   <- winner
TOTAL LADDER WALK      = 4024 ms
clipDeadlineMs(1 sentence) = 4048 ms
```

**The admission walk costs 4024 ms of a 4048 ms budget — it wins by 24 ms on the host.** Inside
React Native it loses about as often as it wins, and `stream.ts:267` latches
`voiceMuted = state.voiceMuted || action.type === "speech_unavailable"` and never clears it.
One slow first turn therefore mutes the entire visit, with a healthy Freya sitting idle.

I observed **both outcomes on the same config within one hour** — the degradation notice present
on three launches, absent on one. That is the coin-flip, on screen.

Filed as **INT-294**.

## The one thing to be clear about: no turn in either run was spoken

The simulator's microphone records silence in this environment (known host-level issue — whisper
transcribes silence as "Thank you."). So:

- **Zero turns were spoken by me.** Every patient answer below was a **tap** on a chip/card, or
  **typed** where the step is free text. Each step says which.
- **Zero turns were heard.** On the runs that reached the dialogue surface, TTS was muted by the
  race above; on the one run where it was not muted, I have the app's own report (no notice) and
  no way to capture simulator audio.

A "full vocal test" is therefore **not** what this document contains, and I am not going to
present it as one.

# Defects found, most serious first

### 1. The voice surface conducts the whole interview and writes nothing into the note — INT-293

On the dialogue surface I answered three consecutive questions through the composer. The
conversation advanced correctly each time. The note did not move:

- after 3 answers the strip still read **"Not oluşuyor 0 / 10 bölüm"**
- opening it, **every** section read "Henüz yanıtlanmadı" — including `Başlangıç`, immediately
  after I answered "Nasıl başladı?" with `Aniden basladi`
- handing over to typed steps then started at **"0 soru yanıtlandı"** — no prefill carried either

→ `INT-293-DEFECT-dialogue-answers-do-not-reach-note-0of10.png`,
`INT-293-DEFECT-voice-3-answers-still-0of10-baslangic-empty.png`

Also, the `today` branch that fires reliably in the typed flow **did not fire here**: after
`Bugun basladi` the agent went straight to `onset`, skipping `onset_today_when`.

**Honest caveat.** My typed Turkish had no diacritics (`Bugun basladi`, not `Bugün başladı`) —
the tooling cannot type them. So this may be an extraction-normalisation failure rather than a
missing write path. **Either way the patient-visible behaviour is the same and is the defect:
the answer is silently discarded, the conversation moves on as if understood, and the note the
clinician receives is empty.** Diacritic-free Turkish is what people actually type.

### 2. TTS admission burns the clip deadline; the mute then latches for the session — INT-294

Measured above: 4024 ms of a 4048 ms budget, so it is a coin flip, and `voiceMuted` is an OR-latch
that never clears. Two independent causes each waste a full budget: a first tier that is healthy
but cannot speak (VoxCPM2 on CPU), and a configured host (`172.20.10.3`) that is not reachable at
all and costs the full 1500 ms health cap. And `make voice-doctor` reports `voice: UP` throughout.

### 3. "Sıranız geldi" is off-screen and unreachable in Detaylı at default text size — INT-295

The mode bar renders `Hastanedeyim` + `Detaylı mod açık` + `Sıranız geldi` as one non-wrapping,
non-scrolling row. At default Dynamic Type it is clipped mid-word at "Detaylı mod aç…" and the
checkpoint chip is simply not there. A horizontal swipe on the row does nothing — verified.
→ `INT-293-DEFECT-siraniz-geldi-offscreen-modebar-clipped.png`

This is the control for "I have been called in, give me my note now" — the exact situation
Detaylı exists for.

### 4. Any reload mid-intake loses the entire visit and drops the patient at the login screen

All session state is in-memory (`SessionProvider`). A reload — from a crash, a memory kill, or a
swipe-away — wipes every answer and returns to `(auth)/welcome`, while the visit card still says
**"Taslak"**. I lost a 14-question and a 9-question run this way. For a patient a third of the way
through a long in-hospital intake this is the whole session.

## The thing behind all of this

Nothing in `packages/protocols` is compiled or served. All 91 specialty packs and
`base/intake.yaml` are `maturity: L0`, `packages/protocols/dist/` holds only `.gitkeep`, and no
cluster pack exists. **The entire live question flow is the TypeScript fixture pack in
`apps/mobile/src/lib/intake/`** (`questions.ts`, `baseLayer.ts`). The YAML DSL, the `GuardExpr`
engine, the frontier and budget governor of files/13 §9, and the Python note composer with its
`Sourced[T]` spans are all real code that no patient session touches.

That is not a defect to file — it is the shape of the project today — but it reframes every
result above. What I exercised is a hand-written fixture, and it behaved well: the branching is
genuine, the mode filter is genuine, the attribution is genuine. What the invariants describe is
a different, unserved system.

# The fix — 3 Sep 2026

> Appended to the walkthrough above, which stays as *how it was found*. This
> section is *what it turned out to be*. Same worktree, same branch; the app was
> not re-driven on the simulator (see "What I did not do" at the end), so
> everything below is a run of the engine the surface uses, not a screenshot.

## Which of the two worlds this is

The issue names two, and says which to test first:

> (a) the answer is extracted into a slot but the slot never reaches the note
> composer — a wiring defect; or (b) the answer is never extracted at all — a
> matching defect, and the note is correctly reporting that it has nothing.

**It is (b), and more sharply than the caveat guessed: there was no matching to
fail.** The dialogue surface had exactly one route from the patient's words to a
declared option id — the extract daemon on port 8804 — and no answer at all when
that route produced nothing. `scripts/dev/voice_doctor.py:22` states the intended
posture in its own words, *"`extract` (8804) — DEGRADED, not required. The
dialogue runs without it"*. What the conversation actually did without it was
mark the question asked (`stream.ts` `takeUtterance` → `asked`), skip it forever
on every later advance, and hand the typed steps only `pending`, which was empty.
Three answers on screen, nothing in the note, and nothing carried across.

Three separate assertions in the red run below pin that reading, and they are
what makes it a determination rather than a guess:

| assertion | red result | what it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| a candidate handed in from outside is committed by the confirm turn and reaches `answers` (*"the write path"*) | **passed** | (a) — the note-ward half of the path was never broken |
| `Bugun basladi` on the `duration` turn fills the slot | failed, `null` | — |
| `Bugün başladı`, fully diacritised, fills the slot | failed, `null` | a normalisation-only defect: perfect Turkish failed identically |

The last row is the one the issue asked for by name. Had diacritics been the
whole story, the second would have passed and the first failed; both failed the
same way, so what was missing was not a fold but the mapping itself.

**What I cannot say.** I did not re-drive the simulator, and I did not exercise
the extract daemon from this machine. So I cannot tell you whether, on 2 Sep, the
daemon was unreachable or reachable-and-unable-to-place `Bugun basladi`. On the
app side those collapse into the same defect and the same fix; if it was the
latter, the daemon is still worth a look, and that is not this change.

## The fix, in three parts

**1. A deterministic floor under the extractor** —
`apps/mobile/src/lib/intake/answerMatch.ts`. The patient names one of the
question's OWN declared options and it is recognised locally: labels folded
through `taxonomy/branches.ts`'s explicit Turkish table (not
`toLocaleLowerCase("tr")` — `"İlaç".toLowerCase()` keeps a combining dot and
`"Ağrı"` keeps its `ı`, so neither side of the dotted/dotless pair reaches ASCII
that way), matched as whole words in sequence so `Sol` is not found inside
`solunum`. It is not an interpreter: no synonyms, no similarity, no threshold.
An ambiguous single-select yields nothing rather than a guess, which is the rule
`lib/extract.ts` already applies to the wire. Every option label in
`questions.ts` and `baseLayer.ts` round-trips through it from its diacritic-free
form and from its full form — 484 options across 103 questions, both directions,
asserted as sets so a new collision fails the build.

**2. The local read is wired into the conversation at the point the words become
known** — `stream.ts`. For the composer that is the same tick the utterance is
taken, so the suggestion is on the question before the turn advances and its
confirm turn opens at once; for a spoken utterance it is `transcript_ready`,
where the suggestion lands and waits for its confirm turn exactly as an
extractor result does, because a reconcile still never moves a live turn.
Ask-ahead is untouched. The candidate is a SUGGESTION with the patient's
verbatim words as its quote — the confirm turn, the panel row and the typed
steps remain the only commit paths, so nothing auto-commits (invariant 1
posture). `extract_settled` now skips a question whose candidate this same
utterance already produced (`>=` where it was `>`): the patient's literal words
outrank the model's reading of the same sentence, the same precedence the
verbatim transcript already had on a free-text step.

**3. An answer nothing can place is no longer swallowed.** When an utterance's
whole pipeline settles — read here, read at the extractor — with nothing for the
question it answered, that question is put back into the conversation instead of
being left behind as asked-and-done: the next advance asks it again with
`dialogue.notUnderstood` in front of it ("Söylediğinizi tam anlayamadım. Aynı
soruyu bir kez daha soralım.", plus its `en` mirror), rendered as its own quiet
bubble so the question that follows is still spoken verbatim from the pack
(invariant 10) and the turn still carries one question (invariant 2). A **second**
miss keeps it asked: a third ask is a loop, and the typed steps take it at the
hand-over. The decision waits for the extractor's verdict on purpose — an
open-ask question answered in the patient's own words is the extractor's to map,
and re-asking before it has answered would turn an open question back into a
multiple-choice one, which is the defect INT-286 removed.
