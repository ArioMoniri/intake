# INT-472: review and send as iOS grouped inset lists

GitHub #802. This is section 4 of the native pass. The spec is `docs/design/library-patterns.md` §5.2, the "Review and summary" row (Astryx Section, the settings template and MetadataList, all MIT). The Mobbin references are Health Details, Medical ID and Bevel's Confirm Health Information.

The screen keeps the app's system theme. ADR-039's stage covers only the dialogue (S1–S6) and welcome and sign-in (S7); this is the iOS Settings idiom in the system palette.

## Stacked on

This branch is `int-472-review-grouped`, stacked on #801 (INT-464), which contains #797 and #792. Merge order: … → #792 → #797 → #801 → this PR.

## What changed

- **Rows as a tested rule (`lib/intake/reviewRows.ts`).**
  - `reviewSectionRows(section, { shared, sent })` decides one group's rows and its footer:
    - the answer lines;
    - the carried-fact block, in medications and allergies only (INT-256);
    - the share switch, read-only once sent;
    - the edit rows, which a sent note never shows;
    - the section's note as the footer.
  - `shareSummaryRows(summary)` builds 02 §5.4's three parts as metadata rows. When nothing is shared it says so in words, and it never shows a clock.
- **The review screen.**
  - Each section is a `ListGroup` under its title, with the header inset to the rows' text as iOS draws it.
  - The share control is the app's `SwitchRow`: "Hekimle paylaşılsın", with "Paylaşılıyor" / "Paylaşılmıyor" in words under it. A screen reader hears the section's name with it.
  - Edits are `ListRow`s with a chevron.
  - The partial note's "Eksik kalanlar" list, the share summary (`MetadataRow`) and the sent ladder are groups of their own.
  - The old cards, tonal chips and the wrapping header row are gone.
- **SwitchRow.**
  - It takes `labelContext`, spoken after the visible label (SC 2.5.3).
  - It takes `disabled`: the control dims, and the words stay at full ink, because what was shared is information the patient still reads.
- **ListRow.**
  - `MetadataRow` is added: a label over its values.
  - `ListGroup` takes a `surface`.
  - `Kicker` takes `inset`.
  - The welcome's development list header uses `inset` too.
- **GlassBar.** The Liquid Glass now sits over the system blur and the fallback wash (see below).
- **One new string:** `review.shareSwitch` = "Hekimle paylaşılsın" / "Share with your doctor". It is in both catalogues and in the same order. It is not an imperative and is siz-safe. The wording matches the learned-facts switch (`learned.shareLabel`).

### Guards moved, each keeping its intent

- `review_send.spec`:
  - the switch names its section (now through `SwitchRow`'s `labelContext`);
  - the summary's three parts, now built in `reviewRows.ts` and read by the screen.

## Found on the simulator, and fixed

**The send bar was see-through.** On first presentation iOS drew no Liquid Glass on this screen, and the bar was a bare `GlassView`. The note's chips showed through under "Hekiminize gönderin" (`found-review-bar-see-through-light.jpg`).

The bar now draws the system blur and the fallback wash under its glass. It is a frosted bar whether or not the glass draws. `reviewGrouped.spec` holds the order, with a near miss. The typed intake, the other `GlassBar` host, gets the same floor.

## Liquid Glass on capture-protected screens: a correction

INT-464 compared welcome, where the glass drew, with the dialogue, where it did not, and read that as support for INT-131's capture protection being the cause. This screen contradicts that reading:
- It is capture-protected, and on first presentation it drew no glass: the bar was see-through and the back button had no glass disc.
- After a hot reload of the same screen, the glass drew: a frosted bar and a glass disc behind the back chevron (`before-review-top-light.jpg`).

So capture protection alone does not decide it, and the cause is not established. The floors (the stage veil in INT-466, this bar's blur) keep every label legible either way. The INT-464 evidence and PR #801 are corrected to say this.

**Capture harness, disclosed.**
- The note was made on the synthetic dev account by typing answers into the dialogue, with the same capture-only ASR health stub as INT-462.
- The before captures were taken by swapping the changed files to `HEAD` and back. The swap copied them first, restored them from those copies and compared them byte for byte.
- One "Hekiminize gönderin" tap was made by mistake while dismissing a debugger toast. It sent the synthetic note in local state only (this screen reaches no network), and it produced the sent-state captures. The account was then signed out and a fresh note was made for the unsent captures.
