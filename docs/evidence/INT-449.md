# INT-449: Turkish voice casting, the best native Turkish ElevenLabs voice and model

**Epic:** E05-voice · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `cloud-clipgen` · **GitHub:** #762 ·
**Depends on:** INT-415 · **Branch:** `int-449-turkish-voice-casting` · **Date:** 2026-10-03

## The ask

The owner's feedback on 3 Oct 2026 was that George, the ADR-028 voice, is an English voice speaking
Turkish. He said the intonation between words "is not fine" and the voice lacks emotion. The
acceptance has two items:

1. Native Turkish voices and the available models are compared on the same catalogue lines, and
   the owner picks from a blind page.
2. The chosen voice and model are pinned with a profile version bump, the bundled set is
   re-rendered, and intonation continuity between words is measured.

## What this PR delivers

- **`scripts/voice/cast_turkish_voices.py`** is the casting tool. Its modes are:
  - `--estimate`: dials nothing.
  - `--plan` (the default): reads the voice library, which is free.
  - `--live`: renders, and is spend-gated.
  - `--analyse RUN`: re-measures offline.

  Its guarantees are:
  - Six catalogue lines are checked verbatim against their sources before any call. They must
    pass `assert_speakable_tr` (invariant 22) and carry no markup (invariant 10).
  - The synthesis request body is the ADR-028 gateway's own body, and a test holds the two equal.
  - The key comes from the environment through `tts_gateway_from_env` and is never printed or
    written. A test asserts it is absent from stdout, stderr and every output file.
  - `--live` needs `EVAL_BUDGET_USD`. It refuses an estimate over the budget or over the 5 USD
    ceiling, and it guards the running spend too.

- **`scripts/voice/voice_cast_library.py`** covers library and premade queries, eligibility
  (native accent, no iconic or custom-rate voice, adult), metadata scoring, and a
  gender-balanced shortlist.
- **`scripts/voice/voice_cast_report.py`** measures continuity from the vendor's character
  alignment: the mid-phrase gap, the pitch jump at mid-phrase word boundaries, and rate evenness,
  with speaking rate and pitch range as context. It ranks by mean rank and writes the blind and
  labelled pages.
- **`docs/voice/casting-2026-10.md`** holds the shortlist, the terms, the measurements and how to
  pick.
- **Backlog row INT-449**, with the three pinned counts raised from 330 to 331 and the reason
  stated. `files/11` is regenerated.

**Not in this PR yet, because it waits on the owner's pick (awaiting-human):**

- The pin change is prepared and verified, but not applied. It adds `CLIPGEN_VOICE_ID` and a
  per-row `voice_id`, bumps every row's version, moves the dev route's default voice to the pin,
  and adds a near-miss test that refuses a George-era `@v1` recording.
- The re-render of the bundled set waits on the pin. It runs with INT-440's tool once INT-440 is on
  main.

## Green run

```

.................................                                        [100%]
```

The tests cover:

- **Lines.** The six lines are one per named context. Lines on main are verbatim in the working
  tree, and branch lines verify only against the named ref. A near-miss line refuses the whole
  run. Numerals and markup are refused.
- **Wire.** The request body equals the gateway's. The aligned call goes to `/with-timestamps`
  with `pcm_24000`. A model outside the casting pair is refused.
- **Library.** Iconic, custom-rate, foreign-accent, non-Turkish and child voices are excluded. The
  shortlist is balanced 4/4 and never includes the control. The library query carries filters
  only.
- **Spend.** The estimate formula is tested. `--live` refuses without a budget and over the budget.
  `--plan` refuses without credentials.
- **Full offline run.** It writes 18 combinations. The blind page carries no name, id or model.
  The key and the base URL appear nowhere. A v3 refusal drops v3 after one call. A refused voice is
  dropped after one call. `--analyse` reproduces the ranking.
- **Measurements, on synthetic speech with known gaps and pitch steps.** Gaps are found and joined
  phrases show none. A 12-semitone step is measured as 12 ± 1. Punctuation boundaries are kept out.
  An unaligned take still reports rate. The ranking puts missing figures last, and vendor names
  are escaped.

## Round 2: expressive casting on eleven_v3 with render-time tags (prepared)

The owner's feedback on round 1, 3 Oct 2026: "ElevenLabs could do better and more emotional". The
coordinator asked for a second round:

- the four kept voices (Cavit, Mine, Toprak, Meloxia), plus two expressive native voices;
- `eleven_v3` only, at two stability levels: Creative (0.0) and Natural (0.5);
- emotion tags by delivery context, at render time only, with none on RED or the instrument;
- the same six lines, a blind page, and at most 5 USD.

What was built:

- **`scripts/voice/voice_cast_tags.py`**, the closed tag table and its checks.
- **`scripts/voice/cast_expressive.py`**, the round-2 tool.
- **`expressive_extras`** in `voice_cast_library.py`: one female and one male eligible,
  middle-aged native voice whose own description claims expressiveness or emotion, best metadata
  score first, with round-1 voices excluded. On the round-1 library data it picks Gamze Özdemir
  (`Hvrobr8BhLPfiaSv2cHi`) and Marcus/Ozzano (`2PwDxni9MpXdYqgALrBh`). The live run re-queries
  the library and prints its picks.
- **The request layer.** `request_body` and `synthesise_aligned` take a wire prefix, which must be
  the table's own for its context, and only on v3. `text_tr`, the catalogue and the checks on
  them are unchanged.
- **The tag measurement.** `split_prefix` separates the tag characters from the line and records
  the audio span they cover (`tag_ms`).
- **The ADR-034 amendment proposal**, in `docs/voice/adr-034-amendment-proposal-render-time-tags.md`.

The estimate is 6 voices × 2 stability levels × 409 wire characters (362 for the lines and 47 for
the tags), which is 4,908 characters. That is 1.47 USD at the planning rate, against a 5 USD
ceiling.
