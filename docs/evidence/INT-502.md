## 1 · What Apple asks for (re-read 2026-10-29)

| subject | what the page says | source |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone screenshot groups | Large Dynamic Island: 1260×2736, 1290×2796 or 1320×2868. Large Face ID (6.5 inch): 1284×2778 or 1242×2688, required only when the large Dynamic Island set is missing. Medium Dynamic Island: 1179×2556 or 1206×2622; a submission needs "at least one screenshot" here. Files are .jpeg, .jpg or .png with no alpha, up to 10 per size. New since INT-495 read the page: two iPhone Duo groups, required from April 2027 for apps built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later. They do not apply here. | developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications |
| product page header | 21:9 at 3840×1646 (.jpeg, .jpg or .png), or 16:9 at 5244×2950 (.png only); no alpha. The page gives no safe area. | developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications/ |
| header content | Keep the focal point in the centre. A short phrase may enhance the visual. No logos or references to other platforms or marketplaces, no pricing or URLs, no unverifiable claims, and 4+ content. | developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/ |

These agree with every size INT-495 recorded on 2026-10-16. `docs/store/asc/assets.yaml` gains the
large Face ID slot (`iphone-faceid-large`) for the owner's 6.5 inch, and
`tests/legal/test_asc_frames.py` holds the manifest equal to this table.

## 2 · What was built

| piece | where | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| captions, tr and en-US | `docs/legal/drafts/store/app-store.screenshots.draft.yaml` | One caption for each frame and one for the header. Each names its screen and quotes the app string or served listing line it rests on. It is a draft for the legal lane, and export reads only its served copy. |
| frame spec | `docs/store/asc/frames.yaml` | The journey (seven frames), the capture device, the theme tokens, the two OFL faces pinned by sha256, the layout as fractions, the target sizes, and what no capture may show. |
| geometry and paint plans | `scripts/store/frame_plan.py` | Pure functions from the spec to whole-pixel layouts and to the painter's JSON. |
| painter | `scripts/store/paint.swift` | CoreGraphics and CoreText. It draws the ground, the caption, a drawn phone body and the capture clipped to the screen. Output is 8-bit RGB PNG with no alpha. |
| render | `scripts/store/render_frames.py` | `--export` reads served captions only (exit 3 otherwise). `--preview` takes any caption file and writes outside the repo. Each output is checked against its slot with `asc_assets.check_file`. |
| capture | `scripts/store/capture_frames.py` | Drives the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator. It sets a clean status bar, the locale and each frame's appearance; checks every shot is 1320×2868; deletes a wrong one; and writes `provenance.yaml` (hashes, device, runtime, app sha, API, data class). |
| manifest | `docs/store/asc/assets.yaml` | Adds `iphone-faceid-large`. The file lists stay empty until real captures exist. |

## 5 · The template, rendered

`render_frames.py --preview` was run on the draft captions, with real evidence captures as
stand-ins. They were scaled to the capture size, rendered to the scratchpad outside the
repository, and never committed. All 22 outputs passed their slot checks: seven frames at three
sizes, plus the header. The stand-ins show what the must-not-show list exists for: an Expo Go
gear, "Sentetik Hastane", and a department name broken mid-word.

A matte grain on the ground was tried. It cannot be seen at store scale and adds about a
megabyte to every file, so it was dropped.

## 6 · Copy truth check (listing and captions against the app and the legal texts)

These findings are about the served listing (INT-495, legal-lane approved). Changing it is the
legal lane's call, so this branch does not edit it.

1. "Şikâyetinizi sesli ya da yazılı olarak anlatabilirsiniz." On main, voice intake is
   `__DEV__`-only (`voiceEligible` in `intake.tsx`), so a store build has no voice. The review
   notes §1 item 8 already makes submission wait for it. The captions say nothing about voice,
   and the chat frame is the typed dialogue.
2. "Raporlarınızı ve tahlil sonuçlarınızı yükleyebilirsiniz." The synthetic host has no ClamAV,
   so upload fails closed (`scan_not_configured`, docs/runbooks/staging.md). A store build
   pointed at that host cannot upload. No frame shows upload.
3. "Sesiniz yazıya dökülür; ses kaydı telefonunuza ya da sunucularımıza yazılmaz." This matches
   the synthetic voice consent text ("Diske, veri tabanına ya da kayıt dosyalarına
   yazılmaz"). Until #964 (INT-559) merges, the dev lane still writes a temporary file. That
   lane is dev-only.
4. Share, review, stop-a-share and one-question-per-turn match the share consent text and the
   app strings.

## 7 · Not done here, and why

- **Captures.** The coordinator's route (d) puts a separate capture API on the synthetic host
  (loopback only, its own database and Redis index, dev gates on that instance alone), reached
  by an SSM port forward. It waits for the host to carry the build-6 sha. Until captures exist,
  the manifest's file lists stay empty and the plan keeps the asset slots blocked.
- **Upload through the App Asset Library** (reserve, put, commit, place, order). This is not
  built in this pass. Nothing is written to App Store Connect before the owner's go.
