# Evidence — INT-349 · Sensitive intake categories: per-slot data-category tag, encrypted storage, register rows

## 1. Scope

The owner's decision D3 (2026-09-26, recorded in
`docs/evidence/verification/owner-decisions-2026-09-26.md`, which reaches `main`
with INT-353) approved specialty questions that collect eight categories of
personal data beyond the patient's own general health:

| category (`DataCategory`) | D3 wording | specialties |
|---|---|---|
| `personal_safety` | forensic / personal safety | adli tıp |
| `sexual_health` | genital and sexual-health symptoms | deri |
| `substance_use` | non-alcohol substance use | ruh sağlığı, klinik psikoloji |
| `household` | household basic needs and household contacts | sosyal pediatri, halk sağlığı, nükleer tıp |
| `relatives_health` | relatives' family health history and consanguinity | tıbbi genetik, onkoloji, pediatri, immünoloji, nefroloji, hematoloji, metabolizma, gastroenteroloji |
| `partner_fertility` | partner fertility data | üreme endokrinolojisi, tıbbi genetik |
| `occupational` | employer / occupational exposure and report purpose | iş ve meslek hastalıkları, sağlık kurulu |
| `literacy` | reading and learning difficulty | hemşirelik eğitim, sosyal pediatri |

D3 left four things open under INT-349: storage (every answer lands in the
generic `slots.value`), encryption (the only writer wrote `PLAIN`), counsel's
per-category legal footing, and a slot-level tag. This PR is the engineering
half of all four. The legal half is counsel's and ships empty.

Acceptance (the INT-349 backlog row, filed on `chore-backlog-int-349-354`):

1. *"the protocol Slot model carries a closed data_category tag (the compiler
   rejects an unknown value), and every specialty candidate slot that collects
   one of the owner-approved categories … declares it"*: **the tag and the
   rejection are delivered here.** Tagging the review-queue candidates is a
   follow-up: those candidates live on `merge-305` and land with INT-353, and
   this PR deliberately does not touch them.
2. *"answers to a tagged slot are written with field-level envelope
   encryption: slots.sensitivity is never SlotSensitivity.PLAIN for them … and
   a test reads the stored row back and proves no plaintext answer is at
   rest"*: **delivered.**
3. *"each category has a data-register/VERBİS row whose legal basis,
   retention, recipients and consent scope are counsel-supplied; while any of
   those fields is awaiting-human the row is incomplete and a slot carrying
   that tag is not servable (fails closed, invariant 18)"*: **the rows and the
   fail-closed gate are delivered; the counsel-supplied values are not**, by
   design. The issue therefore stays open (`Refs #555`, not `Closes`).

## 2. What was built

- **Tag.** `protocols.types.DataCategory`, a closed `StrEnum`:
  `general_health` plus the eight D3 names, each documented with its D3
  wording and specialties. `Slot.data_category` defaults to `general_health`,
  so no existing pack changes meaning. An unknown value is refused by the
  schema (`ValidationError`), by the pack loader (`PackSchemaError`), and again
  by the compiler for a slot that bypassed validation
  (`DATA_CATEGORY_UNKNOWN`).
- **Register.** `docs/legal/data-categories.yaml`: one row per non-general
  category with `{category, purpose, basis, retention, recipients,
  consent_scope, other_subject_basis_and_notice, counsel_reviewed_at}`.
  Engineering filled `purpose` from D3's own wording and nothing else. Every
  counsel field is `null`. `other_subject_basis_and_notice` is required only
  for `household`, `relatives_health` and `partner_fertility`, the three where
  D3 §3 says another person is the data subject and asks for a separate basis
  and notice. `protocols.data_register.load_data_register` refuses a missing
  file, invalid YAML, a non-mapping, an unknown top-level key, a missing or
  blank version, a missing row, a second row, a `general_health` row, an
  unknown category, a row with a key left out or an extra key, a blank
  purpose, and a review date that is not a date.
- **Serve gate.** `protocols.compile.data_categories`, called from
  `resolve_protocol`:
  - `DATA_CATEGORY_REGISTER_INCOMPLETE`: a resolved slot whose category row
    misses any counsel field is refused, with a message naming INT-349, the
    category, the register path and every missing field. With no register
    passed, the compiler reads `NO_REGISTER` (no rows), so it fails closed
    rather than open. `protocols compile` loads the committed register by
    default (`--data-register`).
  - `DATA_CATEGORY_RELAXED`: a later pack in the chain may tag a slot its
    parent left `general_health`, but may not drop or change a category the
    parent set. `_merge_chain` replaces a redeclared slot wholesale, so
    without this an untagged redeclaration would have written the answers in
    plaintext.
- **Storage.** `intake_api.store.write_slot_answer` / `read_slot_answer`, next
  to the existing writer. `slot_sensitivity(general_health)` is `PLAIN` and
  every other category is `SENSITIVE`. A sensitive answer is the existing
  `EnvelopeCipher.encrypt_field` (per-field AES-256-GCM DEK wrapped by the
  tenant KEK, no new crypto) serialised by the existing `envelope_json`. It is
  bound by AES-GCM associated data to `slots.value:<session_id>:<slot_id>`, so
  an envelope moved under another question fails authentication instead of
  reading as that question's answer. The category is a required argument
  with no default, and an unknown one raises before anything is written.
  `create_visit`'s `visit.*` facts are untouched and stay `PLAIN`.
- **Inventory.** The `slots.sensitivity` and `slots.value` annotations now
  name the mechanism and point at the register (invariant 18). The
  `data-at-rest` threat-model row lists the new test (invariant 16).

## 7. What stays open, and who closes it

- **Counsel (with the DPO, instructed by the owner).** For each of the eight
  categories:
  - the KVKK m.6 basis;
  - the retention;
  - the recipients;
  - the consent scope;
  - for `household`, `relatives_health` and `partner_fertility`, the separate
    basis and notice for the other data subject;
  - the review date.

  Until a row is complete, no slot carrying its category compiles into a
  served pack. The ask is in `docs/backlog/blockers.md` (INT-349). No
  HUMAN_BLOCKERS id is minted here.
- **Tagging the candidates.** Every specialty candidate slot that collects a
  D3 category declares it. The candidates live on `merge-305` and land with
  INT-353; tagging them is a follow-up on top of this mechanism.
- **Resolved hash.** `Slot` gained a field, so every `resolved_protocol_hash`
  moves once. Nothing is served yet: every real pack is L0, and INT-297's
  identity test already substitutes the hash before comparing. No emitted
  artefact byte other than the hash changes.
