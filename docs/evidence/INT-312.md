# INT-312 — a TC Kimlik number said aloud is masked

> **Issue.** `docs/backlog/issues.yaml` → INT-312, epic E01-platform, phase P0,
> `sec_row: identifiers`, depends on INT-102.
> **Invariants.** 6 (no PHI/PII in logs, traces, analytics or error reports),
> 14 (identifiers tokenized), and — as constraints on the fix rather than as its
> subject — 3 (raw audio is never retained), 9 (the Talker never awaits Backend
> Work), 20 (no accuracy claim outside a dated measurement of a named corpus).
> **Dates.** Red run 3 Sep 2026. Green run and gates 3 Sep 2026.

---

## 1. The defect, as INT-102 recorded it

Both redaction tiers matched `\b[1-9]\d{10}\b` — a **contiguous** eleven-digit
run:

- `packages/intake_platform/src/intake_platform/redaction.py` → `_TCKN_CANDIDATE`
- `packages/intake_platform/src/intake_platform/deid.py` → `_TCKN`

A patient reading their identity number out loud does not produce one. A
recogniser emits digit groups (`100 000 000 78`), hyphenated runs, Turkish digit
words, and mixtures of the three inside a single utterance. None of it was
masked, so the number reached wherever the transcript went.

INT-102 **asserted the gap rather than pre-normalising its own input to make
itself pass**, and committed the reproduction as `SPOKEN_TCKN_FORMS` in
`tests/safety/adversarial_corpus.py`. That corpus is the input here — read as it
stands, not joined or folded first.

---

## 2. The judgement this turns on, and the shape of the answer

Turkish digit words are ordinary clinical speech. *"On beş gündür ağrım var"* is
fifteen days of pain, not part of an identifier, and a recogniser splitting a
long number and a patient describing a duration produce overlapping token
streams. **Over-masking a symptom description is a clinical harm, not a
nuisance: a redacted duration is a lost history.** So the checksum is not an
optimisation, it is the whole discriminator — assemble candidate digit
sequences, test the published checksum, mask only what passes.

Two design commitments do the rest.

**The candidate window is token-aligned.** A *chain* is a maximal run of
digit-bearing tokens — a run of ASCII digits, or one of the ten Turkish digit
words — separated by **exactly one** space, non-breaking space or hyphen. Inside
a chain, a candidate begins at the first digit some token contributed and ends
at the last digit some token contributed. It can never begin or end inside a
token, so **it cannot consume part of a neighbouring number**:

| input | tokens (digits each) | eleven-digit windows | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Ürün barkodu 8690123456789` | `[8690123456789]` (13) | none — the only aligned window is 13 wide | untouched |
| `Oda 4 100 000 000 78` | `[4][100][000][000][78]` (1,3,3,3,2) | one, starting after the `4` | `Oda 4 [TCKN]` |
| `2026-08-30-12-34-56` | (4,2,2,2,2,2) | none: no boundary sits 11 digits from another | untouched |
| `… yedi sekiz beş gündür` | 12 one-digit tokens | one, ending before `beş` | identifier masked, `beş gündür` survives |

**One separator character, and only three of them.** A looser rule reaches
across `0,5 - 5,5 mIU/L` and across a thousands separator, which is how a
redactor becomes a number shredder by accident. `100, 000 000 78`,
`100.000 000 78`, `100  000 000 78`, `100 - 000 000 78` and `100/000 000 78` are
all left alone, each with a test.

**Two identifiers in one utterance.** The scan is left to right and resumes at
the token *after* a match, so a second identifier in the same sentence is found
by the same pass and the words between the two survive. Both the ordinary case
(`Kimlik numaram <groups>, eşiminki <words>, ikisini de yazın.` → two `[TCKN]`,
`eşiminki` intact) and the adjacent case with nothing between them
(`100 000 000 78 100 000 000 78` → `[TCKN] [TCKN]`) are asserted. The two values
are different synthetic numbers, so the test cannot pass by masking one span
twice.

**Only 0–9 are digit words.** `on`, `yirmi` and `yüz` are how durations and
readings are spoken and are deliberately not admitted. Case handling is the
canonical spelling plus its sentence-initial capitalisation — a recogniser
capitalises the first word of an utterance and nothing else — rather than
`re.IGNORECASE`, which would drag in Turkish dotted/dotless folding where `I` is
both the capital of `ı` and the ASCII capital of `i`. That ambiguity has no
right answer and no place in a redactor.

## 3. Where the normalisation lives, and where it does not

It is in `packages/intake_platform/src/intake_platform/redaction.py`, beside the
checksum it depends on, and `deid.py`'s pattern tier calls the same function. It
sees the whole utterance at rest and cannot reach the note.

Three assertions hold that placement (`tests/platform/test_spoken_identifiers.py`):

1. The module is **text in, text out** and its import set is exactly
   `{__future__, dataclasses, re, typing}` — no transport, no model, no runtime,
   so there is nothing here that could hold a buffer (invariant 3).
2. **Nothing in `services/voice-runtime/src` reaches it.** The test greps the
   whole runtime tree for `intake_platform.redaction`, `mask_spoken_tckn` and
   `find_spoken_tckn_spans` and requires zero hits. The redaction tier was
   already unreachable from the runtime; the spoken normalisation must not be
   the change that makes it reachable (invariant 9).
3. No ASR-side normalisation was added. The acceptance says this is a redaction
   concern, not an ASR feature, and INT-102's own note about "an ASR-side
   digit-run normalisation" is **declined** on that basis rather than built.

**Cost, measured** (3 Sep 2026, this laptop, `uv run python`): 20 000 ordinary
log lines through `redact_text` in **0.073 s** (~3.7 µs/line). Pathological
inputs stay linear — a 200 000-character digit run 0.006 s, 100 000 hyphen-joined
tokens 0.21 s, 420 000 characters of digit words 0.16 s. These are timings of
this machine on this day, not a property of the product.

## 4. The controls that must not move, and did not

**INT-102's checksum-failing near-miss passes unchanged.**
`test_the_near_miss_control_a_checksum_failing_number_survives` was not edited:
`ACCESSION_NUMBER_11_DIGITS` = `98765432101`, eleven digits, checksum fails, and
`redact_text` and `Deidentifier.deidentify` both return the sentence identically.
It passed in the red run and passes in the green one.

**And it was extended in the only direction that could have hidden a
regression.** `SPOKEN_ACCESSION_FORMS` is the *same four shapes* over the same
checksum-failing number, built by the same `spoken_forms()` factory as
`SPOKEN_TCKN_FORMS`. The masked case and the surviving case are now one field
apart — the checksum — so if a shape starts or stops being masked, only the
checksum can be the reason.

**A spoken duration is not an identifier.**
*"On beş gündür ağrım var, iki üç gece de uyuyamadım."* survives both tiers
untouched, and so does `beş gündür ağrım var` when it is one space from the last
digit of a masked identifier.

**The two controls that held the line before the fix still hold, asserted before
and after:**

1. `deidentify_for_egress` **fails closed entirely** without the names/addresses
   NER tier — now asserted over a spoken form as well as the written one. A
   masking improvement is not a reason to soften it.
2. `LlmSpan` has **no field a transcript could occupy**: `LLM_SPAN_FIELDS`
   contains no `transcript`/`text`/`prompt`/`utterance` member, and
   `refuse_unless_ref_shaped` refuses a sentence carrying an identifier by field
   name without echoing the value — now asserted over the digit-word form too.

**A leading zero is still refused.** `is_valid_tckn` requires `[1-9]` first, so a
TR mobile number said aloud (`sıfır beş üç iki …`) is eleven digits that this
tier does not claim; the phone pattern owns it.

**A grouped IBAN keeps its digits.** `TR33 0006 1005 1978 6457 8413 26` comes out
as one `[IBAN]` from the deid tier — no token boundary in that chain sits eleven
digits from another, and the IBAN entity wins the longest-first overlap
resolution regardless.

## 8. The measurement (invariant 20)

**Measured 3 Sep 2026 on `SPOKEN_TCKN_FORMS` as committed in
`tests/safety/adversarial_corpus.py`** — four spoken shapes of one checksum-valid
synthetic TC Kimlik number (`100000000` plus the two digits the published
algorithm forces; it belongs to nobody), each inside `utterance_with(...)`, put
through both tiers:

- **8 of 8 masked; 0 survived** (4 shapes × `redact_text` and
  `Deidentifier.deidentify`).
- On the matched control — the same four shapes over the checksum-failing
  accession number `98765432101` — **8 of 8 survived untouched; 0 masked.**

That is a measurement of that corpus on that day. It is not a catch rate, a leak
rate for speech in general, or any property of the product; four hand-authored
shapes are what a recogniser was reasoned to produce, not a sample of what one
does produce. The assertion form lives in
`test_the_measured_leak_over_the_committed_corpus_is_zero`, so the figure cannot
drift away from the run that produced it.

## 10. What was deliberately not done

- **`tests/fixtures/deid/pattern_corpus.jsonl` was not touched, and neither was
  its `sha256` in `infra/ci/no-phi-policy.yaml`.** Adding spoken rows there was
  the obvious way to raise the leak-rate corpus, and the policy file says what
  that pin is for: *"a new identifier can only reach CI's fixture inputs through
  a reviewed change to this file"* — the hash records what a **human** read.
  Re-pinning it as a side effect of a redactor change is exactly the act the
  control exists to prevent. The spoken measurement lives on the committed
  adversarial corpus instead, which is the corpus the acceptance names.
- **The OTel collector config was not extended.** Its statements are OTTL and
  cannot compute a checksum, so a spoken pattern there would be a number
  shredder at pipeline level with no discriminator. The contiguous pattern stays
  as the second, coarser layer; the checksum-bearing tier is the one that gained
  the spoken forms. Recorded in the module docstring so the asymmetry is
  deliberate rather than forgotten.
- **No ASR-side normalisation in the voice runtime**, per acceptance clause 3 and
  invariant 9. INT-102's note anticipated one; it is declined, and section 3
  says why.
- **INT-102's own `status` was not changed.** Its one open clause is *"spoken
  TCKN masking"* and this issue closes the behaviour behind it, but flipping
  another issue's status is a second issue in one PR. Reported to the
  coordinator instead.
- **No id was minted** and no blocker row was added; nothing here waits on a
  human.
