## Scope

Owner request, 4 Oct 2026, in chat: *"the admin should have metrics and telemetry of users and
questions as legal agent allows for research and enhancement purposes"*. Step 1 is a legal
decision in the INT-471 register under D25 — **synthetic and test use only; a licensed Turkish
lawyer countersigns before any real patient**. This PR delivers step 1 (LD-06) and leaves the row
`doing`; step 2, the build, follows the LD-06 verdict.

## Acceptance (verbatim from docs/backlog/issues.yaml)

- step 1 — LD-06 in the INT-471 legal register decides, sourced: whether improvement analytics
  ride the existing service purpose; whether research use is a separate purpose needing explicit
  consent; anonymised versus pseudonymised data in the admin analytics; the minimum cell size
  before a count shows; retention — anything without a source stays draft with a legal_gap, and
  anything touching consent scope goes to the owner as one recommendation
- step 2 — aggregate, de-identified analytics per question and protocol version (completion and
  drop-off, time-to-answer, repeat and clarify rate, typed versus voice share, ASR quality bands,
  share of sessions reaching RED / YELLOW / GREEN, device and locale) and user funnels (invited,
  started, finished, shared), small cells suppressed, built within the LD-06 verdict
- no free text, no PHI or identifiers, no per-patient drill-down (invariant 6, label-set scan
  test); every metric in metrics_catalog.yaml (invariant 15); feeds services/learning Loop-2
  proposal evidence only (invariant 12)

## LD-06 — outcome by question

| | Outcome | Primary source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) improvement | A purpose of its own, not the care purpose (KVKK m.4(2)(c)-(ç)). It needs no ground and no consent while only **anonymised aggregates** come out (m.28(1)(b); m.3(1)(b)); anonymisation is the condition. In the hospital deployment the vendor processes on the hospital's authority (m.3(1)(ğ)), so aggregates for the vendor's own improvement need the hospital's authorisation in the DPA; the hospital may analyse its own service under m.6(3)(e) | KVKK m.4, m.28(1)(b), m.3(1)(b), (ğ), m.6(3)(e) | sourced; DPA wording draft (vendor contract) |
| (b) research | Anonymised aggregates: same route, no consent (m.28(1)(b); KSVY m.16(1)). Record-level or pseudonymised research: a separate purpose on special-category data, on the patient's açık rıza under the existing `research` purpose (m.6(3)(a)); never in the admin panel. The m.28(1)(c) / KSVY m.16(2) route is HB-L26 | KVKK m.28(1)(b), m.6(3)(a); KSVY m.16 | sourced; m.28(1)(c) route draft (HB-L26) |
| (c) what the panel holds | **Anonymised aggregates only.** Pseudonymised rows (a keyed or hashed id) can be reversed by the key-holder, so they stay personal health data and are never admin analytics (Yönetmelik m.10(2)). Indirect identifiers count, and every combination of dimensions is a cell (Kurul guide §3.1, §3.1.3) | Yönetmelik m.10(1)-(2); Kurul guide (Kasım 2017) | sourced |
| (d) cell size | No retrieved text sets a number. The legal test is m.10(2)'s outcome; the Kurul's guide names k-anonymity without a k. The product floor — ten distinct people per cell, whole views only (files/06 §8; INT-171's `K_ANONYMITY_FLOOR`) — is a product rule | Yönetmelik m.10(2); Kurul guide §3.1.3 | legal minimum draft |
| (e) retention | Session inputs are deleted, destroyed or anonymised once their reason ends (m.7(1)); a VERBİS-registered controller keeps a retention and destruction policy (Yönetmelik m.5(1)) and destroys at most every six months (m.11(2)). Anonymised aggregates are not personal data. The periods themselves are a data-retention decision | KVKK m.7(1); Yönetmelik m.5(1), m.11(2) | periods draft (stop-and-ask) |

**Stop-and-ask (CLAUDE.md):** consent scope, the retention periods and the DPA's
controller-authorisation term. **One recommendation for the owner, on consent scope:** keep the
admin panel to anonymised aggregates only (KVKK m.28(1)(b); Yönetmelik m.10) and add no consent
purpose for it; the `learning:service_improvement` opt-out may stay as a courtesy but is not the
legal ground. Anything record-level or pseudonymised, for research or for improvement, is used only
under opt-in açık rıza (the existing `research` purpose) and never under an opt-out.
