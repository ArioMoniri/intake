## LD-06 — outcome by question

| | Outcome | Primary source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) improvement | A purpose of its own, not the care purpose (KVKK m.4(2)(c)-(ç)). It needs no ground and no consent while only **anonymised aggregates** come out (m.28(1)(b); m.3(1)(b)); anonymisation is the condition. In the hospital deployment the vendor processes on the hospital's authority (m.3(1)(ğ)), so aggregates for the vendor's own improvement need the hospital's authorisation in the DPA; the hospital may analyse its own service under m.6(3)(e), where that is necessary | KVKK m.4, m.28(1)(b), m.3(1)(b), (ğ), m.6(3)(e) | sourced; DPA wording draft (vendor contract) |
| (b) research | Anonymised aggregates: same route, no consent (m.28(1)(b); KSVY m.16(1)). Record-level or pseudonymised research: a separate purpose on special-category data, which the product never does without the patient's açık rıza under the existing `research` purpose (m.6(3)(a); files/06's research rule) — a floor, not a finding that açık rıza suffices. HB-L26 stays with counsel in full: which limb of KSVY m.16 a consented, pseudonymised research export rides (m.16(1) on data the controller anonymised, KVKK m.28(1)(b); or m.16(2) under KVKK m.28(1)(c)), whether it may ride either at all, and whether açık rıza is even an available basis or m.28 displaces it. Never in the admin panel | KVKK m.28(1)(b), m.6(3)(a); KSVY m.16 | floor sourced; the route and whether açık rıza suffices draft (HB-L26) |
| (c) what the panel holds | **Anonymised aggregates only.** Pseudonymised rows (a keyed or hashed id) can be reversed by the key-holder, so they stay personal health data and are never admin analytics (Yönetmelik m.10(2)). Indirect identifiers count, and every combination of dimensions is a cell (the Kurum's guide §3.1, §3.1.3). All-or-nothing within each view; across views, step 2 needs fixed, non-overlapping period buckets, no free-form filters and a cross-view check | Yönetmelik m.10(1)-(2); the Kurum's guide (Kasım 2017) | sourced |
| (d) cell size | No retrieved text sets a number. The legal test is m.10(2)'s outcome; the Kurum's guide names k-anonymity without a k. The product floor — ten distinct people per cell, all-or-nothing within each view (files/06 §8; INT-171's `K_ANONYMITY_FLOOR`) — is a product rule | Yönetmelik m.10(2); the Kurum's guide §3.1.3 | legal minimum draft |
| (e) retention | Session inputs are deleted, destroyed or anonymised once their reason ends (m.7(1)); a VERBİS-registered controller keeps a retention and destruction policy (Yönetmelik m.5(1)) and destroys at most every six months (m.11(2)). Anonymised aggregates are not personal data. The periods themselves are a data-retention decision | KVKK m.7(1); Yönetmelik m.5(1), m.11(2) | periods draft (stop-and-ask) |

**Stop-and-ask (CLAUDE.md):** consent scope, the retention periods and the DPA's
controller-authorisation term. **One recommendation for the owner, on consent scope:** keep the
admin panel to anonymised aggregates only (KVKK m.28(1)(b); Yönetmelik m.10) and add no consent
purpose for it; the `learning:service_improvement` opt-out may stay as a courtesy but is not the
legal ground. Anything record-level or pseudonymised is used only under opt-in açık rıza and never
under an opt-out, subject to HB-L26: for research, the existing `research` purpose; for improvement,
a consent whose text names that purpose, because açık rıza is given on a specific subject and
consents are unbundled.
