  `node-forge@<1.4.1: ^1.4.1`, `glob@>=9.0.0 <14.0.2: ^14.0.2` and
  `minimatch@>=10.0.0 <11.0.1: ^11.0.1`.

  - glob 13.0.6 → 14.0.3, under @expo/cli, @expo/config, @expo/config-plugins,

## The one remaining finding: minimatch 3.1.5 (owner-applied)

minimatch 3.1.5 sits only under eslint 9.39.5, @eslint/config-array 0.21.2,
@eslint/eslintrc 3.3.6, eslint-plugin-import 2.32.0 and eslint-plugin-react 7.37.5.
eslint is a devDependency of the root and of apps/mobile. The two plugins come in through
eslint-config-expo 10.0.0 in apps/mobile. No version clears it:

- The record's range is `introduced 0, fixed 11.0.1`, so every 3.x is in range.
- `npm view`, 31 Oct 2026: the newest eslint-plugin-import (2.32.0) and
  eslint-plugin-react (7.37.5) declare `minimatch ^3.1.2`, and the newest
  @eslint/eslintrc (3.3.7) declares `^3.1.5`. eslint 10.14.0 and @eslint/config-array
  0.23.5 moved to minimatch 10. Even so, an eslint major would leave the 3.x line in the
  tree under the two plugins and eslintrc, and minimatch 3.1.5 is the newest 3.x.
- An override to 11 would force a major into eslint's config loader and two plugins whose
  ranges exclude it, untested.

The code the record names is not in 3.x. It describes the pattern preprocessing passes of
the 10.x line (`firstPhasePreProcess` / `secondPhasePreProcess`, reached at
`optimizationLevel >= 2`). minimatch 3.1.5 ships one `minimatch.js` with no
`firstPhasePreProcess`, no `secondPhasePreProcess` and no `optimizationLevel`: grep count 0.
eslint expands only the repository's own config globs and lint targets, never patient
input. It is in neither production `expo export` bundle, and no server runtime image
installs npm packages.

That makes it an exception, and the suppression files and their pins are the owner's.
`owner-597-minimatch-exception.patch` (issue-drafts) holds it for the owner's own commit:

- one `osv-scanner.toml` `[[IgnoredVulns]]` row for GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249, with
  `ignoreUntil = 2026-11-30T00:00:00Z` (30 days) and a reason shaped like the deepmerge row;
- a MAX_OSV_SUPPRESSIONS paragraph;
- the id in the `expected` set of `test_the_osv_no_fix_exceptions_are_named_reasoned_and_expire`;
- a new `test_the_minimatch_row_is_scoped_and_expires`.

The same patch retires the node-forge 1.4.0 rows. node-forge resolves to 1.4.2, which is
outside GHSA-86w9-cpqp-85rv (trivy CVE-2026-85393), so the osv entry and the trivy row go,
and their ids join ADVISORY_IDS.
