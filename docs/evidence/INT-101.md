## 1. The design question the acceptance leaves open

"Budget" is three different objects, and the acceptance does not say which. They fail in
three different directions:

- a **cap** refuses work — and a cap that fires mid-intake abandons a patient;
- a **target** refuses nothing and raises an alert;
- an **accounting** record refuses nothing and only reports.

So each component gets exactly one kind, chosen from a committed fact rather than from
taste, and the reason is recorded beside it in
`services/voice-runtime/src/intake_voice_runtime/metering/budgets.py`:

| component | unit | kind | why that kind, and not another |
|---|---|---|---|
| `llm_extraction` | tokens | **CAP** | `extract_slots` is Layer B Work and CLAUDE.md invariant 9 puts Layer B off the spoken critical path — the Talker never awaits it, so refusing one delays no sentence. A refused extraction leaves the slot unbound, the state files/13 §11.1 already knows how to act on. |
| `tts` | chars | **TARGET** | CLAUDE.md invariant 10 speaks scripted questions, red-flag scripts and instrument items **verbatim** from `text_tr`. A character cap has exactly two possible behaviours — stop mid-sentence, or improvise something shorter — and both are worse than an overspend. |
| `asr` | audio-seconds | **ACCOUNTING** | The only cap that actually bounds audio seconds is a cap on session length, and session length is **HB-C5**, stated there as *"a clinical decision about what gets cut, not an engineering one"*. Choosing one here would be answering an open clinical blocker from an engineering issue. |

**The refusing kind fails in the direction that keeps the conversation alive.** The cap
refuses the **elective** lane only — a speculative or repeated extraction, a shadow lane —
and never the first extraction of an answer the patient has just given. That asymmetry is
the whole reason a cap is safe to have at all: a patient's answer that is never extracted
is a red-flag phrase nobody evaluated, which is the failure HB-C21 is about. `ChargeClass`
carries the distinction and `SessionLedger.admits` never refuses `OBLIGATORY`, whatever the
totals say.

**With no limit declared, the elective lane is refused rather than admitted.** That is
files/HUMAN_BLOCKERS.md rule 2 applied honestly — build the mechanism, wire the gate, make
it fail closed, leave the content empty — and it is the *cheap* closed direction here,
because the closed state costs quality and never safety.

## 4. What was built

`services/voice-runtime/src/intake_voice_runtime/metering/`, seven small modules:

| module | what it holds |
|---|---|
| `components.py` | the three components, the three units, the one-to-one pairing, and `ChargeClass` |
| `budgets.py` | `BudgetKind`, `SessionBudget` (a schema that refuses a mismatched unit, a limit on an accounting row, and an unset limit with no stated reason), and `PUBLISHED_BUDGETS` — every limit unset |
| `ledger.py` | `SessionLedger`: running totals, the admission table, and the two properties below |
| `meter.py` | `SessionMeter` and `TurnLabels` — the charging surface and the one label set |
| `adapters.py` | `MeteredExtractionGateway` (can refuse, before the endpoint is dialled) and `MeteredSpeechSynthesizer` (cannot refuse, by construction) |
| `cost.py` | `PriceBook`, `Unpriced`, `priced_cost` — and the refusal |
| `__init__.py` | the surface, and a pointer at `budgets.py` as the module to read first |

Wired at the composition root: `build_residency_tr_session` and `build_typed_session` take
an optional `meter` and wrap the two seams a session actually spends at. `None` leaves every
existing caller unchanged and is a **visible** `None` on `SessionStack` rather than a meter
this function invented — the tenant and branch labels are the caller's to supply and a
guessed label is a wrong dimension on a real series.

### The four invariants this touches, and how each is held

**Invariant 3 — no retained audio, and no derived duration that reconstructs one.** The
ledger holds a scalar per component: a total, a charge count, a refusal count. A
*per-utterance list* of audio-second charges is the turn-taking timeline of a recording
nobody kept, so there is no container one could occupy, and the attribute set is pinned by
test. Separately, `SessionMeter.charge_audio_seconds` takes a **measured** duration and
there is deliberately no byte-length overload: the ASR seam declares no sample format, so a
duration derived there would be an invented number about audio that does not exist.

**Invariant 6 — no PHI in labels.** The ledger keeps `sha256(session_id)` and never the id
(asserted against `repr` and `vars`). The one emitted series carries files/08 §4 group B's
own four labels — `tenant, profile, branch, vendor` — proved disjoint from
`PHI_RISK_LABEL_NAMES`, with the session key asserted **not** to be among the label values:
a per-session identifier on a series would make the cardinality of the exposition the number
of patients seen.

**Invariant 9 — the Talker never awaits Backend Work.** Nothing in the package is
awaitable; a test walks `vars()` of all three classes for a coroutine member. The measured
half runs on `talker_harness.FakeClock`: a metered turn and an unmetered turn produce the
*same* virtual latency, and a metered turn against a backend stalled 10 s and one stalled
10 000 s also agree — so metering did not reintroduce a wait. No `sleep` anywhere.

**Invariant 19 — one production voice profile plus `typed`.** The `vendor` label carries
the constant `self-hosted` because there is no vendor to name, and it is fixed rather than a
parameter. And the per-profile half of the acceptance is a differential run through the
composition root: the same protocol, the same answers, the same gateway double, and a
**typed session spends zero characters while a voice session spends more than zero** — with
the token axis identical across both, because the clinical work is. A budget that assumed a
voice turn would have been wrong for half the sessions.

## 5. Where every number ended up

There are only two kinds of number in this change and neither is a claim about the product.

- **Histogram buckets** for `voice_tts_chars`: `16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024`. They
  bracket the *shape of the content* (a confirmation, a scripted question, a follow-up, the
  long tail of a red-flag action script), not a target, because files/08 §4 states no target
  for this series. They are in `services/voice-runtime/src/intake_voice_runtime/metrics.py`
  beside the reason.
- **Test fixtures**: the caps and targets in `tests/voice/test_budgets.py` (`100.0`, `10.0`,
  `50.0`, `2.0`, `1.0`) and the price rates in `test_a_supplied_price_book_prices_what_it_was_given`.
  Every one is local to the test that constructs it, exists to exercise a path that is
  otherwise unreachable while the blockers stand, and none is reachable from
  `services/`.

**No measurement of the product is produced by this issue, so nothing in it engages
invariant 20 in the other direction either** — there is no cost figure, no latency figure
and no rate anywhere in `docs/regulatory/`, `docs/legal/`, a README, a docstring, a
dashboard title or patient copy. `PUBLISHED_BUDGETS` ships three `limit: None` rows.
`Unpriced` ships `rates: {}`.

## 6. The acceptance, line by line

> **"token/char/audio-second budgets enforced"** — **met as a mechanism; the limits are
> unset and no limit was invented.**

All three axes are metered per session and per profile, each with a declared kind and a
declared behaviour at exhaustion, and the enforcement path is exercised in both directions
(a declared cap admits within it and refuses past it; an undeclared cap refuses the elective
lane outright). What no file in this repository publishes is a *number* — files/10 §5 names
the control and states no figure — so every row ships `limit=None` with its reason, and the
undeclared state fails closed rather than open. Read against the property asserted rather
than the number hoped for: **the mechanism enforces, and there is nothing yet to enforce
with.** The ask is in `docs/backlog/blockers.md`.

> **"cost.per_session emitted"** — **NOT met.**

A cost is a quantity times a price. The quantities are metered. There is no price:
CLAUDE.md invariant 19 makes every model self-hosted, so there is no vendor list price to
read a rate off, and **HB-I4** (no GPU bought) and **HB-I5** (no capacity contract, every
in-country price UNVERIFIED) mean there is no GPU-hour rate to amortise either. So:
`PriceBook` ships empty; `priced_cost` raises `CostNotPriced` rather than returning a zero,
because zero is the claim that a session was free; **no `Gauge` is declared for the name
anywhere**; and the catalogue row stays `status: planned` with the reason written into its
own description. A rate written here would be exactly what rule 1 forbids — authoritative
to every later reader, with no diff that makes it look wrong.

> **"exhaustion degrades gracefully"** — **met.**

Exhaustion cuts the elective lane and nothing else. The obligatory lane is admitted whatever
the totals say (`test_an_exhausted_cap_still_admits_obligatory_work`), the TTS axis cannot
refuse at all (`test_the_metered_synthesizer_never_refuses_to_speak`, past a declared
target), the refusal lands *before* the endpoint is dialled so it does not spend the tokens
it was trying to save, and the overspend is still recorded — `over_budget` reports it rather
than the ledger declining to write down a fact.

**INT-101 therefore stays `todo`.** INT-190 (Cost module) depends on it, and `todo` is the
correct state to hold it in: a cost module whose input raises `CostNotPriced` has nothing to
render.

## 8. Findings reported rather than fixed

**F1 — Backend extraction tokens have no catalogued series, and no id was minted.**
files/08 §4 group B's `voice.talker_tokens` is *the Talker's* LLM, per turn;
`extract_slots` is Layer B Work and a different thing. Group J states
*"tokens/chars/audio-seconds by vendor"* **without an id**, and the catalogue's own trailing
comment already lists that row among the id-less prose KPIs. `analytics_view_refused_total`
is the precedent for cataloguing such a row under a name of ours — but that precedent was
exercised for a KPI whose *meaning* the spec had settled and whose id it merely omitted.
Here the spec's axis is "by vendor", and under invariant 19 there is no vendor: the axis
that survives is *component*, which is what `cost.per_session` is already labelled by. So
the shape of the series is genuinely unsettled, and minting `voice_extraction_tokens` would
be inventing a spec id in a blueprint file. The ledger counts them; nothing exports them.

**F2 — `voice.stt_audio_s` is catalogued and has no source in this tree.** Nothing in
`services/voice-runtime` sees patient audio: `TalkerSession.submit_utterance` takes a
transcript, and `HttpTranscriber.transcribe` takes opaque `bytes` addressed by
`sha256(audio)` with no declared sample rate or width. So the only way to fill this series
today would be a duration derived from a guessed format — an invented number, and one about
a recording invariant 3 says does not exist. `charge_audio_seconds` exists and takes a
measured duration; the row stays `planned` until INT-090's media plane supplies one. This is
the reason `asr` is the accounting axis in practice as well as by design.

**F3 — files/08 §4 group J is a vendor-era row.** Three of its four clauses have no subject
under the current architecture: *"by vendor"* (invariant 19 leaves none), *"GPU hours"* (no
host — HB-I4, HB-I5), *"forecast vs budget"* (no published budget). Recorded in the
catalogue's trailing comment rather than acted on, because rewriting a blueprint section is
not this issue's to do.
