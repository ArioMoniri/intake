## Items 2 and 3 — the cluster gates and the per-question mappings

**Gates.** `cluster.medical` `sorular.bagisiklik_tedavisinde_enfeksiyon` reads
`immunosuppression != 'hayır'`, and `cluster.womens_health`
`sorular.gebelik_dogrulandi` reads `pregnancy_status != 'hayır'` (CD-112
`gate_decided`). The proposals' `gen:` lines carry the same literal, so
`scripts/dev/gen_specialty_packs.py cluster_medical cluster_womens_health`
regenerates the two packs byte for byte (run; no other line moved and the tr

'hayir'`, which the register checks verbatim against the file. It now quotes

**CD-100 T4(b)'s five kadın items.** CD-112 `also_applies_to` and INT-519's
engineering list say the five items take the same literal "when T4 is
applied". T4 is not applied: its base-owner part is an open owner question
(CD-100.md, *Owner questions*, T4) and CD-106 holds the kadın reorder op on it
("Until T4 is applied (it waits on the base owner's confirmation)"). Re-gating
the five items without T4(a) would leave `gebelik_durumu` asked and read by
nothing. So the five items keep `gebelik_durumu == true`, and
`test_the_five_t4b_items_carry_todays_gate_or_the_decided_literal_never_a_third`
holds each pack gate and `gen:` line to today's gate or the decided
`pregnancy_status != 'hayır'`: whoever applies T4 cannot write `'hayir'`.

**The mappings** (`packages/protocols/src/protocols/answer_mappings.py`, new):

- Read from CD-112 through the clinical-decisions register: each `gates[*]`
  entry carrying a `hedge_mapping`. No slot, choice, label or Turkish surface is
  written in the module; a test reads its source to pin that.
- The hedges are CD-04's `*` entries (the same `AnswerStateTable` the anchor
  reads). On a mapped slot the mapping is read first, so a hedge records the
  decided choice and never UNCLEAR. A CD-04 entry that names a mapped slot
  itself is refused, because the precedence would then be a guess.
- `pmh_fh_sh.immunosuppression` → "bilmiyorum": a hedge is `mapped_from_hedge`;
  an answer still off-choice after the slot's follow-up is
  `mapped_from_unresolved` (CD-112 `unresolved_after_follow_up`).
- `pmh_fh_sh.pregnancy_status` → "emin değilim" for a hedge
  (`mapped_from_hedge`, CD-100 T4(c)). An answer still off-choice after the
  follow-up is **not** mapped: CD-100 round 4 names an UNCLEAR
  `pregnancy_status` the remaining gap that item 4's OR term closes.
- Each mapped choice must be a committed value-set member, must not be the
  plain negative, must be in the gate's `opens_on`, and the gate's
  `closes_on` must be the plain negative alone. Every way the decision could
  ask for something else is refused at load (15 refusal cases, each one change
  from a table that builds).

`test_the_restated_pregnancy_mapping_is_cd100_t4c` pins that the choice

## Items 5 and 8 — the anchor's bound by cause, and the hedged choice

- `protocols.answer_states` reads CD-04 round 3's
  `tense_anchor_follow_up_tr_asks` and validates it: a mapping with exactly the
  causes `hedge` and `no_choice`, each a whole number. It also reads the CD-08
  item `unresolved_after_follow_up` names (decision and kind).
- `SessionAnchors.follow_up_bound(cause)` is that number;
  `follow_up_bounds` hands the engine the larger (1) as the anchor's ceiling.
- The engine takes a tighter bound per answer: `IntakeEngine.clarify(step,
  bound=)`, the clarification half of `apply_extraction(step, {}, True)`,
  refused if looser than the step's own. `apply_extraction`'s signature is
  unchanged (it is pinned: values in, never a model). `clarifications_left(step)`
  reads what is unspent.
- The handler passes the cause's bound. A hedge on the anchor (alone or beside
  a guessed choice) is UNCLEAR on the Work that read it; CD-05 resolves it to
  the reference day and that Work is evaluated. An answer that binds no choice
  keeps its one follow-up.
- Item 8: the choice a hedge leaned to is kept as `hedged_choice` on the
  reading and on the record (`AnchorResolution.hedged_choice`), never bound.
  The day is the reference day, not the leaned-to band's. Repr shows neither.
- Item 9 (already INT-540's build) is pinned: a plain "Yok." to the onset is
  UNCLEAR `no_choice` with its follow-up and never retracts
  `chest_pain.present`; the near miss "Yok." to `chest_pain.present` is
  ASKED_ABSENT.
- The record of an unresolved anchor carries CD-05 round 3's label for its
  cause (`record_label_by_cause`: hedge, no_choice, session_closed).

## Items 10 and 11 — the anchor at close

- `SessionCloseHandler`: for each CD-05 anchor still NOT_ASKED whose gate is
  open, `SessionAnchors.close_never_asked` marks it. The anchor's gate is
  `chest_pain.present == true`, which is exactly CD-05's condition ("while
  chest_pain.present reads true"); shut or Unknown, nothing is read. Nothing
  binds and the slot stays NOT_ASKED ("Sorulmadı").
- The rules engine evaluates the close with the anchor read as CD-05's
  `never_asked_at_close` day (`SessionTriage._answered`, beside the UNCLEAR
  reading), so the queue's before/after compare runs it once, and a RED takes
  the existing path. The record carries `basis`/`label`
  `never_asked_at_close`.
- `protocols.anchor_choices` reads CD-05 round 3's `never_asked_at_close`
  (active, NOT_ASKED, the day, the label, the CD-08 item) and refuses a reading
  less acute than a choice.
- Item 11: an anchor asked and unanswered at close is UNCLEAR with the
  `session_closed` label (the INT-540 build plus the label), and is handed to
  the CD-08 hook at close.
- INT-540's `test_near_miss_an_anchor_that_was_never_asked_is_not_resolved_at_session_end`
  is replaced by `test_never_asked_anchor_evaluated_at_close`. Its near miss is
  `chest_pain.present = hayır`; a second near miss is `chest_pain.present`
  never answered.
- Golden family 17 (6 cases): never asked and session_closed each fire K2 and
  the SARI sibling; the near misses (pain denied, pain never answered) fire
  nothing.

## The CD-08 hook

The CD-08 `chest_pain_arms_unresolved` item is built by INT-547 (#927, branch
`int-547-cd04-arms-inbox-item`, not on origin when this was written). Its
author was told the shape below and agreed to plug in rather than add a second
port.

- `AnchorUnresolved(anchor, label, kind, decision_ids)`: ids and labels only.
  `kind` and the decisions are read from CD-04 `unresolved_after_follow_up`
  and CD-05 `never_asked_at_close.inbox_item`, never written.
- `AnchorUnresolvedHook.anchor_unresolved(trigger)`: called once per anchor
  when its cause's bound is spent (at once for a hedge), at close for
  `session_closed`, and at close for `never_asked_at_close`.
- Both composition roots take `anchor_unresolved=`; omitted, every trigger is
  still kept on `SessionAnchors.unresolved_triggers`. No inbox entry is
  invented here.
- One interaction is recorded for INT-547: its generic trigger (UNCLEAR and the
  follow-up asked at least `unclear_follow_up_tr_asks` times) never sees a
  hedge on the anchor any more, because a hedge now gets no follow-up. That
  case arrives only through this hook.
