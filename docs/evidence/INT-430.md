# INT-430: agent council for dynamic questioning, delivered as a post-bind verifier

**Epic:** E05-voice · **Phase:** P0 · **Sec-Row:** `llm-boundary` · **GitHub:** #732 ·
**Depends on:** INT-081, INT-083, INT-092, INT-095, INT-101 · **Branch:** `int-430-agent-council` ·
**ADR:** ADR-036 (Proposed; the owner must ratify it) · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The ask

Owner, 2 Oct 2026, verbatim:

> "the structure should be dynamically asking question with regard to previous questions patients docs added and answers to previous question and so on as described also in roadmap with any model could be an agent council which should work both with on prem and with api models"

Two challenger reviews of INT-428/INT-430, one medical and one AI/ML, were made binding by the coordinator on 2 Oct 2026. Together they reshaped the council into a **verifier**:

- Primary extraction feeds triage exactly as before.
- The council verifies afterwards, on red-flag and numeric slots only.
- It may only append a scripted confirm or clarify step, compare-and-swapped on the slot revision. It may not skip or reorder.
- Adjudication is asymmetric.
- The free follow-up ranker and the critic are cut. The document reader stays the existing `parse_document` Work.
- Multi-member mode is off until studies exist.
- No self-reported confidence is used.
- The admission gate sheds under load. The verifier is an elective charge on HB-O8. Each session gets its own `cache_salt`, and span attributes come from an allow-list.

ADR-036 records the result, and why.

## What changed

- **`clinical_agent/council/`** (new; no network client, per `TestNoLlmInside`):
  - `scope.py`: the verifiable refs, taken from the served pack. Covers red-flag `when` atoms and numeric slots. Excludes tense anchors, `text_only` slots and every other slot.
  - `readings.py`: the closed member schema, with verbatim spans, the Turkish hedge enum and no confidence field. Instructions are built from the schema alone.
  - `policy.py`: self-consistency and multi-member modes, gated on evidence, with per-member `LaneBinding` hashes.
  - `lanes.py`: the structural port onto INT-428's role router.
    - Pins are taken per session, and drift is refused.
    - `mock` is unrepresentable.
    - On a real-PHI deployment, `hosted` is unrepresentable.
  - `transport.py`: one OpenAI-compatible payload for self-hosted and hosted alike, plus a recorded replay transport that refuses a mismatched recording.
  - `orchestrator.py`: concurrent draws with a clock deadline. Refusal, truncation and empty output map to one `abstained` class. Spans are emitted as closed `LlmSpan` fields.
  - `adjudicate.py`: the pure, asymmetric verdict. Its only actions are `REASK_POSITIVE`, `CONFIRM_BACK` and `CANDIDATE_FLAG`.
  - `ledger.py`: PHI-free proposal events, field records with provenance and no confidence, and add-only candidate flags as `LlmCandidateTriage`.
  - `desk.py`: the Backend Work.
    - It is a sink that never blocks and an async pump.
    - It admits work through the budget and the admission gate.
    - It applies verdicts through the state lane (`plan_follow_ups`), compare-and-swapping each action and re-running triage.
    - A late verdict becomes an addendum plus a triage re-run.
- **Engine (`fsm.py`, `appends.py`, `plan.py`, `delta.py`):**
  - A per-slot revision.
  - `gate_open`.
  - CAS `append_verification`, which uses only the slot's own `ask_tr` or `confirm_tr` and shares the two-attempt limit.
  - Rebinding verification re-entries.
  - `red_flag_refs` and `tense_anchor_refs` on the plan.
  - Delta never pre-fills a red-flag slot or a tense anchor (`safety_full_ask_fact_keys`).
- **`extraction/handler.py`:** an optional `verifier` sink, called after the primary bind. The default `None` leaves every composed session unchanged.
- **`intake_platform.observability.spans`:** `SpanKind.VERIFICATION`.
- **`metrics.py` + `observability/metrics_catalog.yaml`:** six `council_*` series, all `live`.
- **Docs:**
  - ADR-036.
  - A files/10 §9 row.
  - The `llm-boundary` section of `docs/security/threat-model.md`.
  - The backlog row, the three pinned counts and the regenerated files/11.
  - `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

The clinical decisions the reviews raised are not authored here. INT-432 owns the register (`packages/protocols/decisions/CD-NN.yaml`), and the verifier's safe default needs none of them (ADR-036, "Physician decisions").
