## Scope, stated once

Owner decision **D18** (files/00_STATE.md §3): agents resolve open **clinical** decisions from
current guidelines and openEHR CKM, independently agent-verified, **for simulated-patient stress
testing only. A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.** D18 does not
reach a decision whose decider is the owner, the base owner or counsel: those parts are recorded
as owner questions and never decided by an agent.

### Decisions worth naming

- **Child feeding (CD-100 T9/T48, CD-103 L75, CD-104 L303–304; closes CD-10's open construct).**
  The two authors decided it differently; the verifier adjudicated from the sources: both are
  kept, ungated, as different thresholds — `az_beslenme` (*"Çocuğunuz her zamankinden daha az
  besleniyor ya da içiyor mu?"*; NG143 1.2.6 amber "poor feeding in infants"; under two months
  it is IMCI's young-infant "Not feeding well") and `icemez_ememez` (*"Çocuğunuz hiçbir şey
  içemez ya da ememez hâle geldi mi?"*; IMCI 2–59 m danger sign "NOT ABLE TO DRINK OR
  BREASTFEED"). A "not at all" answer is never recorded as "hayır".
- **Pregnancy gate (CD-100 T4).** The five pregnancy items open on `pregnancy_status !=
  'hayir'`, and "bilmiyorum" or any hedge on that slot is the "emin değilim" answer, never
  UNCLEAR — an UNCLEAR would leave the gate Unknown, and Unknown opens nothing (files/13 §9.2).
- **Fetal movements (CD-105 wave7 L88).** "Artık hissetmiyorum", "durdu", "kesildi" record
  PRESENT_QUALIFIED and open the reduced-movements item (NG201 1.2.34).
- **Post-period bleeding (CD-105 wave6 L57).** Bleeding or spotting after periods stopped is
  PRESENT_QUALIFIED and reaches the note, never ASKED_ABSENT (NG12 1.5.12, 1.5.14). No level set.
- **Gate rule.** A specialty gate may read only base slots and the pack's own earlier slots
  (`scripts/dev/gen_specialty_packs.py`); CD-104 L306/L428 and CD-101 T81 re-declare the
  cluster's support item in the pack before gating on it.
- **Non-asserting ids.** `nedenini_bilmedigi_ates` replaces `aciklanamayan_ates`: the patient's
  report is never asserted as NG12's clinician-defined "unexplained" (CD-14).
- **Mental health Q17/Q18 rejected (CD-103).** Persecutory ideation and risk to others stay
  held before the visit: PH50 Rec 6, NG10 1.2.8–1.2.10 and CG115 1.2.1.1 assign that enquiry to
  trained staff; the category questions go to the owner.

No change removes or relaxes a red-flag rule (invariant 7): no rule gate slot is left unaskable,
and every removed or renamed slot was checked at `151cf34f` for readers.
