## The exploration (4 Oct 2026), kept as the record

The rest of this file is the 4 Oct record of the three variants behind the flag. The blob, the field and `VoiceOrbParts.tsx` it names no longer exist (see above).

### What changed

- **The plan (`lib/stage/orbVariants.ts`, pure).** Three bodies, `blob`, `field` and `lens`, each built from the S1 motion that `stageOrbMotion` returns for `orbStateFor`'s state. A turn is the S1 turn times a factor of at least 1. The breath and the voice gain are S1's, passed through. Every part is drawn inside the orb's own circle, and every part turns one way. The parser `orbVariantFrom` reads anything it does not know as `classic`, the sphere.
- **The flag.** `app.config.ts` copies `EXPO_PUBLIC_ORB_VARIANT` into `extra.orbVariant`. That is the INT-328 mechanism the API base already uses: build-time config, from the environment only. The production profile never carries a variant. `lib/stage/expoOrbVariant.ts` reads it once and parses it. The dialogue hands it to the one PresenceOrb beside the S1 motion, and PresenceOrb hands it to `VoiceOrb`.
- **The drawing (`components/stage/VoiceOrb.ios.tsx`).** `classic` renders the sphere's body, untouched. A variant renders `VariantBody`, which draws from its plan:
  - `blob`: the sphere's turning colours under a mask. The mask is a core and four lobes, white on black, melted by a blur, cut by a soft contrast threshold and read as alpha. While speaking, the lobes swell out and back with the breath.
  - `field`: 46 points on four face-on rings round a soft nucleus. The inner rings turn a little faster.
  - `lens`: a sphere lit from the top left, a darker rim, two rim lights that travel round the rim, a crisp edge, and the clear lens where the plan says glass.
  All three run their loops in SwiftUI, on the render server. Every period is the plan's, and nothing is awaited or reported back. Every gradient, glow and lens of the variants is drawn in this one file, so the gradient allow-list (ADR-039 S2) does not grow. Their plain parts (the loops, a lobe, a ring of points) are in `components/stage/VoiceOrbParts.tsx`, which draws no gradient and no glass and which only the iOS orb imports. Off iOS, every variant is the still disc.
- **No new dependency, and nothing copied.** Every source is web code. The licences are in `library-patterns.md` §6.1, each sha256 taken from the raw blob on 4 Oct 2026.

### Found on the simulator, and fixed

- **The blob filled its whole circle.** With the first lobe sizes, the threshold grew the melted shape past the circle, so the mask cut nothing and the blob was the sphere. A steep contrast also showed SwiftUI's low-resolution blur as steps. The lobes are smaller now, the cut is softer (contrast 4) and a hairline blur smooths it.
- **`strokeBorder` draws a rectangle unless told otherwise.** The rim and its lights came out square. Every stroke now names its shape.
- **The first particle field read as an atom symbol.** It had three tilted, squashed rings, and squashing also flattened every point into a dash. It is now four face-on rings: round points and a slow vortex.
