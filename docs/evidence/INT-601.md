# INT-601 — patient app design fixes from the November 2026 audit

GitHub #1026 · Sec-Row: `mobile` · branch `int-601-design-fixes`, on `origin/main` `5d6984a7`
plus the audit commit `3bf12408` (`docs/design/audit-2026-11/README.md`, Part A findings 1-7).

## 1. Acceptance, restated verbatim

1. Bugün shows the next upcoming visit, never a past one. After the intake is finished it reflects
   the delivered state, not "Muayeneye hazırlanın" at %70 (`today.tsx`).
2. A visit detail opened from Bugün always has a working back control. Its primary action is never
   hidden under the tab bar (`visits/_layout.tsx`, `[visitId]/index.tsx`).
3. Light-theme `inkFaint` and the `success` pill text meet WCAG AA, at least 4.5:1, asserted by a
   test in both themes (`theme.ts`).
4. A selected chip is distinguishable without colour (check mark or shape change) in both intake
   lanes, with an accessibilityState.selected test.
5. The multi-line answer field uses a rounded-rectangle radius, not a pill.
6. The OTP code boxes fit at 320pt width, scale with Dynamic Type, do not mask the digits, and the
   countdown carries its unit.
7. On review, "Düzenleyin" and the share toggle each meet the minimum tap target, with spacing
   between them, and the share toggle sits in the same position on every card.

## 2. Item 1 is INT-594's, and is not duplicated here

Open PR #1013 (INT-594, `origin/int-594-today-truthful` at `37adf7b8`) rewrites `today.tsx` around
a pure `todayFocus` (`apps/mobile/src/lib/today.ts`):

- it picks only upcoming visits (`splitVisits(visits, todayIso).upcoming`, soonest first), so a past
  visit is never "Sıradaki vizit";
- the state is `effectiveStatus` (the draft on this phone plus the server's confirmed delivery,
  INT-587), so a delivered note shows as sent with "Viziti açın";
- the prep gauge (the "%70") and the unmeasured "≈3 dk" are removed.

Its `lib/today.test.ts` tests all three, with near misses. That covers item 1 in full, so this
branch does not touch the focus, the gauge or the CTA copy. Nothing in item 1 is left uncovered.
Item 1 is delivered when #1013 merges. Open PR #1023 (INT-598, Vizitlerim chips) touches
`visits/index.tsx` only, and nothing here overlaps it.

The one edit this branch makes to `today.tsx` is item 2's: its two pushes into the visits stack
gain `{ withAnchor: true }`. That is one line in the hunk #1013 also rewrites (`next.id` becomes
`focus.visit.id`), so whichever PR lands second resolves a one-line conflict. The fix is to keep
#1013's line and add the options argument.

## 4. Two readings to flag

- **`accessibilityState.selected` (item 4).** The chips are `checkbox` (multi-select) and `radio`
  (single-select) roles, and their platform state is `checked`. Adding `selected` too would make
  VoiceOver and TalkBack announce the state twice. The test (`chip.test.ts` › "the
  accessibilityState names the selection, both ways") asserts that the rule's `accessibilityState`
  is `{checked: true}` when selected and `{checked: false}` when not. It also asserts `selected` is
  absent on purpose, and both screens are wired to that rule (`designAudit.spec.ts`). If the owner
  wants the literal `selected` key, it is one line in `chipLook`.
- **Dark `inkFaint` (item 3).** The acceptance names the light theme. The dark hex changed only so
  that the both-themes AA test holds on the raised ground. Reverting it fails one assertion (see
  the mutation table).
