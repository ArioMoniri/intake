**How the mapping is kept honest.** The measure ids are parsed out of the snapshot (items 1
and 6 have no letters; items 2–5 are lead-ins whose lettered sub-items are the measures,
Turkish letters included), so a skipped or invented measure is refused. A quote that differs
from the snapshot by one character is refused. A control path must exist inside the
repository (an absolute path or `..` is refused), a test id must name a real test function
(parsed by AST, never imported), a metric must be catalogued in
`observability/metrics_catalog.yaml` **with the status the catalogue gives it** (a planned
metric declared live is refused), a probe must be a registered component probe. A gap's owner
must be in the file's own owner vocabulary and its ask exactly one question; an HB id or
issue it cites must exist. There is no `status` field: a measure with no control is
`not_implemented`, any open gap makes it `partial`, and only a control with a live signal and
no gap is `implemented`. A measure whose controls have no live signal must own that as a
`signal` gap.

**The packet** (`make evidence-2018-10`, JSON on stdout; exit 2 with every problem listed when
the mapping has drifted) carries five sections — `decision_source`, `measures_2018_10` (one
row per measure, derived status, quote), `controls`, `gaps`, `summary` — every cell through
the invariant-47 guard, sealed with the INT-189 content digest (base64url), and source digests
shown in groups of eight so no cell is a 64-hex chain-hash shape (HB-I12).

## 4. Findings worth having

1. **files/06 §1 misquoted the decision.** Its KVKK-12 + 2018-10 row said the list was
   "implemented verbatim" and named "test-data masking", which the decision does not contain,
   while omitting its physical-environment items (4a, 4b), its transfer-channel items (5a–5ç)
   and the pointer to the Kurum's guide (6). Corrected in place, dated, with the mapping named
   as the measure-by-measure source.
2. **The console does not require a second factor.** files/08 §2.3 makes MFA mandatory for
   every console role and WebAuthn mandatory near patient content; the verifier
   (`intake_platform.admin.oidc`) checks no `amr`/`acr` claim and the committed Keycloak realm
   configures no OTP or WebAuthn policy. For measure 3(e) that is the largest engineering gap.
3. **No mechanism exists for measures 2(ç) and 2(d)'s organisational half.** The quarterly
   access review of files/08 §2.7 is not generated anywhere (`admin_access_review_age_days` is
   planned), and neither the one-action offboarding nor the 60-day auto-disable of §2.4 exists.
4. **Encryption at rest is proven only by tests.** No catalogued signal shows production is
   still writing special-category fields under envelope encryption; the mapping owns that as a
   `signal` gap rather than letting 3(a) read as done.
5. **`lan_plaintext` inbound feeds.** INT-162 lets a plaintext hospital-LAN feed be registered
   behind its own recorded approval. Measure 5(c) names VPN or sFTP between servers in
   different physical environments; whether such a feed may ever carry special-category data
   is recorded as a counsel determination.
6. **HB-O6 looks stale.** Its three one-line fixes to `.github/workflows/security.yml`
   (gitleaks `pull-requests: read`, the osv-scanner subdirectory action, `trivyignores`) are
   present on main, while the HUMAN_BLOCKERS row still reads open. This issue neither cites nor
   edits it; the coordinator may verify and close it.
