## What changed

**`/compliance`** (`src/compliance/{load,copy,console}`, `app/compliance/page.tsx`)

- It reads the five INT-189 routes with the caller's bearer, through the INT-180 gate (compliance
  officer, auditor, platform owner). The first refusal stops the load.
- The audit search asks for the last 24 hours only (`since=`), so the page never pulls the whole
  chain. It shows the newest 100 rows.
- Tiles:
  - the data transfer register, each row with its lawful basis and notification deadline, worded
    in tr/en from the raw deadline (`VendorRow.notificationDue` was added for that);
  - consent coverage;
  - the open data subject requests, each on time or overdue;
  - the audit rows;
  - the evidence bundle's outline.
- **k-floor:** a consent count from 1 to 9 renders as "10'dan az" / "fewer than 10". The note above
  the table says why. 0 is a known zero and shows.
- A body the view-model refuses — a transfer with no basis, an evaluable cell, a chain hash — paints
  no tile.

**`/models`** (`src/models/{load,copy,console,actions}`, `app/models/page.tsx`, `app/models/action/route.ts`)

- It reads `GET /admin/models`. The release gate is the first tile, as files/08 §6 requires. Then
  come the registry rows in registry order: licence, probe, budget, fallback, PHI path, findings
  and measurements, each through `renderMeasurement`.
- A body that claims a clear release over a blocking row is refused (`InconsistentReleaseState`).
- The benchmark is the tier-1 `admin.models.benchmark.run`:
  - each lane the API enables is a plain form posting to `/models/action`;
  - the route refuses a cross-site post and sends exactly `{kind}` to
    `POST /admin/models/{component}/benchmark`, with an id-shaped component and a lane of latency
    or quality;
  - a lane the API disables is a disabled button whose title carries the API's reason. Quality is
    disabled today: there is no named corpus (HB-C10, invariant 20).
  - Outcomes come back as `?result=` codes only.

**`/experience`** (`src/experience/{load,copy,console}`, `app/experience/page.tsx`) and **`GET /admin/experience`**

- The issue named `/v1/admin/experience/wait-times` as the API. That route serves the patient-token
  `Principal` (`require_admin`), and the console's Keycloak bearer cannot reach it. The view-model
  in `src/experience` also expects `GET /admin/experience` (CSAT, NPS, pulse, filters and the SDS-01
  wait-time export), which had a recorder in `scripts/dev` but no route.
- So the console route is built in `services/api/src/intake_api/admin/experience_console.py`. It is
  registered in the descriptor table, audited like every console read, and readable by
  `tenant_admin`, `tenant_clinical_responsible`, `clinical_lead`, `platform_owner` and `auditor`.
  It reads the same platform rollups the wait-time route uses (`csat_rollup`, `nps_rollup`,
  `pulse_rollup`, `wait_rollup` with the consent catalogue) at serve time, in the tenant's RLS
  session.
- **The window is required, with no default** (invariant 20): `422 invalid_window` with reason
  `window_required`, `window_not_iso8601`, `window_not_utc_offset` or `window_not_forward`. The page
  always sends one: its `?from=`/`?to=` days, or the 30 days before today. It shows that window
  and offers a GET form to change it.
- **The tenant is the caller's claim**. `?tenant=` below `platform_owner` is refused by the gate.
  A claim that names no tenant UUID reads the empty state, never another tenant's rows.
- **Below k ≥ 10 only the count survives.** The rollups enforce it. The page shows the count and
  says why, and the view-model refuses a body that breaks it.
- `scripts/dev/record_experience_fixture.py` now records the e2e fixture through the route's own
  `experience_payload` over empty rollups. A test holds the committed fixture equal to it, so the
  route and the fixture cannot drift. The server notes changed wording, and the fixture was
  re-recorded.

**Shell.** All three pages are in the sidebar:

- Deneyim under İzleme, Modeller under Hizmetler, Uyum under Yönetim;
- with new glyphs (chart, cube, shield) and tr/en labels;
- Palette B tokens only, no `style` attribute.
