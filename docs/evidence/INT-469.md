## Why

The legal lane's decision **LD-01** (INT-471, GitHub #800; branch `int-471-legal-lane`,
`docs/legal/decisions/LD-01.md`; the legal reviewer agreed the outcome) decides that the allied
cluster's report-purpose item is not carried to Adli Tıp. A forensic examination and report are
made on a competent authority's proper request and must answer that authority's questions, so the
purpose of an adli rapor is the authority's, not something the person examined declares. Its legal
basis, all from Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği (RG 19/5/2026, sayı 33258):

- Madde 40(4): "Adli muayene ve sonucunda rapor tanzimi işleminin, yetkili bir resmî makamın usulünce
  talebi üzerine yapılması esastır."
- Madde 50(4): "Adli makamlar tarafından sorulan sorular muhakkak cevaplandırılır."
- Madde 50(1) and 48(1): an adli rapor rests on the physician's own findings, never on another
  person's statement, so no intake answer enters one.

LD-01's values: `allied_q3_rapor_amaci.carried_to_adli_tip: false`;
`allied_q3_rapor_amaci.other_allied_branches`: unchanged. `adli_tip_replacement_item` stays draft,
so nothing is asked in its place.

Before this change, `taxonomy/branches.yaml` routed `adli_tip` to `allied_and_reports`, and
`review/specialty/adli_tip.yaml` extended `cluster.allied_and_reports`, which declares
`sorular.rapor_amaci`. Only L0 and HB-C1 kept the item unserved.

## Mechanism

This is a `suppress_slot` op, from the closed op set of files/12 §3.1. It is the sanctioned DSL
mechanism, and it is legal on this slot:

- `rapor_amaci` is not `must_ask`. A suppression of a must_ask slot would be a hard compile error
  (files/12 §3.2; `validator.py` `SUPPRESS_MUST_ASK`).
- No red-flag rule reads it in `when` or as a `tense_anchor`, so INT-382's
  `RULE_READS_REMOVED_SLOT` does not fire.
- No gate reads it either. Its siblings are gated on `rapor_icin_basvuru`, which stays.

The op comes from the protocol source, not from a hand edit:

1. **Generator** (`scripts/dev/gen_specialty_packs.py`). It had no path for pack-level ops, so it
   gains a `pack: suppress=<section>.<slot>[,…]` option. The option emits one
   `{op: suppress_slot, pointer: /sections/<section>/slots/<slot>}` per target, after `sections`
   and before `red_flags`. The parent chain is `base.intake` plus the extended cluster (served if
   promoted, else its review candidate). The generator refuses four kinds of target before
   anything is written:
   - a target the parent chain does not declare;
   - a must_ask target;
   - a slot the pack declares itself;
   - a slot that a surviving gate reads (that question would silently never be asked).
2. **Proposal** (`docs/evidence/proposals/adli_tip.md` §3). It carries
   `pack: suppress=sorular.rapor_amaci`, and a dated paragraph citing LD-01 and the articles
   above.
3. **Candidate** (`packages/protocols/review/specialty/adli_tip.yaml`). It was regenerated by the
   generator, and its only diff is the `ops` block. Regenerating all 99 proposals was
   byte-stable for the other 95 candidates (96 OK, 3 skipped as before).

The served tree is untouched: `specialty/adli_tip.yaml` stays the `generate-l0` draft.

## The resolution head

`resolve_branch` serves a branch's own L1+ pack, else its cluster pack (files/09 §2). If
`cluster.allied_and_reports` were promoted before `specialty.adli_tip`, an Adli Tıp booking would
fall back to the cluster pack and be asked `rapor_amaci` after all. A test pins that this never
happens on the served tree. Today nothing is served for either pack, and the coverage matrix is
unchanged. A synthetic test shows the guard firing on an early-promoted cluster, and the near miss
shows the other allied branches may still fall back to it. The constraint for a future promotion
is therefore explicit: adli_tip must be signed with, or before, its cluster. Invariant 13 still
holds, because adli_tip then resolves to its own L1+ pack.
