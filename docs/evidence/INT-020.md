### The held clause (1c): data-subject notice templates

- **What is missing:** the text of the data-subject breach notice, which the acceptance puts
  in the runbook.
- **Why it is not built:** it is a legal text. Legal texts render only from docs/legal/ with
  hash binding (CLAUDE.md invariant 18), and legal texts are on the stop-and-ask list. Nor
  can an agent even list the notice's required fields: no source snapshot of Kurul Kararı
  2019/10 is committed (docs/legal/sources holds only `kvkk-2018-10`), and a field list
  from memory is exactly the invented legal value invariant 17 forbids.
- **Who answers:** counsel. The runbook's Roles section names owner + counsel as the only
  people who decide a notification, and they are the only consumers of the INT-214 packet.
- **The one sentence they must answer:** *"Will counsel supply the Turkish data-subject
  breach notice template with its Kurul Kararı 2019/10 source, or does that template belong
  to INT-214's breach packet rather than to INT-020?"*
- **Why the second option exists:** files/06 §5 lists "breach packet templates (Board form,
  data-subject, USOM, hospital)" among the legal artefacts of INT-210…INT-227. INT-214's
  title names "data-subject notices", and INT-214 `depends_on: [INT-020]`. Holding INT-020
  open until INT-214's content lands is therefore circular. Moving the clause is a change to
  acceptance, which is for the coordinator or owner, not this PR.
- **Recorded, not minted:** no HUMAN_BLOCKERS row covers breach notice templates. HB-L6 is
  the break-glass notice window only. No HB id is minted here; allocating one is the
  coordinator's.
