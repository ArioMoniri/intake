# INT-424: ElevenLabs delivery profiles v2 — sample-tuned naturalness + expressive-model ADR proposal

**Epic:** E05-voice · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `cloud-clipgen` · **GitHub:** #719 ·
**Depends on:** INT-415 · **Branch:** `int-424-voice-profiles-v2` · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The ask

On 2 Oct 2026 the owner asked for the questions to sound more natural and emotionally attuned,
while staying medical and professional. That means tuning the INT-415 delivery profiles from
rendered samples of catalogue sentences, and writing a proposal on expressive models and emotion
audio tags. These are voice-tuning values, not clinical values. Nothing here sets a triage level
or anything a clinician reads.

## What changed

- **Profiles v2.** `voice_clips.delivery.PROFILE_VERSION = 2`, and every row is `@v2`. The
  version is in every profile key, `delivery.<context>@v2`, and so in every clip object key. A
  v1 clip is therefore a cache miss and is re-synthesised, never replayed. A take recorded under
  `@v1` is refused for the `@v2` profile.
- **Every v2 row is one of the rendered candidates.**
  `tests/voice/test_render_delivery_samples.py::test_the_shipped_v2_row_was_rendered` checks
  that, so no value can ship that has no clip behind it.
- **The sample-render tool**, `scripts/voice/render_delivery_samples.py`. It renders 20
  catalogue sentences, two or three per context. Each is spoken on the merged v1 row and on three
  candidates, which makes 80 clips.
  - **Through the existing gateway.** It uses `tts_gateway_from_env()` and the ADR-028 adapter
    with the model pinned and the normaliser off. The key and base URL are read from the
    environment only, and nothing prints them.
  - **Verbatim lines only.** Every line is checked to appear verbatim in the file it names.
  - **Spend-capped.** `--plan` is the default and dials nothing. `--live` needs
    `EVAL_BUDGET_USD`, an estimate inside it, and an estimate inside a 5 USD ceiling.
  - **What it writes.** WAVs, `index.html` (labelled), `blind.html` (coded and shuffled) and
    `index.json` (settings and signal statistics), all under the gitignored
    `.cache/voice-samples/int-424/`.
- **Text-side pacing, and the markup gate.**
  - **The gate.** `assert_speakable_tr` now also refuses `<`, `>`, `[` and `]` by raising
    `MarkupInScriptError`. Its sibling `NumeralInScriptError` shares the new base
    `UnspeakableScriptError`. So no SSML break, whose duration is a numeral (invariant 22), and
    no audio tag, which is unauthored words (invariant 10), can ride inside verbatim `text_tr` on
    any lane.
  - **The dev route** answers 422 `not_speakable` before the catalogue door and before any dial.
  - **Punctuation is admitted unchanged.** That covers the ellipsis, dash, semicolon and comma.
  - **The rules for lead-in copy** are in `docs/voice/on-prem-tts-wiring.md` §3, "Text-side
    pacing": punctuation only, on device-composed lead-ins only (INT-414), each lead-in its own
    utterance, and never a mark added to a scripted item.
- **ADR-034 (Proposed)** evaluates `eleven_v3` and `eleven_v4` against invariants 10, 21 and
  22, ADR-028 and the latency budget. It recommends staying on `eleven_multilingual_v2` and lists
  the gates that come before any model change. Nothing switches model.
- **The guide.** `docs/voice/on-prem-tts-wiring.md` gains:
  - the v2 table;
  - the text-side pacing rules;
  - "The ElevenLabs sample render (owner, INT-424)" in §8, with the one-line command, where the
    samples land, the budget cap, and how a choice feeds back as a version bump;
  - §10, pointed at ADR-034.

**Why each pick.** The vendor documents that lower stability gives a broader, more emotional
range, and that style adds exaggeration at some cost to stability. So the warm contexts take a
little more range and style, within the 0.3 ceiling. The neutral contexts keep their steadiness.

- **welcome and closing** take the warmest candidate that keeps style under the ceiling.
- **acknowledgement** gets a touch more warmth at an unchanged pace.
- **routine** loosens slightly from v1's near-default flatness, still professional.
- **sensitive** is gentler and slower, with style held at 0.
- **instrument** keeps v1. Validated items gain nothing from range, and its candidates exist to
  confirm that.
- **red** is steadier still, with style 0.

Rules fixed in code and asserted by tests, not by ear:

- RED and instrument have style 0, are the most stable rows, and are never less stable than v1.
- Welcome and closing are the warmest rows.
- Sensitive is slower and steadier than routine.
- Acknowledgement never drags.
- Similarity and speaker boost are constant across rows.
- Every value is inside the vendor's bounds.

## Sources — ElevenLabs (retrieved 2026-10-02)

- Voice settings per model: Multilingual v2 has all five. v3 has style only. v4 has stability
  and similarity only. Speed runs 0.7 to 1.2.
  <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech>
- Prompting best practices:
  - the SSML `<break time="x.xs" />` works on v2 only, up to 3 s;
  - v3 and v4 read no SSML;
  - ellipses and dashes pace text, "less consistent" than breaks;
  - tags "may be interpreted as a request for a sound effect";
  - narrative emotional guides are spoken aloud.

  <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices>

- Models: Turkish is listed for v3, Multilingual v2 and Flash v2.5. v3 is "not suitable" for
  real time. <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/models>
- Eleven v4 (`eleven_v4`, `eleven_v4_turbo`): stability and similarity only, no SSML, audio
  tags. <https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/eleven-v4>
- API price, 0.08 USD per 1,000 characters for Multilingual v2 and v3:
  <https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api>

What the new tests prove:

- **The v2 table:** version 2 on every row; every context resolves to `delivery.<context>@v2`;
  RED and instrument have style 0 and are never less stable than v1.
- **The cache key:** for every context, the v1 → v2 bump changes both the profile key and the
  clip object key. A near-miss control holds the settings equal and changes only the version,
  and the key still moves. A `@v1` take is refused for a v2 profile.
- **The markup gate:** five SSML and tag shapes are refused before the wire. A near-miss control
  (ellipsis, dash, semicolon, comma) reaches the wire byte for byte. The dev route answers 422
  `not_speakable` with zero dials.
- **The sample tool:**
  - the corpus is verbatim in its sources, and a missing line or a one-line context is refused;
  - the voice equals the app's `GEORGE_VOICE_ID`;
  - every candidate is a valid pinned-model profile, and RED and instrument candidates have
    style 0;
  - every shipped v2 row is a rendered candidate;
  - the spend cap refuses no budget, a NaN or a too-small budget, and anything over the ceiling;
  - the signal statistics measure a synthetic clip correctly (pause count, edges, pitch);
  - a fake gateway render writes 80 WAVs and both pages, and the blind page names no setting;
  - `--analyse` rebuilds identical pages from disk.
