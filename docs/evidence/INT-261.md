# INT-261 — voice and text get mixed up because a lost voice is treated as a lost conversation

The field report was five words: *"voice and text based dyamic questoins gets
mixed up"*. It is one screen, and the screen is unambiguous once you see it.

## What the owner was seeing

Reproduced twice on iPhone 16 Pro (`47783B7E-775E-4B01-A8A3-9AA2633C6859`),
Ayşe Yılmaz → İç Hastalıkları visit → *Muayeneye hazırlanın*, on
`origin/main` at `e417259`, **2026-08-29**.

The intake opens in the spoken dialogue, correctly: kicker *Karşılıklı
konuşma*, the first question in its bubble, the persistent composer below it
(`docs/evidence/screens/INT-261-before-01-dialogue-q1.png`). Answer that
question — by typing, which the surface offers as an equal way in and which
its own header calls "one conversation, never two modes" — and the app
leaves the conversation entirely. It lands in the typed step flow, which
re-asks the question just answered, from zero, reading **0 soru yanıtlandı**,
under the line *"Ses özelliği şu an kullanılamadı. Yazarak devam
edebilirsiniz."*
(`docs/evidence/screens/INT-261-before-02-typed-message-kills-dialogue.png`).

So the patient is asked the same question twice, in two different registers,
one turn apart, and told the voice feature broke — while they were typing.
That is the mixing.

A second reproduction, reported independently from another simulator on the
same commit, showed the other face of the same defect: the turn never left
*"Soru hazırlanıyor…"* at all — spinner, no mic, no *Sizi dinliyoruz*,
screenshots pixel-identical a minute apart — with all five dev lanes
answering `/health` in milliseconds. It also showed the third defect below,
in the same bubble.

### Three defects, one screen

**1. A clip that cannot be synthesized ended the whole intake.** The speaking
driver in `useDialogueSession.ts` had one `catch` for everything and it
dispatched `failure`, which is terminal: `voiceIntakeReducer` moves to the
typed steps and the conversation is over. But nothing in that conversation
needs the agent's audio. The question TEXT is in its bubble the moment the
turn opens — it is rendered from the same catalog string the clip is
synthesized from — the microphone records, the composer sends, extraction
reconciles and the confirm turn commits. Escalating "the agent lost its
voice" to "the intake is over" is a category error.

**2. Nothing bounded a clip end to end.** Measured on this host, 2026-08-29,
against the two dev daemons — a measurement of a dev bridge on one machine on
one day, not a property of either model:

```
GET  http://127.0.0.1:8805/health      (VoxCPM2) -> 200 in 0.108 s
POST http://127.0.0.1:8805/synthesize  (VoxCPM2) -> 200, 61484 bytes, 2.777 s
GET  http://127.0.0.1:8803/health      (Freya)   -> 200 in 0.168 s
POST http://127.0.0.1:8803/synthesize  (Freya)   -> 200, 103724 bytes, 1.006 s
```

`make voice-doctor` on the same host, same minute: every lane **UP**. Against
`TURN_ROUND_TRIP_BUDGET_MS = 1684`, VoxCPM2 is over budget by construction
and Freya sits close enough to the line that a cold first call trips it. That
is INT-249 working exactly as designed — and INT-249 bounded one *request*.
Nothing bounded the *clip*, and the layers multiply: `selectTtsLane` walks
every (tier, host) pair in sequence, each costing a health timeout plus a
full probe synthesis; `synthesizeWithFallback` may invalidate and re-walk
that ladder once per candidate; `synthesizeClipWith` runs all of it once per
SENTENCE. With three hosts configured that is minutes of legitimate,
as-designed waiting behind a `/health` that says 200. In this session's
reproduction it terminated in `tts_unreachable`; in the other it did not
terminate at all. Both are the same missing bound.

**3. The spoken turn read the typed form's control copy.** The first bubble
rendered, and the agent spoke:

> Sizi bugün hekime getiren nedir?
> **Birden fazla seçebilirsiniz.**

That second line is `intake.q.complaint.hint` — the chips grid's multi-select
helper, which the pack carries so the *typed* screen can explain its control.
In a spoken turn it is simply false: chips render at `confirming`, never
while the question is asked, so there is nothing to select more than one of.
The same for `intake.q.extra.hint` ("you may leave it blank": no field), and
the same for the composed `dialogue.optionsOnScreen` ("you can see the
options on screen": they are not on screen). Invariant 10 requires the
scripted question verbatim; it does not carry the form's affordance copy
along with it.

## The fix, and why it is at these layers

- **`stream.ts`** gains `speech_unavailable` and a `voiceMuted` latch. The
  action moves the turn exactly where `speech_done` would have — to
  `listening` after a question, to `confirming` after a confirm clip — and
  records that the session is silent. `failure` now means only what genuinely
  ends a conversation: no microphone, no ASR. This is the reducer's job, not
  the driver's, because it is a statement about what the conversation is,
  and it is where a test can hold it.
- **`useDialogueSession.ts`** splits its one `catch` in two by *where* the
  failure happens rather than by inspecting an error string: `clipStore()`
  asks for the microphone, so its failure stays `failure`; everything after
  it is audio output and raises `speech_unavailable`. Once muted it stops
  asking the ladder at all, so a dead lane costs one probe per session rather
  than one per turn.
- **`tts.ts`** gains `clipDeadlineMs` and `withDeadline`. The deadline is one
  lane admission plus one synthesis per sentence —
  `TURN_ROUND_TRIP_BUDGET_MS + n × SYNTHESIZE_TIMEOUT_MS`, both figures
  already committed and sourced to files/03 §4.4, multiplied by the work the
  clip actually contains. **No duration is invented here.** The abandoned
  synthesis is deliberately not cancelled: the clip store dedupes by name, so
  letting it finish lands the file in the cache for a later turn. What it may
  not do is hold the turn.
- **`speech.ts`** stops speaking the pack's `hintKey`, stops claiming the
  options are on screen, and exports `questionAsideTr` — the one composition
  that both the clip and the chat bubble now render, so the two channels
  cannot drift. This matters more after this change than before it: a
  silenced session is only usable if the screen carries everything the voice
  would have. `dialogue.optionsOnScreen` is deleted from both catalogs rather
  than left behind a flag — a string that is never true is not copy to keep.
- **`voiceFirst.ts`** makes `failure` carry a `PrefillMap`, required rather
  than optional. A failure and a hand-over land the patient in the *same*
  typed steps, so both must arrive carrying the same thing; the failure
  branch used to hard-code `prefill: {}` and throw away suggestions the
  dialogue had already extracted from the patient's own words.

New copy, one key, `tr` first (invariant 5), `siz` with `-iniz` for
instructional prose, no numeral, no diagnosis language:

```
dialogue.voiceMuted: "Sesli okuma şu an çalışmıyor. Soruları ekrandan
                      okuyabilir, konuşarak ya da yazarak yanıtlayabilirsiniz."
```

Invariant 9 is untouched: this is the dev-tier HTTP dialogue, not the LiveKit
Talker, and no tool, preemption rule or insertion window is changed.
Invariant 10 is honoured more closely than before, not less — the scripted
question is still spoken verbatim; what stopped being spoken is copy that
was never part of the script.

## What is still mixed, and why it is not fixed here

- **The lanes are still slower than the budget they are admitted against.**
  VoxCPM2 at 2.78 s against a 1684 ms admission is a deployment problem
  (files/00 §1: it runs on CPU because the GPU is a nearly-full MIG slice;
  HB-I4 is the open blocker). This issue makes that survivable, not fixed. A
  patient on this host will usually get a silent conversation, which is the
  honest outcome until there is a GPU.
- **Re-entry mid-conversation is still a form.** `voiceEligible` requires
  `note.answers.length === 0`, and the dialogue commits per confirm, so a
  patient who taps the header's back arrow after one confirmed answer and
  returns lands in the typed steps with no explanation. It is a real defect
  and a different one — it is about resuming a session, not about a failing
  clip — and folding it in here would put two issues in one PR. Not filed as
  a separate issue by this branch; recorded here so the next reader has it.
- **The composer still takes a typed utterance during a `confirming` turn**
  and silently drops the candidate that was on screen. Same reasoning: a
  distinct defect in `stream.ts`'s `typed` action, which unlike `utterance`
  does not check the phase.
