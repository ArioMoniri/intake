## ADR-033 status
ADR-033 Q5 (a scale of at most 1.06 on the orb) is open, and its fail-closed default is opacity-only.
This PR ships that default:
- `OrbVisual.scale` has the literal type `1`;
- `motion.orb` has no scale key;
- listening stays visible through its halo's opacity (`orb.haloBreathLow`).

A yes to Q5 adds the scale tokens and one animated `transform` on the orb, and changes nothing else.

## Problem
- The dialogue signalled whose turn it was in three different ways:
  - a core `Animated.loop` pulse (`PulseDot`) in the listening and speaking bubbles;
  - three `ActivityIndicator` spinners, in the preparing bubble, the transcribing bubble and the
    settling row.

  With ask-ahead, two spinners could spin at once while the patient was still interacting
  (motion-verification.md §2.1 #8). That breaks 02 §5.2a rule 8, and it leaves SC 2.2.2 only a weak
  exemption.
- The pulse ran on the JS-driven `Animated` API, so a stalled JS thread would stop it.
- Nothing reflected the patient's own voice back to them while the microphone was open.

## Change
- **Tokens** (`src/lib/theme.ts`), each with its first consumer here:
  - `duration.fast` (90) and `easing.linear`;
  - `loop.breathePeriodMs`, `loop.speakPeriodMs` and `loop.shimmerPeriodMs` (4000, 1600 and 1400);
  - `level.*`, and `orb.*` (opacities only).

  `loop.pulsePeriodMs` is removed along with the pulse.
- **Pure policy** `src/lib/motion/orb.ts` (no React Native, Reanimated or clock):
  - `orbStateFor` follows the precedence list of intake spec §4.2, from events only: the lane, the
    turn phase, playback, voice muted, the transport, and a presented RED's `yields.orb` (INT-333).
  - `orbVisual`: opacity-only, and still under reduce-motion.
  - `orbTransition`:
    - hiding is instant (0 ms);
    - any other change is a 120 ms fade;
    - speaking → listening is 90 ms;
    - every transition is out-eased, with delay 0, and is 0 ms under reduce-motion.
  - `workRing`: a static modifier, never a loop.
  - The level pipeline:
    - `normalizeLevel`, `smoothLevel` and `quantizeLevel`;
    - `levelSamplerPlan`, which runs only while listening, never under reduce-motion, with samples
      `level.sampleMs` apart.
- **Device half:**
  - `src/lib/motion/native/useLevelSampler.ts` is the one timer:
    - it samples the recorder's metering at the plan's rate;
    - it writes a Reanimated shared value only when the 0.05 step changes;
    - it sets no React state;
    - it is classified in BK13's `NOT_A_TIME_LIMIT` ("a meter; it limits nothing").
  - `src/components/PresenceOrb.tsx` draws the orb's layers (halo, level halo, work ring, core, retry
    core) on the UI thread:
    - it is hidden from accessibility, has `pointerEvents="none"`, and is not pressable;
    - it calls `useReducedMotion` and `loopingDecorationEnabled` itself (BK14);
    - it reads loop periods from `motion.loop` (BK15);
    - a state change freezes the running value, and the next rest is reached from there, so the orb
      holds and never resets.
- **Dialogue** (`src/components/DialogueIntake.tsx`):
  - The orb leads the glass bar's first row, beside `SectionStrip`.
  - The pulse is gone, and the bubbles' dots are still.
  - The three spinners are gone, and their captions stay as static text.
  - `orbStateFor` computes the orb's state once. This dev lane has no media transport and no RED
    channel, so it passes `transport: "connected"` and `red: null`; the LiveKit lane feeds both.
- **Session** (`src/lib/dialogue/useDialogueSession.ts`): `readLevel()` returns the recorder's current
  metering, which is a scalar. Nothing is stored or sent.
- **Guards** (`e2e/motion.spec.ts`):
  - The MOTION-7 block is appended at the end of the file, with every name scoped inside it.
  - The MOTION-6 pins move from the dialogue to the orb.
  - The MOTION-4 pinned list gains `src/lib/motion/orb.ts`, because it reads a RED's yield.
- **Proposal doc:** `docs/design/motion/enhancements.md` (the motion lead's proposals beyond the rows,
  carried over unchanged from #702). It decides nothing and adds no token.

**New, the fake-clock test:** "the orb stalled 30 s changes no turn, and every turn is decided at its
event".
- It drives one whole conversation through the real `dialogueStreamReducer` three ways:
  - with no orb at all;
  - with the whole orb policy consulted at every step, on a clock that never moves;
  - with a 30 s stall before every event.
- The turns are identical across all three runs.
- The orb states are identical between the frozen run and the stalled run.
- Each turn and its orb are decided in the tick of their event (0 ms on the fake clock).
- The only time that passes is the injected stall, and the policy leaves `vi.getTimerCount() === 0`.
- Non-vacuity: the run passes through listening, speaking, settling and confirming, and the orb
  shows listening, speaking, thinking and idle.

Near-miss controls:
- a DEFERRED_CLINICIAN yield (`"unchanged"`) gives exactly the state it would with no RED;
- `voiceMuted` never produces `retry`;
- a muted, playing agent is idle, not speaking.

## Device evidence: PENDING
**Waiting on the owner's two reference devices**, which are still unnamed (see INT-330). The steps
below need a release build on iOS and on Android. Record each run here with the device, the OS, the
build and the date.
1. **JS-thread stall.** During a listening turn, block the JS thread for about two seconds (for
   example with a busy loop behind a dev-only toggle in a release-profile build). The orb's halo must
   keep breathing and the core must keep its state, because both run in UI-thread shared values.
2. **Render count.** Wrap `DialogueIntake` in a React Profiler, or count its renders with a commit
   hook, across a ten-second listen with speech. The count must stay flat:
   - the level reaches the orb only through `useLevelSampler`'s shared-value writes;
   - the orb's state changes only on turn events.

   The source guards hold both properties today; the device run measures them.
3. **Also check:**
   - on barge-in, speaking → listening lands within 90 ms of the audio stopping;
   - reduce-motion, from the OS and from the in-app switch, makes the orb still, with no halo movement.

Nothing in this PR is a device measurement. Under invariant 20, a measurement is recorded with a device
and a date, and is never stated as a property of the product.

- **17.** Every token has a consumer here, and no scale token ships.
