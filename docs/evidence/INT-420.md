## Classification

The old brand is a Turkish word: *vizit* (a visit) with `-e`, the dative ("to a
visit") and the TDK noun *vizite* (a doctor's visit or its fee). Each
occurrence was read in context and put in one of three classes.

- **Brand**: renamed to Önvizit, or `onvizit` / `ONVIZIT` in identifiers.
- **Common noun**: kept. Turkish copy uses the word as a word ("Belgeler bir
  vizite eklenir", "Vizite ekleyin", "viziteniz"), and so do fixtures and one
  comment quoting a button label.
- **Dated record** (`former_name` in the allow-list): kept. Evidence files of
  other issues, PROGRESS entries and the 2026-09-26 audit record the product
  under the name it had on their date, including verbatim pytest ids such as
  `test_a_disclaimer_is_not_a_diagnosis[Vizite tanı koymaz.]`. Rewriting them
  would falsify recorded runs; the audit's "Vizite remnants" row would become
  nonsense. This class is an addition to the issue's two, stated here so the
  owner can overrule it: it is one reviewed list, and retiring it is a scripted
  replace plus deleting the entries.

## The guard: `tests/release/test_brand_name.py`
- Scans every `git ls-files` text file (NUL byte = binary, skipped) for the old
  spelling in any letter case, identifiers included.
- The token is derived (`"vizit".capitalize() + "e"`), never spelled. The
  guard asserts that its own source does not contain the token.
- The lookbehind `(?<!on)(?<!ön)` exists because `onvizitEmbed` lower-cases
  into the old spelling. Near-miss controls prove that the new brand, its
  suffixes, `ONVIZIT_…` and the bare noun `vizit` (`Vizitlerim`) are not
  caught.
- Every hit must be an allow-list entry (path plus exact trimmed line). Every
  entry must still match a line. Entries are well formed (kind, why, tracked
  path).
- `common_noun` entries must be words in running text, never identifier-shaped
  (`_`, `-`, `/`, a camelCase capital, `.host`). This is proven on both sides by
  a parametrized control.
- It runs in `make check-py` and in `make check-i18n` (`tests/release`), so a
  copy change is checked in under a second.
