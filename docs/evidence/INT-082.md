## 0. Provenance — this is a port, and it says so

The simulator was **not** written from scratch here. A stranded 40-commit branch,
`origin/claude/github-actions-billing-34om2r`, carried commit `97c3fa8`
(*"test(sim): INT-082 headless patient simulator — 14 scenarios, 14 personas, and
six findings"*, 30 Aug 2026) which was never packaged into a PR. That commit was
cherry-picked onto current `main` and then extended. Judging it before rewriting
it was the right call: it drives the **real** compiler, the **real**
`IntakeEngine`, the **real** `TalkerSession` and the **real** `evaluate_triage`,
and it proves invariants 1, 3, 9, 10 and 21 structurally rather than by
assertion. Nothing of that shape was worth re-deriving.

Four conflicts, all resolved **towards current main**: `Makefile` (kept main's
`egress`/`audit-verify` blocks and appended `test-sim`), `docs/PROGRESS.md` (kept
every main entry, appended this one), `files/11_ISSUE_BACKLOG.md` (regenerated
from `issues.yaml` rather than merged — it is a generated file, and the ported
counts were four issues stale), and a phantom rename of `docs/evidence/INT-264.md`
onto `docs/evidence/INT-285.md`: the ported commit carried a `ruff format` fix for
an evidence file whose **id no longer exists on main**, so main's file was taken
unchanged. `ruff format --check .` is green on the result, so the defect that fix
addressed is not present here.

### What was added on top, and why

The ported work satisfied the acceptance line but left two things open that the
repository owner had asked for in his own words — *"a full vocal test also with
complicated patients or fast and complete mode"*:

1. **Neither intake mode existed.** Every run was one protocol profile. §5 below.
2. **The personas were hard along the axes the ported author chose — proxy,
   low-literacy, crisis, oncology, unreadable, delta, ask-ahead — but not along
   the ones that break the *turn order*.** Nobody answered a different question
   than the one asked; nobody volunteered a fact after its question had closed;
   nobody reversed an earlier answer; nobody answered in two words. §6 below.

Those two additions are what produced findings **F7–F9**, and F8 is the one worth
reading first.

`files/04 §6` is the source of record for the simulator. Its "starter set (40)"

| id | line, verbatim from `files/04 §6` |
|---|---|
| IM-02 | `IM-02 uncontrolled HTN with headache (YELLOW)` |
| IM-09 | `IM-09 passive SI on PHQ-9 (YELLOW + safety script)` |

`files/10 §9` cites `IM-14` once more, as the test for the companion/guardian

## 3. What is blocked, and how the gate fails closed

`files/04 §6` makes a scenario's *hidden truth* its symptoms, medications and red
flags, and its *expected output* a triage level. **None of that exists in this
repository.** Every red-flag family in `packages/protocols` is
`status: draft_needs_source`; the triage engine refuses a draft rule outright
(`DraftRuleError`), so no real pack can drive a simulated session at all.

`Scenario.clinical_truth()` therefore **raises `ClinicalContentBlocked` for all
fourteen scenarios** — the mechanism is built, the gate is wired, the gate fails
closed, the content is left empty (HUMAN_BLOCKERS Rule 2). There is no
`dev_only` flag, no demo mode and no stand-in rule set. The `ClinicalTruth` type
exists so the shape is fixed before the content arrives; no instance of it is
constructed anywhere in the repository.

| blocker | what it gates here | scenarios |
|---|---|---|
| **HB-C1** | `base.life_threat` sign-off — no pack may be promoted past L0 | all 14 |
| **HB-C2** | the ~30 missing adult rules — no real RED/YELLOW rule to fire | all 14 |
| **HB-C7** | every `TODO: … requires clinical sign-off` in the tree | all 14 |
| **HB-C5** | the fast-mode turn budget is an open clinical decision, so the simulator asserts **no** turn budget in either mode | IM-01 |
| **HB-C8** | the `04 §4.2` escalation policy — the crisis path has no responder, no abandonment handler, no safety plan | IM-09 |
| **HB-L2** | C-SSRS electronic sublicence — the instrument cannot compile into a pack | IM-09 |
| **HB-C3** | the paediatric rule set does not exist at all | IM-14 |
| **HB-C9** | the `ON_SITE` companion-separation question — what a carer is told in advance | IM-14 |

`test_every_blocker_id_exists_in_human_blockers` reads `files/HUMAN_BLOCKERS.md`
at test time, so a blocker id cannot rot. **No new blocker row was opened**: every
gap this issue meets is already someone's named row, and re-asking is forbidden.

## 4. Design decisions

**The simulator lives in `tests/sim/`.** CLAUDE.md invariant 17 puts mocks in
tests only, and a patient simulator *is* a mock patient; shipping one inside
`services/voice-runtime/src` would put a synthetic-patient generator on the PHI
path. It is also the established pattern — `tests/voice/talker_harness.py`,
`tests/lanes/lane_harness.py` — and it avoids a `packages/llm-evals` workspace
member that would have had to depend on a *service*, inverting a layering no
package in this repository breaks today. `files/04 §6`'s parenthetical
`packages/llm-evals/scenarios/*.yaml` is therefore **not** followed, deliberately
and recorded rather than silently; INT-094's latency harness under
`intake_voice_runtime/simulator/` stays where it is, because moving it is a
refactor with its own blast radius and its own issue.

**It drives the real components, not re-implementations.** The synthetic pack
under `tests/sim/fixtures/tree` goes through `protocols.cli.load_registry` →
`protocols.compile.resolve.resolve_protocol` against the committed operational
template, and is driven through the real `IntakeEngine`, the real `TalkerSession`
and the real `evaluate_triage`. The only doubles are the speech egress ports and
the Layer-B extraction, both recorded fixtures. The signed life-threat set is the
committed INT-041 fixture, reused rather than copied.

**Everything is synthetic** — `files/04 §7`: *"packs and goldens contain synthetic
data only; simulator transcripts are synthetic"*. Every id, slot, choice, rule and
Turkish string in `tests/sim/fixtures` is invented and carries no clinical
meaning. No numeral appears in any spoken string.

**No ADR.** The simulator adds no component, port, boundary or dependency.

## 5. The two intake modes — Hızlı and Detaylı

`files/02 §2` heads its own table *"The two intake modes (same engine, different
protocol profile)"* and closes it with *"Both modes run the Protocol Registry:
`base.intake` → `specialty.*` → `visit_type.*` → patient overlays"*.

**So the mode is a resolution, not a flag — and it has to be.** `IntakeEngine`
takes a resolved protocol, a reference time and an overlay; there is nowhere to
put a mode. `files/13 §9.5`'s GuardExpr whitelist, the one place a `mode()` gate
could be written, is **refused at plan build** by INT-081. A simulator carrying
its own mode flag would be testing a mechanism the product does not have.

`IntakeMode.FAST` resolves `base.sentetik_simulator`; `IntakeMode.DETAILED`
resolves `visit_type.sentetik_detayli`, which `extends` it and adds two sections
shaped like `files/02 §2`'s *"+ full specialty module, ROS, PMH/FH/SH deltas"*.
Measured on the control persona (P01, typed):

| | asked | must-ask obliged | coverage | triage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hızlı | 10 | 6 | 1.0000 | GREEN |
| Detaylı | 15 | 9 | 1.0000 | GREEN |

Five properties are asserted, and one deliberately is not:

- **Detaylı asks a strict superset of Hızlı.** `files/02 §2`'s Content row is
  written as "+"; a mode that *replaced* questions would be a second protocol
  wearing the same name.
- **The obligation set may grow and may not shrink**, for every scenario. A
  `must_ask` that Hızlı owes and Detaylı does not would mean the longer session
  quietly dropped a clinician-mandated question — `files/13 §9.4`'s named harm.
- **Lengthening the session does not move the determination.** Same answers, same
  level, same fired rule ids. If this ever fails, either a rule started reading a
  mode-only slot or the mode changed an answer; neither is visible from the
  coverage figures.
- **Typed/voice parity holds in Detaylı too** — the longer session is exactly
  where a channel could drift.
- **Detaylı adds no free-text critical-path step.** `files/02 §3`'s low-literacy
  path is a property of the product, not of the short mode, and the mode that adds
  questions is the one that could quietly add an unreadable one.
- **No turn budget is asserted, in either mode.** `files/02 §2`'s ≤ 14 / ≤ 18 pair
  collides with its own content list in the same table and is an open clinical
  decision (**HB-C5**). `MAX_TURNS = 200` remains a documented runaway guard, and
  every scenario asserts it ended on `Termination.FRONTIER_EMPTY`. No number here
  is a clinical claim.

Mutation control, recorded in §8: collapsing the two modes to one pack fails
`test_detailed_resolves_a_longer_chain_than_fast` and
`test_detailed_asks_a_strict_superset_of_what_fast_asks`.

## 6. The four patients who break the turn order

`files/13 §9.6` makes the engine **under-bind by construction** — *"nothing binds
to a question that was not asked"* — because inferring a slot from a neighbouring
answer is how a note comes to read as *elicited* what was *inferred*. That rule
is only exercised by a patient who talks out of order, and none of the ported
fourteen did. Four were added, and each drop they cause is recorded as an
`UnboundUpdate`, **because the engine keeps no trace of one**: a value it declines
to bind leaves no state, no gap and no event, so a patient who volunteers a
red-flag answer three turns late and a patient who never says it produce
identical sessions.

| persona | what it does | measured result |
|---|---|---|
| **P15** answers a different question | asked why they came, talks about the symptom instead — a fluent reply a recorded extraction reads *correctly*, as an answer to a question nobody put yet | `ziyaret.basvuru_nedeni` stays `ASKED_PENDING` for the rest of the session, asked exactly once, **no** clarification, **no** data gap; coverage 0.8333 |
| **P16** discloses after the question closed | three steps after `ziyaret.basvuru_nedeni` closed on the calm code, says the **RED rule's own trigger code** | dropped, `already_resolved`, `differs_from_bound`; session stays GREEN with no rule fired |
| **P17** contradicts an earlier answer | reverses `oyku.belirti_var` later in the same session | dropped; the first answer stands unmarked and still counts as **covered**; `CONTESTED` is never assigned |
| **P18** answers telegraphically | every reply is one or two words, same recorded extractions | byte-identical state, values, coverage and triage to the fluent control — the sentence is not the channel, the extraction is |

The four behaviour markers on `Persona` are **checked against the script** by a
validator, so a persona cannot claim to be difficult without being difficult.
`UnboundReason` is a closed set of five and
`test_every_reason_in_the_closed_set_is_reached_by_a_persona` asserts every member
is produced by a different persona — a reason nothing reaches is a rule nobody is
testing.

`UnknownSlotInScript` is the guard that keeps all of this honest:
`apply_extraction` ignores an unknown reference in **silence**, which is correct
for it and useless here, so a persona naming a slot the resolved protocol lacks
raises. `test_a_script_naming_a_slot_this_protocol_lacks_is_refused` proves the
guard fires.

## 10. Findings

**F1 — an abandoned `must_ask` is recorded, but the Talker cannot see it.** IM-06
confirms the drop is not silent for the note: after two clarifications
`oyku.seyir` is `UNCLEAR`, it appears in `data_gaps`, and `must_ask_coverage`
reports it obliged and outstanding with the ratio falling to `0.8333`. **But
`EngineSnapshot` carries only `version`, `next_step`, `awaiting` and
`invalidated`** — no `data_gaps`, no coverage — so Layer A, which reads nothing
else, has no way to know a mandated question was abandoned and nothing in the
conversation can react. Whether it *should* react is a clinical decision and is
left open rather than designed here.

**F2 — a section holding an abandoned `must_ask` still reports `section_done`.**
`files/03 §5` defines the field as "nothing offered and nothing awaiting", and an
`UNCLEAR` step is neither. The definition working as written; `must_ask_coverage`
is the compensating control. Recorded because a consumer reading `section_done`
alone would draw the wrong conclusion.

**F3 — the IntakeEngine has no path to `DECLINED` or `CONTESTED`.**
`files/13 §9.3` names seven answer states and `coverage.COVERED_STATES` counts
both as covering; `apply_extraction` can only produce `ASKED_PRESENT`,
`ASKED_ABSENT`, `UNCLEAR` and (via invalidation) `NOT_ASKED`. A patient who
declines leaves the slot `ASKED_PENDING` permanently — the *safe* direction, but
not the state the blueprint names. **Sharpened by P17:** a patient who reverses an
answer produces no `CONTESTED` either, the first answer stands unmarked, and it
still counts as covered.

**F4 — `files/13 §9.5`'s "every turn strictly decreases the work remaining" is
false turn-by-turn.** A clarification turn takes one step out of `NOT_ASKED` and
puts one re-entry into the queue: a wash. What holds — and what the suite asserts
for every scenario — is that the measure is **non-increasing**, that no slot is
asked more than `1 + MAX_CLARIFICATION_ATTEMPTS` times, and that every scenario
terminates because the frontier emptied. This corrects INT-081's own reading; the
`UNCLEAR`-leaves-the-frontier resolution still stands, resting on §11.1's "no
third attempt".

**F5 — the simulator asks no instrument item**, because `build_plan` walks section
slots only and never turns `ResolvedSection.questionnaires` into a step. INT-116's
scope, not a defect here, but it means "instrument items are spoken verbatim"
(invariant 10) is asserted at the *pack* level in this suite and not yet at the
runtime level.

**F6 — no turn budget is asserted anywhere**, in either mode, because HB-C5 is
open. `MAX_TURNS = 200` is a documented runaway guard.

**F7 — an answer to a *different* question is the quietest failure in the set.**
P15 is asked `ziyaret.basvuru_nedeni` — a `must_ask` — and replies about the
symptom instead. A recorded extraction reads that reply *correctly*, as an answer
to a question nobody has put yet, so it binds nothing (`never_asked`) and
**reports no problem**: `clarification_needed` is false, because from the
extraction's side nothing went wrong. Measured outcome: the mandated step stays
`ASKED_PENDING` for the whole session, is asked **exactly once**, never re-enters
the frontier, and produces **no `data_gaps` entry**. The only place it is visible
at all is the coverage ratio (`0.8333`) — which F1 already established the Talker
cannot see. IM-06's abandoned slot at least reaches `data_gaps`; this one does not.

**F8 — a value volunteered after its question closed vanishes without trace, and
that includes a red-flag trigger.** P16 answers `ziyaret.basvuru_nedeni` with the
calm code and, three steps later, says the trigger code instead. The synthetic
RED rule's first conjunct is `ziyaret.basvuru_nedeni == 'sentetik_tetik'`, and
IM-04 proves the rule fires when that value binds on its own turn. Here it binds
nothing: the session ends GREEN with no rule fired, no `data_gaps` entry, no
`invalidated` entry, and the step still reading `ASKED_PRESENT` on the earlier
answer. **The two runs differ only in *when* the patient said it.**
This is `files/13 §9.6` working exactly as written, and it is written for a good
reason — binding it would make a note read as *elicited* what was volunteered.
Both halves are true at once, which is why it is recorded as a finding for a human
rather than fixed here: **whether a late disclosure should re-open its slot, queue
a confirmation turn, or reach the note as an unbound patient statement is a
clinical-safety decision.** Nothing in the acceptance covers it and no blocker row
owns it yet.

**F9 — a slot the engine gave up on cannot be re-answered from the side.** P11's
`oyku.seyir` goes `UNCLEAR` after two clarifications (`files/13 §11.1`: *"there is
no third attempt"*), which removes it from the frontier. Four steps later the
patient says it clearly and unprompted; the value is dropped (`abandoned`). The
"no third attempt" bound is about *asking*, and it currently also forecloses
*receiving*.
