# Evidence: INT-461 · ADR-038, the owner-ratified amendment to ADR-033

**Epic:** E00-governance · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** mobile · **GitHub:** #786 ·
**Depends on:** INT-332 (ADR-033, PR #571)

## Why

On 3 Oct 2026 the owner said the app still reads as AI-generated and asked, section by
section, for native message bubbles with a spring-in motion, haptics on selection, a mic
button with a haptic in the empty chat composer, and native sheets with detents. ADR-033
(accepted 27 Sep 2026) forbids all three:

- §1 allows opacity only, at most 120 ms, with no spring or translate. ADR-037 (PR #771,
  approved by the owner on 3 Oct 2026) adds a press scale, interactive glass and the
  collapsing large title, but no arriving bubble.
- §6 allows only the note-send `success` inside the intake. INT-340 (PR #710) gives the
  `intakeChip` surface no event.
- A UIKit-presented sheet is drawn above the root view controller's view, where §5 mounts
  the RED root layer, so a presented sheet would sit above RED.

The amendment was drafted with a bound for each exception and put to the owner, who
answered in chat on 3 Oct 2026: "yes approve 1-3".

## Change

- `docs/adr/ADR-038-motion-amendment-bubbles-selection-haptics-in-tree-sheets.md`, status
  Accepted, with the quote and date. It has three exceptions:
  - **A1, spring-in bubbles:** an 8 pt rise on ADR-037 P1's critically damped spring
    (stiffness 1500, damping 77.5, ζ = 1, overshoot clamped), with a 120 ms opacity fade
    and no scale. It is the arbiter's `content` class, never on RED, and keeps only the
    fade under Reduce Motion.
  - **A2, selection haptics:** `select` on `intakeChip` and `dialogueMic` through INT-340's
    one gate. It never fires while agent speech plays or is being prepared, or the mic is
    hot. It never fires on RED or after a SILENT RED or third party, and never on
    validated instrument options.
  - **A3, detent sheets:** in-tree only, inside the navigator container, so the RED root
    layer paints above an open sheet. There is no `Modal`, `formSheet`, `modal`
    presentation or zoom, privacy and RED closes stay instant, and Reduce Motion gives a
    fade in and an instant close.
- `files/00_STATE.md` §3 adds row **D21**. D18 is claimed three times by open PRs (#571,
  #723, #757/#760), D19 by #761 and D20 by #772, so D21 is the next free number across
  `main` and every open PR.
- `docs/index.md` registers the ADR.
- `tests/docs/test_adr_038.py` pins the status, the quote, what the ADR amends, every
  bound of A1 to A3, the index entry and the D21 row.
- Backlog row INT-461 is added (status `done`). The three pinned counts move 330 → 331,
  and `files/11` is regenerated.

No code changes here. Each bound is implemented, test first, in the issue that ships it.
The chat (INT-462) carries A1 and A2. Section 5 of the native pass carries A3.
