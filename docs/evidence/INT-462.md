## The ask

The owner, 3 Oct 2026: the app reads as AI-generated and not native Apple. For the chat, the brief asked for:

- a Liquid Glass capsule composer with send inside;
- native bubbles that spring in;
- typing, listening and transcribing states;
- native capsule chips with a checked state.

The owner then added a mic in the empty composer. It opens only on tap, never automatically. It has an accessible name and a haptic, swaps to send once there is text, and leaves RED behaviour unchanged.

## What changed

- **P1, P2: the composer.** One filled capsule (`radius.chip`) on the glass bar holds the field and one trailing control. It follows INT-433's field rule:
  - a solid `surface` fill;
  - a 2 pt tint ring while focused;
  - no outline at rest, with the boundary back under Increase Contrast.

  The control is a 32 pt disc centred in its own 44 pt target box. The field and chip rules move to `lib/controlChrome.ts`, so the spoken lane never imports `lib/intake/questionScreen.ts` (invariant 22).
- **P3: the trailing control.** `lib/dialogue/composer.ts` (`composerTrailing`) picks it:
  - **mic**, while the draft is empty. It barges in while the agent speaks, or takes the spoken correction on the confirm turn.
  - **send**, once there are words.
  - **stop** ("Bitirdim"), while listening.
  - **disabled send**, while settling.

  The mic opens the microphone only from its own press, through the session's own `bargeIn` and `speakCorrection`. It has a name ("Konuşarak yanıtlayın") and a hint ("Mikrofonu açar.").
- **P4: states.** INT-336 already delivered these: the transcribing bubble is a static caption, and the presence orb is the one moving state. Nothing is added.
- **P5: chips.** The confirm chips are `chipChrome` capsules (44 pt). The selected chip is tinted and gets a leading SF Symbol checkmark, so selection is not shown by colour alone.
- **ADR-038 A1: bubbles.** `lib/motion/bubbles.ts` and `native/bubbleEntering.ts`. A new agent bubble that the arbiter grants rises 8 pt on ADR-037 P1's critically damped spring (stiffness 1500, damping 77.5, overshoot clamped), under the 120 ms fade. Under Reduce Motion only the fade remains. Nothing moves while a RED shows. The patient's own bubbles keep INT-337's instant render.
- **ADR-038 A2: haptics.** The `select` tick fires on answer chips (both lanes) and on the mic, through INT-340's gate.
  - For that gate, the dialogue counts as active only while agent speech plays or the mic is hot (`useDialogueSession`'s probe reads the turn phase).
  - So the mic's tick fires before the recorder opens. A barge-in over speech is not felt.
  - `instrumentOption` has no event.
- **The follow fix.** This was found on the simulator (below). The transcript's scroll-to-end now also depends on the bar's measured clearance. A confirm turn adds two buttons to the bar after the follow has run, and the last chip row had sat under the bar.

The follow defect appeared on the first after run and did not appear in the before run. That fits a timing race between the bar's measurement and the scroll. The fix removes the race instead of relying on the order.

**Capture harness, disclosed.** The dev dialogue needs its speech-recognition health probe to answer before it renders. For the captures only, a local stub answered `GET /health` with 200 on 127.0.0.1:8802 and every other request with 503. The stub is not committed. No speech was transcribed. Text-to-speech was unavailable, so the agent shows its honest muted state ("Sesli okuma şu an çalışmıyor…"), and answers were typed. The patient is the synthetic dev account; the answers are synthetic.

**Not verifiable on the simulator.** The simulator has no Taptic Engine, so the haptics are proved by `haptics.test.ts` and the wiring guards, not felt. The spring is proved by `bubbles.test.ts` (numbers, RED exclusion and the Reduce Motion fallback), not timed. Any device timing goes here later with a device and a date (invariant 20).
