## The fast-mode turn budget — arithmetic, options, and no decision

**An agent may not choose this.** HB-C5 and `00 §5` Q8 both name `ClinicalAuthor` as the owner. What follows is the measurement and the option set, so the decision can be made on numbers rather than on adjectives. The collision is also measured in CI (`test_the_fast_mode_turn_budget_is_over_subscribed`) so it cannot be closed quietly by trimming a slot.

### The arithmetic

| line | turns | source |
|---|---|---|
| ceiling, fast mode | **≤ 18** | 04 §1 "Turn budget: fast ≤ 18 turns" |
| 02 §2 content list, clean case, zero clarifications, **before** the six additions | **16 – 21** | 02 §2 turn-budget caveat |
| this pack's declared fast must-ask steps, before the additions | **24** | 27 measured minus the 3 additions that are `must_ask` |
| ⇒ open-narrative retirement implied by the two lines above | **3 – 8 steps** | 13 §4 (Langewitz 2002, Marvel 1999): the open turn retires structured steps |

The six HB-C6 additions on top of that baseline:

| addition | costs a turn? | why |
|---|---|---|
| `pregnancy_status` | **always** | 13 §4: pregnancy/LMP early and **universally** — ruptured ectopic classically presents in a woman who does not know she is pregnant, and NG255 scopes sepsis to pregnant or recently pregnant people of any age |
| `immunosuppression` | **always** | 13 §4: **universally** — neutropenic sepsis is a one-hour emergency and must not depend on an overlay that may not exist |
| `anticoagulant_use` | **always** | `overlay.anticoagulated` exists but overlays may only add or tighten (04 §2.3), so the base slot must be asked universally |
| `weight_kg` | **always, as authored** | intended conditional on age band; `age_years()` is in 13 §9.5's whitelist but is **not projectable** into the compiler's `enableWhen` grammar (`<slot> <op> <literal>`), so it cannot be gated today |
| `time_last_known_well` | **always, as authored** | intended armed by the stroke probe (13 §9.1 class 4 `RF_PROBE_ARMED`); the probe's trigger is unsigned, so gating it on the probe would make it permanently unreachable |
| `lmp_date` | **conditional** | the only one the DSL can gate: `ask_if: "pregnancy_status != 'hayir'"` projects |

| scenario | turns | against ≤ 18 | minutes at 16.3 s/item (Bennett 2016, IVR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline, no additions | 16 – 21 | −2 … **+3** | 4.3 – 5.7 |
| + the three universal gatekeepers | **19 – 24** | **+1 … +6** | 5.2 – 6.5 |
| + `weight_kg` and `time_last_known_well` as authored | **21 – 26** | **+3 … +8** | 5.7 – 7.1 |
| + `lmp_date` when it arms | **22 – 27** | **+4 … +9** | 6.0 – 7.3 |

**Even the single most favourable reading is over.** Blueprint floor, only the three universal gatekeepers, zero clarifications, no red-flag probe armed: **19 turns against 18**. There is no arrangement of the authored content that fits.

Two levers that look available are already spent: 02 §2's own correction moved PHQ-2/GAD-2 out of the spoken flow (they are `text_only` and offered after the note is assembled), and the red-flag probes cost zero turns today because they are unaskable.

### The options, each with what it costs

1. **Cut content.** The only block large enough is the 12-step HPI. 13 §4's shortening evidence is about the **ROS** (Verdon & Siemens: yield 10.5 %, PPV of a given "yes" 3.3 %) and base.intake's ROS is a detailed-mode add, so a fast-mode cut has to come out of OLDCARTS/SOCRATES coverage — a clinical choice about which coverage axis to lose. Not free of measurement consequences either: Brookes 2018 (RCT, 116 patients + 71 professionals) found order alone changed which items survived for **26–29 % of items** and changed completion by **31.3 pp**.
2. **Raise the budget.** ≤ 24 turns is ≈ 6.5 min at Bennett's IVR rate. 02 §2 is explicit that the target is the **pair** (≤ 4 min at ≤ 14 turns, or ≤ 5 min at ≤ 18) and "never the minutes alone", so the pair has to be restated together. Cost: Pew 2021 breakoff — web 5 %, IVR short form 11 %, **IVR long form 20 %**.
3. **Compound the unarmed block.** 13 §14 item 13 already specifies the form: *"the compound-turn form (≤ 4 items, 'hayır' default) for the unarmed life-threat block"*. Applied to the three universal gatekeepers it buys 2 turns → 17–22 clean case, which still breaks the ceiling at the top. Cost: 13 §5.2 (voice is the worst common mode on data quality) and 13 §5.3 (Krosnick & Berent: **two 2–3-option questions beat one 7-option question** on both time and order exposure — compounding runs the other way), plus a "hayır" default is an explicit satisficing invitation on a safety block.
4. **Make the additions conditional.** `lmp_date` already is. `weight_kg` and `time_last_known_well` need a DSL/compiler change, not a pack edit (see finding 3). `pregnancy_status` and `immunosuppression` **cannot** be conditionalised: 13 §4 requires both universally, for the two reasons quoted in the table above.
5. **Measure the open-narrative retirement rate.** The 3–8-step band is the single biggest term in the arithmetic and it is entirely unmeasured for Turkish. 02 §2 already flags the adjacent assumption as *"a design requirement to be met, not an assumption to be inherited"*. This is an evidence task rather than a decision, and it could move the answer by more than options 1–3 combined.

**Status: `awaiting-human` — HB-C5 / `00 §5` Q8, owner `ClinicalAuthor`. Nothing was cut and no budget was raised in this commit.**

## Findings

**1. No committed guideline snapshot exists for any family — for any guideline at all.**
`packages/protocols/sources/` does not exist in this repository. 04 §8.1 rule 3 and 12 §2.5 both require `source.quote` to appear verbatim in `packages/protocols/sources/<guideline_id>.txt`, and `files/protocols/base.life_threat.yaml` already cites `packages/protocols/sources/TR-EK4-2022.txt` in two exemplars against a path that is not there. This is why **every** family here is `draft_needs_source` rather than only the ones whose clinical content is missing: even EK-4 — binding Turkish law, quoted verbatim in 04 §4 — cannot be cited from this repository today. Somebody has to commit the snapshots before the first family can go `active`.

**2. `make protocols-compile` cannot reach any pack refusal, and the blueprint skeleton cannot simply be moved to fix it.**
The CLI's `--life-threat` default is `packages/protocols/base/life_threat.yaml`; that file does not exist, so the command dies before it looks at any pack. `scripts/ci/no_placeholders_allow.txt` already reserves that exact path, so the move was planned. But the skeleton at `files/protocols/base.life_threat.yaml` **does not validate** against `LifeThreatSet` — 21 errors, including dotted `tense_anchor` values (`chest_pain.onset_at`) that violate the `SlotKey` pattern, a missing `status` on every rule, and extra keys the model forbids (`source.note`, `audio`, `numbers`, `subject_is_child`, `suppress_attention_interrupt`, `consent_independent`, `offline_draft_path`, `abandonment_handler`, `carry_child`, `open_question`, plus the top-level `TODO` and `goldens_index` blocks). Reconciling the two is INT-041/HB-C2 territory and was left alone per this issue's scope.

**3. Four DSL gaps that shape what this pack could express.** None is a defect of INT-040; all four are recorded rather than worked around.
- **No English field.** `ProtocolPack.language` is `Literal["tr"]` and no model carries an English string, so the acceptance's "Turkish + English" is met by a sibling document keyed by the tr resource key. Invariant 5 arguably *wants* that shape; it is stated here so the choice is visible.
- **No priority-class field.** 13 §9.1's nine classes and 13 §14 item 13's promotion of pregnancy/LMP and immunosuppression to **class-1 gatekeepers**, with a compiler assertion that each precedes every step whose gate references it, cannot be expressed. Section order is the only ordering available, and 04 §1's own table puts these in row 6.
- **No fast/detailed mode marker.** 04 §1 has two columns; the DSL has `must_ask` and `required`. Fast must-asks are encoded as `must_ask: true`, detailed adds as neither. `mode()` is in 13 §9.5's whitelist but is not a slot, so it does not project into `enableWhen`.
- **No `datetime` or `time` in `SlotType`.** `time_last_known_well` is therefore a `duration` ("ne kadar süre önce"), which is also how a patient answers — but a stroke window is measured in hours and `date` would lose the hour outright. If a served rule needs a wall-clock instant, the DSL needs a member first.

**4. 04 §1 does not enumerate the red-flag families, and 04 §3 calls itself an excerpt.**
§1's `red_flags` row reads "universal set (§3)", and §3's heading is *"Universal red-flag rules (excerpt; full table lives in `protocols/base/red_flags.yaml`)"*. That file does not exist anywhere in the repository. The 13 families here are §3's table exactly — nothing added, nothing dropped — but §3 tells the reader it is not the whole set, so the physician should expect to add families as well as fill these in. `base.life_threat`'s own TODO block puts the full adult universal set at ~34 rules.

**5. 13 §4's cheapest verified win does not transfer to Turkish.**
Heritage 2007's randomised finding is the contrast between "is there **something** else" and "is there **anything** else" (OR 0.154, p = .001). Turkish renders both as *"başka bir şey"*, so the contrast has no Turkish form. `expectations_wrapup.anything_else` claims none of that effect and says so in the pack; whether an equivalent Turkish lever exists is an open question for the clinician rather than something to assert by translating an English result.

**6. Two safeguarding probes are `text_only` on 13 §13 grounds.**
`rf_suicidal_intent` and `rf_suicidal_ideation_passive` carry `delivery: text_only`, so the compiler's `text_only` check guarantees they can never be spoken. 13 §13's finding is stronger than a privacy preference: Fincher 2015 (RCT, N = 368) found face-to-face produced **more** IPV disclosure than computer (sexual IPV aOR 4.31), and the mechanism — disclosure as help-seeking directed at someone who can respond — means a voice agent that cannot respond may *suppress* the disclosure it is trying to elicit.

## Design decisions worth review

**Why every family sits at `YESIL` / `EXPEDITE` / `notify_clinic: never`.** 04 §3's table states RED or YELLOW per family. The colour-semantics correction directly beneath it replaced that three-level scheme with EK-4's five and says in terms that *"a naïve three-to-three colour map systematically under-triages"*. Reading §3's RED as `KIRMIZI_K1` or as `KIRMIZI_K2` is therefore the named failure mode rather than a transcription: K1 is *immediate*, K2 carries the only numeric target in the annex, and choosing wrongly in either direction is a patient-safety defect. The floor asserts nothing, the compiler refuses the rule anyway, and INT-057's generator already established the same form for the same reason.

**Why no family declares `register` or an `action` block.** This is the one place where the issue brief and the compiler pull in different directions, so it is stated plainly. Invariant 21 makes both mandatory **on a RED rule** with no permitted default; no family here is RED yet, and a register chosen by an engineer before the acuity exists is precisely the default invariant 21 exists to prevent — 02 §5.3's paediatric-airway case is where an `ALERT` default causes the deterioration. Writing an emergency action script under a `YESIL` rule would also be internally incoherent. So the axes are left absent and the **gate is proved instead**: `test_a_family_promoted_to_red_must_declare_register_and_all_three_locations` takes a family from this pack, sets its level to `KIRMIZI_K2`, and asserts the compiler returns `RED_MISSING_REGISTER` and `RED_ACTION_UNRESOLVED`. The mechanism is wired, fails closed, and the content is empty — HUMAN_BLOCKERS Rule 2.

**Why the 13 red-flag probe slots have no `ask_tr`.** The wording of a red-flag probe *is* the trigger — "sudden face droop / arm weakness / speech difficulty", phrased for a patient — so writing it is the clinical determination HB-C1 is waiting on. The slots exist so the families have declared subjects and the shape is ready; they carry no content and no engine can fill them.

**Clip-manifest entries.** The acceptance asks for them. They are produced by the compiler from the pack's `ask_tr` / `confirm_tr` / `choices_tr` and the action scripts, keyed by `sha256(expanded_text, voice_id)` after Turkish expansion — and the compiler emits nothing for a pack it refuses, which is this pack. Every authored string is already expansion-clean (no digit reaches any of them, asserted by `test_no_patient_facing_string_carries_a_numeral`), so the manifest is one signature away rather than one edit away. `screening` is `text_only` and contributes no clip by design, which is a build-time guarantee that nothing sensitive can be spoken.
