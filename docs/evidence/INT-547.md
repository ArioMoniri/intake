## What the decisions decide, and how it is built

Nothing below is re-decided. Every value is read from the register.

- **CD-04** `value.unresolved_after_follow_up` (active, verifier agree):
  - trigger: "the slot is still UNCLEAR after its follow_up_tr has been asked
    once";
  - `applies_to`: the seven CD-01 slots;
  - `action: clinic_inbox_item`, `decision_ref: CD-08`, kind
    `chest_pain_arms_unresolved`;
  - `never_dropped: true`, `pinned_to_note: true`;
  - "once" is carried machine-readable as `unclear_follow_up_tr_asks: 1`
    (INT-540).
- **CD-01** (active) declares the seven slots. The build refuses a CD-04
  `applies_to` slot that is not one of CD-01's.
- **CD-08** (`draft_needs_source` as a whole). The keys this build reads are all
  `value_status: active`:
  - `applies_to` (it routes `chest_pain_arms_unresolved`);
  - `item_kind_notes`;
  - `channel: clinic_inbox`;
  - the constraints never shown to the patient, never a patient-facing urgency
    path, never auto-cleared, delivery failure counts as an SLA breach, and a
    breach is an emitted event.

  The SLA, out-of-hours cover and escalation role and window are the
  invariant-17 literal under `draft_needs_source`. Its `fail_closed_while_draft`
  says the item "is still delivered to the inbox … a missing SLA never drops or
  delays delivery".

## Left to the D18 lane (no id minted)

1. **CD-08's SLA, out-of-hours cover, escalation role and escalation window.**
   These are already the invariant-17 literal under `draft_needs_source`. The
   build states none: the item has no SLA in the inbox or on the console. A
   test fails the day CD-08 marks the SLA decided, so it gets wired.
2. **"That leaves both chest-pain arms Unknown".** CD-08's decision prose
   describes the item as "a chest-pain slot still UNCLEAR after its follow-up
   that leaves both chest-pain arms Unknown". CD-04's machine-readable trigger,
   and CD-08's own `item_kind_notes` ("which can leave"), have no such
   condition. The build follows CD-04's value, the superset and the fail-closed
   reading. The lane may confirm.
3. **"Closes only on a recorded clinician outcome"** (CD-08
   `failure_path.constraints`). No outcome vocabulary is decided. The item closes
   on the inbox's existing acknowledgement, which records who and when and is
   audited. The lane may decide whether an outcome code set is needed.
4. **CD-04 round 3 (INT-519 items 5, 10 and 11, pending its verifier, not on
   main).** Round 3 raises the onset's item when a hedge is read with no
   follow-up, and at close for an onset never asked or never answered. This
   build follows rounds 1 and 2: an onset closed before its follow-up was asked
   raises nothing, and a near-miss test pins that. INT-546 (`apply-546`) is
   building those triggers as a hook (`AnchorUnresolvedHook.anchor_unresolved`).
   Whichever of #927 and INT-546 merges second wires the hook into
   `ClinicItemWatch`.
