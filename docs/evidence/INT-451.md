## Captures, and the three defects they found (3 Oct 2026)

The owner granted `INT451-16e` (iPhone 16e, iOS 26.1, Expo Go 57.0.9). The first pass found three defects, so the hold stayed until they were fixed on this branch, test first:

1. **Content drew through the glass nav bar.** On visit detail, content held under the bar showed the card's date and title on top of the back chevron and the bar title (`found-01-glass-nav-content-collision-light.png`). A full-bar `GlassView` (`glassEffectStyle="regular"`) gave no legibility. A clear bar asking UIKit for its iOS 26 scroll-edge effect was tried next and did the same in Expo Go: the effect did not attach to the screen's scroller. **Fix:** on iOS 26 the bar takes UIKit's own system chrome material (`headerBlurEffect: "systemChromeMaterial"`), with no app background and every scroll-edge effect hidden. Below iOS 26 and on Android it keeps the blur and wash, and under Reduce Transparency the solid page ground (`lib/a11y/glass.ts` `navBarChrome`, `withGlassNav(options, mode)`). The typed question (INT-433) now uses the same helper, and `GlassNavBackground` no longer draws a `GlassView`.
2. **The dark-mode back chevron was invisible** (INT-433's report). Tapping its place still went back, and a red `headerTintColor` did not change it, which ruled out the tint. Cause: the native stack passes its React Navigation theme's `dark` flag to each native bar as its user-interface style. With no theme provided the flag was always false, so every bar was forced light, and the iOS 26 chevron took the light label colour, black on the black page (`found-03-dark-chevron-missing.png`). **Fix:** `app/_layout.tsx` wraps the navigators in a theme built from the app's own scheme and tokens (`lib/navigationTheme.ts`), and the status bar follows the same scheme, outside the kiosk gate.
3. **The pressed switch row swallowed its off track**, because the track and the press fill are both `surfaceRaised`. **Fix:** `switchTrackToken` steps the off track to `surface` while pressed.
