# INT-451: mobile interaction patterns from the owner-listed libraries and Mobbin, within ADR-033 and Liquid Glass

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `mobile` · **GitHub:** #764 ·
**Depends on:** INT-433 (with INT-422 and INT-423) · **Branch:** `int-451-mobile-interactions` · **Date:** 2026-10-03

## The ask

On 3 Oct 2026 the owner asked for the app's UI to draw on seven web libraries (kobra.systems, beautifului.dev, kinetics.colorion.co, beui.dev, transitions.dev, astryx.atmeta.com, reverseui.com) and on Mobbin. He also asked for more Liquid Glass and a native Apple feel.

ADR-033 binds the work: opacity only, at most 120 ms, the arbiter, and no motion on RED. Anything that needs a spring, a scale or a translate is an amendment proposal, not shipped code. The acceptance is the INT-451 row in `docs/backlog/issues.yaml`. The survey, the decisions and the captures are in `docs/design/interaction-patterns.md`.

## Why INT-422, INT-423 and INT-433 are merged in

This issue builds on the design refresh, the chat materials and the question screen. All three PRs are still open: #725 and #740 conflict with `main`, and #728 is mergeable. `origin/int-433-question-screen` already contains the other two, so it is merged here (commit `6e114563`).

The only conflicts were the backlog count pins and `files/11`:
- both sides' rows and comments were kept;
- the pins moved from 330 to 330 + 3 = 333;
- `files/11` was regenerated.

That part of the diff drops out once those PRs land. This PR should merge after them.

ADR-033 itself is Accepted on PR #571, which is also not on `main`. This branch cites it and does not merge it.

## What changed

- **`apps/mobile/src/lib/theme.ts`**: `rowPressFill` (`surfaceRaised`), the row press highlight token, with its rationale. No palette value or motion token changed.
- **`apps/mobile/src/components/ListRow.tsx`, `SwitchRow.tsx`**: a press steps the row's fill to `theme[rowPressFill]` instead of dimming the row to `motion.press.rowOpacity`.
- **`apps/mobile/src/components/GlassBar.tsx`**: `withGlassNav(options)`. It makes the native header transparent over INT-433's `GlassNavBackground`.
- **`apps/mobile/src/components/Themed.tsx`**: `StackScreenOptions` is exported, so the helper can be typed against it.
- **The glass nav bar on four screens.** Visit detail (`[visitId]/index.tsx`), the share centre (`shares.tsx`), the review (`review.tsx`, but not its empty state) and the new-visit form (`new.tsx`) take `withGlassNav(...)`. Each clears the bar by `useHeaderHeight()`.
- **Tests:**
  - `src/lib/a11y/rowPress.test.ts` (new): contrast on the pressed fill in four palettes, the press visible against the cell, and a near-miss for the old dim;
  - `e2e/interactionPatterns.spec.ts` (new): the row wiring, the helper, the four glass screens and the three solid ones, the empty-state solid bar, and no motion or `emergency` token in any touched file;
  - `e2e/motion.spec.ts`: MOTION-12's list of dimming components drops the two rows, with the reason in a comment.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/design/interaction-patterns.md` (new);
  - `docs/adr/ADR-037-motion-amendment-press-and-glass-response.md` (new, Proposed), linked from `docs/index.md`;
  - `docs/design/interaction-patterns/` holds the captures.
- **Backlog:** the INT-451 row was added, the three pins moved from 333 to 334, and `files/11` was regenerated.

No i18n key, catalogue string, protocol, RED file or legal file changed. No dependency was added.
