# INT-499: ADR-039 and the Liquid Glass research on the remaining screens

GitHub #849. The owner's mandate, 16 Oct 2026, came after #842 (the lens orb) and #843 (the in-bounds progress capsule). It reads: apply ADR-039 and the Apple Liquid Glass research across the visits list, documents, the share centre and settings, with "glass on controls only, content solid; capture-protected screens solid per INT-477". The mining covers every relevant component page of spaceui.one (primary) and the other owner-listed libraries, plus Mobbin. Code may be copied only from MIT sources. Anything beyond the ADR bounds is an owner approval item and does not ship.

The research and the component → screen map are in `docs/design/library-patterns.md` §8. The decision is the dated amendment at the end of ADR-039, noted in `files/00_STATE.md` D23. No D-row and no HUMAN_BLOCKERS id is minted.

The shared pieces:

- **`lib/stage/screens.ts`** (pure) holds the policy for these screens.
  - `STAGE_SCREENS` lists the four screens with their capture protection.
  - `stageScreenChrome` answers glass only for an unprotected screen that has system Liquid Glass, and only without Increase Contrast. It draws the wash only when the screen is unprotected and has system glass.
  - `stageSurface` gives the solid card at 22 pt, with a boundary under Increase Contrast.
  - `withProtectedStageBar` is the sign-in screens' solid bar with every scroll-edge effect hidden.
- **`components/stage/useStageScreen.ts`** resolves the palette and the chrome. **`components/stage/StageSurface.tsx`** hands the surface to the shared content components:
  - `Card` and `ListGroup` become stage cards;
  - `Segmented` and `SecondaryButton` become capsules.
  - Outside a provider nothing changes, so Today, the visit detail and the other screens look as they did.
- **`StageButton`** takes `solid`, which forces the solid stage fill.

What is unchanged:

- The glass allow-list: FloatingTabBar, GlassBar and StageControls.
- The gradient allow-list: the two stage drawing files.
- RED: no RED path reads the stage.
- `text_tr` and question text.
- Copy: there are no new strings, and every visible line is an existing key.

### What the tests hold, each with its near miss

- **Each screen renders its new chrome** (`stageScreens.spec.ts`):
  - it resolves its palette from `useStageScreen("<name>")`;
  - it draws the stage base under the `Screen`;
  - its large title is a heading in `palette.ink`;
  - its content sits inside `StageSurfaceProvider`;
  - its page actions are `StageButton`s that are inert and never `prominent`;
  - no `PrimaryButton` or `SecondaryButton` is left at the screen level;
  - the share centre has no page action.
- **Capture protection:**
  - A stage screen is marked protected exactly when its file calls `useClinicalScreenPrivacy()`.
  - A protected screen mounts no wash and no glass host (expo-glass-effect, expo-blur, GlassBar, `withGlassNav`, `GlassNavBackground`, `withStageNav`, `StageGlassGroup`, `GlassComposer`, `AmbientBackdrop`), and every one of its actions carries a bare `solid`.
  - **Near miss:** the visits list and settings draw the wash and pass `solid={!chrome.glass}`, and the protected-screen predicate refuses them. In `screens.test.ts`, the same inputs without protection give glass.
- **Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast:**
  - `solid` mode and `blur` mode are solid and flat, never a blur.
  - Increase Contrast gives solid actions and keeps the wash.
  - Glass implies the wash, and the wash implies an unprotected screen, across every combination.
  - The hook reads the glass mode, the scheme and Increase Contrast.
- **Reduce Motion:**
  - None of the four screens, the hook or the surface imports Reanimated, `Animated`, `withTiming` or `withSpring`.
  - None uses a press scale or `isInteractive`. Nothing moves, so there is nothing to stop.
- **AA:** `ink`, `inkSoft`, `inkFaint` and `primary` reach 4.5:1 on every wash stop, the base and the card, in both schemes, both contrasts and both transparencies. **Near miss:** `#8E8E93` on the light base fails.
- **A defect found on the simulator:**
  - Under Increase Contrast, the share centre's empty groups are zero-height, and their new boundary drew as a 2 pt rule under each header.
  - An empty group now draws no boundary. The spec pins the rule.
  - The fixed screen is `shares-increase-contrast-light.png`. The defect was seen on the simulator and not kept as a file.
- **The ADR:** `tests/docs/test_adr_039.py` pins the amendment's bounds and the D23 note.
