## Tests

`tests/protocols/test_int427_dynamic_wave.py` checks the following for each of the 8 departments:

- the candidate has at least two gated questions;
- every gate is a yes-gate on an earlier yes/no slot in the pack;
- no gate reads a slot that the resolved chain (base → review cluster → candidate, merged as the resolver merges) asks after it;
- the candidate stays a generator-marked L0 with exactly one `draft_needs_source` stub at YESIL/EXPEDITE/never;
- the candidate is the generator's byte-exact output of its proposal;
- every ask and follow-up is one question, has no numeral, has at most 15 words and passes the shipped Turkish PolicyGuard;
- the verification doc has a non-empty independent section, and the sign-off packet carries the candidate's sha256.

Near-miss controls:

- a synthetic chain whose gated question comes before its gate is caught;
- non-yes-gates are refused: `!= false`, `== 'evet'`, a gate on `subject_role`, and `&&`;
- a plain yes-gate is admitted.
