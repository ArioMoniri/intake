# INT-370: Visits calendar view and Geçmiş (past) list on Vizitlerim

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** mobile · **GitHub:** #604
**Slice:** S3 of INT-354 (#560), int-demo `148a7dbd`. See `docs/evidence/INT-354.md` (commit
table row 15). **Depends on:** INT-341 (press tokens, merged in PR #572).

## Acceptance (verbatim)
- "Vizitlerim offers a calendar view and a Geçmiş list of past visits, built on the existing
  visits store with no new data field"
- "the `a11y.visits.dayHint` register finding is recorded in PROSE_REGISTER_FINDINGS and routed
  to clinical-reviewer, not rewritten"
- "INT-341's calendar press tokens apply"

## Change
The change is carried from `148a7dbd` by `git cherry-pick -x`. `en.ts` and `tr.ts`
auto-merged; git's own conflict list (`git diff --name-only --diff-filter=U`) was empty.

- `apps/mobile/src/lib/calendar.ts` (new, pure, no import at all):
  - a Monday-first six-row month grid;
  - visit counts per day;
  - past visits ordered most recent first;
  - the opening month and pre-selected day, taken from the soonest booked visit;
  - Intl-backed tr/en month, weekday and long-date names.

  Every visit-shaped parameter is structurally `{ readonly date: string | null }`, so the
  model reads a visit's date and nothing else. A dateless visit sits on no day (02 §2b).
- `apps/mobile/src/components/VisitCalendar.tsx` (new): a View/Pressable grid with no native
  calendar module.
  - Visit days carry one dot.
  - The selected day is a `cta` pill: luminance, not hue.
  - Today is an accent ring.
  - At the large-text scale the grid changes form into an agenda of the month's visit days.
  - Every in-month day is a `MIN_TAP` button, named from the catalogue, with
    `accessibilityState.selected`.
- `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/visits/index.tsx`: a Liste/Takvim `Segmented` toggle over
  `useSession().visits`.
  - Liste keeps the upcoming group and always shows a Geçmiş group, with
    `visits.pastEmpty` when it is empty, rather than hiding the section.
  - Takvim shows the grid and the chosen day's visits below it.
- i18n: ten new keys in tr, the source of truth, mirrored in en:
  - `visits.view.label`, `visits.view.list`, `visits.view.calendar`;
  - `visits.pastEmpty`;
  - `visits.calendar.prevMonth`, `visits.calendar.nextMonth`,
    `visits.calendar.hasVisits`, `visits.calendar.noVisitsOnDay`,
    `visits.calendar.monthEmpty`;
  - `a11y.visits.dayHint`.

**No new data field.** `VisitFixture`, the session store, `lib/visits.ts` and every schema are
untouched. The calendar is a view over what the session already holds, and nothing new is
persisted.

### Adapted to main
- **INT-341 press tokens.** On `main`, `e2e/motion.spec.ts` fails as soon as the calendar lands
  as written:
  ```
  × every Pressable takes its pressed and disabled opacity from motion.press
  +   "src/components/VisitCalendar.tsx:165 pressed ? 0.6 : 1",
  +   "src/components/VisitCalendar.tsx:211 pressed ? 0.6 : 1",
  +   "src/components/VisitCalendar.tsx:262 pressed ? 0.85 : 1",
  ```
  The month arrows and the day cells now read `motion.press.rowOpacity`, **0.6 → 0.7** (the
  normalisation INT-341 recorded for this component). The agenda row reads
  `motion.press.opacity` (0.85, unchanged).
- **Register finding, recorded, not rewritten.** `a11y.visits.dayHint` ("O günün vizitlerini
  görmek için iki kez dokunun.") uses the short imperative of every sibling `a11y.*Hint`. It
  is added to `PROSE_REGISTER_FINDINGS` in `tests/release/test_i18n_complete.py`, as
  build-source `dea9bb8a` first did. A lone `-iniz` would trip
  `test_no_short_in_flow_control_uses_the_doubled_polite_form` and split the register
  mid-screen. **Routed to clinical-reviewer.** The register decision is the files/00 §2 copy
  owner's.
- INT-339 (motion) and INT-362 (`AnnouncedText`) are not on `main` (PRs #599 and #600 are
  open), so there is nothing to adapt to.

## Tests (TDD)
- `apps/mobile/src/lib/calendar.test.ts`: ported from `148a7dbd`, 16 tests. They cover ISO
  helpers, month stepping across years, leap February, per-day counts that skip dateless
  visits, the 6×7 Monday-first grid with spill days, today only in its own month, the
  past ordering (today, future and dateless dropped), the opening month and day, and the
  Intl names.
- `apps/mobile/e2e/visits.spec.ts`: a new describe block with 6 tests (the acceptance as
  source facts):
  - the Liste/Takvim toggle and `<VisitCalendar>`;
  - both views read `useSession().visits` (`pastVisitsRecentFirst(visits,`,
    `visitsOnDay(visits,`, `visits={visits}`);
  - the Geçmiş kicker is no longer behind `past.length > 0 ?` and falls through to
    `visits.pastEmpty`;
  - `lib/calendar.ts` has no import and no storage or network, and the calendar's `visits`
    prop is `{ date }` only;
  - day targets have `minHeight: MIN_TAP`, the catalogue hint and the selected state;
  - the ten tr keys exist.
- `tests/release/test_i18n_complete.py`: `a11y.visits.dayHint` recorded ahead of the key, so
  the gate is red until the key exists and green after.
- `apps/mobile/e2e/motion.spec.ts` (INT-341, unchanged): the press-token guard, red on the
  as-ported calendar (above).
