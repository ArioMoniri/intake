# INT-284 — The screens INT-260 did not reach, and a bar that reserved less room than it takes

Reported on the simulator against `origin/main`, at the largest step INT-260's own control can
now ask for:

> the **welcome screen** renders its title **under the status bar and the notch**, its footer is
> **cut off at the bottom**, and **it does not scroll** — I swiped 400 pt and the screenshot was
> pixel-identical.

That last clause is the whole issue. Clipping that a scroll can undo is ugly; clipping that a
scroll cannot undo is content the patient cannot reach — and on this screen the unreachable copy
included the screen's own explanation of what the app is, plus the "Vizite tanı koymaz" line.

## 1. What actually broke, and what did not

Everything below was checked on the device at step 5 (2.0) in **both** themes, not inferred.

| screen | at 2.0 | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `(auth)/welcome` | title under the notch, footer past the bottom edge, **no scroll at all** | **broken — fixed** |
| `(tabs)/visits/[visitId]/review` | the provenance line `Bu not hasta beyanına dayanır; tanı içermez.` sits under the glass bar at the END of the scroll; the scope chips (`Düzenleyin`, `Paylaşılıyor`) run off the right edge | **broken — fixed** |
| `(tabs)/visits/[visitId]/intake` | reserves 128 for a bar measuring 131 — the last chip row rests 3 pt under the glass | **broken — fixed** |
| `components/DialogueGate` | pads for a glass bar it does not render, inside a `flex: 1` View with no scroll | **broken — fixed** |
| `components/DialogueIntake` | reserves `128 + 176` for a bar that stacks a section strip, up to two CTAs and a composer | **broken — fixed, but see §7** |
| `components/BiometricGate` | centres its card in a `flex: 1` box with no scroll — survives at 2.0 today, but by margin, not by construction | **structurally the same defect — fixed** |
| `(auth)/phone`, `(auth)/otp`, `(auth)/consent` | scroll and fit; but all three drop the bottom safe edge with no rail and no bar to own it | **survives; inset fixed** |
| `(tabs)/today`, `(tabs)/me/*`, `me/appearance`, `visits/index`, `visits/[visitId]` | reach their own ends, nothing clipped | **already fine — not touched** |

## 2. Why `justifyContent: "center"` was the bug

`welcome.tsx` centred its column inside `Screen`'s `flex: 1` box. Flexbox centring does not clip
when the content outgrows its container — it distributes *negative* free space, pushing the
overflow out of **both** ends, past the safe-area padding the `SafeAreaView` had just applied.
Nothing scrolls, because nothing scrollable exists. So the top went under the notch and the
bottom went past the home indicator, symmetrically, and the patient had no gesture that could
bring either back.

The fix is one rule, not two: a `ScrollView` whose `contentContainerStyle` carries
`flexGrow: 1` and the same `justifyContent: "center"`. Below the viewport height, `flexGrow`
makes the container fill the box and the centring is the design; above it, the container is
sized by its content, there is no free space left to distribute, and the same container scrolls.
`flex: 1` there would reintroduce the defect — it caps the container at the viewport and hands
the overflow straight back.

The test bans the pattern tree-wide rather than auditing the two sites, and it scans every module
that renders `<Screen>`, not only `src/app/**` — which is how `components/BiometricGate.tsx`, a
full screen living outside the route tree, got caught.

## 3. The bar's height and the room reserved for it were two numbers

This is INT-260 §3.2's defect ("content ran underneath the navbar") on the screens where the rail
unmounts. `GLASS_BAR_CLEARANCE` was the literal `128`, chosen when a body line was 17 pt.

Measured on an iPhone 16 Pro (402×874 pt) at step 5:

| bar | children | height |
|---|---|---|
| intake, chips/text step | one CTA, label fits one line | **131 pt** |
| review | one CTA, `Hekiminize gönderin` **wraps to two lines** | **≈ 178 pt** |
| dialogue | section strip + up to two CTAs + composer | not reachable here (§7) |

So on the review screen 50 pt of content sat under the glass at the end of the scroll, and what
happened to be in those 50 pt was the note's provenance line. A screenshot before and after a
further five swipes was byte-identical below the status bar: the scroll was at its end and the
sentence was unreachable.

`glassBarFloor(2.0, 34)` returns **130** — `spacing.md` above the children, one CTA
(`max(56, 2·spacing.md + lineHeight("body", scale))`), and `max(insets.bottom, spacing.md)` below
— against the 131 the device rendered. That is the bar's own anatomy read out of
`components/GlassBar.tsx`, not an estimate of it, which is why the test asserts equality within a
point rather than a loose bound.

But the floor is not the answer, and this is the part a formula cannot fix: **nothing derivable
from the type scale knows whether the CTA label wrapped.** `review.send` is 19 characters and
wraps at 2.0; `intake.next` is 11 and does not. A second button appears only in a confirming
dialogue turn. So the bar now reports its laid-out height through `onLayout`, hosts hold it, and
`useGlassBarClearance(height)` prefers the measurement whenever there is one, falling back to the
floor for the first frame (and for a `0` some layout passes emit). `GLASS_BAR_CLEARANCE` and
`DIALOGUE_BAR_CLEARANCE` are deleted, and a test keeps them deleted.

The focal CTA's `56` moved to `lib/prefs/appearance.ts` as `CTA_MIN_HEIGHT`, because the floor has
to predict the button's height and a second copy of the number is exactly the drift this issue is
about. That move broke the pinned SC 2.5.8 gate — `e2e/a11y.spec.ts` resolved `MIN_TAP` by name
but read every other height as a literal, so `minHeight: CTA_MIN_HEIGHT` fell through to `0`. It
is fixed by teaching the gate the second named floor and resolving it to its imported value, so
lowering either constant under 44 still reddens the gate. Nothing was exempted.

## 4. The safe edge is dropped only where something else owns it

Design pass 4's rule has a reason attached: a screen drops the bottom safe edge **because** the
floating tab rail or the glass bar occupies that zone. The `(auth)` flow has neither, and dropped
it anyway — so at the largest step, where those screens are long enough to reach the physical
bottom, the last control rests under the home indicator. `phone`, `otp` and `consent` keep the
edge now. `DialogueGate` is the same mistake in the other direction: it padded for a bar that only
its *siblings* render (intake.tsx shows the gate, the dialogue **or** the typed steps), so it now
reserves the inset it actually owns and scrolls.

## 5. The review card header

At 2.0 `sectionHeader` — a `space-between` row holding a `Kicker` and the scope chips — ran past
the right edge. React Native flex children do not shrink by default (unlike the web), so nothing
gave: `Paylaşılıyor` was clipped mid-word at the screen edge and the `Düzenleyin` Pressable was
partly off-screen and untappable. Which section is being shared is not optional reading, and a
control the patient cannot reach is not a control. `flexWrap: "wrap"` drops the chips onto their
own line; at every smaller step there is nothing to wrap and the row is unchanged.

Note what this is **not**: it is not truncation. `00 §2`'s no-truncate ruling and the build gate
INT-260 added for it are untouched, and no `numberOfLines`, `ellipsizeMode` or
`adjustsFontSizeToFit` was introduced anywhere in this change.

## 6. What this change deliberately does not do

`AppText` already sets `allowFontScaling={false}` and sizes through `scaledSize`. No second
scaling mechanism was added, no font size was hardcoded, and no text was clamped to fit. Every
fix here is a container or an inset. Both remaining escape hatches are closed by `00 §2` —
truncation inverts a Turkish clinical fact (`ağrımıyor` → `ağrım…`), and shrinking takes back the
size the patient chose — so room the patient can reach is the only answer left, and it is the one
taken in all six sites.

`useTabBarClearance` was reused as the model rather than duplicated: `glassBarClearance` adds the
same trailing `scaledSize("body", scale)` breathing term, for the same reason (the last row should
clear the chrome, not touch it), and lives in the same pure module where the node suite can hold
it.

## 7. Honest gaps

**`DialogueIntake` is fixed but not photographed.** The spoken-dialogue surface is unreachable in
this checkout — the ASR bridge on :8802 is down, so `DialogueGate` falls straight through to the
typed steps (visible in the dev banner in every intake screenshot). Its clearance is now the same
measured hook as the typed steps', which is strictly better than `128 + 176`, and it is pinned by
`e2e/overflow.spec.ts`; but I did not watch it render and I am not claiming I did.

**`(auth)/otp` needed a stub to be reached at all.** The dev API is not running, and the screen
redirects to `(auth)/phone` unless `requestOtp` resolved. A throwaway HTTP harness in the
scratchpad answered `/healthz`, `/v1/auth/attest/challenge` and `/v1/auth/otp/request` — three
endpoints, no verification, nothing stored — purely to land the simulator on the screen for the
photograph. It was killed immediately afterwards, nothing about it is committed, and no mock
entered the repo (invariant 17).

**The `İç Hastalıkları` wrap is still there** and is not mine to close. INT-260 measured it
(371 pt needed against a ~330 pt measure), recovered 16 pt of card padding, tried and reverted a
serif→sans swap, and filed it as a copy decision for the `00 §2` owner (R2). I found no lever it
missed.
