# INT-425: App Store / Play screenshots, story-led, real captures, app-store-screenshots skill

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `mobile` · **GitHub:** #720 ·
**Depends on:** INT-422, INT-423 · **Branch:** `int-425-store-screenshots` · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The ask

The owner asked on 2 Oct 2026 for App Store and Google Play screenshots that are structured, meaningful and well designed, made with the app-store-screenshots skill, and that do not look AI-generated. Acceptance, verbatim:

- "a story-led sequence (one benefit per frame, in the order of the patient journey) from real captures of the refreshed app, using simulated-patient data only"
- "every required size for iOS and Android, Turkish first, no accuracy or medical claims (invariant 20), and the Önvizit brand"
- "the source project is in the repo, and the rationale is in docs/design/store-screenshots.md"

## What was built

- **Captures** (`docs/store/screenshots/captures/`): eight iPhone 16e simulator captures (1170×2532) of the refreshed UI. INT-422 (#725) and INT-423 (#728) are not yet on `main`, so their branch is merged into this one, and that merge drops out once they land. The data is the synthetic dev account plus typed simulated-patient answers. The status bar was set to 9:41 with `simctl status_bar`. For the capture session only (never committed), the fixture's hospital names became "Örnek Hastanesi" and the visit moved to an upcoming date. Local stubs answered the dev voice lanes' health check with silence, so the dialogue screen opened. No audio was recorded.
- **Source project** (`docs/store/screenshots/`):
  - `app-store-screenshots.json`, the skill's project file, written by `tools/gen_project.py`.
  - `tools/build_editor.py`, which copies the upstream template (pinned to `8bd7520`, not vendored) and applies an asserted, presentation-only theme patch: a flat ground, a sentence-case eyebrow, SF Pro on Apple frames and Roboto on Android frames, a neutral bezel, and a flat feature graphic.
  - The brand icon, from INT-420's `icon.png`.
- **Exports** (`docs/store/screenshots/export/`): 8 frames × 4 iPhone sizes (1320×2868, 1284×2778, 1206×2622, 1125×2436), 8 Play phone frames (1080×1920) and the 1024×500 feature graphic. All are RGB with no alpha, about 10 MB in total. There is no iPad set, because `supportsTablet` is false.
- **Rationale** (`docs/design/store-screenshots.md`): each frame's benefit and copy, the grid and type, the colour tokens, the data used, and what the owner still has to do.
- **Guard** (`tests/store/test_store_screenshots.py`): every size exists for every frame and is RGB; one capture per frame in journey order; the brand is Önvizit and the retired name is absent; the Turkish copy comes first, with two lines of at most 16 characters each; and the copy passes `claims_scan.sh --strict` and names no diagnosis, AI or figures.
