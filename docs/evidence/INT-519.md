## Safety direction, item by item

- **1.** Asks more patients than today once applied (the items never open today), and never asks a relative. Neither item is a red-flag probe.
- **2 and 3.** Ask fewer patients than today's accidental behaviour: `'hayir'` matches no answer, so the gates open after a plain "hayır" too.
  - The patients dropped are exactly those whose answer denies the question's premise, and no rule reads either item.
  - The pregnancy safety net is the LMP, asked after every answer (item 4).
  - Against the intent recorded when the gates were written (the proposals, CD-100 T4), nothing narrows.
  - Item 2's hedge mapping widens: a hedge opens the item, where UNCLEAR would close it.
- **4.** Never fewer askers than today. The guard only adds the patients whose pregnancy answer is missing or UNCLEAR.
- **5, 8, 9, 11.** At least as acute as before. A hedge reaches the most acute day without a turn that could only lower it, and a negative to the timing question cannot silence both arms.
- **6.** Unchanged while draft: over-triage (K2 can fire for this band), accepted only within D18's simulated scope.
- **7.** Never less acute than any band the answer fits, nor than today's fallback.
- **10.** More acute than today, where the anchor stays Unknown and neither chest-pain rule can fire. The cost is over-triage of an affirmed pain that was long past (CD-04's "eskiden vardı"); the qualifier and the label reach the clinician.

**No paediatric extrapolation.**

- NG253 is for people aged 16 or over, and NG254 (under 16s) was not retrieved. In a child chain, item 2's mapping keeps the same direction on the CKM presence value set alone, and no adult threshold is carried to a child.
- CG84 is cited only for the direction NICE takes under doubt, as CD-05 already cites it.
- Every under-18 session is refused today (INT-223, HB-L20).
