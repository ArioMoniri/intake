## Design decisions

- `allQuestions` became an explicit canonical-anamnesis-order list: pack
  concatenation could not preserve per-pack order once packs share questions.
  A test pins every pack as a subsequence of it — this is what makes
  `buildNote`'s HPI lines follow the interview order. `note.ts` itself needed
  no change; the ordering authority lives in `allQuestions`.
- `complaint` and `extra` intentionally carry no `hpiLabelKey` (they feed the
  CC section and the "Hasta notu" line); the shape test documents the
  exemption.
