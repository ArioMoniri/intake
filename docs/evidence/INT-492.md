## What ships

- **The "Soru N / M" capsule** (`components/QuestionProgress.tsx`, rules in `lib/intake/progressCapsule.ts`). It replaces INT-433's thin bar and line.
  - A solid capsule holding one segment per section of the flow (the HPI dimensions, medicines and allergies, the history block, the closing note), each as wide as its share of the questions. The filled length is exactly the old bar's fraction at every question; a section change is the next segment becoming current. More than six sections fall back to one continuous track.
  - The count is "Soru 3 / 9" as one non-breaking atom (U+00A0 throughout), never grouped, in two weights, with tabular figures. It sits beside the segments at the everyday sizes and moves under them from a text scale of 1.35.
  - It draws inside the one progressbar, so a reader hears one value ("Soru 3, toplam 9"); no part of it is read on its own and nothing in it announces itself.
  - It moves nothing: it swaps with the step, and the question's 120 ms fade stays the step's one animated element (ADR-033 §2, C9).
  - It is solid on the capture-protected intake (INT-477): opaque theme fills, no material.
- **Answer chips** (`components/ChipSlot.tsx`, rule `chipSlot` in `lib/controlChrome.ts`): the check slot is reserved in both states, a ring when empty and a tinted disc with the check when selected, so a tap never widens a chip or reflows the chips after it. Instant (ADR-033 §4). The selection tick (ADR-038 A2) is unchanged.
- **The press** (`components/PressScale.tsx`, `lib/motion/native/pressScale.ts`, numbers in `lib/motion/micro.ts`): ADR-037 P1, ratified, on "Devam edin", "Özeti görün" and "Gönder" only. 0.97 under the finger on the app's one critically damped spring (`PRESS_SPRING = BUBBLE_SPRING`), the instant opacity step kept, the commit on release unchanged. Absent under Reduce Motion and on RED; never on consent, a gate, RED or an instrument option. The motion guard's spring allow-list holds this one module, the guard change ADR-037's table names.
- **The loading wait** (`components/DialogueGate.tsx`): the voice probe's status row is a filled capsule; under Reduce Motion it shows a still "•••" glyph (`ellipsis`) instead of the platform spinner.

No copy changes: every visible line is an existing key. No RED file, consent screen, instrument step or gate is touched.
