**`hosted_tts` stays empty.** `gpt-4o-mini-tts` is listed only per token ($0.60 text input, $12.00 audio output per 1M), and the speech route reports no usage. There is no per-character list price for it, and dividing by a guessed tokens-per-character ratio would be an invented number. The hosted TTS lane is therefore **refused under the cap** (`hosted_lane_unpriced`) until it has a price. This is an owner ask.

The other self-hosted components (ASR, dynamic TTS, Talker and extraction) have no per-unit list price, because a host-hour figure says nothing about throughput. Their spend is therefore counted per GPU host-second at the model-call level, not per unit. SMS has no provider. These items stay open in `docs/backlog/blockers.md` (INT-190).

## What changed

- **`intake_platform.cost.caps`** (new) adds `SpendCap` and `ModelRate`. `CostConfig` gains `spend_caps` and `model_rates`, and `Price` gains `url` (https only, and only on a priced row).
  - A model rate holds no number of its own: it points at a priced component. One figure therefore lives in one place, and the console, the rules and the runtime ledger all read it there.
  - The schema refuses a rate on an unknown component, on an unpriced component, with a unit the route cannot be charged in, under a provider that does not bill that component, or as a second rate for a lane it already prices.
- **`infra/costs/unit-prices.yaml`** gains:
  - the two sourced prices;
  - the cap `elective-models-month`: 80 USD per UTC calendar month, warning ratio 0.8, sourced to the owner's words and D19;
  - two model rates: self-hosted g6.2xlarge, and hosted `eleven_multilingual_v2`.
- **`intake_voice_runtime.metering.spend`** (new):
  - `price_call` charges self-hosted lanes per host-second and hosted lanes per token, character or audio-second. An unpriced lane is `UnpricedCall`, never zero.
  - `MonthlySpendLedger`:
    - **Elective:** admitted while the month is below the cap. Refused once the cap is reached, when the store cannot be read, or when no cap is configured.
    - **Obligatory:** always admitted, and still counted.
    - **Warning:** fires once per month on crossing 64 USD.
  - Stores:
    - `InMemorySpendStore`.
    - `RedisSpendStore`: integer micro-USD with `INCRBY`, one key per deployment, month and charge class, a 62-day TTL, and fail-closed reads.
- **The existing ledger enforces it.**
  - `SessionLedger(spend=…)` puts every elective charge behind the monthly cap first, using three new `AdmissionReason` members.
  - `SessionMeter.charge_model_call` prices a call and counts it against the month.
  - The per-session extraction row says where the bound now lives.
- **Admin cost module.**
  - `compose_cost_payload` gains `spend_caps`: the limit with its provenance, the 64 USD warning point, `max(model_spend_month_usd)`, and a verdict (`within`, `warning`, `reached` or `not_evaluable`). There is never a "0 of 80".
  - Each price now carries its `url`.
  - admin-web gains `src/cost/spendCap.ts`, which refuses unsound verdicts, unsourced caps and non-https pages, and `module.ts` renders it.
- **Alerts.** `infra/observability/cost-rules.yaml` gains the group `model-spend-cap`, with `ModelSpendCapWarning` (≥ 64 and < 80) and `ModelSpendCapReached` (≥ 80). Both raise a ticket, and their runbook headings are in `docs/runbooks/cost-budgets.md`.
- **Metrics.** `model_spend_month_usd` and `model_spend_cap_events_total` are catalogued as `live`.
- **Decision and blockers.**
  - 00_STATE §3 **D19**. D18 was already minted twice on unmerged branches (INT-421, INT-332), so this decision takes D19 to collide with neither.
  - HUMAN_BLOCKERS HB-O8 is **closed** with its reason.
  - The INT-190 row in `docs/backlog/blockers.md` is narrowed to what is still open.
  - The threat-model `observability-pipeline` section gets a note.
- **INT-428 `dispatch` (stacked on #751).**
  - The spend core moved to `intake_platform.cost.spend`, because `intake_platform.model_lanes` cannot import the voice runtime. It now owns `ChargeClass`, which the voice runtime re-exports, and the two series.
  - `ModelRate` now names a component per measure (`charges`), so input and output tokens carry their own prices.
  - `intake_platform.model_lanes.spend.HostedSpendGate` admits each hosted attempt before the provider is asked. It refuses when the cap is reached, when the store cannot be read, when no cap is configured, or when the lane has no rate. After the call it charges what the provider reported.
  - `dispatch` calls the gate on the first attempt and on the fallback retry.
  - `LaneRouter(spend=…)` carries the gate. A router with none refuses every hosted call (`hosted_spend_unmetered`).
  - `model_lanes_from_env` builds the gate from `infra/costs/unit-prices.yaml` and a Redis spend store on the control store's URL, keyed by the deployment's data class.
  - INT-428's test kit gets a default gate with synthetic whole-cent test rates.
- **Backlog.** Row INT-439 is added, now depending on INT-428. The pins are 330 on the stack (main + INT-428 + INT-439), and files/11 is regenerated.
