# INT-423: chat interface — natural rhythm and Liquid Glass materials (HIG), content stays solid

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `mobile` · **GitHub:** #718 ·
**Depends on:** INT-422 · **Branch:** `int-423-chat-rhythm` (stacked on `int-422-design-system-refresh`) · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The ask

On 2 Oct 2026 the owner asked for a chat that feels natural and native to Apple, with Liquid Glass used as the HIG uses it:
- glass on chrome and floating controls, content on solid fills, and Reduce Transparency giving solid;
- grouped turns, a readable measure, and Dynamic Type up to the AX sizes;
- motion per ADR-033, with RED unchanged;
- contrast tested in light, dark and Increase Contrast.

The rationale is in `docs/design/chat-rhythm.md`.

## Why stacked on INT-422

The acceptance asks for Increase Contrast coverage. The Increase Contrast palette, `colorsFor`, the `question` role and the refreshed tokens all arrive in INT-422 (PR #725), and none of them exists on `origin/main`. So this branch is cut from INT-422's tip, and its PR targets that branch, which keeps the diff to INT-423 alone. When #725 merges, the PR retargets to `main`.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/dialogue/rhythm.ts`** (new, pure):
  - `placeTurns` groups consecutive same-speaker turns, and only a run's last turn carries the tail;
  - `GROUPED_MARGIN` closes the 16 pt turn gap to 4 pt within a group;
  - `bubbleMaxWidth` sets the measure: 84 % of the transcript, capped at 560 pt, and the full width from the large-text scale;
  - `sideOf` maps a stream entry to its speaker.
- **`src/components/ChatLines.tsx`** (new): `AgentQuestionLines`. An optional lead-in renders as a Subheadline in the secondary ink, above the Body question and its Footnote aside. INT-414's composer passes its resolved lead-in as `leadIn`; no lead-in field exists on any branch yet, so the wiring is one prop in whichever PR lands second.
- **`DialogueIntake.tsx`**, styles and placement only:
  - every bubble (agent question, confirm, unclear, transcribing, patient words, open mic) takes `shape(index)`, which gives the measure, the grouped margin and the tail only on a run's last bubble;
  - the measure reads the transcript's laid-out width (`onLayout`), not the window, so landscape insets are already out of it;
  - the fixed tails and the 84 % `maxWidth` left the base styles;
  - the question bubble renders through `AgentQuestionLines`.

  No copy, accessible name, motion, RED path or glass host changed.
- **Not changed:** `GlassBar` (already the chat's chrome, with a solid branch), `PulseDot` (the one opacity loop, still at rest under reduce motion), RED files, i18n.

### First green run

Two failures came up, and both were fixed:
- **`a11y.spec.ts` (SC 1.3.4)** refuses a size read from `useWindowDimensions()`, the long side in landscape. The measure now reads the transcript's own `onLayout` width, which is also more correct.
