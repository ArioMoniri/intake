# INT-250 — the universal history sections, and what they refuse to decide

Owner directive, verbatim: *"the questions should be dynamic and covering a specific
per department per chief complaint, dynamic to previous questions and answers and
making a complete previsit (in 2 modes of fast and in hospital) like allergy previous
medicine familial issues and so on"*.

## The boundary this issue works inside

The guideline-grounded, department-specific clinical content is the INT-043 →
INT-044..051 chain and it is `awaiting-human` on a physician's sign-off (HB-C1).
None of it is here.

What is here is the part that needs no clinical judgement and was missing: the
**universal history sections every intake needs regardless of specialty** — allergies,
current medications, past medical history, family history — authored as QUESTIONS,
plus the mode and deferral wiring that makes them behave under Hızlı and Detaylı.

Asking *"do you have any allergies?"* is not a clinical decision. Deciding what an
answer **means** is. Every refusal is listed in **§6**, with its reason and its owner.

## What was built

### 1. The block, composed rather than copied

`apps/mobile/src/lib/intake/questions.ts` gains `historyQuestions` — 18 steps in
04 §1's own two sections — and `packForVisit(department, profile)`, which composes
them onto a department pack between the last anamnesis dimension and the free note
(04 §1 order: `hpi` → `meds_allergies` → `pmh_fh_sh` → `expectations_wrapup`).

Composition, not duplication, because that is what the protocol registry does
(03 §6: base → cluster → specialty). `packForDepartment` is unchanged and still
returns the ten authored HPI packs; the base copy is authored once and every branch
inherits the same wording. It is also why the blast radius on the existing suite was
four assertions rather than forty.

Wording follows `packages/protocols/base/intake.yaml`'s own `ask_tr` / `follow_up_tr`.
That file's header records the same line this issue draws: question copy is product
copy an agent may write; every clinical **value** in it is left empty for the physician.

| note section (04 §5) | protocol section (04 §1) | questions | of which gated | modes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `allergies` | `meds_allergies` | 4 | 3 | both |
| `medications` | `meds_allergies` | 4 | 2 | both |
| `pmh` | `pmh_fh_sh` | 7 | 4 | Detaylı |
| `family_social` | `pmh_fh_sh` | 3 | 2 | Detaylı |
| **total** | | **18** | **11** | |

Two vocabularies, deliberately, and a test pins that they agree
(`PROTOCOL_SECTION_OF`): 04 §1 groups **questions** into protocol sections, 04 §5
groups **answers** into note sections, and `meds_allergies` splits in two on the way
across. The note ids are `pmh` / `medications` / `allergies` / `family_social` —
the `PreVisitNote` field names, which are already `NOTE_SECTION_IDS` in
`packages/clinical-schemas` and already have renderers in `mappers/document.py`.
Nothing invented a parallel vocabulary.

### 2. Free-text-light structure

Closed choices where the blueprint gives them, a plain free-text path otherwise:

- `allergy_kind` — six chips; the five named groups plus *Başka*.
- `pmh_conditions` — the five chronic-disease deltas **quoted from 04 §2.1
  `cluster.medical`** ("DM, HTN, COPD/asthma, CKD, thyroid"), plus *Başka*. The list
  is the blueprint's, not a selection made here.
- `fh_who` — a relationship list (anne · baba · kardeş · çocuk · büyükanne ya da
  büyükbaba · başka bir yakın). Structural, identical for every branch.
- Every closed question carries an escape (`other`, `not_sure`, `dont_remember`) **or**
  a free-text follow-up gated on it. A test enforces it.

**Invariant 22.** No numeric field exists anywhere on the clinical path. A dose is
text in the patient's own words (`meds_other_detail`), and its hint deliberately asks
for the **colour of the box**, not a number. `pmh_surgery_detail`'s hint invites a year
— typed into a plain text field, with no unit, no arithmetic and no numeric keyboard.
Three tests hold the line: the pack model has no numeric `kind`; no history string in
either catalogue contains a numeral; and neither clinical-path screen
(`intake.tsx`, `DialogueIntake.tsx`) mentions `keyboardType` or `inputMode`.

### 3. Chief-complaint depth

Gates stay exactly the INT-239 shape — a further detail of something the patient has
**already named**. Two additional structural rules now hold for the history block and
are tested: **no history gate reads the chief complaint** (history is universal), and
**no history gate crosses a section boundary** (nothing is inferred across sections).

the answers, never by the file. Inside the history block the depth-two chains are
`allergy_known → allergy_kind → allergy_detail`, `pmh_known → pmh_conditions →
pmh_other_detail` and `fh_known → fh_who → fh_detail`.

**No department HPI gate was added.** Every gate that would have deepened a
department pack further is in §6 as clinical judgement.

### 4. Both modes

02 §2 sets the split and this issue follows it exactly rather than re-deciding it:
Hızlı is *"meds/allergies confirm"*, Detaylı adds *"PMH/FH/SH deltas"*.

- Hızlı costs **three unconditional turns** (`allergy_known`, `meds_other`,
  `meds_otc`); everything else in the block is gated, so a patient with no allergy and
  no extra medicine pays exactly three.
- Detaylı opens with seven — the same three plus the four "is there one?" openers.
- Mode and deferral are **session-constant**, so they select the *profile* (which
  questions the pack contains) rather than gating per answer. `askIf` stays purely
  answer-driven, exactly as `dsl.py Slot.ask_if` is, and files/13 §9.5's guard
  whitelist already carries `mode()` as a first-class input.
- Wired at all four pack call sites: `intake.tsx`, `review.tsx` (the checkpoint
  `eksik` list no longer lists a question this mode never asks), `today.tsx` (the prep
  ring's denominator) and, through `intake.tsx`'s `questions` prop, the spoken
  dialogue engine and the realtime section panel.

**Turn budget, stated rather than quietly absorbed.** 02 §2 targets ≤ 14 turns / ≤ 18
turns for Hızlı and already records the content list at 16–21 in the clean case; 04 §1
puts the whole `meds_allergies` row in the fast column. The three unconditional turns
added here push a Kardiyoloji Hızlı session from 7 to 10 opening turns, and a patient
who reports an allergy and an extra medicine reaches ~16. That collision is HB-C5 and
00 §5 Q8 (owner `ClinicalAuthor`) — **it is not resolved here, and no content was cut
to make the arithmetic work**, because cutting content is the clinical decision.

### 5. Carried facts — consumed, not forked

INT-247's `carriedFacts.ts` already said what to do: *"Conditions, allergies and
baselines are also `PatientOverlay` facts (files/09 §3) and join this union when their
note sections exist."* Their section now exists, so:

- `CarriedFactKind` gains `"allergy"`. `reconcileCarriedFacts` is untouched — the
  producer, the buckets and the type-level rule (`ReconciledFact` **requires** a
  `FactDecision`, so no note line can be built from an undecided fact) are INT-247's.
- The three copy lookups became **kind-aware** (`factStateKey`, `factActionKey`,
  `factNoteMarkerKey`) because a patient does not *stop* an allergy. A note line
  reading *"hasta bıraktığını söyledi"* against a penicillin allergy would be a false
  clinical statement, not an awkward one. Same three outcomes, same 04 §5
  reconciliation vocabulary, different words.
- `buildNote` files each kind under its own section, and `review.tsx` renders the
  review block per kind, so an allergy is never answered inside the medication list.
- Fixture: Ayşe carries `cf-ayse-penisilin`, `kind: "allergy"`, asserted 2026-05-12.
  It arrives **UNCONFIRMED**; the note shows it with `bu ziyarette onaylanmadı` and
  the earlier record's date, and it becomes current only when this visit says so.

### 6. Privacy deferral — family history is the second consumer

The mechanism supported it once the question layer could see the deferral list, so it
is wired and this is the "say so": **`fh_known` / `fh_who` / `fh_detail` are absent
from a Detaylı flow whose 02 §33 self-check found a third party present.** Not asked
more quietly, not moved to text — absent, with `Aile öyküsü — eksik: ortam uygun
değildi` on the note.

The reason is 02 §33's own: family history is a disclosure about people who are not in
the room to consent to it. **This is a policy call a clinical/legal owner should
confirm** — family history is *not* one of 13 §13's named `text_first` sections, so
this extends the third-party rule rather than applying a written one.

`WELLBEING_DEFERRAL` still behaves exactly as INT-236 built it; the reducer, the note
section and the `eksik` marker are now a list rather than one hard-coded branch.

## What awaits the physician

Everything below is a question I could have written and deliberately did not, because
writing it would have been a clinical determination. Each names its owner.

### Gates refused as clinical judgement

1. **Allergy severity or reaction grading.** `allergy_reaction` records what happens
   and orders its options in the order `base/intake.yaml`'s own `follow_up_tr` names
   them. Nothing reads that list, ranks it, or maps it to anaphylaxis. Which reaction
   is dangerous is `rf.anaphylaxis`, and its trigger is unsigned (HB-C1).
   *files/04 §5 `AllergyItem.severity` therefore stays unpopulated by this tier.*
2. **Allergy × medication interaction.** A patient who reports a penicillin allergy and
   then names a penicillin is not flagged, not warned and not cross-checked. Interaction
   logic is a clinical decision-support function and would put the product inside
   MDR Rule 11 (06 §0.3, `device_functions`).
3. **Immunosuppression / chronic-condition cross-gates.** `pmh_conditions` opens no
   red-flag probe. A "diabetes" answer does not open a hyperglycaemia probe, an
   "asthma/COPD" answer does not open a breathlessness probe, and no answer arms
   `rf.sepsis_like` or `rf.neutropenic_fever`. Those are 04 §2.1 `cluster.medical` and
   04 §3 rules, all `draft_needs_source`.
4. **`anticoagulant_use`** — a base `meds_allergies` slot, `must_ask: true`, not
   authored here. `base/intake.yaml`'s own comment says why it exists: *"the BASE slot
   must exist or the bleeding and head-injury rules have nothing universal to gate on."*
   Selecting a drug-class question because a red-flag rule will need it is that rule's
   decision, not a copy decision.
5. **Family-history condition checklist.** `fh_who` is a relationship list and
   `fh_detail` is free text. Which familial conditions matter is per-specialty —
   04 §2.2 names *"family history of GI cancer/IBD"* for gastroenterology alone — so no
   universal checklist was invented.
6. **Department-conditioned history.** No history question is gated on the department
   or on the chief complaint. Asking about thyroid disease only in Endokrinoloji, or
   about family cancer history only in Gastroenteroloji, is a differential.
7. **A recall window on the PMH delta.** `pmh_recent_change` asks *"since your last
   visit"*, which is 04 §1's own framing. "In the last year", "in the last six months"
   and "since you were last admitted" are all thresholds and none was invented.
8. **Any threshold at all.** No history question carries a number, a duration bound, a
   count or a cut-off.

### Content not authored, and why

9. **Pregnancy status and LMP** (`pregnancy_status`, `lmp_date`). 13 §4 asks for them
   early and universally and 13 §14 item 13 wants them promoted to class-1 gatekeepers.
   Not authored: 13 §13 marks them `text_first`, the dev-tier `IntakeQuestion` has no
   `delivery` field, and the same pack drives the spoken dialogue engine — so authoring
   them now would put a `text_first` question into a voice flow. The whole pregnancy
   rule set is additionally absent (HB-C4).
10. **Substance use** (`smoking_status`, `alcohol_use`). Same reason: 13 §13 puts
    substance use in `text_first`, and there is no `delivery` field to honour it with.
    The copy itself is settled in `base/intake.yaml`; it needs the mechanism, not a
    clinician.
11. **The rest of the social history** (`occupation`, `living_situation`,
    `caregiver_present`). Out of the owner's named scope, and `living_situation` /
    `caregiver_present` touch third parties the way family history does, so they belong
    in the same deferral conversation.
12. **ROS.** 04 §1 lists an "associated symptoms sweep" for Detaylı and 13 §4 records
    Verdon & Siemens' 10.5 % case-finding yield with a 3.3 % PPV. Which ROS items earn
    their turn is exactly the judgement 13 §4 says to make deliberately.

### Decisions to confirm

13. **Is family history a sensitive section under 02 §33?** Wired as one, on the
    reasoning that it is a disclosure about absent third parties. It is not in 13 §13's
    named list. Owner: clinical + legal.
14. **The fast-mode turn budget.** Three unconditional turns added; 02 §2's ≤ 18 was
    already over-subscribed. Owner: `ClinicalAuthor` (HB-C5, 00 §5 Q8).
15. **`changed` → `conflict` for an allergy.** INT-247 flagged this mapping as
    provisional for medications; it is inherited here. *"It is different"* against an
    allergy may deserve its own reconciliation value. Owner: pack owners.

## Gaps reported rather than edited

`packages/protocols/**` and `services/**` are out of scope for this issue; these are
findings for their owners.

- **`base/intake.yaml` has no family-history slot at all.** Its `pmh_fh_sh` section
  carries PMH and social history but nothing familial, while 04 §1 names the row
  `pmh_fh_sh` and 04 §5 gives `FamilySocial.family` a field. The base pack is short a
  slot.
- **`services/api/src/intake_api/schemas.py:54` pins
  `SectionId = Literal["chief_complaint", "hpi", "medications"]`.** The mobile note
  already emitted `device_health` and `deferred` outside that literal, and now also
  emits `pmh`, `allergies` and `family_social` — which *are* in
  `clinical_schemas` `NOTE_SECTION_IDS`. The API literal is the odd one out and will
  reject share scopes for the new sections when the mobile note starts posting.
- **`base/intake.yaml`'s `allergies` slot is a single free-text string** where 04 §5
  models `AllergyItem{substance, reaction, severity}`. The structure this issue
  authored (kind → detail → reaction) is closer to the schema; the base pack could
  adopt it once a physician reviews the option lists.
