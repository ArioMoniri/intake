## 2. Metrics and their sources

Every metric is a count of distinct people, per closed ISO week (UTC), per
protocol id and version; shares and drop-offs on the page are sums and
differences of cells of the same table.

| View kind | Partition (closed domain) | Population | Source | Status on this build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `funnel` | `stage`: invited / started / finished / shared (furthest reached by the week's end) | everyone with a visit opened in the week | `sessions` (id, patient, protocol, version, instants), `notes` (session, created_at), `shares` (note, created_at), `documents` (session, created_at) — metadata only | **live** |
| `locale` | `locale`: the locale registry (today `tr`) | everyone | `sessions.locale` (INT-296) | **live** |
| `triage` | `triage_level`: red / yellow / green / not_evaluated | finished intakes | the level exists only inside the envelope-encrypted previsit document | not collected — `triage_level_not_recorded` |
| `platform` | `platform`: ios / android / web | people with a platform on record | raw telemetry (nothing stores it) | not collected — `raw_telemetry_retention_undecided` |
| `question_outcome` | `outcome`: answered / skipped / abandoned (completion and drop-off) | people who reached the question | raw telemetry | not collected — same |
| `question_mode` | `input_mode`: typed / voice (typed vs voice share) | people who answered | raw telemetry | not collected — same |
| `question_repeat` | `repeat_band`: none / one_or_more (repeat/clarify rate) | people who reached the question | raw telemetry | not collected — same |
| `question_time` | `time_band`: under 10 s / 10–30 s / 30–60 s / over 60 s (time-to-answer) | people who answered | raw telemetry | not collected — same |
| `question_asr` | `asr_band`: high / medium / low (a **measured** recognition-quality band of each spoken answer) | people who answered by voice | raw telemetry | not collected — same |

**Why the triage level is not decrypted.** Counting RED/YELLOW/GREEN from what
is stored would mean the API decrypting patient previsit documents on a staff
request. files/10 §9 gives exactly one read path to patient content
(break-glass), so this build does not open a second one. On this slice the
only producer of previsit outputs (`record_previsit_output`) writes
`not_evaluated` in any case. The source the view needs is a content-free record
of the rules-engine level, written where the engine runs.

**Why per-question and platform are not collected.** No stored row holds a
question's outcome, answer mode, time, repeats or recognition band, or the
device platform. The mobile app keeps answers on the device until the note is
composed, and the voice runtime persists nothing per turn. Recording them is
per-person raw telemetry that is linked to health questions. Under D28 that
needs an opt-in consent naming the purpose (HB-L26), and LD-06 leaves its
retention to the owner. The views, the suppression, the payload, the page and
the Loop-2 bridge are all built and tested for these kinds over synthetic
records (`analytics.full.payload.json`). The page reports them **not
collected, with the reason**. It does not estimate them.

**Metrics catalogue (invariant 15).** Three new families, all
`owner: learning`, labels from the closed rule:
`analytics_research_view_shown_total{view}`,
`analytics_research_view_withheld_total{view,reason}`,
`analytics_research_tenant_refused_total{reason}`. The `view` label is a
view **kind**. A protocol, version, question or week never becomes a label
value, because that would publish to Prometheus the very cell a refusal
withheld. `tests/observability/test_analytics_dimension_keys.py` checks the
values actually incremented, not just the keys.

## 3. Suppression rules

The floor is INT-171's `K_ANONYMITY_FLOOR` (10). It is imported, never
redeclared. `k` is keyword-only with no default, and may be raised but never
lowered.

1. **Listing.** A view is listed only if its population holds at least 10
   distinct people. A population of 1–9 is not listed at all, so it cannot be
   told apart from an empty one (the INT-171 "refusal is not an oracle" rule).
2. **Within a view (LD-06 `whole_views_only`).** Each view is a partition of
   its population into a closed domain. If any cell holds 1–9 people, the
   **whole view** is withheld. The page shows `<10` and the reason, never a
   figure, and never which cell was small. Zero is published, as INT-171's
   materialised views already publish it (an absent row reads as empty).
3. **Across views (LD-06 `across_views`).** There is one fixed grain: closed
   ISO weeks in UTC, with no open week served. There is one parameter
   (`period`). Any other parameter is refused, and a tenant cut is refused. The
   auditor then asks the general question: *is any group of 1–9 people
   computable by adding and subtracting shown counts?*
   - People are grouped into classes by exactly which shown cells they fall
     in.
   - A union of classes is recoverable exactly when its indicator lies in the
     row space of the cells × classes matrix.
   - The search runs in reduced row-echelon form over GF(2⁶¹−1), with classes
     of ≥10 ordered first. Only rows whose pivot is a small class can take part
     in a group under 10, and the search is pruned by the pivot sizes.
   - Both approximations fail closed. Modular arithmetic can only over-report.
     A node budget that runs out counts as a violation.
   - On a violation, the lowest-priority view among the cells the recovering
     combination uses (including the subtracted ones) is withheld
     (`cross_view`), and the check runs again.
4. **At the boundary.** The API runs `assert_payload_safe` on its own answer
   before replying. It refuses, whole, on any of these:
   - a key off the closed lists;
   - a string that is neither id-shaped nor an ISO instant;
   - anything UUID-shaped;
   - a non-count number;
   - a shown cell of 1–9;
   - a withheld view that kept its cells;
   - k below 10.

   The admin-web view-model independently refuses the same set.

Tests with near misses (`tests/analytics/test_research_release.py`,
`test_research_payload.py`):

- **The floor.** A cell of 9 withholds the view and a cell of 10 shows it. A
  population of 9 is not listed, and one of exactly 10 is.
- **Isolating combinations.** A thin protocol version beside a wide one is
  withheld. A question outcome of 3 abandoned is withheld, and so is every
  view that would give the 3 back by subtraction (15 started − 12 answered).
- **Cross-view subtraction.** The funnel population minus the platform
  population = 3 is found, and the platform view is withheld.
- **Independent oracle.** An exhaustive `fractions.Fraction` oracle confirms
  the attack existed without the check and that nothing small is recoverable
  after it, on fixed scenarios and on five seeded random populations.
- **Overlapping views.** Views that overlap but determine nothing small are
  left alone.
- **Budget.** Running out of search budget fails closed.

## 4. Retention — the fail-closed reading, and a recommendation for the owner

**In code (no number, no default).** `INTAKE_ANALYTICS_AGGREGATE_RETENTION_DAYS`
and `INTAKE_ANALYTICS_RAW_TELEMETRY_RETENTION_DAYS` are read per request. If
either is absent or empty it is *undecided*. A value that is not a positive
whole number of days is refused (503 `retention_config_refused`), never read
as undecided.

**The reading chosen, and why.** A retention period governs something
**kept**, so while a period is undecided nothing of that kind is kept:

- **Aggregates undecided.** The release is computed per request from source
  rows that sit inside their own files/06 §3 clocks. It is answered `no-store`
  and written to no table, file, cache or export. A Loop-2 evidence bundle,
  which rides a pull request and is therefore kept, is refused with
  `RetentionUndecided`.
- **The live read is not refused.** LD-06's own `fail_closed_while_draft` lets
  aggregates that pass the gate reach the panel while retention is draft. The
  stricter reading ("serve nothing older than an unset limit") would keep
  nothing more safely and would only blank the page.
- **Raw telemetry undecided.** Nothing records it. Setting a period does not
  invent the recording: the views then say `raw_telemetry_not_recorded` until
  a producer lands.

**Recommendation for the owner (not in code).**

**(a) Anonymised aggregates: 24 months**, kept as frozen snapshots of each
closed ISO week.

- They are not personal data (KVKK m.3(1)(b); m.28(1)(b); Yönetmelik m.10), so
  neither m.7 nor the Yönetmelik's destruction cycle binds them, and LD-06
  records the same. The period is therefore a governance choice, not a
  statutory one.
- 24 months gives two full year-over-year cycles of the same ISO weeks. That
  is what improvement and research need to see seasonality, and what Loop 2
  needs beyond files/09 §4's "stable over 2 weeks".
- It is bounded rather than indefinite. Anonymity is judged "başka verilerle
  eşleştirilse dahi" (m.3(1)(b); Yönetmelik m.10(2)), and the stock of data an
  aggregate could be matched against only grows. A fixed horizon, reviewed at
  each six-monthly destruction-policy review, bounds that.
- Freezing closed weeks also closes the one residual risk in §6 (temporal
  differencing through later deletions).

**(b) Raw per-person telemetry, if it is ever collected under D28's opt-in
consent: 30 days.** No 1-year cold copy. Patient-deletable at once on a DSAR
erasure. Swept at least daily.

- It is personal data: pseudonymous and linked to health questions. `slots`
  already annotates a question id as special-category health. KVKK m.7(1)
  therefore applies: it is deleted once the reason to process it ends.
- Its only reason is computing the aggregate of a closed week, and a week
  closes within 7 days of any event in it. 30 days covers late events and a
  reprocessing window after a computation defect.
- 30 days equals files/06 §3's "Telemetry: 30 d hot" and the existing
  `RET_EVENTS` hot window. The "1 y cold" tier is excluded because it is
  premised on telemetry being "PHI-free by construction", which person-linked
  question telemetry is not.
- Yönetmelik m.11(2)'s six-month ceiling bounds the destruction **cycle**, not
  the period. A 30-day class therefore needs a sweep cadence well inside it,
  not a six-month one.

One sentence: *keep anonymised weekly aggregates 24 months as frozen per-week
snapshots, and any raw per-person telemetry 30 days with no cold copy, swept
daily, well inside the Yönetmelik's six-month destruction ceiling.* Setting
them would be `INTAKE_ANALYTICS_AGGREGATE_RETENTION_DAYS=730` and
`INTAKE_ANALYTICS_RAW_TELEMETRY_RETENTION_DAYS=30` — the owner's call
(CLAUDE.md: data retention is stop-and-ask). Nothing is set in this PR.

## 6. Residual risks, stated

- **Temporal differencing through deletions.** Stage membership is judged as
  of the week's end, so later shares do not move a closed week. A DSAR
  erasure, however, removes a person's rows, and two reads of the same week on
  either side of it differ by that person. Freezing closed weeks closes this,
  but freezing is *keeping* an aggregate, which is §4(a)'s undecided period.
- **Zeros are published**, as INT-171 already does. A table can show that all
  of a group of 10 or more share a value. LD-06 records k-anonymity as the
  product floor and leaves whether 10 is enough for these dimensions to
  counsel (`cell_size.legal_minimum`: not decided).
- **Exact linear recovery is checked; non-negativity bounds are not.** Bounds
  of the "between 0 and n" kind remain. Exact recovery of a non-empty group of
  1–9 people is what the check excludes.
