**Screenshots: none rendered here, on purpose.** #1009 already holds four Turkish captures from
the real iOS build 7 on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator, at 1320×2868, with provenance hashes. Its
renderer frames them for 6.9", 6.5" and the medium group Apple requires. An Expo-web render would
be a second, lower-fidelity set of the same screens. Expo web was checked: `expo export --platform
web` builds. But the web build has neither the native chrome nor the iOS fonts, so it would not
show what the store build shows. Two findings carry forward into the checklist:

- the raw captures carry the simulator's alpha mask, so App Store Connect cannot take them as they
  are, and `asc_submit.py` refuses them (near-miss test);
- the framed export waits for the legal lane to promote INT-502's caption draft.
