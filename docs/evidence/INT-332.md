## What this PR delivers, and what it cannot
- `docs/adr/ADR-033-motion-system-v1.md`, with the status **Proposed — awaiting owner ratification**. An agent cannot ratify it: the issue carries `approval:owner`, and Q1 and Q5 are the owner's to answer.
- The ADR number is the next free one. A scan of `docs/adr/` on `origin/main`, every remote branch and every local branch found ADR-032 as the highest. ADR-031 is an unexplained gap left when ADR-028 (edge) was de-collided to ADR-032 (merge `9addc95a`); it was not reused.
- `docs/index.md` registers ADR-033, marked proposed.
- `docs/design/mobile-ui.md` §Motion points to ADR-033.
- **`files/00_STATE.md` is not touched.** Its §3 records settled decisions only: CLAUDE.md says the §3 decisions "are settled — including the withdrawn ones", every §3 status is adopted, withdrawn or an owner instruction, and the file's preamble admits only a decision, a verified fact, an open question with an owner, or a blocker on a named person. A Proposed row would make an unratified proposal read as settled. The row lands in the ratifying commit, together with the Status flip and the test pin change. The first acceptance clause is therefore met only at ratification.
- The five open questions are recorded in ADR-033 §8. Each row carries the verbatim question, its named owner (owner: Q1, Q5; ClinicalAuthor Dr Efe Onganer: Q2–Q4), `awaiting-human`, and its fail-closed default. No `files/HUMAN_BLOCKERS.md` row exists for them, and none is minted here, because agents do not allocate numbered artefacts.
