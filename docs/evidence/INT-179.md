## What the tests pin

- **Route tree**: `visits/[visitId]/{index,intake,review}` exist and the nav
  map equality still holds (stealth surfaces fail).
- **Fixture discipline**: exactly three steps (chips → single → text); every
  question and option label resolves through the tr catalog — no literals.
- **Note builder**: sections in fixed order; medications come from the
  profile fixture (as reported, no doses invented).
- **The two scope rules**: scope-off excludes a section from `sentSections`;
  turning every scope off still sends (02 §5.1 — sending is the patient's
  call, never blocked by scope choices).
- **Resume**: answers accumulate in the reducer; the screen derives its step
  from the first unanswered question.
- **Register**: catalog sweep fixed sen-form imperatives ("Devam et" →
  "Devam edin", "Vizit ekle" → "Vizit ekleyin", "hazırlan" → "hazırlanın").
