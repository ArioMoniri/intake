# INT-422: design system refresh — native Apple feel, restrained palette, SF Symbols, no AI-generic styling

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `mobile` · **GitHub:** #714 ·
**Depends on:** INT-412 · **Branch:** `int-422-design-system-refresh` · **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The ask

The owner said on 27 Sep 2026 that the visual system looked "too AI-generated". The fix had three parts:

- It should feel native to Apple, calm and clinical.
- Gradients, glows, oversized round cards and decoration go.
- Contrast is tested in light, dark and Increase Contrast, and RED surfaces are unchanged.

A follow-up direction asked for it to be warmer and friendlier as well, with clearer hierarchy and friendly empty states, still with no flourishes.

The design rationale and the before/after captures are in `docs/design/system-refresh.md`.

## What changed

- **Tokens** (`apps/mobile/src/lib/theme.ts`):
  - Colour: the grouped iOS palette in light and dark plus an Increase Contrast palette. Each token names its UIKit and Material 3 role in `SYSTEM_ROLES`, and RED's two colours are unchanged.
  - Type: the Dynamic Type ladder, with a new `question` role at Title 2.
  - Geometry: an 8-pt grid with a 16 pt margin, and 12-pt continuous corners.
  - Fonts: the platform face. `ROUNDED_ROLES` (the Large Title and the numeral) take SF Pro Rounded.
  - Removed: `shadow`, `tracking`, the bundled faces, and the `hero` radius.
- **Increase Contrast** has a pure policy (`lib/a11y/contrast.ts`) and a lazily loaded native adapter (`lib/a11y/native/increaseContrastSource.ts`; iOS Darker System Colors, Android High contrast text). It is read through the shared OS-setting store in `lib/a11y/prefs.ts` and applied once in `useTheme()`.
- **Components** (no screen-level styling):
  - `Themed`: tinted filled CTA, grey secondary, borderless grouped `Card`, separator `Hairline`, role weights.
  - `ListRow`: bare tinted glyph, no shadow.
  - `Kicker`: Footnote header, no dot or tracking.
  - `VisitCard`: no gradient.
  - `Segmented`: iOS thumb.
  - `GuidanceBlock`: bare tinted mark.
  - `PrepGauge`: tint arc, rounded numeral.
  - `FloatingTabBar`: tint for the selected tab.
  - `VisitCalendar`, `DialogueIntake`, `SourceBoxPanel`, `ConfirmCard`, `DevVoiceBanner`, `KioskGate`: continuous corners.
  - New `EmptyState`.
- **Removed:** `GlassCard` (content on glass). The welcome dev card is a `Card`. Glass now lives only on the rail and the bottom bar.
- **Screens:**
  - Four question sites take `role="question"` (`intake.tsx` ×3, `InstrumentStep`). The question text is unchanged.
  - Bugün and Vizitlerim show `EmptyState` with their existing strings.
  - The welcome dev card changed from `GlassCard` to `Card`.
  - The root layout no longer loads fonts.
- **Not changed:**
  - Any i18n string or catalogue.
  - RED colours and RED-path files. The `e2e/motion.spec.ts` RED surface pin is unchanged and green.
  - Legal text rendering, motion tokens and routes.
  - `package.json`. No dependency was added or removed: the `@expo-google-fonts/*` packages stay because the e2e width specs read Inter from them.
- **Conformance report.** It is re-rendered from its YAML source, and 1.4.3 and 1.4.11 are re-assessed to Supports, code-verified, with DA-05 still to confirm on hardware. `test_a11y_report.py`'s two near-miss controls that rode on 1.4.3 being open are rebuilt for the closed state.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/design/system-refresh.md` is new.
  - `mobile-ui.md` gains a pointer marking its visual register as superseded.
  - `apple-native/spec.md` §1.1 retires the `GlassCard` row.
- **Tests updated** where they pinned the old design:
  - `visitCard.spec.ts`: card geometry, title face proxy, and the `COLUMN_RESIDUAL` list emptied (it may only shrink).
  - `appearance.spec.ts`: card measure, and the ListRow hook consumer.
  - `nativeChrome.spec.ts`: glass hosts.
  - `motion.spec.ts`: `STATIC_OPACITY` emptied, since the hairline's 0.9 is gone.
  - `fontMetrics.ts`: comment.

## Font proxy measurement (for the width specs)

The app now sets SF Pro, which is not redistributable, so the node width specs keep measuring in Inter, the OFL face they already read. Measured on the build Mac (macOS, `/System/Library/Fonts/SFNS.ttf` default instance, and CoreText via `NSFont.systemFont`):

- SF Pro Regular advances are 0.849 to 0.914 of Inter Regular (mean 0.877) across all 1066 Turkish catalogue strings at 17 pt.
- SF Pro Semibold at 44 pt, set by CoreText with kerning:

  | Word | SF Pro Semibold | Inter SemiBold upper bound |
  |---|---|---|
  | görüşmenizden | 306.3 pt | 332.2 pt |
  | alışkanlığınızda | 301.7 pt | 327.8 pt |
  | hatırlamıyorsanız | 337.9 pt | 367.6 pt |

  That is about 0.92 of Inter SemiBold.

These are measurements of the faces on one machine, not a property of the product. They justify using Inter as an upper bound: a word that fits in Inter fits in SF.
