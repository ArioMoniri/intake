# INT-432: clinical decisions register — guideline/openEHR-sourced, agent-authored, independently agent-verified

**Epic:** E03-protocols · **Phase:** P0 · **Sec-Row:** `clinical-content` · **GitHub:** #735 ·

## Scope, stated once

Owner decision **D18** (files/00_STATE.md §3), 2 Oct 2026, in chat: *"agent-only sign-off anyway"*.
Open clinical decisions are resolved by medical-professional agents from current guidelines and
openEHR CKM and verified by an independent agent. **The scope is simulated-patient stress
testing. A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.** D18 fills no
`clinical_signoff` field (D17 stands), promotes no pack past L0 and closes no HB-C1 row.

## Acceptance (verbatim from docs/backlog/issues.yaml)

- a clinical decisions register (docs/clinical/decisions/, with machine-readable
  packages/protocols/decisions/) resolves the open decisions from the INT-428/430 reviews,
  INT-414, INT-427, HB-C3 and HB-C6, and the #642 packets; each entry carries the decision, its
  source (guideline id, version, date, url, retrieved_on against a committed verbatim snapshot
  under packages/protocols/sources/, or an openEHR CKM archetype id and revision), the authoring
  agent role and the independent verifier's verdict
- no value without a source: anything unsupported stays draft_needs_source with the
  invariant-17 sanctioned literal and is reported, never invented; a typed loader refuses a
  quote that is not verbatim in its snapshot
- red-flag changes keep invariant 7, with golden tests and near-miss negative controls, and
  make safety passes
- the decision is recorded in files/00_STATE.md §3, scoped to simulated-patient stress testing,
  with the note that a licensed physician's review is required before any real patient

This PR is the **first batch**: HB-C6, the eight physician decisions of the INT-428/430
medical review, HB-C3, and the INT-427 asks. INT-414's 48 delivery lines and the #642 sign-off
packets follow in later PRs, so the PR says `Refs #735`.

## What stays draft_needs_source, and why

Every item below carries the sanctioned literal `TODO: <what> requires clinical sign-off` in a
`draft_needs_source` decision or entry. The loader lists them (`unsourced_paths`) and a test
pins that nothing else is unsourced. Nothing was defaulted.

- **CD-01** — proxy copy cannot be selected until the DSL can choose copy by subject role; until then
  a proxy session skips the chest-pain screen and no chest-pain RED can fire there (finding F13, an
  under-triage gap that blocks any real patient). Simulated proxy personas with chest pain are
  reported as unscreened, never negative.
- **CD-04** — whether *"bayılmadım ama bayılacak gibi oldum"* and *"kanama yoktu, sadece siyahtı"*
  upgrade from UNCLEAR to PRESENT_QUALIFIED: no source for presyncope or black stool as layperson
  triggers.
- **CD-05** — the final resolution of the 12–72 h band (NICE CG95 1.2.1.8 asks for same-day
  assessment, which no active rule offers; while draft it resolves to the reference day, labelled
  `while_draft_resolution`, over-triage as the fail-closed direction); the airway and crisis anchors
  (their rules are draft).
- **CD-06** — a staleness window for each of the seven document classes: no retrieved source states one.
- **CD-07** — the confirmation cap: no source, and it shares the budget HB-C5 has not reconciled.
- **CD-08** — the SLA, out-of-hours cover, escalation role and escalation window: no Turkish
  regulation covers a pre-visit inbox, and NG143/EK-4 time only red features and KIRMIZI Kategori 2.
- **CD-09** — the sensitivity floor, the non-inferiority margin, labeller count and adjudication,
  and the per-rule minimum sample and acceptance statistic: GMLP and PCCP leave them to the
  manufacturer's benefit-risk analysis. Until they exist no PHI-path lane swap can pass.

## Findings recorded, not applied (rule changes are for the signing clinician)

CD-01 records thirteen findings against the two active chest-pain rules; the verifier confirmed
them. None is applied — each is a rule change for the signing clinician, and invariant 7 allows
only adding or tightening, which is still a clinical act.

- **F1** EK-4 prints *"Kardiyak"* with a capital K; the rules quote it lower-case.
- **F2** the URL both rules cite (`20220913-6.htm`) returns 404; the Tebliğ is `20220913-5.htm`, EK-4
  the scanned annex `20220913-5-1.pdf`.
- **F3** NICE's nausea/vomiting is not in the K2 gate. **F4** nor is pain longer than fifteen minutes.
- **F5** pain spreading only to the neck or shoulder fires neither rule.
- **F6** burning pain routes to SARI (ESC 2023 §3.1.1 could not be retrieved to settle it).
- **F7** the anchor is named `onset_at`; NICE's windows run from the last episode (CD-01 defines it so).
- **F8** no arm sends a 12–72 h cardiac-feature episode to same-day assessment.
- **F9** with chest pain present, no positive feature and any feature UNCLEAR, neither rule fires
  (CD-04 routes it to the clinic inbox).
- **F10** chest pain after trauma with no K2 feature falls outside all three EK-4 chest rows.
- **F11** under the Tebliğ, triage is done by trained duty staff, so a pre-arrival EK-4 level is
  signposting, not triage under MADDE 8 — a regulatory-file point.
- **F12** EK-4 KIRMIZI Kategori 2 lists *"Herhangi bir nedenle olan ciddi ağrı"*; neither rule
  reads `hpi.severity_nrs`.
- **F13** no chest-pain RED can fire in a proxy session until the DSL selects proxy copy.

Two source problems the INT-427 author found in sources the proposals already cite:
the January 2019 Scottish referral guide is replaced by the 2025 Scottish Referral Guidelines
(rows gh-1, gh-2, gh-A1, hm-1, hm-4 need re-sourcing), and TARD's 2015 preoperative document is
replaced by the February 2025 adult and paediatric guidelines (the "1 ay" postponement figure is
no longer supported).

Pre-existing defect outside INT-432, offered as its own task: `identity_check.proxy_reported`
gates on `subject_role != 'kendim'`, a value no choice produces, so it is asked in self sessions.
