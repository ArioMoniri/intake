## What changed

### 1. The detent sheet closes at once for a RED or a privacy close

Reanimated reads a view's `exiting` in `componentDidMount` and `componentDidUpdate` (`AnimatedComponent.tsx`, `_configureLayoutAnimation`), never in `componentWillUnmount`. Its `updateLayoutAnimations` flushes on the first item, so the update is applied before the next commit. The open sheet registers the patient's spring. A RED hides the sheet in the same render that sets `closeReason="red"`, so unmounting in that commit played the spring under the RED.

- `lib/motion/sheet.ts` `sheetMount(drawn, { visible, close })` → `"open" | "parked" | "closed"`. An instant close of an open sheet is `parked` for one commit, and then `closed`. A spring close (the patient's own) unmounts at once.
- `DetentSheet.tsx` keeps what the last commit drew in state. A layout effect stores it, and that renders the parked sheet once more. In the parked commit the `Animated.View` is still mounted, styled `display: "none"`, with the backdrop gone and `pointerEvents="none"`. Its `exiting` is still `sheetExiting(sheetClose(closeReason, { reduce }), height)`. For a RED or privacy close that is `undefined`, so the update clears the registered spring. The next commit unmounts it, and there is nothing left to play.
- The doc claims are corrected: `DetentSheet.tsx` (header), `lib/motion/sheet.ts` (header) and `lib/motion/native/sheetMotion.ts`, which had said the sheet "is gone in the commit that closes it".
- `sheet.test.ts` models Reanimated's rule: an unmount plays the exit the commit before it registered. The pre-fix rule plays `spring` for a RED close; that test is kept as the defect's record. With `sheetMount`, every RED and privacy close, at both Reduce Motion settings, runs `open → parked → closed` and plays `instant`. The near miss is the patient's close: `open → closed`, playing `spring`.

### 2. Haptics hold under a RED

- `lib/red/hapticHold.ts` `holdHapticsUnderRed(store, gate)` subscribes to the RED store and follows the register the patient is shown:
  - ALERT and CALM: `gate.presentRed(register)`, released only on unmount;
  - SILENT: `gate.markSilentOrThirdParty()`, which nothing clears;
  - no RED, or DEFERRED_CLINICIAN: nothing.

  It reads `trigger.register`, never the screen model, so it cannot throw into the store's notify pass, which also notifies the RED layer. It runs synchronously and loads no engine.
- `app/_layout.tsx`: `useEffect(() => holdHapticsUnderRed(redStore, appHaptics), [])`.
- `hapticHold.test.ts` drives the real store, fed the recorded route catalog, and the real gate over a fake engine:
  - Under ALERT and CALM, at every location, a server-confirmed success is not felt. With no RED the same request is felt (the positive control).
  - SILENT is sticky after unmount.
  - DEFERRED_CLINICIAN holds nothing (the near miss).
  - A RED already standing at mount is held. A DEFERRED_CLINICIAN followed by an ALERT holds.
  - A spy gate pins the exact calls.

### 3. No microphone under a RED

The defect, traced in the code at `ed2bbd96`:

- the frozen last agent bubble stayed the `Pressable` that calls `session.bargeIn`, which moves the turn to listening, and the listening driver then opened the mic;
- the speaking driver checked only `stale`, and under a RED the turn never moves, so a clip still being made played after the RED and `armBargeInWatch` opened the mic;
- `leave()` stopped the recorder only in the listening phase, so the barge-in mic the speaking turn had opened kept recording under the RED.

What changed:

- `DialogueIntake.tsx`: `liveAgentIndex = !stopsDialogue && turn.phase === "speaking" ? … : -1`, so no frozen bubble is a barge-in control.
- `lib/dialogue/drivers.ts` (new; pure, no expo-audio import) holds the async steps:
  - `openMicrophone` reads `closed` before the session switch, the prepare and `record()`;
  - `speakTurn` reads `halted` (stale or closed) after the clip prepare and after the session switch, and dispatches nothing into a closed dialogue;
  - `watchMayArm` opens the mic only while the dialogue is open;
  - `closeDialogue` sets the flag first, then stops whatever mic is open (recording or listening).
- `useDialogueSession.ts` adapts the device to those ports:
  - The microphone port is the one `prepareToRecordAsync` and the one `record()`.
  - The speaking driver runs `speakTurn(…, halted)` with `halted = () => stale || closedRef.current`. The listener's `didJustFinish` reads `halted()`.
  - The listening driver returns on `closedRef.current`.
  - The barge-in tick stops on `halted()`.
  - `bargeIn` and `speakCorrection` dispatch nothing once closed.
  - `leave` is `closeDialogue({…})`.
- `drivers.test.ts` holds each await open, lands the RED there, then lets it go:
  - mid session switch and mid prepare: no `record()`;
  - mid-clip-prepare: no play, no watch and no `speech_unavailable`, even when the clip then fails;
  - mid session switch to play-and-record: no play;
  - mid-speaking, end to end: the clip plays, the RED lands while the watch opens the mic, `leave` finds nothing recording yet, and the mic never records;
  - `leave` mid-speaking with the barge-in mic open stops and discards it.

  The near misses: with no RED, a failing clip still raises `speech_unavailable`, a stale turn halts as it always did, and a barge-in during the open keeps its recording.

### 4. The orb follows ADR-033's yield

Chosen: **derive**, not amend. The reasons:

- ADR-033 §5's yield (`lib/motion/red.ts` `rootLayer: orb "unmount"`), `lib/motion/orb.ts`'s header, `docs/design/motion/intake-voice-motion-spec.md` §4.2 (`hidden` when "RED ALERT/CALM is showing"), RED-3 and RED-12 all say the orb is unmounted under a takeover. RED-12 says the orb is the barge-in visual and the emergency script has no barge-in. Amending would weaken five documents to match one line of code.
- Every existing RED spec stays green with no assertion changed:
  - `red.test.ts` already pins `yields.orb === "unmount"` for ALERT and CALM;
  - `orb.test.ts` pins `"unmount" → hidden`;
  - chatStage and orbVariants pin `stageOrbMotion(orbState, { reduce, redPresented: stopsDialogue })`, which is unchanged;
  - MOTION-4's pinned RED-surface list does not grow, because DialogueIntake reads the yield through `lib/red/store` (`redOrb`) and names nothing in the RED contract.
- `lib/motion/red.ts` `redYield(register)` is the table `redFirstPaint` reads (`redFirstPaint` now calls it). `lib/red/store.ts` `redOrb(state)` returns `redYield(register).orb` while a RED the patient sees stands, and null otherwise. DialogueIntake passes `red: redOrb(red)`. ALERT and CALM give `"unmount"` (the orb is hidden, 0 ms), SILENT gives `"idle"`, and DEFERRED_CLINICIAN and no RED give `null`.

## Mutation check

19 mutants were applied one at a time. Each file was restored from a copy, never with `git checkout`. 18 were killed:

- `drivers.ts`: the closed check after the prepare, after the clip prepare, after the session switch and in the catch; the recording branch of `closeDialogue`; the `closed` passed to the watch's open.
- `hapticHold.ts`: the SILENT branch, the subscribe-time read and the DEFERRED_CLINICIAN exclusion. The first and third survived the real-gate tests, because the gate treats SILENT and DEFERRED_CLINICIAN the same either way. The spy-gate test was added and kills them.
- `sheetMount`: never parking, and parking on a spring close.
- `redOrb` returning idle.
- The source wiring: `redOrb` in DialogueIntake, `!stopsDialogue` in `liveAgentIndex`, the listening driver's guard, `halted` without `closedRef`, the root layout's hold, and the parked style.

The one survivor is equivalent: it reverts `redFirstPaint` to read `YIELDS[mode]` directly, which is the refactor.

## Not done here (reported, not fixed: out of the issue's scope)

- Under a RED that lands mid-speaking, `session.playing` stays true and `turn.phase` stays `speaking`. The polite live announcement can therefore still say the agent is speaking or preparing once after the RED. The orb, the bubble and the controls no longer show it.
- The clip pre-warm loop does not read the closed flag. After a RED it may still synthesise agent clips into the cache that `leave` cleared. These are agent clips, not patient audio, so invariant 3 is unaffected.
