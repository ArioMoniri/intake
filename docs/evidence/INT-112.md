## 1 · What "crisis bypass to triage" can legitimately mean

The acceptance reads *"crisis lexicon hits route deterministically to C-SSRS path"*. The naive
implementation of that sentence — a lane that, on seeing a phrase, produces an escalation —
breaks **two** invariants at once:

- **invariant 11**: lanes *"write `ObserverState` only, never slots or triage"*;
- **invariant 1**: RED/YELLOW *"can only be produced by `packages/protocols/triage` rules"*.

They are not in conflict with the acceptance, because files/03 §4.7.5 says what the clause
actually means, and the wording is exact:

> `linguistic` (M2) … **crisis lexicon hits bypass the lane** and go straight to the
> deterministic triage detector → C-SSRS path / RED per rule, **regardless of wellbeing
> consent**

**"Bypass the lane" is the whole answer.** The hits do not travel through the lane's write
surface; they are not the lane's output at all. So what a lane may legitimately do here is:

**observe, and let something entitled to decide read the observation.**

Concretely, three separations, each of which is a shape rather than a rule:

1. **The observation is a presence fact, not a determination.** `CrisisTermPresence` carries
   `{session_id, section_id, seq, family_id, hits}` — a *family id and a count*. It has no
   field, property or open map that a level, a route or a register could occupy, and
   `triage_update`, `level`, `route`, `register` exist as setter-less properties over
   `ClassVar` `None`. That is INT-083's structural cap on LLM-derived material — the type
   *cannot express* the forbidden value, rather than a check refusing it — and INT-172's
   `CandidateRedFlag` shape, copied rather than reinvented.
2. **The decision is the rules engine's.** `CrisisSignpostRouter` is a `Protocol` the observers
   package declares and cannot satisfy: INT-110's AST gate forbids any observer module from
   importing `protocols` or `clinical_schemas.triage`, so there is no path from the detector to
   a level, not merely no call. The only admissible implementation hands the presence to
   `protocols.triage.evaluate_triage` as an **input** — files/03 §4.8's *"a lane signal is an
   input to the engine, never a computation of it"* — and returns whatever the engine says. The
   suite wires exactly that (`RulesEngineRouter`) and asserts that the RED, its `CALM` register
   and its location-resolved action all come back **from the engine**, with
   `result.source == "rules"`.
3. **The detector is not the lane.** `CrisisBypass` has no `spec` and no `observe`, so
   `LaneRuntime.admit` cannot take it (asserted: it raises `AttributeError`), it holds no
   `ObserverStore`, and a crisis hit never appears anywhere in `ObserverState` — asserted by
   dumping the whole state to JSON after a hit and searching for the family id.

This is the same move INT-093 made for the RED path: the rules engine decides, and the runtime
carries the decision. Nothing here can mint one.

## 2 · Is the acceptance as written satisfiable? No — and the reason is a blueprint defect

**Stated plainly: "route … to C-SSRS path" cannot be delivered, and should not be.** Four
independent reasons, three of which are open human blockers:

1. **There is no C-SSRS path to route to, and there cannot be one.** C-SSRS is licence-`BLOCKED`
   (`protocols.licences.INSTRUMENT_LICENCES`, HB-L2: Clario holds the exclusive RFMH electronic
   licence). Invariant 45's `instrument_licence_gate` means *a fixture pack referencing C-SSRS
   must fail the build*. `packages/protocols/base/intake.yaml` says so at the point where the
   slot would be: the `cssrs_screen` slot exists and is **unaskable**, and the instrument is
   deliberately unreferenced.
2. **The two crisis rules that would fire are `draft_needs_source`.** `rf.suicidal_intent` and
   `rf.suicidal_ideation_passive` both carry the invariant-17 sanctioned literal as their
   trigger, because files/04 §3 states both triggers *in C-SSRS terms* and those terms cannot be
   written at all. The compiler refuses them into a served pack; `evaluate_triage` refuses them
   again (`DraftRuleError`), and the suite asserts that too.
3. **The escalation policy does not exist (HB-C8).** files/04 §4.2 is unambiguous: *"Running
   C-SSRS in production before that policy exists is not defensible."* Four defects must close
   first, none optional. `protocols.compile.resolve._crisis_path_refusals` already gates on
   this: any `consent_independent` rule is refused into a served pack while HB-C8 is open.
4. **C-SSRS is the wrong destination for this signal even once it is licensed.** This is the
   part worth writing down, because it is a contradiction *inside* the blueprints and the
   backlog line preserves the wrong half. files/04 §4.2 defect 3 asks for *"a **consent-
   independent** crisis response — **not a screener, not a score**, but a deterministic
   phrase-triggered **signposting** response"*. C-SSRS is a screener, and it runs under
   wellbeing consent. files/03 §4.7.1 says the same thing from the regulatory side: the
   crisis-lexicon **signposting** response is defensible as non-device precisely because it is
   *"static, non-algorithmic notice, no urgency grading, no condition-specific inference,
   identical text every time"*, whereas *"bringing PHQ-2/GAD-2 forward is device-adjacent … so
   it sits under `device_functions`, not under the lane."* Routing a lexicon hit into an
   instrument cascade is the device-adjacent branch wearing the consent-independent branch's
   clothes.

**So what was built is the part that can be**: the detection, the presence fact, the port to the
entitled decider, and the proof that a real rules-engine evaluation satisfies that port. The
destination is INT-116's (`Wellbeing engine: … C-SSRS, crisis script`), which depends on this
issue and is itself blocked on HB-L2 and HB-C8. **The backlog line for INT-112 is recorded as a
defect against files/04 §4.2 defect 3 and files/03 §4.7.1** — see Finding 1.

## 3 · The lexicons — what a human must supply, and who

**No clinical vocabulary is authored anywhere in this change.** A list of Turkish words asserted
to mean hopelessness is a clinical judgement in exactly the way a threshold is: it decides what
counts as a signal, in which dialect, at which reading level and — for the crisis family — who
gets caught. HUMAN_BLOCKERS rule 1 forbids inventing one, and a plausible invented word list is
worse than an empty one because there is no diff that makes it look wrong.

`packages/protocols/lexicons/tr/linguistic.yaml` therefore ships **five families and zero
terms**, each `status: draft_needs_source` with a written `source_gap`, and `compile_lexicon`
refuses every one of them — the same shape `base.life_threat`'s unsigned rules take. Two new
blocker rows record the ask:

- **HB-C16** — the four *marker* families (`negative_affect`, `hopelessness`, `anhedonia`,
  `somatic_worry`). Owner: **physician of record, with a Turkish-speaking clinical
  psychologist.** The one sentence they must answer: *"Which Turkish surface forms belong in
  each of the four marker families, and against which committed Turkish source?"*
- **HB-C17** — the *crisis* family (`crisis_disclosure`). Owner: **physician of record plus
  counsel.** The one sentence: *"Which Turkish phrases must trigger a consent-independent
  signposting response, and what is that response?"* — which cannot be answered before HB-C8's
  escalation policy exists.

files/00 §2 is also relevant and is not worked around: **no Turkish vocal biomarker model
exists**, open or commercial, verified by exhaustive search, and D14 dropped Kintsugi DAM.
Nothing in this change implies otherwise: the lane runs no model at all. files/03 §4.7.5's
*"a small classifier later"* is left for later, with no stub.

**Fail-closed is asymmetric between the two halves, deliberately.**

- The **crisis** detector *refuses to be constructed* from an unsigned lexicon
  (`CrisisLexiconUnsigned`). A crisis detector that silently never fires is precisely the
  failure files/04 §4.2 defect 3 is about, so the absence is loud at wiring time. Absent, not
  idle — INT-110's phrase, one layer in.
- The **marker** half keeps running and simply emits **no lexicon key**. Not `0.0`: zero reads
  as *measured, none found*, and an unsigned family is a different fact. Prosody still lands,
  because it needs no vocabulary.

The suite proves the gate is a real comparison rather than a permanent refusal by injecting a
**synthetic signed** lexicon whose terms are nonsense (`mor kaplan`, `sarı tebeşir`) — exactly
as INT-110 opened the `acoustic` gate with a test-only ICF-bearing registry record. A suite that
needed the real terms to pass would have smuggled the blocked content in through the test.

## 4 · Consent — and why the bypass has none

files/04 §4.2 defect 3, verbatim: *"Consent-gating creates a hole exactly where it matters …
the people most likely to decline are the people most needing to be caught."*

So `CrisisBypass.__init__` takes `{session_id, lexicon, router}` and **no consent argument** —
asserted by signature introspection, and mutation I (adding one) is caught. The sharpest test is
the session-level one: a session that **refused** `lane:linguistic` admits no linguistic lane at
all (`runtime.lanes == frozenset()`, INT-110's absent-not-idle), and the bypass still scans and
still routes.

**INT-110's consent map is not widened.** `LANE_CONSENT_SCOPE` is still exactly
`{affect: lane:affect, linguistic: lane:linguistic}` — asserted. `asr_quality` and `engagement`
still raise `LaneConsentUndeclared` (HB-L22), and this issue neither needed nor took a purpose
for anything. The bypass needs none because it is not a lane: it is the duty-of-care signposting
path files/03 §4.7.1 qualifies as non-device.

## 5 · Off the speech critical path — the proof

**Structural.** `CrisisBypass.scan` is an ordinary function (asserted with `inspect`), like
INT-110's `LaneRuntime.offer` and INT-092's `WorkSubmitter.submit`. This matters more here than
for the other lanes: a crisis response *must* run on the transcript path — one that waited for a
section boundary would be useless — so the guarantee has to be that it costs the path nothing,
not that it sits elsewhere. 200 scans consume **0.0 virtual ms**, measured.

**Measured**, on `tests/voice/talker_harness.py`'s virtual clock — the *same* harness INT-092,
INT-093 and INT-110 use, so the number is comparable to theirs:

| configuration | turn latency (virtual ms) |
|---|---|
| no lane runtime, no bypass | **32.0** |
| the real `LinguisticLane` pumping + 8 bypass scans on the transcript path | **32.0** |
| awaited 30 s stall on the turn's own path (**positive control**) | **30 032.0** |
| 200 crisis scans | **0.0** |

The two 32.0 values agree and are `> 0` — the turn really spoke. The positive control awaits the
same lane runtime *on* the turn's path and costs the full stall, so the equality is a property
of where the work sits, not an artefact of the fake clock. **No test sleeps.**

INT-110's own fake-clock stall test is untouched and still passes.

## 6 · Invariants 3, 6, 13 and 22

**Invariant 3 — raw audio, and nothing derived, outlives the buffer.** The `linguistic` lane's
input is *not* audio: files/03 §4.7.5 gives it *"final transcripts + STT word timestamps"*, and
prosody is computed from the timestamps alone. `ProsodyStatistics.of` contains no reference to
frames — asserted by source inspection. The transcript segments still travel through INT-110's
ring, deliberately: a transcript is PHI, and the one buffer in this service already proven to
zero itself is the one it should live in. The suite holds a reference to the ring's own
`bytearray` across a `pump()` and asserts it is all zeroes afterwards. The lane's own
accumulators hold **integers only** — no text enters `ProsodyAggregate` or `_SectionState` —
asserted by sweeping `lane.__dict__`.

F0 mean/SD (files/03 §4.7.6) is deliberately **not** computed: it needs audio and a pitch
tracker, and a model that is not in files/03 §4.9.1 is not pinned. Mean response latency is the
`engagement` lane's, which cannot be built at all (HB-L22).

**No threshold exists in the prosody module, and a test enforces it.** The usual definition of a
"pause" is a silence longer than some number of milliseconds, and that number is exactly what
HUMAN_BLOCKERS rule 1 forbids an agent from choosing — picked once in a review, never revisited,
quietly deciding what counts as disfluent speech in a language whose word-final consonant
clusters are not English's. So every statistic is defined *without* one: `pause_ratio` is
non-speech time over span; `mean_inter_word_gap_ms` averages **every** gap, including
zero-length ones. An AST scan asserts `prosody.py` declares no numeric module constant at all.

Syllables are counted as vowel letters, which is **exact** for Turkish orthography rather than
an approximation of it — Turkish has no diphthongs and no syllabic consonants, so every syllable
carries exactly one vowel letter. That is a property of the writing system, not a clinical
judgement.

**Invariant 6 — nothing reaches a log, swept decoded.** The suite runs the lane, the bypass, a
malformed frame, a withdrawal and a close with `caplog` at DEBUG, then sweeps **every** record —
the rendered message and every attribute — for the synthetic Turkish sentence, both lexicon
terms, `göğsümde` and the session id, **twice**: once raw and once after
`.encode("ascii", "backslashreplace").decode("unicode_escape")`, because `json.dumps` with
`ensure_ascii=True` would hide an escaped Turkish sentence from a plain substring search.

Two design consequences of the same invariant: `CrisisTermPresence` carries **no matched text** —
the patient's own words about wanting to die are the most sensitive string the product will ever
hold, and a family id is all a rule needs; and `TranscriptFrameError` names the frame's *byte
length and the failure class*, never its bytes. Both are asserted.

`encode_segment` uses `ensure_ascii=False` deliberately, so the ring bytes literally *are* the
Turkish sentence: that makes the leak sweep catch a raw substring as well as an escaped one.
Hiding `göğsümde` behind `̈` would make the test weaker, not the product safer.

**Invariant 13 — no metric was added, and that is a decision.** `observability/metrics_catalog.yaml`
is unchanged. The linguistic lane inherits `lane.dropped_frames{lane}`, which INT-110 made live
and which already carries `lane="linguistic"`. A `crisis_signposts_total` counter was considered
and **rejected**: a counter that increments exactly when a patient discloses suicidal ideation is
re-identifying in a small tenant, which is the invariant-6 harm with a metric name on it.
`crisis.path_completion` stays `status: planned` — it belongs to INT-116, which owns path
completion end to end.

**Invariant 22 — `üre` is never substring-matched.** The lexicon matcher tokenises and compares
**token runs**, never substrings, so `üreme sağlığı` does not satisfy the term `üre` while
`üre nitrojeni` does (one whole-token match). The crisis side carries the same negative control:
`sarı tebeşirlik` contains the term and does not route. Case folding is Turkish, not ASCII —
`I → ı`, `İ → i` — because `str.casefold` merges `ışık` with `işik` and leaves a combining dot
behind; `IŞIK` matches `ışık` and `İŞİK` does not.

## 7 · INT-110's five locks — all still hold, none weakened

| lock | status after INT-112 |
|---|---|
| a lane is handed nothing it could write through | unchanged — `LaneContext`/`LaneWindow` field sets untouched, and INT-110 asserts them |
| a lane's answer has no field a slot or level could occupy | unchanged — `LinguisticLane` returns a plain `LaneObservation` |
| `markers` is `dict[str, float]` | held — every marker this lane emits is a `float`, asserted |
| `triage_update`/`slot_updates` are setter-less `ClassVar` constants | unchanged, and the same shape is reused for `CrisisTermPresence`'s four |
| the AST scan: no observer module imports the engine, talker, triage types or `protocols` | **re-run in this suite with a longer name list** (`TriageLevel`, `RedRegister`, `RedLocation`, `RulesTriageResult`, `evaluate_triage`, `EK4Level`, `RedFlagRule`) — all four new modules pass |

`LaneRuntime`, `ObserverStore`, `consent.py`, `study.py` and `contract.py` are **not modified**.
The only edit to an INT-110 file is `observers/__init__.py`'s export list and docstring.

A sixth lock is added and is the one that makes the bypass a bypass: **the lane's declared input
type has no `match_crisis`.** `MarkerLexicon` and `CrisisLexicon` are separate Protocols over the
same `CompiledLexicon`; the lane takes the first, the bypass the second. Under `mypy --strict`
the lane cannot read a crisis hit — it is not a rule about what the code chooses to read but a
type that cannot express the read. Mutation B (making the lane read both) is caught twice.

## Findings — what was refused rather than invented

**1. The backlog line for INT-112 names the wrong destination, and files/04 §4.2 says so.**
`crisis lexicon hits route deterministically to C-SSRS path` reproduces one half of files/03
§4.7.5's row (*"→ C-SSRS path / RED per rule"*) and drops the half that governs it — that the
hits *"bypass the lane"* and that the response runs *"regardless of wellbeing consent"*.
files/04 §4.2 defect 3 asks for signposting, **"not a screener, not a score"**; C-SSRS is a
screener under wellbeing consent, and files/03 §4.7.1 records that bringing an instrument
forward from an inferred state is device-adjacent and belongs under `device_functions`. The two
readings cannot both be right. **Recorded rather than reconciled by an agent**: which of the two
the product ships is a clinical and regulatory decision, and it is the same decision HB-C8 is
waiting on. The mechanism built here serves either — the router port is agnostic about what the
entitled decider does — so nothing has to be unpicked when the answer arrives.

**2. The lane framework's ingress is homogeneous; files/03 §4.7.5's lane inputs are not.**
`LaneRuntime.offer(frame)` fans one frame out to *every* admitted ring, but §4.7.5 gives the
five lanes four different inputs: audio frames (`affect`, `acoustic`), final transcripts + word
timestamps (`linguistic`), interim/final transcripts (`asr_quality`) and timing events
(`engagement`). Today that is latent, because nothing outside tests calls `offer` — the session
wiring does not exist on any branch. **This issue did not change `LaneRuntime`**: routing by
input kind would rewrite ~20 of INT-110's tests hours after they landed, for a producer that has
no caller. Instead the `linguistic` lane owns its own frame codec
(`observers/transcript.py`), which keeps the transcript inside the one buffer already proven to
zeroize. **The fix belongs to whoever wires `LaneRuntime` into the session** — declare the input
kind on `LaneSpec` and route `offer` by it — and is recorded here so it is not discovered by a
lane receiving the wrong bytes.

**3. `files/03 §4.7.6`'s clinician block cannot be rendered, and this is a new human ask.**
The "Konuşma özellikleri" block requires each measure to carry *"a population reference range
and a recording-condition caveat"*. No Turkish reference range for speech rate, pause ratio or
articulation rate is committed anywhere in this repository, and files/00 §2's ledger records no
Turkish norm study. So the statistics are **computed** by this issue and are **not renderable to
a clinician** by it: printing a number next to no reference range invites the reader to supply
their own, which is the accuracy-claim failure mode invariant 20 exists for. Recorded as
**HB-C18**; the note composer (INT-084) does not read `linguistic_markers` today, and must not
start to before it closes.

**4. Pronoun and negation ratios (files/03 §4.7.5) are not implemented, and a lexical-only
version would be wrong.** Turkish negation is primarily **suffixal** (`-mA-`), not lexical, so a
matcher over the free negators (`değil`, `yok`, `hiç`) would systematically under-count exactly
the utterances the ratio is meant to detect — and it would under-count them *differently* by
register, which is worse than not measuring. Doing it properly needs a Turkish morphological
analyser, and files/03 §4.9.1 pins none: a model that is not in that table is not pinned, so
there is nothing to use and choosing one would be minting a pin. Deferred, with the reason,
rather than shipped in a form whose failure mode is invisible.

**5. `confidence` is `1.0`, and that is a claim about the measurement, not the lexicon.**
files/03 §4.8's gate reads `confidence ≥ τ AND dropped_frames_in_window ≤ D`. This lane runs no
model: a whole-token count and arithmetic over integers are exact, so there is no estimate to
put a confidence on, and any value below 1.0 would be an invented number. It is emphatically
*not* a statement that the lexicon is right — the lexicon is empty and unsigned. When files/03
§4.7.5's "small classifier later" lands, it brings its own confidence and this constant goes
with it. Written down because a reader could otherwise take `confidence: 1.0` on a psychiatric
signal as a claim, which is exactly what invariant 20 forbids.

**6. No metric was added.** See §6 above: a `crisis_signposts_total` counter is re-identifying
in a small tenant. `lane.dropped_frames{lane="linguistic"}` already covers the lane's
backpressure, and `crisis.path_completion` stays `planned` for INT-116.
