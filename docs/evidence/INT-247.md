# Evidence — INT-247 · Carried-forward facts are unconfirmed until this visit

## Where this sits in the backlog

There is no INT-247 row. The owning issue is **INT-170** (E11-learning, P0,
status `todo`):

> "PatientOverlay: store, confirmation rules, /me/learned API, engine integration"
> acceptance: "facts commit only with patient/clinician confirmation (type-level);
> 12-month reconfirm; provenance shown; delta-mode works in simulator"

This change delivers the **client half** of that acceptance — type-level confirmation
and provenance on the surface where the harm actually lands, the note. The store, the
`/me/learned` API, the 12-month reconfirm clock and delta mode stay with INT-170; the
backlog is untouched, so INT-170 stays open and correctly describes what is left.

The owner's report, verbatim: *"it says patient uses metoprolol from previous ones but
these should be updateble mayne they stopped using and also it is hardocded"*.

## Defect 1 — the hardcoded medication

`apps/mobile/src/lib/dev/accounts.ts` seeds Ayşe with `"Metoprolol (düzenli kullanım)"`.
That is legitimate synthetic fixture data, and it is already contained:

- the module is imported for its **values** by exactly one file,
  `apps/mobile/src/app/(auth)/welcome.tsx`, behind `__DEV__`, so release builds drop it;
- `signInOtp` — the real INT-131 account path — constructs its account literally, with
  no fixture read at all.

So the seed **cannot** reach a real account today. It was unpinned, which is the actual
defect: nothing would have failed if a later edit had wired a fixture into the OTP path.
Four tests in `apps/mobile/src/lib/dev/accounts.test.ts` now pin it — the single
value-importer (found by scanning the source tree, type-only imports excluded), the
`__DEV__` guard being reached before any fixture use, `carriedFacts: []` in `signInOtp`,
and every seeded fact pointing at a visit the fixture actually has.

## Defect 2 — a carried fact presented as current

`buildNote` printed `account.medications` — a bare `string[]` — into a section titled
*"İlaçlar — hasta beyanı"*. A clinician reads that as a current medication. Nothing on
any screen let the patient say they had stopped.

### Invariant 12 in the type, not the UI

`apps/mobile/src/lib/intake/carriedFacts.ts`:

- `CarriedFact` has no field that can be read as "current". Holding one tells you what
  was written down before, by whom, on what day — and nothing about today.
- `ReconciledFact` **requires** a `FactDecision`. There is no value of that type
  describing a fact nobody decided on, so no note line can be built from one.
- `FactReconciliation.unconfirmed` is `readonly CarriedFact[]` — a *different type* from
  the buckets the current-medications renderer reads.
- `reconcileCarriedFacts` is the only producer of `FactReconciliation`, and it is total:
  every carried fact lands in exactly one bucket, so nothing is silently dropped either.

`NoteAccount` lost `medications: string[]` and gained `carriedFacts: readonly
CarriedFact[]`, so the old shape is not reachable from the note builder at all.

### "Stopped" is not "absent"

Four line forms in the one `medications` section:

| bucket | note line |
|---|---|
| current | `Metoprolol (düzenli kullanım) — hasta bu ziyarette devam ettiğini söyledi — önceki kayıt: 12 Mayıs 2026` |
| conflict | `… — hasta değiştiğini söyledi; yeni bilgi alınmadı — önceki kayıt: …` |
| stopped | `… — hasta bıraktığını söyledi — önceki kayıt: 12 Mayıs 2026` |
| unconfirmed | `… — bu ziyarette onaylanmadı — önceki kayıt: 12 Mayıs 2026` |

An **absent** medication produces no section at all. A **stopped** one produces a line
that says it was recorded before and that the patient reports stopping it. Those are
different notes, which is the whole point of files/04 §2 `meds_allergies` ("confirm
imported list; new/stopped meds").

One section, not four, because the section id is also the share-scope unit the patient
toggles (02 §5.1) and the server's `SectionId` literal
(`services/api/src/intake_api/schemas.py`).

### Borrowed vocabulary, no invented semantics

| concept | source |
|---|---|
| provenance ("where from") | `clinical_schemas.overlay.LearnedFactSource` — the same three confirmed sources, no unconfirmed member (files/09 §3) |
| who asserted it | INT-133 `Attribution` (`apps/mobile/src/lib/profile/attribution.ts`) — the review screen passes the session's derived attribution, not a parallel notion |
| the outcome | files/04 §5 `medications.reconciliation`: `matches \| new \| stopped \| conflict` |

`new` is not produced here — it belongs to a medication the patient *adds*, not to a
carried one.

## What was NOT decided here — for the pack owners

1. **`changed` → `conflict`.** A patient saying a medication "changed" is mapped to
   files/04 §5 `reconciliation: conflict`, on the reading that the recorded value and
   the patient's report disagree and the clinician resolves it. files/04 §5 lists the
   value but does not define it. If the pack owners mean something narrower by
   `conflict` (e.g. two *records* disagreeing), the mapping is one line in
   `RECONCILIATION_OF`.
2. **No replacement value is captured.** The brief asked for confirm / correct /
   stopped. A medication string carries a dose, and free numeric entry on the clinical
   critical path is barred by invariant 22, so "correct" records the disagreement and
   captures no new value. The patient's own words already have a route — the pack's
   `extra` ("Hasta notu") question. A structured dose-and-form editor is a protocol
   question, not a UI one.
3. **What stopping a drug means.** Nothing infers anything from a retraction: no
   triage input, no red-flag interaction, no adherence conclusion. The note records who
   said what, and when it was first written down.
4. **Whether an unconfirmed carried fact should appear in the note at all.** It does,
   marked — on the precedent of files/04 §5 `pmh.status: confirmed|unconfirmed`, and
   because dropping it would trade a visible error for a silent one. Reversible in
   `medicationLines` if the pack owners disagree.
