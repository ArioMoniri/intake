## 1. What was false, and what is true now

| # | finding | now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | After a share-consent withdrawal the server revokes the delivered note's share and links (consents.py). The note could not be shared again, and `shares.issue.error.notShared` ("Bu not henüz paylaşılmadı. Önce notu gönderin.") was false: the note was sent. | A delivered note that lost its share to a later withdrawal (`needsReshare`) shows a re-share card on the review screen, with the served share text. A fresh grant **for that note** re-sends the same frozen body (`reshareDelivery`), and the server recreates the share in that PUT. The line is now "Bu not şu anda paylaşılmıyor, bu yüzden bağlantı oluşturulamadı.", which is true whatever took the share away. |
| 2 | Sign-out (and the kiosk wipe) shreds a queued, unsent note silently. | Çıkış yapın first checks the outbox (`unsentItems(useDeliveries())`). With an unsent note it warns: "Henüz gönderilmemiş notunuz var. Çıkış yaparsanız not bu telefondan silinir ve hekiminize gitmez." It then offers three choices: **Notu açın** (to send or retry it), **Oturumda kalın** (keep it), and **Yine de çıkış yapın**. The kiosk idle wipe is out of scope: its warning is INT-363's. |
| 3 | The server share expired 72 h after the send (`store.create_share`). review.consent.untilValue promised three days after the appointment, and issuing a Doctor Link needs a live share. So a note sent more than 72 h before the appointment could not be linked at the visit. files/02 §2b names this defect for the link: "A note prepared five days early currently expires before the clinician opens it." | New `services/api/src/intake_api/share_lifetime.py`: `share_expires_at(schedule, sent_at)`. A dated visit's share lives until `visit_instant + 72 h`, the Doctor Link's own anchor (the appointment's hour where known, else the start of the visit date in Türkiye). It never lives less than 72 h from the send, so a late send is not born dead. An undated visit keeps `send + 72 h`. A reschedule re-pins every live share by the same rule from its own send (`repin_shares`, audited as `api.note.share_expiry_repinned`). A revoked or expired share is never revived. The copy says the same (see §2). |
| 4 | `review.consent.recipient` ("Kime gidiyor") printed the visit's hospital and department. The hospital receives nothing: no reader is registered for it. | The summary names who can open the note, as share.tr.md says: whoever is shown the link and the opening code. `shareSummary` carries `opener: "link_holder"` and no hospital field at all. |
| 5 | (re-check) A withdrawal the server refused was reported as done: `dropRefusedHead` dropped it and `withdrawShareLocal` returned true. | `withdrawShareLocal` returns `landed`, `unsent` or `refused`. Only `landed` says done. A refused share withdrawal sets the standing back to `unknown`, so the control is offered again. It keeps `withdrawnAt`, so a note granted before that instant stays parked. No server path refuses a well-formed patient withdrawal today; this is the truthful answer if one ever does. |
| 6 | (re-check) `engine.ts` compared the grant and the withdrawal as ISO strings. | They are compared with `Date.parse`. An unreadable instant fails closed: the note waits. |

**Notes for the reviewer.**
- "üç gün" is the 72 h rule as words, so it matches the existing copy and has no numeral. 02 §2
  forbids a computed instant in this copy. "en geç" covers the undated-hour fallback, which
  closes earlier: at the start of the day + 72 h.
- The re-share card never shows `delivery.consent.body` ("…yalnızca bu telefonda kalır"). That
  line is false for a delivered note.
- The legal text itself is the served share.tr.md, rendered by the same `ShareText` block as the
  first send (invariant 18).

## 3. Data and retention

- No new field, table or category.
- The `shares.expires_at` inventory annotation is updated to describe the rule: "72 h after the
  visit, never under 72 h from the send (02 §2b, 06 §3, INT-589)".
- The retention class `doctor_link_tokens` (expiry, 72 h) is unchanged. The share now closes on
  the window share.tr.md already states for the link.
- **For counsel:** the window now runs from the appointment and no longer from the send. That is
  the rule 02 §2b and share.tr.md state. A note sent days early stays shareable until three days
  after the visit, where it used to close three days after the send.
