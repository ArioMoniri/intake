# INT-406 — RED acknowledgement: clinic Safety inbox within 60 s, acknowledgement clock and SLA paging (invariant 49)

**Epic:** E09-clinician · **Phase:** P0 · **Sec-Row:** triage · **GitHub:** #681 ·
**Labels:** area:clinician, risk:clinical · **Depends on:** INT-025, INT-080, INT-092,
INT-093, INT-180 (all merged)

## Acceptance (verbatim, GitHub #681)
- "every RED (rules-engine RED only, invariant 1) produces a clinic inbox entry within 60 s, **whatever connector is present**; the acknowledgement clock starts at the entry; `tenant_clinical_responsible` acknowledges (audited, ids only)"
- "unacknowledged past the SLA pages the on-call route; the SLA value comes from 02 §8 or is owner-set (never invented; fail closed to paging immediately if unset)"
- "a 02 §2b reschedule or cancel on a RED visit raises an entry, regardless of connector presence"
- "`clinic.red_ack_latency_min` and `clinic.red_unacknowledged_total` are emitted, catalogued and alerted (runbook linked)"
- "invariant 49 is enforced by a simulator/runtime test with a fake clock (entry ≤ 60 s; page at SLA + ε; a transition on a RED visit raises an entry), with near-miss controls"

## Problem
files/10 §9's RED acknowledgement row: *"the alert fires and **nobody answers** — no
queue, no clock, no metric, no alert"*. On `main` the RED path's clinician route
(`ClinicNotifier`, INT-093) had no implementation at all; the composition root binds no
RED preemption path (its scripts are HB-C1), so a rules-engine RED reached nobody at the
clinic. Both files/08 §4 E metrics were catalogued `planned`; no alert rule, runbook,
console surface or test carried invariant 49 (INT-020's close-out found it unowned).

## Where the SLA comes from
files/02 §8.2, verbatim: *"RED → clinic inbox ≤ 60 s. RED → human acknowledgement within
a tenant SLA, **default 4 business hours**, with a documented out-of-hours path"*. So the
SLA is **not** an owner value that is missing — 02 gives a default — and the issue stays
`done`, not `awaiting-human`. What 02 does not give is the *business calendar* (which
hours, days, holidays, zone). No calendar was written: the 4 hours are counted as
**wall-clock hours**, which can only page at or before the business-hours reading, never
after (the fail-closed direction). A tenant SLA is `AckSlaPolicy.per_tenant`, shipped
empty; a non-positive value is refused, so no tenant can be left with no SLA.

## Change
- **`intake_platform.safety_inbox`** (new package, shared by the runtime and the API the
  way INT-182's voice control is):
  - `model.py` — `InboxEntry`: tenant, session, visit, kind (`red` · `visit_rescheduled` ·
    `visit_cancelled`), rule ids, source ref, `created_at`, acknowledgement (who/when).
    Ids only (`RuleFire.evidence` has no field); every id shape-checked, never echoed;
    `entry_id` derived from tenant + kind + source, so a replayed RED (same
    content-addressed `work_id`) lands on its first entry and first clock.
  - `sla.py` — `INBOX_ENTRY_BUDGET` (60 s) and `DEFAULT_ACK_SLA` (4 h) with 02's sentence;
    `AckSlaPolicy`; `AgeBand` (`within_sla` / `past_sla`) — past is strictly after the
    deadline.
  - `store.py` — `RedisSafetyInboxStore` (`INTAKE_SAFETY_INBOX_REDIS_URL`): entry record +
    `open`, per-tenant and per-visit (RED only) indexes, written in one `WATCH`/`MULTI`;
    first write wins; acknowledgement is compare-and-set, once. Every read fails closed
    (`SafetyInboxUnavailable`: unreachable / malformed / a record under another id) —
    never a skipped row.
  - `outbox.py` — `SafetyInboxOutbox`: the RED path's `put` is an append under a lock (the
    `ClinicNotifier` port has no awaitable member and must not delay the stop); `pump`
    writes on the put and retries every `PUMP_INTERVAL` (1 s) while the store refuses,
    keeping order; `clinic_red_inbox_pending_age_s` is computed at scrape time.
  - `service.py` — `SafetyInbox.visit_transition` (a visit carries a RED when the inbox
    holds a RED entry for it, acknowledged or not — nothing records a RED as *resolved*),
    `acknowledge` (observes `clinic_red_ack_latency_min`), `sweep` (recomputes
    `clinic_red_unacknowledged_total{tenant,age_band}`, returns breaches; an unreadable
    inbox sets `tenant="inbox_unreadable"` to +Inf, which pages), `queue` (unanswered
    first), and `sweep_forever` every `SWEEP_INTERVAL` (60 s), surviving a faulting sweep.
  - `metrics.py` — the two 08 §4 E families plus `clinic_red_inbox_pending_age_s` and
    `clinic_red_inbox_route_failures_total`.
- **Voice runtime** (no triage rule, level or RED semantic touched; `red_path.py`
  unchanged):
  - `clinical_agent/work_queue.py` — `BackendAgent.clinic`: after `authorise_preempt`
    (so only a pinned rules-engine RED, invariant 1) and before the result settles,
    Layer B hands the RED to the clinician route. A session that ends on the answer that
    fired the RED therefore still reaches the clinic, and the clinic no longer waits on the
    HB-C1 scripts. A route that raises is counted and logged by work id; the RED settles
    and seals regardless.
  - `safety_inbox.py` — `SafetyInboxNotifier`, the production `ClinicNotifier`: one entry
    into the process outbox; ids checked at binding, not at the RED.
  - `composition.py` — `clinic` is a **required** keyword of both `build_residency_tr_session`
    and `build_typed_session` (parity), bound to the `BackendAgent`.
  - `app.py` — `/readyz` gains `safety_inbox` on **both** profiles (a typed RED is a RED);
    the process owns one outbox and, with a store wired, runs its pump for the app's life
    and gives it a last pass on the way down.
- **API** — `GET /admin/safety-queue` (TCR, `clinical_lead`, `platform_owner`, `auditor`;
  tenant from the claim) and `POST /admin/safety-queue/{entry_id}/acknowledge`
  (`tenant_clinical_responsible` only, own tenant, once; no body — a field is 422, not
  ignored). Registered as `admin.safety_queue.acknowledge`: **T1, SAFE** — it adds no
  capability, and a second key on the answer to a safety alert is the availability
  control §0c F17 forbids. Audited by the route wrapper *and* a write-ahead chain row
  `safety_inbox.acknowledge` naming `safety_inbox:<tenant>:<entry_id>`. Unwired or
  unreachable → 503, never an empty queue. The API starts the sweep at boot; with no inbox
  wired it reports the inbox unreadable, which pages.
- **Observability** — catalogue: both 08 §4 E ids `live`, plus the two new families;
  `infra/observability/safety-rules.yaml` (`RedAcknowledgementOverdue`,
  `RedInboxEntryLate` > 60 s, `RedInboxRouteFailed`, all `severity: page`, no `for:`),
  loaded by `prometheus.yml` and mounted in `docker-compose.obs.yml`; runbook
  `docs/runbooks/red-acknowledgement.md` (anchors pinned by the test).
- **Backlog** — the INT-406 row (E09-clinician, P0, done); the three pinned counts
  309 → 310 (main plus this row) with the reason; `files/11` regenerated. Threat model `triage` section names
  INT-406 as the owner of inv. 49.

## Owner steps (not decided here)
1. **Paging delivery.** The rules carry `severity: page`; no Alertmanager or receiver ships in
   this repository. A deployment routes `severity: page` to its on-call pager.
2. **Business-hours calendar and out-of-hours path per tenant** (files/02 §8.2). Until
   recorded, the SLA counts wall-clock hours (pages early, never late).
3. **Tenant SLA overrides** — `AckSlaPolicy.per_tenant` ships empty.
4. **Deploy wiring** — `INTAKE_SAFETY_INBOX_REDIS_URL`, the same Redis in the API and the

   and are outside the DSAR executor; how long an answered entry is kept and how a patient
   erasure reaches it is the data owner's question.

## Findings for the coordinator
- **The files/02 §2b visit lifecycle has no producer and no backlog row.** Nothing in the
  tree moves a visit to `rescheduled` or `cancelled` (visits are INT-197 session rows with
  list/create/read only; the INT-318 sync is read-only). `SafetyInbox.visit_transition` is
  the one door such a producer must call; it is proven here, but no surface calls it yet.
- **The voice agent worker that composes sessions does not exist** (the session router is
  "handed builders by the worker"). It must bind each session's `SafetyInboxNotifier` to
  `VoiceRuntimeWiring.outbox`; the composition roots now refuse a session without one.
- **PR #680 (INT-020)** rewrites the same `triage` threat-model bullets and adds "unowned";
  whichever merges second replaces "unowned" with INT-406.
