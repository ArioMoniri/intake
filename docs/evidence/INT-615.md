## 3. What stays draft, and why

No value was filled that a snapshot does not state, and nothing was relaxed (invariant 7).

1. **Level.** The rule is `KIRMIZI_K1`. EK-4 prints "Havayolu tıkanıklığı riski" in Kategori 1, but it prints the two named features ("Şiddetli stridor veya yutkunma güçlüğü ile beraber olan havayolu tıkanıklığı riski") in **Kategori 2**. Lowering the rule to K2 would relax it. Keeping K1 is a reading, not a quote. Physician decides.
2. **Age band** (`subject.age_years < 18`). Neither RCH nor EK-4 states an upper bound. RCH's only age content is croup "rare <3 months". WHO IMCI (2 months up to 5 years) would narrow the rule.
3. **Stridor qualifier.** RCH puts "Intermittent stridor" under *mild* obstruction, and EK-4 says *severe*. The gate fires on any stridor, the more sensitive reading, and LT-03-N guards the croup near miss.
4. **Crisis rule.** No guideline supplies a Turkish crisis lexicon. It must be clinician-authored (HB-C17), and the §4.2 escalation policy does not exist (HB-C8).

The compiler also keeps the airway rule out regardless, because of `ACTION_OPEN_QUESTION` (HB-C9) and `PAEDIATRIC_SIGNOFF_MISSING` (HB-C1).
