## E. Verdict

**CHANGES REQUESTED.** The three capabilities do what the tests say. Labels are base's own bytes, every candidate is byte-identical, and each refusal is pinned by a test that goes red on mutation. But `duration=omit` ships a chain `suppress_slot`, and it is justified only by the `must_ask` check. The compiler refuses a slot suppression that silences a red flag for overlays alone (H-1), and the generator neither refuses it nor records it. Required before merge: H-1's in-PR guard, or at minimum an INT-379.md and PR-body statement that omit must not be used under a cluster, with the follow-up compiler issue opened; M-1's and M-4's evidence corrections. M-2 and M-3 are small generator refusals that keep regeneration byte-identical. L-1 to L-4 and the notes may go to a follow-up. **Whether and where to use omit, `code` and `skip_if` stays with the clinician (HB-C1, HB-C3, HB-C5); CODEOWNER review of the widened review-candidate surface is still required.**
