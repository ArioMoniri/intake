# INT-433: question screen redesign, native progress, filled inputs, capsule chips, Liquid Glass bars (Mobbin-referenced)

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P1 · **Sec-Row:** `mobile` · **GitHub:** #739 ·
**Depends on:** INT-422, INT-423 · **Branch:** `int-433-question-screen` · **Date:** 2026-10-03

## The ask

The owner reviewed the store shot of the question screen on 2 Oct 2026. He found the outline, the question-count circles and the Liquid Glass and native Apple feel not good enough, and asked for Mobbin to be the reference. The acceptance is in `docs/backlog/issues.yaml` INT-433, and the rationale and references are in `docs/design/question-screen.md`.

## Why INT-422 and INT-423 are merged in

The redesign uses INT-422's Title 2 `question` role, its Footnote `caption`, the `separator` token and the Increase Contrast palette. It also uses INT-423's materials-on-chrome rule. Neither PR (#725, #728) is on `main` yet, so `origin/int-423-chat-rhythm`, which contains INT-422, is merged into this branch (commit `d4243254`). That part of the diff drops out once they land, and this PR should merge after them.

## What changed

- **`apps/mobile/src/lib/intake/questionScreen.ts`** (new, pure):
  - `questionPosition` gives the place and total over the applicable flow, with the growth flag.
  - `positionLabel` and `positionSpoken` give the display line "Soru 3 / 9" and the reader's value "Soru 3, toplam 9". Integers are never grouped.
  - `filledFieldChrome` gives the fill, the focus ring, and the Increase Contrast boundary at one constant width.
  - `chipChrome` gives the filled capsule, and for the selected chip the tint, the label colour and the check.
- **`apps/mobile/src/components/QuestionProgress.tsx`** (new): a thin capsule bar and the "Soru N / M" line, as one `progressbar` with a value. The growth line sits outside it.
- **`apps/mobile/src/components/GlassBar.tsx`**: `GlassNavBackground`, the nav bar's material. It uses the same policy as the bottom bar: liquid, blur, or solid under Reduce Transparency.
- **`intake.tsx`** (typed steps):
  - the dot row is gone;
  - the glass nav bar is used for the typed steps, but stays solid when a warning banner is shown;
  - filled fields have a focus ring;
  - chips are filled capsules with a check;
  - hints are Footnote secondary;
  - the ± buttons are filled.
- **`VoiceNote.tsx`**: the dev voice-note button is a tinted capsule rather than an outline.
- **i18n:**
  - `intake.progress.answered` is retired;
  - `intake.progress.name`, `.question` and `.of` are added, words only, in tr first with the en mirror.
- **Tests updated to the new rule:**
  - `a11y.spec.ts` (BK8: progressbar, not dots);
  - `overflow.spec.ts` §7 (the stacked line fits 375 pt at the largest step);
  - `typed_intake.spec.ts` (the denominator is the applicable flow and the growth line travels with it; the chip bound includes the check);
  - `transients.test.ts` (the progress sits outside the keyed step body);
  - `appearance.spec.ts` (intake no longer consumes `useScheme`);
  - `voiceFirst.test.ts` (key list).
- **Backlog:**
  - the INT-433 row is added;
  - the three pins move from 328 to 329;
  - `files/11` is regenerated.
- **Captures:** `docs/design/question-screen/` holds the before capture and three after captures (light, typing, dark).

### First full run after implementing

Four guards caught real consequences, and each was resolved by changing the code or by restating the test's own rule:
