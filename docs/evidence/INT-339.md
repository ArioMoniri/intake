# INT-339 — Motion: typed lane, fallback and completion

**Epic:** E08-mobile · **Phase:** P2 · **Sec-Row:** intake-typed · **GitHub:** #538 · **Depends on:** INT-330, INT-335 (both merged)
**Spec:** `docs/design/motion/motion-verification.md` §4 MOTION-10 (C14–C16 APPROVE, P13/C14
revisions), and `intake-voice-motion-spec.md` C14, C15, C16, C9 and §3.4 ("never chain app logic
to an animation callback"). MOTION-10 depends on MOTION-1 (INT-330) and MOTION-6 (INT-335) only.
It does not depend on MOTION-3, so nothing here waits on ADR-033 (PR #571, still Proposed).

## Acceptance (verbatim)
- "the typed fallback is enter-only within the fade token and visible inside INT-135's 2 s budget at every setting; a step change never shows two questions in one frame"
- "no navigation waits on an animation; the review screen renders every section in one commit with no stagger"

## Problem
- The typed steps had no motion of their own. On a voice failure, a hand-over or
  "Yazarak devam edin", the dialogue unmounted and `StepFlow` appeared in the same commit, and
  a step change swapped the question in place. That is legal (instant is always legal), but C15
  and C16 approve one enter-only 120 ms fade for each, and nothing held the two properties that
  make it safe: that there is never an exit, and that a question never shares a frame with the
  next one.
- Nothing guarded completion. Every review navigation was a plain call in a synchronous handler,
  but no test said so, and nothing stopped a later change from putting it behind a completion
  callback, a timer, a frame or `runAfterInteractions`.
- Nothing guarded the review screen's one-commit render against a stagger, a delay or a
  virtualised list.

## Change
- New pure `apps/mobile/src/lib/motion/transients.ts` (no React Native or Reanimated import):
  - `typedFallback({ reduce })` returns `{ enter, exitMs }`, where `enter` is `enterFor` from
    `fade.ts` (opacity, `motion.duration.fade`, delay 0, `easing.out`, or `undefined` under
    reduce-motion) and `exitMs` is the literal type `0` (`motion.duration.instant`).
  - `typedStep(questionId, { reduce })` returns the same shape, plus `key: questionId`.
  - No new token and no number: every value comes from `theme.ts` through `fade.ts`.
- New `apps/mobile/src/lib/motion/native/reanimated.ts`: `enteringFor(spec)` turns a `FadeSpec`
  into `FadeIn.duration(…).delay(…).easing(Easing.bezier(…))`, or `undefined`. This is the builder
  INT-335's `fade.ts` said would land "with its first consumer in `src/lib/motion/native/`". It has
  no exit builder. Only the screen imports it; no pure module or test does.
- `apps/mobile/src/app/(tabs)/visits/[visitId]/intake.tsx`:
  - **Fallback (C15).** The lane switch sits inside `<LayoutAnimationConfig skipEntering>`, and
    `StepFlow` sits inside `<Animated.View entering={enteringFor(fallback.enter)} style={styles.lane}>`.
    The lane that is on screen first (an edit, a typed-first patient) arrives with the route's own
    fade, so its entrance is skipped. A lane that replaces another fades in over 120 ms, enter-only,
    and the dialogue or gate it replaces is gone in the same commit. `styles.lane` is `{ flex: 1 }`,
    the same box `StepFlow` had inside `styles.body`, so the floating glass bar anchors to the
    same edge.
  - **Step change (C16).** The question's body (the voice-failed notice, the heading or instrument
    item, the hint, the provenance quote, the answer field, chips, cards, stepper and text box) is
    one `<Animated.View key={step.key} entering={enteringFor(step.enter)} style={styles.step}>`,
    inside its own `<LayoutAnimationConfig skipEntering>`. A new question is a new key, so React
    removes the old body in the commit that mounts the next one, and only the next one fades.
    The same question keeps its key, so toggling a chip re-renders without re-running the
    entrance. The first step arrives with its container instead of fading a second time inside it.
    The "Bitirdim" chip, the deferral notice, the progress dots and counter, the "grows" caption
    and the glass-bar CTA stay outside the body and swap instantly with the step (C9, C16).
    `styles.step` is `{ gap: spacing.md }`, the rhythm the scroll content already had.
  - Both components read `useReducedMotion()` from `@/lib/a11y/prefs`, which is INT-331's
    fail-closed store. Reanimated also follows the root `ReducedMotionConfig`.
- Completion (C14) and review: **no product change was needed.** Every review navigation was
  already a synchronous call in the tick that finishes, and the review already rendered every
  section in one `ScrollView` map. Both properties are now held by tests.

## Tests (TDD) — `apps/mobile/src/lib/motion/transients.test.ts` (new)
The five MOTION-10 DoD titles, verbatim:
- **"fallback is enter-only and ≤ fadeMs".** The plan is `{ enter: fade, exitMs: 0 }`, and under
  reduce-motion it is `{ enter: undefined, exitMs: 0 }`. From the source: `StepFlow` is the direct
  child of an `Animated.View` whose `entering` is `enteringFor(<typedFallback plan>.enter)`, the
  plan reads `useReducedMotion()`, and the container sits inside a `skipEntering` config.
- **"the fallback visible budget stays ≤ 2000 ms at every setting".** Every setting means all
  six combinations of the app switch (off/on) and the OS answer (motion/reduced/none yet), through
  the real `reducedMotion(app, os)`. For each one, delay plus duration is at most `fadeMs` and
  2000 ms, and it is 0 whenever reduced. The swap being timed is a single reducer step: every exit
  from the voice lane (dialogue + failure/candidates/skip, gate + voice_unavailable/failure/skip)
  lands in `steps` in one dispatch. The session's terminal effect calls `onFailed` and `onHandoff`
  synchronously, and no `dispatchVoice` sits behind an animation.
- **"a step change is enter-only and never shows two questions in one frame".** For every
  consecutive pair in the longest shipped pack (Detaylı): the plan is enter-only, the keys differ,
  the same question keeps its key, and a frame model, sampled every millisecond from the frame
  before the commit to the end of the fade, shows exactly one question each time (the old one
  before the commit, the new one from it). The model keeps a removed element on screen only for
  `exitMs`. Two further checks:
  - App-wide, nothing registers an exit animation (an `exiting` prop or key, or a `…Out` builder),
    which is the only way Reanimated keeps a removed element on screen. Three samples prove the
    detector.
  - From the source: `StepFlow` has exactly one `Animated.View`, keyed and entering from a
    `typedStep(question.id, …)` plan under `skipEntering`. The question title and options are
    inside it, and the progress row and every `GlassBar` are outside it.
- **"completion navigation is never chained to an animation".** A detector finds every router or
  navigation call that runs only after any of these:
  - core `start(cb)`;
  - `withTiming`, `withDelay`, `withSequence`, `withRepeat`, `withDecay` or `withClamp` callbacks;
  - a layout animation's `withCallback`;
  - `runAfterInteractions`, `requestAnimationFrame`, `setTimeout` or `setInterval`;
  - a navigator's `transitionStart`/`transitionEnd` listener, or an `onAnimation…`/`onTransition…`
    handler;
  - an awaited one of these, or an awaited sleep.

  Eleven chained shapes are caught. Three are accepted: the same tick, a timer that navigates
  nowhere, and an awaited session teardown (not an animation). Over the whole app, more than 20
  navigation calls are found and none is chained. Each review navigation in the typed lane and
  the dialogue is in a non-async handler, and so is the session's `onDone()`.
- **"the review screen renders every section in one commit, with no stagger".**
  - `sections` comes from `buildNote(` in render.
  - There is exactly one `sections.map` and it takes no index, which is what a stagger multiplies.
    `summary.sections`, the consent summary's title list, is excluded by the lookbehind.
  - No section carries `entering`.
  - Neither the screen nor any of the 7 components it imports has a delay, a timer or frame, a
    deferred render (`useDeferredValue`/`startTransition`), a partial `sections.slice` or a
    virtualised list. Six samples prove the detector.

## What only a device can show: not recorded
- **Felt timing.** No release-build device run is recorded. The owner's two reference devices are
  still unnamed (INT-330). Two things are worth watching on a device:
  1. **Blur under the fallback fade.** For 120 ms the glass bar's `BlurView` sits under a parent
     whose opacity is below 1. iOS renders a visual-effect view under a fading ancestor the same way
     during every native-stack fade this app already runs (INT-330 pins those to 120 ms), so this
     adds no new kind of frame.
  2. **The keyboard between two free-text steps.** The step body is now a new element per
     question, so the text field is too. On a device, the keyboard may therefore close between two
     consecutive free-text steps where it used to stay up. C16 asks for exactly this ("the old
     content is removed at 0 ms"). The patient reads the next question before typing, and the CTA
     stays outside the body.
- **The 2 s end to end.** It belongs to INT-135's device measurement. This issue bounds the motion
  share of it at 120 ms (0 under reduce-motion). One await sits on the way into the typed lane,
  and it is not an animation: "Yazarak devam edin" awaits `session.leave()` (recorder stop and
  clip-cache deletion) so the microphone is closed before the typed lane shows, which P17 needs.
  Failure and hand-over reach the lane from the session's terminal effect without awaiting anything.
