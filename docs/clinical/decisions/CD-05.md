# CD-05 — Tense anchor per RED rule and the scripted anchor question

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

For each RED rule, which slot anchors its tense, how is the anchor asked as a scripted closed choice, how does each choice resolve against the session reference time, and how do NICE CG95's twelve-hour and seventy-two-hour windows map onto the engine's calendar-day tenses?

## Decision

Only the two active chest-pain rules are evaluable; both anchor on chest_pain.onset_at, which holds the time of the most recent episode (NICE CG95 1.2.1.1 asks when the last episode was). The paediatric-airway and crisis rules are draft and their anchors stay draft_needs_source. The anchor is asked as one scripted closed choice whose four non-overlapping bands are NICE's own: 'şu anda var', 'son on iki saat içinde', 'on iki saatten önce ama son üç gün içinde', 'üç günden daha önce'. The illustrative calendar words of the physician review (şu an / bugün / bu hafta / daha önce) are not used, because 'bugün' cannot express a twelve-hour band that crosses midnight and 'bu hafta' changes meaning with the weekday. The engine's tense windows are calendar days, with now and today both equal to the reference day (context.py temporal_within). 'şu anda var' and 'son on iki saat içinde' resolve to the reference day: NICE refers both for emergency assessment when an ECG is not available, which is every pre-visit session, so the now-tensed K2 rule may fire even when the twelve hours began the previous calendar day. 'üç günden daha önce' resolves to the reference day minus three, the most acute day consistent with 'more than seventy-two hours ago'; neither active rule fires, matching NICE 1.2.1.10, which sends that patient to detailed clinical assessment rather than emergency or same-day referral. The final resolution of 'on iki saatten önce ama son üç gün içinde' (twelve to seventy-two hours) stays draft_needs_source: NICE 1.2.1.8 asks for urgent same-day assessment, the K2 rule has only tense now (resolving to the reference day sends the patient to 112 for a pain one or two days old, the over-alarm files/04 §8.1 rule 4 warns against), resolving to an earlier day fires nothing (under-triage against NICE), and the Tense enum has no seventy-two-hour member. While it is draft the band is never left Unknown: it resolves to the reference day, accepting over-triage as the fail-closed direction inside D18's simulated scope, and the record labels the resolution as the while-draft one. An answer that fits no choice, or 'bilmiyorum', is recorded UNCLEAR and resolved to the more acute reading, the reference day. The council never fills an anchor and a document never feeds one.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-05.yaml`.

**Why item 10 is decided.** NICE CG95 1.2.1.1 checks first whether the pain is current. A session that ends before the onset question cannot exclude current pain. Today the anchor stays Unknown, and neither chest-pain rule can fire for a patient who may have left because they were getting worse. Evaluating it at close as the reference day applies `unresolved_answer`'s more-acute rule to the case of least information. A RED that fires at close takes the existing RED path (INT-540's `close_session`), and both chest-pain rules notify the clinic always. This decision changes no RED delivery. The cost is over-triage of an affirmed pain that was long past (for example CD-04's "eskiden vardı"). The qualifier travels to the clinician with the label.

**Item 7 today.** While the twelve-to-seventy-two-hour band resolves to the reference day, every straddle resolves to the reference day, the same day as the fallback. The tie-break first changes an outcome when that band's final resolution lands.

**Item 6: what closing the band needs.**

1. A Tense member covering seventy-two hours. The nearest member after `today` is `within_7d`. This is a DSL change.
2. A cardiac-features arm for this band, with route `SAME_DAY_ACIL` (NICE CG95 1.2.1.8, urgent same-day assessment). It is a new `base.life_threat` rule, with goldens and a near-miss control at both band edges (invariant 7; `make safety`; CODEOWNER).
3. That arm's EK-4 level and register. **No committed source fixes them.** EK-4 grades chest pain at the acil door by its character ("Kardiyak ağrıya benzer göğüs ağrısı") and has no time-since-last-episode axis. No committed NICE, ESC or Turkish source sets a level for a pain-free patient whose last episode was twelve to seventy-two hours ago. This is the signing clinician's choice (HB-C1).
4. A representation in which the now (K2) and today (K1) arms do not both read this band as the reference day. While the rules read one calendar day, any day either fires K2 or neither cardiac arm. Moving the band off the reference day would also stop K1 for a non-cardiac pain in the band. So the band must reach the rules as a band, or K2's window must be set in hours.

**Safety direction.** Items 5, 9, 10 and 11 resolve at least as acutely as before; item 10 is more acute than today. Item 7 is never less acute than any band the answer fits, nor than today's fallback. Item 6 is unchanged while it is draft: it over-triages, accepted only within D18's simulated scope.
