# CD-19 — opening and resume (rows 1-9)

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026, INT-432). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

INT-414 (#726) adds conversational delivery lines around each question. Do the opening and resume lines (three welcomes, two voice orientations, two typed orientations, two resume lines) pass PolicyGuard (no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising), stay B1 plain Turkish in the siz register, ask nothing, carry no numeral, and fit the moment they are said?

## Decision

All nine pass unchanged. They greet, say what will happen and state only the affordances the product really has, in short sentences of everyday words and the siz register, and none of them asks anything or carries a numeral. None says where the answers go: that wording renders only from docs/legal/ (invariant 18), and docs/legal/strings.yaml is not authored yet (HB-L27). Each line below is its own entry with its six checks, the author's verdict and the verifier's verdict; the Turkish is byte-equal to the catalogue key it names.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-19.yaml`.

### What each check means

- **PolicyGuard**: PolicyGuard.filter(text, scripted=False) on the dynamic path: no violation, nothing dropped, no bridge; and by reading, no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising.
- **B1 plain Turkish**: at most two sentences of at most fifteen words, everyday words, no medical jargon (Sağlık Bakanlığı plain-language aim).
- **siz register**: siz throughout; no sen; no doubled-polite imperative; warmth from -elim/-alım and -ebilirsiniz.
- **not a question**: no '?', no mı/mi/mu/mü, no interrogative opening a listener hears as a question.
- **no numerals**: no digit in tr or en (invariant 22).
- **fits its context**: true to what the product does at that moment, and right for the slot, the delivery context and the lane.
