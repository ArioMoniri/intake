# CD-16 — Göğüs cerrahisi Q12b: regurgitation is asked of every patient

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

V-GC-5: should food or saliva coming back up (Q12b `yiyecek_geri_gelmesi`) be asked of every thoracic-surgery patient? Or only after a swallowing complaint, which is the current gate `yutma_guclugu == true`?

## Decision

Ask it of every patient: the gate is removed.

The UEG and ESNM European achalasia guideline lists regurgitation as a symptom of achalasia in its own right, next to dysphagia, chest pain and weight loss. It calls regurgitation a hallmark of achalasia and poor oesophageal emptying. A patient with regurgitation and no perceived swallowing difficulty is therefore exactly the patient the thoracic-surgery pack exists to see (TGCD lists achalasia surgery among its consent forms, TOR-S7). The current gate encodes a clinical association, not a presupposition, and it would hide that patient.

NICE NG12 1.2.1 points the same way. Its second arm refers people aged 55 and over with weight loss and reflux, without dysphagia. The caveat is that NICE's word is reflux, while the item's wording is achalasia-type regurgitation. The pre-visit never maps one onto the other.

The item stays symptom capture:

- no red flag is proposed on regurgitation alone;
- the dysphagia rule (row 4) keeps its own trigger.

The cost is one extra yes/no turn for patients without a swallowing complaint. The verifier's scratch run with the gate removed passed `check_proposal` and `verify_chain`.
