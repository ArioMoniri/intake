## Question

Which current source states the weight-loss threshold, window and urgency?

## Decision

Write rf.weight_loss_unintentional as SARI_K2 / EXPEDITE, notify always, on two slots: weight loss without trying of more than five per cent in six months, and, after a yes, aged sixty or over. NICE NG12 1.13.2 (amended April 2026) states all three values and asks for urgent investigation or a suspected cancer or non-specific symptoms pathway referral. Level: EK-4 Sarı Kategori 2's moderate and prolonged symptoms with potential seriousness, the pack's mapping of an outpatient urgency onto the five-level scale. The anchor is the complaint's onset date, the dated slot every session asks; the window is in the probe. Age is asked as a yes or no on NG12's bound, not as free numeric entry (invariant 22).

## Inference disclosure

NG12's 'unexplained' means not explained after a primary-care assessment, which a patient cannot report; 'without trying' is the pack's proxy. The EK-4 Sarı Kategori 2 mapping is the pack's.
