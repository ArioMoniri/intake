# CD-111 — rf.anaphylaxis: sudden tongue or throat swelling, breathing or swallowing difficulty after a trigger, at EK-4 Kırmızı Kategori 2

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18; owner, 18 Nov 2026: *"yes write the 13 red flag rules"*, INT-619). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient. No `clinical_signoff` field is touched (D17); HB-C1 stays open.

Rule: `rf.anaphylaxis` in `packages/protocols/base/intake.yaml`. Status: **active**. Author: emergency medicine and allergy physician (agent).

## Question

Which current source states a layperson-answerable anaphylaxis trigger, its urgency and its do-nots?

## Decision

Write rf.anaphylaxis as KIRMIZI_K2 / EMERGENCY_NOW / ALERT on one probe: present now, after something such as a food, a drug or an insect sting, a sudden difficulty breathing or a throat swelling that makes swallowing hard (RCUK 2021 §3, §3.1, §3.2 Airway problems). Swelling alone is not asked for, because RCUK does not count angioedema without an airway or breathing problem as anaphylaxis. Level: EK-4 Kırmızı Kategori 2's 'yutkunma güçlüğü ile beraber olan havayolu tıkanıklığı riski'. EK-4's Kategori 1 'Havayolu tıkanıklığı riski' is the reading a physician may prefer; raising K2 to K1 is a tightening they can make. Do-not: 'Ayağa kalkmayın ve yürümeyin' (RCUK §4.4). ON_SITE does not send the patient walking to the acil servis: it keeps them in place with staff notified (STAY_IN_PLACE_STAFF_NOTIFIED). Self-injected adrenaline is not scripted: it is a treatment instruction and no committed passage states it for a patient; the physician decides whether the REMOTE script names a prescribed auto-injector.

## Sources (verbatim in the committed snapshots)

- RCUK-ANAPHYLAXIS-2021 (3. Recognition of anaphylaxis): "Anaphylaxis is likely if a patient who is exposed to a trigger (allergen) develops a sudden illness (usually within minutes of exposure)" — supports `trigger`
- RCUK-ANAPHYLAXIS-2021 (3.2 Airway problems): "throat and tongue swelling causing difficulty in breathing/swallowing" — supports `trigger`
- RCUK-ANAPHYLAXIS-2021 (3.2): "Consider anaphylaxis in a person with sudden onset breathing difficulties, especially if known to be allergic to a food or insect sting." — supports `trigger`
- RCUK-ANAPHYLAXIS-2021 (4.4 Patient positioning): "Patients must not walk or stand during acute reactions." — supports `action.do_nots, action.ON_SITE`

- TR-EK4-2022 (EK-4 Kırmızı Kategori 1): "Havayolu tıkanıklığı riski" — supports `level_alternative`

## Inference disclosure

The probe joins RCUK's exposure, suddenness and airway or breathing problem into one self-report; skin changes are left out because RCUK says they may be absent and are not a sign of anaphylaxis alone. The probe's examples (food, medicine, insect sting) illustrate RCUK's 'trigger (allergen)'; the committed excerpt names food and insect sting, and medication as a trigger is RCUK Table 2, which the excerpt omits.
