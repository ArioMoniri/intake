# CD-111 — Cardiac RED do-not lines and the unanswered location wait

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-111.yaml`. Status: **active** (one value, the wait, stays draft_needs_source). Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient. The rule's gate, tense, level, route, register and location branches are untouched, and nothing extends base.life_threat.

## Question

Which of the cardiac RED rule's do-not lines are kept, changed or removed, on what current source, and does a blanket "Bir şey yiyip içmeyin." conflict with the care a 112 call handler or the acil servis gives? And what wait, if any source states one, applies before an unanswered location question resolves to REMOTE with 112?

Origin: INT-472 (GitHub #805), from the INT-465 (#796) clinical re-review of 3 Oct 2026: CD-24 kept the generic do-nots and the no-answer wait draft_needs_source, and the served cardiac rule carries the three do-not lines with no source.

## Decision

REMOTE: "Kendi aracınızla yola çıkmayın." changes to the sourced "Hastaneye kendiniz araba kullanarak gitmeyin."; "Bir şey yiyip içmeyin." is removed; "Tek başınıza kalmayın; yanınızdaki kişiye durumu söyleyin." changes to "Yanınızda biri varsa durumu ona söyleyin ve sizinle kalmasını isteyin.". ON_SITE: "Poliklinik sırasında beklemeyin." and "Tek başınıza gitmeyin — yanınızdaki kişiden ya da bir görevliden yardım isteyin." are kept; "Bir şey yiyip içmeyin." is removed. The nil-by-mouth line contradicts current care: chest-pain first aid and NICE CG95 give aspirin by mouth while help is awaited, and the 999 call handler gives the advice on what to do. Nothing replaces it: a medicine instruction is the call handler's or the clinician's to give, after an allergy check the device cannot make. The wait: no current source states a number, so it stays draft_needs_source with the invariant-17 literal, and the device's constant stays an unsigned engineering value; the qualitative rule is decided: an unanswered question resolves to REMOTE with 112 shown (silence may be a person who can no longer communicate), a late "evet" switches to ON_SITE, and CD-24's ask-first verdict stands.

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the constructs: do not drive yourself; the call handler advises what to do; aspirin by mouth while help is awaited, after an allergy check; rest; someone stays with the person; escort to the acil servis; silence may be someone who can no longer communicate. These choices are this author's, made on those sources: narrowing the driving line from "your own vehicle" to driving oneself (the call handler, not the device, decides any other transport); rendering "stay with the person" for the patient as a conditional line, so a patient alone is not given an instruction they cannot keep or sent to look for someone instead of resting; removing the nil-by-mouth line without a replacement; and the late-answer switch. The Turkish wording of the two changed lines is this author's B1 rendering, PolicyGuard-clean.

Decided lists:

- **REMOTE**: "Hastaneye kendiniz araba kullanarak gitmeyin." · "Yanınızda biri varsa durumu ona söyleyin ve sizinle kalmasını isteyin."
- **ON_SITE**: "Poliklinik sırasında beklemeyin." · "Tek başınıza gitmeyin — yanınızdaki kişiden ya da bir görevliden yardım isteyin."

**No medicine line.** No aspirin or other medicine line replaces the removed one, in either location. A medicine instruction needs an allergy and contraindication check the device cannot make and is treatment language patient copy may not carry (invariant 2); the 112 call handler, or the acil servis on site, gives it.

### The unanswered location question

- **Rule (active).** An unanswered location question resolves to REMOTE with 112 shown, never to silence and never to ON_SITE: silence after an emergency contact may be a person who can no longer communicate. CD-24's ask-first verdict stands: 112 is not shown during the wait, because showing it first guesses REMOTE.
- **Late answer (active).** An answer that arrives after expiry still decides the branch: 'evet' switches to ON_SITE (112 withdrawn, staff notified, the ON_SITE script shown); 'hayır' keeps REMOTE.
- **The wait (draft_needs_source).** Value: the invariant-17 pending literal (see the YAML record). No retrieved current source states how long to wait for an answer before taking the no-answer branch. Searched on 3 Oct 2026: the EENA silent-calls operations document (no timing; it says what silence may mean), NICE CG95 1.2.3, the ERC 2025 first aid guideline as RCUK publishes it, the NHS heart attack page, files/02 §5.3 and files/04 §8.1 rule 7 (they fix the branch, not a wait), and EK-4, whose only time figure is a treatment target, not a question timeout. WCAG timing is the legal lane's. Any number here would be invented.
- **Meanwhile.** While no_answer_wait is draft, the device's constant (UNSIGNED_LOCATION_NO_ANSWER_WAIT_MS on #796) stays an unsigned engineering value, recorded as such (CD-24); on expiry the branch is REMOTE with 112 shown, never silence, and the clinic notification fires in every case.
