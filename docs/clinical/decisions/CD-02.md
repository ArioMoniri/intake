# CD-02 — The never-silently-filled slot list

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

Which slots or slot classes may never be filled from narrative, the council, a document or drug-name inference, and may be filled only by a direct answer to the scripted question or a keypad/chip value rendered back to the patient?

## Decision

Eight classes are never silently filled. Two are derived, not listed: every slot that any red-flag rule reads in its gate (rule_gate_slots) and every slot that any rule names as its tense_anchor (tense_anchors), at any rule status, so the list grows when a rule is written and cannot shrink when one is promoted. Six are explicit: the base.intake red_flags probes, the class gates (anticoagulant, immunosuppression including chemotherapy, pregnancy), numeric inputs, validated-instrument items, the subject role, and the two text_only safeguarding probes. A slot in any class is filled only by the patient's direct answer to its scripted question, or by a keypad or chip value that is rendered back on screen. Narrative, the council, a document and a drug name may arm the scripted probe (CD-03) and may be shown to the clinician with their provenance, but they never set the value and never yield ASKED_ABSENT. The clinical basis: NICE CG95 makes the chest-pain check a direct, immediate question; the CKM screening archetype models presence and absence as answers to pre-defined questions, while the history archetype records positive presence and sends an explicitly reported absence to a separate exclusion archetype, so keeping narrative from ever producing ASKED_ABSENT is this author's conservative choice (see inference_disclosure), resting on the repository's own rule that an unanswered slot renders as 'Sorulmadı', never as an elicited negative; and NICE NG5 makes medicines reconciliation a task for a trained health professional with the patient involved, which an automated drug-name-to-class inference is not. Instrument items, numeric inputs and the subject role are on the list because project invariants already bind them (CLAUDE.md invariants 10 and 22, files/04 instrument fidelity); they are recorded here for completeness, not resolved by this decision.

Inference disclosure: openEHR does model an explicitly reported absence (CLUSTER.exclusion_symptom_sign, named in the Symptom/Sign misuse text), so no cited source forbids recording an absence volunteered in narrative. Refusing it, so that ASKED_ABSENT comes only from a direct answer to the scripted question, is this author's conservative choice: a narrative denial is not the elicited negative a pertinent-negative list asserts. The repository's own spec states the same asymmetry (spec_basis).

## Near-miss tests that pin it

- `council_cannot_fill_rule_gate_slot` — positive: a direct `evet` to the scripted chest_pain.present question fills the slot as ASKED_PRESENT. Near-miss negative: the same words arriving only in the open narrative or from the council leave the slot NOT_ASKED and arm the probe.
- `drug_name_does_not_set_class_gate` — positive: a scripted `evet` to the anticoagulant question sets meds_allergies.anticoagulant_use. Near-miss negative: an imported list containing an anticoagulant drug name leaves the gate NOT_ASKED (the name may be shown back for confirmation).
- `document_cannot_fill_or_clear_rf_slot` — positive: a dated document mentioning chest pain arms the probe. Near-miss negative: a document stating no chest pain neither fills ASKED_ABSENT nor suppresses the scripted question.
- `rule_gate_class_is_derived` — positive: adding a slot reference to any rule gate adds it to the class without editing this decision. Near-miss negative: promoting a draft rule to active does not remove any slot from the class.
