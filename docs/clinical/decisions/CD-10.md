# CD-10 — One polarity for child_health hydration items; the urine-output question (HB-C3)

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-10.yaml`. Status: **active**. Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient. No triage level, route, register, location or red-flag rule is created or changed here, and HB-C3's paediatric rule set is not authored here.

## Question

The cluster question "Çocuğunuz her zamanki kadar çiş yapıyor mu?" (`cis_miktari`) makes `false` the alarm direction, unlike every other symptom slot in the pack. Its specialty twins record the concern as `true`: neonatoloji `islak_bez_azalmasi` and `beslenmede_azalma`, cocuk_gastroenterolojisi `az_cis`, and cocuk_nefrolojisi `idrar_azalmasi`.

Which single polarity do hydration items use across child_health, what is the Turkish question for the urine-output item, and on what source?

Origin: HB-C3; docs/evidence/verification/cluster-wave-b.md rows "Q5, Q7" and §F; docs/evidence/proposals/cluster_child_health.md Q7 and §4 gap 8.

## Decision

1. **Polarity convention: `true_is_concern`** for every boolean hydration item in the child_health packs. The ask names the abnormal finding, and "evet" records that the finding is present.
   - The sources state the finding in its abnormal direction: "reduced urine output" (NG143), "decreased urine output" (CG84), "İdrar çıkışı ... Azalmış" (HSGM Tablo 9).
   - CG84 1.2.1.1 tells the assessor to ask whether the child "has decreased urine output".
   - In the openEHR symptom/sign screening model, "Yes" records that the named symptom or sign is present.
2. **The urine-output item becomes `az_cis`**, asked as:
   > Çocuğunuz her zamankinden daha az çiş yapıyor mu?
   - Checks: B1 Turkish, `siz` register, one question, eight words, no numerals, no double negative.
   - It is the same text the independent wave 1–3 verification prescribed for cocuk_gastroenterolojisi.
   - **The id changes with the polarity on purpose.** A flip under the old id would silently invert anything that reads `cis_miktari`; a removed id fails loudly at compile time (`no_rule_references_an_undeclared_slot`).
3. **Exact changes for the lead to apply** (review-queue candidates):

| pack | current slot and ask | change | gen line |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cluster.child_health` (proposal Q7) | `cis_miktari`, "Çocuğunuz her zamanki kadar çiş yapıyor mu?" | rename and reword to `az_cis`, "Çocuğunuz her zamankinden daha az çiş yapıyor mu?" | `gen: slot=az_cis; type=boolean` |
| `specialty.cocuk_sagligi_ve_hastaliklari` | `cis_miktari`, same ask as above | same as above | `gen: slot=az_cis; type=boolean` |
| `specialty.neonatoloji` | `islak_bez_azalmasi`, "Bebeğinizin bezi her zamankinden daha seyrek mi ıslanıyor?" | rename to `az_cis` and keep its nappy text: same fact for nappy-wearing infants, so it is asked once | `gen: slot=az_cis; type=boolean` |
| `specialty.cocuk_gastroenterolojisi` | `az_cis`, already the target text | none | `gen: slot=az_cis; type=boolean` |
| `specialty.cocuk_nefrolojisi` | `idrar_azalmasi`, marked reduction or near-absence | none. It already follows the convention, asks a narrower construct and feeds the AKI gate, so it stays separate; renaming it would replace the broader cluster ask in nephrology sessions | `gen: slot=idrar_azalmasi; type=boolean` |

4. **Feeding and drinking items fall under the same convention, but their construct choice is a separate paediatric decision.** NG143 Table 2 puts poor feeding in infants in the circulation and hydration row.
   - Pending items: `beslenme_sivi_alimi` in the cluster and in cocuk_sagligi_ve_hastaliklari, `emebiliyor_icebiliyor` in cocuk_enfeksiyon_hastaliklari, and `beslenmede_azalma` in neonatoloji.
   - The open choice is CH-S1's inability to drink or breastfeed against feeding as usual.
   - Until that choice is made, each item keeps its id and text, and none is renamed onto another's id, because a rename across opposite polarities inverts the answer.
   - When the construct is chosen, its ask follows `true_is_concern`.
5. **Already conforming:** `agiz_kuru_goz_cokuk` (cluster and cocuk_gastroenterolojisi).

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the construct and state it in its abnormal direction, but none of them mandates a boolean polarity.

- These are this author's representation decisions made on that basis: choosing `true_is_concern`, renaming to `az_cis`, and folding the nappy item into `az_cis`.
- NG143 and CG84 are scoped to children under five. The polarity rule does not depend on age, but whether their features apply above five remains the paediatric applicability question in cluster_child_health.md §4 gap 2.
