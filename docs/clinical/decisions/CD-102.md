## Question

For each open item that the #642 sign-off packets route from the cluster wave A verification (medical, surgical, sensory and women's health clusters), what is the clinical decision, on what current source, and which part stays with the owner?

Origin: INT-448 (GitHub #759): items of the #642 sign-off packets (docs/signoff/*.md at 151cf34f) whose row lives in docs/evidence/verification/cluster-wave-a.md.

## Decision

Thirteen items. Accepted (4): the women's health heavy-period and between-period bleeding items stay ungated for pregnant patients; the sensory cluster takes no domain gates, because sudden hearing loss and sudden dizziness with new deafness or unsteadiness are time-critical whatever the booking; el_cerrahisi keeps its rebind to travma_sonrasi_basladi; odyoloji's dizziness-or-imbalance item stays apart from the cluster's spinning item. Changed (4): surgical Q9 keeps its reworded text, its source anchors move to the TARD 2025 comorbidity sections, and a stroke or transient stroke history item is added because TARD postpones elective surgery after one; cluster.medical gains the ungated hyperglycaemia-with-vomiting input, because TEMD 2026 records DKA as the first sign of diabetes in many patients (a note input only; a DKA rule, when written, sits in base.life_threat on a base-level slot); the pregnancy swelling item returns to NICE NG133's sudden swelling of the face, hands or feet; ic_hastaliklari adopts the cluster's night-sweat question and keeps its fever-of-unknown-cause item apart as nedenini_bilmedigi_ates, recorded as the patient's report and never asserted as NG12's "unexplained". Rejected (1): the base fever, weight-loss and pregnancy-bleeding probes do not take the cluster ids and are never filled from a cluster answer, because each probe carries a severity criterion the screen does not ask; the mechanics are the base owner's. Three items stay draft_needs_source: the base red-flag probe wording, inputs for six surgical and sensory patterns, and the fast-mode turn budget (HB-C5). One item is an owner question (D3 text_first prerequisites).

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the clinical constructs. The representation choices are this author's and are made on those sources: which id carries which fact, keeping two facts apart, a restating follow-up, and the Turkish wording of the added stroke item and the reworded swelling item. The stroke item asks history only; the one-month window TARD sets for postponing surgery is left to the clinician at the visit and is not asked, so no number enters patient copy.
