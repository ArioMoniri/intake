# CD-13 — Placement of the INT-427 red-flag proposals

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

HB-C1 placement: where does each red-flag proposal row in the seven INT-427 verification documents live? The seven are göğüs hastalıkları, beyin ve sinir cerrahisi, hematoloji, nefroloji, fiziksel tıp ve rehabilitasyon, göğüs cerrahisi and anesteziyoloji. The working assumption was:

- universal time-critical patterns (cauda equina, thunderclap headache, focal neurological deficit) go to `base.life_threat`;
- the rest go to the department's own draft_needs_source stub.

## Decision

Placement follows files/04 §8: patients do not present according to their booking. A time-critical pattern that any patient can present with must therefore be evaluated on every session. Four placements apply:

1. **`base.life_threat`** takes a pattern that meets both conditions:
   - its source sets an emergency, immediate or same-day action;
   - its presentation does not depend on the booked branch.

   The HB-C2 author writes the rule in `files/protocols/base.life_threat.yaml` itself. It is never reached through `extends:` or an overlay op. Its trigger slots must be declared at base level, because a department slot does not exist in a session booked elsewhere. The department items stay gate inputs and are rebound to the base id once it exists.
2. **A base universal family.** A universal but non-emergency arm that matches a family already declared in base.intake's universal red_flags (files/04 §3) merges into that family. The two families are `rf.new_neuro_deficit_subacute` and `rf.weight_loss_unintentional`.
3. **The department stub.** A referral signal stays in the department's own draft_needs_source stub when its source sets an urgent or routine referral (an NG12 pathway, a specialist referral, a very urgent blood count) or needs a laboratory value.
4. **Unsourced time-criticality.** A pattern whose time-criticality no retrieved source establishes stays in the department stub as draft_needs_source. It moves to `base.life_threat` only when a source states an emergency or same-day action for a trigger a layperson can report.

**Source-currency finding.** The January 2019 Scottish quick reference guide, cited as GH-S7 and H-S4, was replaced by the Scottish Referral Guidelines for Suspected Cancer 2025 (published 6 August 2025). Every row that cites it must be re-sourced before it can be signed: gh-1, gh-2, gh-A1 and hm-1. hm-4 cited H-S3 at be5b21b8; H-S4 was only the INT-427 verifier's suggested re-anchor for it, and CD-14 does not use it.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-13.yaml`.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

- **The rules themselves.** Placement is decided; the rules are not. Every placed rule keeps its own draft_needs_source trigger, level, register and location actions until it is sourced and signed (HB-C1, HB-C2).
- **nf-1, ftr-2, ftr-5 and bvsc-5.** No retrieved source sets an emergency or same-day action for a trigger a layperson can report, so their time-criticality is unsourced. They stay in their department stubs, and move to `base.life_threat` only if such a source is snapshotted.
- **Rows citing the 2019 Scottish guide** (gh-1, gh-2, gh-A1, hm-1). That document has been replaced, so these rows must be re-sourced before signature.
