## Question

## Decision

Fifteen items. Accepted (3): the chest-indrawing ask "kaburgalarının altı ya da arası" names the WHO lower chest wall sign and adds intercostal indrawing; the non-blanching rash item stays ungated; the vaccination item stays boolean, because "bilmiyorum" is recorded as UNCLEAR (CD-04, CKM Unknown). Changed (2): hydration polarity is CD-10's true_is_concern, and the cluster keeps both feeding constructs because their thresholds differ: feeding or drinking less than usual (az_beslenme, CD-100 T9; NG143's poor feeding, kept and not merged) and, as a separate ungated item beside it, the IMCI general danger sign of inability to drink or breastfeed (new id icemez_ememez); beslenme_sivi_alimi and emebiliyor_icebiliyor retire into these two, and neonatoloji's infant item takes az_beslenme with its own text; the child fever ask becomes "oldu mu" with a recency frame, and its duration follow-up no longer presumes an ongoing fever. Rejected (2): the persecutory-ideation item and the risk-to-others item stay held and are not asked before the visit, because a pre-visit yes/no cannot do what NICE puts with trained staff in private (PH50) or in a verified, multidisciplinary risk assessment (NG10, CG115); the category questions go to the owner. Eight items are owner questions (guardian answers to base items, the psychosis item's category, the dental airway posture and two pedodonti items, the forensic report scope, the base HPI gate, and carer answers in Evde Sağlık).

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the constructs. Asking the danger sign as its own item beside the feeding-less item (az_beslenme, whose text is CD-100's), the new slot id, and the Turkish wording of the danger-sign and fever items are this author's choices made on those sources. The feeding ask puts the question particle on a positive verb ("hâle geldi mi") so that "evet" means the danger sign is present and no negative-question ambiguity arises. NG143 and the WHO pocket book are written for young children; whether their features apply above five years stays the paediatric applicability question recorded in cluster_child_health.md §4 gap 2.
