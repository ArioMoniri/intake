# CD-01 — Chest-pain slots the two active RED rules read (HB-C6)

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

Which slots, with which scripted Turkish questions and closed choices, must base.intake declare so that rf.chest_pain_cardiac_features and rf.chest_pain_no_cardiac_history can evaluate, and which patients are asked each one?

## Decision

Declare three sections and seven slots: chest_pain {present, character, radiation, onset_at}, associated {diaphoresis, dyspnoea} and trauma {recent}. chest_pain.present is asked of every session as one direct scripted question that names pain or discomfort, now or recently, because NICE CG95 counts discomfort as chest pain and requires an immediate check of current pain and, when pain free, of the last episode. The other six are asked only when chest_pain.present is true. All seven are must_ask: the K2 rule can fire on chest_pain.present, one positive feature and the anchor with the others unanswered (T or U is T), but the SARI rule fires only when all seven are answered, so leaving any of them unasked silently removes the SARI arm. Every code slot offers a closed choice containing each literal the rules test, spelled exactly. character adds 'yanma' (a pain character the CKM Symptom/Sign archetype names) and 'başka bir his' (the archetype's free character phrase); radiation adds 'boyun' and 'omuz' (NICE CG95 1.3.3.1) and 'başka bir yer'. An answer naming more than one site or more than one character word is recorded CONTESTED with every named value and evaluated on their union (files/12 §7.3); it is never stored as a list (a list reads Unknown) and never reduced to one value, and 'yok' is exclusive. The associated symptoms are asked as occurring with the chest pain (NICE CG95 1.2.1.3: marked sweating, breathlessness). trauma.recent is anchored to the current chest-pain episode rather than to a numeric window, because no retrieved source states a window. onset_at holds the time of the most recent episode, not the first onset (NICE CG95 1.2.1.1), and is answered from the four-band closed choice of CD-05. Each slot also carries a proxy form, spoken when identity_check.subject_role is 'yakınım adına', because the CKM Symptom/Sign archetype records symptoms as reported by a parent, care-giver or other party; the DSL holds one ask_tr per slot, so until it can select the proxy form the proxy stop-gap below applies, and the under-triage gap it leaves is recorded as finding F13. Disagreements between the sources and the two rules are recorded as findings and are not applied to base.life_threat.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-01.yaml`.

The DSL holds one `ask_tr` per slot. Until it can select the proxy form, the self-worded copy is not spoken in a proxy session and the seven slots stay NOT_ASKED (rendered "Sorulmadı"). This stop-gap is not fail-closed: meanwhile neither chest-pain RED rule can fire in a proxy session, a known under-triage gap recorded as finding F13 that blocks any real patient, and a simulated proxy persona with chest pain is reported as unscreened, never as negative. A CD-08 clinic-inbox item of kind `proxy_chest_pain_screen_unasked`, pinned to the note, is raised only when the subject is in the two rules' adult age band or of unknown age, never for a subject known to be outside it (for example a child intake). The subject-neutral wording "Şu anda ya da yakın zamanda göğüste ağrı veya rahatsızlık oldu mu?" was considered and not adopted, because it does not say whose chest is meant.

`chest_pain.onset_at` holds the most recent episode, not the first onset; its four bands do not overlap and resolve as in CD-05. The numeric bands are spelled in words because NICE defines them in hours. A multi-site or multi-word answer is recorded CONTESTED with every named value and evaluated on their union (files/12 §7.3); it is never stored as a list and never reduced to one value, and `yok` is exclusive.

### Findings (recorded, not applied to base.life_threat)

- **F1** — The rules' evidence quotes are written with a lower-case 'kardiyak'; EK-4 prints both entries with a capital 'K', so a case-sensitive verbatim check of the rules' quotes against this snapshot fails. Not changed here. Source TR-EK4-2022: "Kardiyak ağrıya benzer göğüs ağrısı"
- **F2** — Both rules cite https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2022/09/20220913-6.htm, which returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-02. The Tebliğ is item 20220913-5.htm of the 13/9/2022 Resmî Gazete and EK-4 is in its scanned annex 20220913-5-1.pdf. Not changed here. Source TR-EK4-2022: "YATAKLI SAĞLIK TESİSLERİNDE ACİL SERVİS HİZMETLERİNİN"
- **F3** — NICE CG95 1.2.1.3 lists nausea and vomiting with chest pain; the K2 rule's associated disjunction reads only diaphoresis and dyspnoea, and no nausea or vomiting slot exists. Adding a disjunct is a rule change for the signing clinician. Source NICE-CG95: "chest pain associated with nausea and vomiting, marked sweating, breathlessness, or particularly a combination of these"
- **F4** — NICE CG95 1.2.1.3 lists pain lasting longer than fifteen minutes; neither rule reads a duration, and hpi.duration is not read by any rule. Source NICE-CG95: "pain in the chest and/or other areas (for example, the arms, back or jaw) lasting longer than 15 minutes"
- **F5** — NICE CG95 1.3.3.1 places anginal discomfort in the neck and shoulders as well as the jaw and arms. The K2 radiation set is {kol, çene, sırt}; a patient whose pain spreads only to the neck or shoulder, with no other feature, satisfies neither rule (not in the K2 set, and not 'yok' for the SARI rule), so no rule fires. Source NICE-CG95: "constricting discomfort in the front of the chest, or in the neck, shoulders, jaw or arms"
- **F6** — The CKM archetype names burning as a pain character. The K2 character set omits 'yanma', so burning chest pain with no other feature routes to the SARI arm rather than K2. Whether burning belongs to the cardiac set stays draft_needs_source here: the ESC 2023 ACS guideline section 3.1.1 primary text could not be retrieved by this author (publisher site behind a bot check). Source openEHR-EHR-CLUSTER.symptom_sign.v2: "pain could be described as 'gnawing', 'burning', or 'like an electric shock'"
- **F7** — Both rules anchor on chest_pain.onset_at, but the NICE windows run from the last episode. If onset_at held the first onset, a pain that began days ago and is present now would fail tense: now. This decision defines onset_at as the most recent episode; renaming the slot is for the rule author. Source NICE-CG95: "If they are pain free, check when their last episode of pain was"
- **F8** — NICE CG95 1.2.1.8 sends a last episode twelve to seventy-two hours old for urgent same-day assessment. The K2 rule carries tense: now only and there is no same-day cardiac-features arm, so a final resolution of that band either over-triages to EMERGENCY_NOW or fires nothing (CD-05 keeps it draft_needs_source and states the while-draft behaviour). Source NICE-CG95: "the last episode of pain was 12 to 72 hours ago."
- **F9** — Three-valued gap, with chest_pain.present true and no feature reading True. If any of character, radiation, diaphoresis or dyspnoea is UNCLEAR, the K2 disjunction reads Unknown (False or Unknown is Unknown) and the SARI conjunction reads Unknown. If only trauma.recent is UNCLEAR, the K2 disjunction reads False (K2 does not read trauma) and the SARI conjunction reads Unknown. In both cases neither rule fires, although EK-4 names chest pain by row in KIRMIZI Kategori 2 and SARI Kategori 1 (and chest injury without chest pain in SARI Kategori 2). CD-04 routes an UNCLEAR that survives the follow-up to a clinic-inbox item so it is never dropped. Source TR-EK4-2022: "Kardiyak öykü ile uyumlu olmayan göğüs ağrısı"
- **F10** — Chest pain with trauma.recent true and no K2 feature fires nothing: K2 needs a feature, the SARI_K1 rule's own gate requires trauma.recent == false (a condition EK-4's SARI Kategori 1 row does not itself state), and EK-4's SARI Kategori 2 row covers a simple chest injury WITHOUT chest pain or breathing difficulty. Writing that third arm (CI gate ek4_chest_pain_split_is_three_way) therefore does not close this gap; it needs a decision on painful chest injury. Source TR-EK4-2022: "Göğüs ağrısı ve solunum sıkıntısı olmayan basit göğüs yaralanmaları"
- **F11** — The Tebliğ defines triage as performed by the duty health personnel, with in-service triage training. An EK-4 level computed before arrival is therefore signposting toward triage, not triage under MADDE 8; this belongs in the regulatory file. Source TR-EK4-2022: "Triyaj yapacak personele hizmet içi eğitim verilmiş olmalıdır."
- **F12** — EK-4 KIRMIZI Kategori 2 also lists severe pain of any cause. Neither chest-pain rule reads hpi.severity_nrs, so severe chest pain without a K2 feature lands at SARI_K1, or fires nothing under F9. Source TR-EK4-2022: "Herhangi bir nedenle olan ciddi ağrı"
- **F13** — Until the DSL can select proxy copy, none of the seven chest-pain slots is asked in a proxy session (subject_role 'yakınım adına'), so neither chest-pain RED rule can fire for an adult patient spoken for by a relative: the immediate check NICE CG95 asks for is not performed. This is a known under-triage gap that blocks any real patient. In simulated stress testing a proxy persona with chest pain is reported as unscreened, never as negative; a subject-neutral wording was considered and not adopted (value.considered_proxy_alternative). Source NICE-CG95: "Check immediately whether people currently have chest pain."
