## Decision

Eighteen resolutions: thirteen owner questions and five base-owner parts (round 4, after the independent verifier's rounds 1 to 3). History taking: the reason for the visit is asked once, by base chief_complaint_text, and the generators stop emitting the fixed basvuru_nedeni (three Q1s that ask a narrower contact fact stay as items); the complaint's timing is asked once, by base onset_date, whose follow-up gains years; base duration gives way to a four-valued episodicity item ("sürekli", "arada bir", "bir kez oldu, geçti", "emin değilim"), and a time since onset is printed only for a complaint answered as continuous (üreme keeps its time-trying-to-conceive item, text_first; perinatoloji keeps its time-known item). lmp_date's gate is widened, never replaced: today's pregnancy term stays and a population guard is added as an extra OR term, in every chain, so lmp_date is skipped only when a recorded fact excludes (a male sex at birth or an age outside the bounds) and the pregnancy answer is not "evet" or "emin değilim"; an unknown sex or age asks it, the guard's atoms are bound as two-valued facts from recorded demographics, and the bounds stay draft_needs_source; pregnancy_status is not guarded and is confirmed as the gate of record; kadın Q2 stays until base asks every patient of that chain. Dental airway: one posture, asked when tooth or gum pain, face, jaw or sublingual swelling or a recent face or jaw injury is reported, never on trismus alone; a branch may only widen that gate; pedodonti asks for the child on the child's pain, gum swelling, face swelling or recent face or jaw injury. Report-only visits: a three-valued base presence screen covering now or recently, asked before onset_date, gates the symptom-detail history, which an unsure or unanswered screen and a later chest-pain yes open; onset_date stays asked as the red-flag tense anchor. sağlık kurulu Q8 leaves its proposal; the FTR fever twin applies CD-101 T68. Seven new slot ids go to the legal lane for their inventory rows.

**Rule.** one fact, one id, one ask: where two slots record the same fact, the parent layer's id stays and the twin goes; where they differ in referent, window or subject, both stay.

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the constructs: one opening question, a timing fact recorded as a date, episodicity with an ongoing value, a symptom history that details a symptom present now or recently, the airway checked within painful, swollen or injured dental presentations, and the facts a disability board records. These choices are this author's, made on those sources: dropping the generators' fixed slots rather than changing base; reading the planned operation, the planned vaccine and the conditions before a board as facts about the contact (openEHR at0002 'contact type', whose examples include a pre-operative assessment) rather than the reason restated; printing a time since onset only for a continuous complaint; keeping onset_date asked of everyone because it anchors the base red-flag families; the presence screen's wording and its chest-pain reopening; the dental gate terms (neck swelling is not a separate term: the presentations that bring it bring tooth pain, and the dental branches' neck items are persistent lumps, not acute swelling); taking the ungated dental re-declarations to the cluster's gate, which in those L0 candidates stops asking a patient with no pain, swelling or injury (a capture-only item no rule reads); the ids of the re-homed items; that gum swelling comes with gum pain, so the cluster needs no separate gum-swelling term (the guidance lists the two signs separately); the episodicity choice for a single ended episode; and putting the chest_pain follow-ups ahead of a reopened history. None of it is a clinical number.
