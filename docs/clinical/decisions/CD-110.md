## Decision

Eighteen resolutions: thirteen owner questions and five base-owner parts. History taking: the reason for the visit is asked once, by base chief_complaint_text, and the generators stop emitting the fixed basvuru_nedeni (three Q1s that ask a narrower contact fact stay as items); the complaint's timing is asked once, by base onset_date, so the fixed sikayet_suresi and base duration are no longer asked (üreme endokrinolojisi keeps its time-trying-to-conceive item); lmp_date is gated by the population it applies to rather than by the pregnancy answer, in every chain, once a population guard exists (its age bounds stay draft_needs_source); pregnancy_status is confirmed as the gate of record and precedes every item it gates. Dental airway: one posture for every dental chain, asked and gated on tooth or gum pain or face, jaw or sublingual swelling, never on trismus; the ungated re-declarations drop their own item; pedodonti re-declares it for the child on the child's pain or gum swelling. Report-only visits: a base symptom-presence screen gates the symptom-detail history, while onset_date stays asked as the red-flag tense anchor. sağlık kurulu Q8 leaves its proposal, because the chain already asks the cluster's sourced mobility-aid item. The FTR fever twin is resolved by applying CD-101 T68.

**Rule.** one fact, one id, one ask: where two slots record the same fact, the parent layer's id stays and the twin goes; where they differ in referent, window or subject, both stay.

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the constructs: one opening question, a timing fact recorded as a date, a symptom history that details a present symptom, the airway checked within painful or swollen dental infections, and the facts a disability board records. These choices are this author's, made on those sources: dropping the generators' fixed slots rather than changing base; deriving duration from the onset date; keeping onset_date asked of everyone because it anchors the base red-flag families; gating the rest of the history on one presence screen and mapping hedges to present; the dental gate terms (tooth or gum pain, face, jaw or sublingual swelling; neck swelling is not a separate term because the presentations that bring it bring tooth pain, and the cluster has no acute neck-swelling item); taking the four ungated dental re-declarations to the cluster's gate, which in those L0 candidates stops asking a patient with neither pain nor swelling (a capture-only item no rule reads, so no red-flag rule is relaxed); and the ids of the three re-homed Q1 items and the üreme item. None of it is a clinical number.
