## Question

The near-twin sweep (`docs/evidence/verification/near-twin-sweep-582.md` at `151cf34f`, not on main) lists rows T51–T99 and a §D named-site row in the #642 sign-off packets. In each, two slots in one extends chain ask the same or an overlapping fact.

For each row: which slot stays, which is dropped, narrowed or renamed, with what Turkish text, or which part belongs to the owner?

## Decision

Each row gets one outcome:

- **accept**: the twin is real, but both slots stay, because each records a fact the other does not (§C "keep under its own id").
- **change**: apply the exact slot, text or gate change given in the row.
- **owner_question**: every decider is the owner or the base owner, which D18 does not reach.

**Count: 21 accept, 28 change, 1 owner_question, 0 reject, 0 draft_needs_source.**

Four rules decide the rows.

1. **Thresholds are kept apart.** Each of these pairs is two facts, so both items stay:
   - vomiting everything (an IMCI general danger sign) against recurrent or frequent vomiting, or not tolerating fluids;
   - chest indrawing against breathing difficulty;
   - waking difficulty against sleepiness;
   - dizziness or imbalance against spinning vertigo;
   - a new or persistent lump against an acute facial swelling.

   The feeding rows follow the verifier's ruling on CD-100 and CD-103:
   - feeding less than usual (`az_beslenme`) and inability to drink or breastfeed (`icemez_ememez`) are different thresholds, both asked ungated;
   - the metabolic and genetic packs' combined vomiting-or-not-feeding screens stay as their own items.
2. **An umbrella that names a cluster item drops what the cluster asks.**
   - The infectious-diseases and internal-medicine chronic-disease umbrellas become the same "another chronic disease" item CD-100 T37 gave family medicine.
   - The cardiology umbrella drops hypertension.
   - The medical-ecology heart-or-lung item excludes asthma and COPD.

   A combined branch item keeps only its other half: weight gain (endocrinology), persistent throat pain (ENT), effortful breathing without grunting (neonatology), and onset after a burn or an operation (plastic surgery).
3. **The cluster's verified text wins where it is the better form of the same symptom.**
   - The branch adopts it under the cluster id: vision change, night sweats, spinning, recent fever in physical medicine, and the function item per CD-17.
   - Or the branch drops a duplicate: ear blockage, and food sticking for the dietitian.
4. **Rows already decided by CD-102 or CD-104 are decided the same way here.** These are the göz, iç hastalıkları Q5, KBB Q5 and Q7, klinik psikoloji Q8 and odyoloji pairs.
   - A specialty gate may read only base slots and the pack's own earlier slots (`readable = FIXED_SLOTS | base_slots | set(sorular)`).
   - So the klinik psikoloji gate is made readable by re-declaring the cluster's support item in that pack.

Further outcomes:

- Site-specific surgical-history items stay ungated.
- The generator's fixed duration slot against base `onset_date` is an owner question.
- Renamed slots take a new id whenever the old id names the part removed. Generated `soru_N` ids after a removed item are pinned, so no other id moves.

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix each construct. The Turkish wording, the choice to narrow rather than drop, the new ids and the pins are this author's representation decisions. Every new Turkish string is ≤ B1, uses `siz`, has one question mark and no digits, and names no diagnosis to the patient.

NICE NG127 and NG222 cover adults. NG143, CG84 and the IMCI chart cover children under five; the IMCI chart covers two months up to five years, plus the young infant up to two months. They are cited for constructs, never for thresholds asked of the patient.
