# CD-14 — Source anchoring for V-HM-1, V-NF-2 and V-FTVR-4

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

HB-C1 source anchoring for three rows:

- **V-HM-1.** Hematoloji red-flag row 4 (`rf.hematoloji_b_belirtileri`) is anchored on a lymphoma staging definition and a CLL treatment-start criterion. What referral source should anchor it?
- **V-NF-2.** Nefroloji row 4 (`rf.nefroloji_sislik_kilo_kopuk`) rests on a lay symptom list with no action. Does any source state an action for the combination?
- **V-FTVR-4.** Fiziksel tıp Q15a and Q15b cite NICE NG59 through an NHS commissioning policy. What are the number and wording of the spinal decompression recommendation in the primary text?

## Decision

### V-HM-1 (haematology)

Row 4 is split into two arms, and neither is anchored on H-S3.

**Weight-loss arm.** Anchored on NICE NG12 1.13.2, a 2026 recommendation. People aged 60 and over with unexplained weight loss of more than 5% within six months get urgent investigation, a suspected cancer pathway referral or a non-specific symptoms pathway referral. The arm belongs to the universal base family `rf.weight_loss_unintentional` (CD-13), not to the hematoloji stub. How the percentage is captured stays draft_needs_source, because no item can capture it without free numeric entry.

**Night-sweats arm.** Anchored on the Scottish Referral Guidelines for Suspected Cancer 2025: an adult with unexplained drenching night sweats is considered for urgent clinical assessment including a full blood count. The arm stays in the hematoloji stub. Its trigger is `gece_terlemesi`, asked in its drenching form. No duration threshold is part of the trigger, because the source sets none.

**Row 3.** NG12 lists night sweats only as an associated symptom of lymphadenopathy. That combination belongs to row 3, which gains 1.10.6 beside 1.10.8.

**Sources not used as anchors:**

- H-S3 stays as provenance for Q10a and Q11a only.
- The verifier's suggested H-S4 anchor is the January 2019 Scottish guide, which the 2025 guidelines replaced. It is not used.

"Unexplained" is a clinician's finding in NG12's own definition. The pre-visit records the report and never asserts it.

### V-NF-2 (nephrology)

Stays draft_needs_source. No retrieved current source states an action for swelling with weight gain and foamy urine:

- NICE NG203 1.5.5 refers on laboratory values (ACR).
- The NIDDK page is a lay description without an action.
- The NICE CKS nephrotic-syndrome page returned HTTP 403 and was not bypassed.

Row 4 stays a candidate that is not compiled. The three items stay symptom capture for the note.

### V-FTVR-4 (physical medicine and rehabilitation)

Confirmed: the recommendation is NICE NG59 1.3.8, verbatim on nice.org.uk. NG59 was published 30 November 2016 and last updated 29 July 2026; 1.3.8 is marked [2016], and the July 2026 update did not touch it. Q15a and Q15b now cite the primary text, and the commissioning-policy reproduction (S5) is retired as their source. NG59 is never cited for cauda equina (files/04 §8.2).

Status: `active`, with V-NF-2 and the capture and route parts of V-HM-1 draft_needs_source. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-14.yaml`.
