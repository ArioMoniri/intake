# CD-07 — Confirmation ceiling and what a confirmation may change

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-07.yaml`. Status: **draft_needs_source** (one value open; every other value active). Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

How many confirmation steps may one intake session contain; what wins when confirmations and safety questions compete for the turn budget; and what may a patient's "evet" to a confirmation, a backchannel, or "bilmiyorum" change?

Origin: INT-428/INT-430 strict medical review (2 Oct 2026), required design change 9 (confirmation fatigue) and physician decision 6.

## Decision

1. **The cap.** Confirmations are capped per session. The number stays draft_needs_source (see the last section).
2. **Safety questions win over the turn budget.** These are never cut for budget and do not count toward the confirmation cap:
   - steps of priority class `SAFETY_SCRIPT`, `GATEKEEPER` and `RF_PROBE_ARMED` (files/13 §9.1);
   - any step that fills a CD-02 `rule_gate_slots` or `tense_anchors` slot, whatever its class. Today the engine mints only `REENTRY`, `MUST_ASK` and `DETAIL` (`plan.py` `StepPriority`), so the seven CD-01 chest-pain slots arrive as `MUST_ASK` and their follow-ups as `REENTRY`; a class list alone would miss them;
   - the positive re-ask of a red-flag question.

   When the budget runs out, the budget yields, not the safety set.
3. **"evet" to a confirmation never clears a red flag.** A red-flag or gate slot reaches `ASKED_ABSENT` only through a direct answer to its positively phrased scripted question. Where a narrative suggests absence, the engine re-asks the positive question ("...var mı?") rather than asking the patient to confirm an absence ("...yok, doğru mu?"). An acquiescent "evet" is treated as doubt, and doubt resolves towards the red flag.
4. **A backchannel is not a confirmation.** A listener token ("hı hı", "tamam", or "evet" spoken during system speech), or any token that is not a reply in a confirmation turn, records nothing.
5. **"bilmiyorum" maps to `UNCLEAR`**, never to `ABSENT`. The openEHR screening model keeps Unknown as a value separate from No.
6. **Document facts are confirmed on the review screen** (`[visitId]/review`, "Anlattıklarınız"), not in the voice flow.
7. **Nothing is silently accepted.** A non-safety confirmation the runtime does not ask, including any beyond the cap, is shown unconfirmed on the review screen.

While the cap stays draft, no safety question is suppressed (point 2 does not depend on the number) and point 7 holds.

**Inference disclosure.** Three keys are this author's safe-direction design choices rather than quotations of a source:

- `backchannel_is_confirmation: false`. No retrieved guideline defines a confirmation turn. Treating a listener token as no answer can only cost a repeated question; treating it as a confirmation could record a value the patient never gave. The spec basis records that backchannels are cooperative listener interruptions (files/13 §4).
- `rf_positive_reask_counts_toward_cap: false`. The re-ask is how point 3 works. Counting it against a cap would let the cap suppress a red-flag question; keeping it out can only add turns.
- `safety_question_slots`. Extending the exempt set by slot rather than by class is this author's reading of the CD-02 list against the classes the engine mints today. It widens the exemption and never narrows it.

## Near-miss tests that pin it

The lead writes the code; each test has a positive case and a near-miss negative control.

| test | positive case | near-miss negative control |
|---|---|---|
| `no_confirm_yes_clears_rf` | A narrative implies a red-flag symptom is absent; a confirmation of absence answered "evet" leaves the slot out of `ASKED_ABSENT` and queues the positive scripted question | A direct "hayır" to the positive scripted question sets `ASKED_ABSENT`; and an "evet" to a non-safety confirmation (for example the medication list) is accepted |
| `backchannel_not_confirmation` | "hı hı" or "evet" spoken while the confirmation prompt is still playing records no confirmation | "evet" given in the confirmation turn, after the prompt ends, records the confirmation |
| `bilmiyorum_is_unclear` | "bilmiyorum" to a red-flag question sets `UNCLEAR` | "yok" to the same question sets `ASKED_ABSENT` |
| `budget_yields_not_safety_set` | With no turns left and an armed red-flag probe, the probe is still asked and `BudgetExceeded` is emitted | Under the same budget, a `DETAIL` step is suppressed |
| `chest_pain_slots_exempt_from_budget` | With no turns left, a step filling a CD-02 slot is still asked even when it was minted `DETAIL` (and so is its `REENTRY` follow-up) | Under the same budget, a `DETAIL` step whose slot is in neither CD-02 list is suppressed |
| `rf_reask_not_counted_in_cap` | A positive red-flag re-ask leaves the confirmation counter unchanged | A non-safety confirmation increments it |
| `document_fact_confirmed_on_review_screen` | A fact read from an uploaded document creates no voice confirmation step and appears on the review screen | A medication the patient spoke still gets its voice confirmation |
| `no_silent_acceptance_over_cap` | With the test's cap reached, the next non-safety confirmation is not asked and the item is shown unconfirmed on the review screen | Below the cap, the same confirmation is asked |

The cap in the last test is a test-fixture setting that exercises the mechanism; it is not a clinical value.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

- **`max_confirmations_per_session`.** No current guideline, regulation or openEHR archetype states a maximum number of confirmation steps for a patient-completed intake, in Turkish or otherwise. The cap would share the fast-mode turn budget, and that budget's reconciliation is itself an open clinician decision (HB-C5: 16–21 turns of content against an 18-turn budget). Any number written now would be invented, so the YAML carries the required draft literal and the status `draft_needs_source`.
- Everything else in this decision is active.
