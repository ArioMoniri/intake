# CD-112 — Review-queue cluster gate literals: self-report, immunosuppression and pregnancy gates

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-112.yaml`. Status: **active**. Issue: INT-519 (GitHub #885), items 1 to 3.

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient. The three clusters are review-queue L0 packs. They are not servable, so no patient meets any of these gates before HB-C1 is signed.

This decision creates, changes or removes no triage level, route, register, location or red-flag rule. No rule reads any of the gated items.

## Question

INT-441 (GitHub #750) found three gates in the review-queue cluster packs that compare a base code slot against a string no authored answer holds:

| pack · gated slot | gate today | what it does today |
|---|---|---|
| `cluster.mental_health` · `diger_madde_kullanimi`, `baskalarinin_duymadigi_sesler` | `subject_role == 'kendim'` | never opens |
| `cluster.medical` · `bagisiklik_tedavisinde_enfeksiyon` | `immunosuppression != 'hayir'` | opens after every answer, a plain "hayır" included |
| `cluster.womens_health` · `gebelik_dogrulandi` (gate for Q3 to Q9) | `pregnancy_status != 'hayir'` | opens after every answer, a plain "hayır" included |

For each gate:

- Which authored literal says what the gate is for?
- Which way does the change move who is asked?
- How is a hedged answer to the gating slot recorded, so that the gate does not read Unknown?

The INT-441 clinical reviewer's recommendations were inputs to this decision, not decisions.

## Decision

| item | gate decided | opens on | closes on | applies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `subject_role == 'kendim için'` (self-report only) | "kendim için" | "yakınım adına" | only once ADR-040 (PR #833) has landed; then carried as `proxy: {skip: true}` with the gate dropped |
| 2 | `immunosuppression != 'hayır'` | "evet", "bilmiyorum" | "hayır" | now |
| 3 | `pregnancy_status != 'hayır'` (and CD-100 T4(b)'s five items) | "evet", "emin değilim" | "hayır" | now |

**Item 1. The substance-use and hearing-voices items are self-report history.**

- They are asked only when the patient answers for themselves.
- They are never put, as worded, to a relative in a proxy session. The second-person question would file the speaker's own substance use or experiences in the patient's record.
- NICE CG178 1.1.4.2 says how information about the service user is shared with carers is negotiated with the service user. A pre-visit app cannot negotiate that.
- The self-report literal is `subject_role == 'kendim için'`, the authored self choice of `vs.subject_role`.
- **The change applies only once ADR-040 (PR #833, INT-483) has landed.** Until then the gate stays as it is. It opens for nobody, and the INT-441 value-set check refuses it before the cluster can be promoted. The hold means the items cannot open before the proxy path exists that guarantees a relative never hears them.
- **Encoding once applied.** ADR-040's proxy compiler refuses a patient-addressed slot whose gate reads the subject role (`PROXY_GATED_ON_SUBJECT_ROLE`). So on landing, both slots take `proxy: {skip: true}` and the subject-role gate is dropped:
  - the self document asks them;
  - the proxy document records them `NOT_ASKED_PROXY` and shows the gap.
- Any subject-role gate kept anywhere for these items reads `'kendim için'`.

**Item 2. The infection item asks about infection during immunosuppressive treatment.**

- Its gate is `immunosuppression != 'hayır'`. It opens on "evet" and on "bilmiyorum". It closes on a plain "hayır", an answer that denies the treatment the question presupposes.
- **Hedge mapping on `pmh_fh_sh.immunosuppression`.** These answers record the authored "bilmiyorum" choice, never UNCLEAR and never "hayır":
  - "bilmiyorum";
  - every CD-04 hedge that reads on any slot, labelled `mapped_from_hedge`;
  - any unmatched hedge, labelled `mapped_from_hedge`;
  - an answer that still fits no choice after the slot's follow-up, labelled `mapped_from_unresolved`.
- Only a plain negative to the question itself (CD-04: "hayır", "yok", "olmadı") records "hayır".
- On this slot the mapping is read before CD-04's `*` entries. CD-100 T4(c) set the same per-slot precedence for `pregnancy_status`.
- Why not UNCLEAR: an UNCLEAR answer makes the gate Unknown, and an Unknown gate opens nothing (files/13 §9.2). A sepsis rule that reads the slot would also read Unknown. Yet:
  - NICE NG253 1.2.1 lists "impaired immune function because of illness or medical treatment" among the factors that may increase the risk of sepsis or of sepsis not being identified promptly;
  - NG253 1.1.4 asks for extra care where the history is hard to take.
- A future rule that reads this slot as a risk factor reads it as `!= 'hayır'`, so "bilmiyorum" counts with "evet". That rule still needs its own source and sign-off (HB-C1).

**Item 3. `gebelik_dogrulandi`, and the five items CD-100 T4(b) moves onto the same gate, open on `pregnancy_status != 'hayır'`.**

- This is CD-100 T4's decided reading, written with the authored literal.
- "evet" and "emin değilim" open them (NICE NG126 1.4.5). A plain "hayır" closes them.
- Hedges follow CD-100 T4(c): they record "emin değilim".
- The five items are `gebelikte_kanama`, `gebelikte_siddetli_bas_agrisi`, `gebelikte_gorme_bozuklugu`, `bebek_hareketlerinde_azalma` and `gebelikte_su_gelmesi`.

### Safety direction

- **Items 2 and 3 ask fewer patients than the gates do today.** `'hayir'` matches no answer, so today these gates also open after a plain "hayır".
  - The patients no longer asked are exactly those whose answer denies the question's premise.
  - No rule reads either gated item.
  - For a woman who answers "hayır" but may be pregnant, the safety net is the last menstrual period. Base asks it after every pregnancy answer (CD-100 round 4).
  - Against the intent recorded when the gates were written (the proposals and CD-100 T4), nothing is narrowed.
- **Item 2's hedge mapping widens.** A hedge opens the item, where UNCLEAR would close it.
- **Item 1 asks more patients than today once it applies.** The items never open today. It never asks a relative.

## Inference disclosure

- **Item 1.**
  - That a relative who hears a second-person question answers about themselves is ADR-040's structural reading. The ADR is on PR #833 and not yet on main. It is not a clinical source.
  - NICE CG178 covers adults with psychosis or schizophrenia. It is cited for the direction of information sharing with carers.
  - Skipping both items in a proxy session, rather than rewording them for the relative, is this author's conservative choice. Collateral history of substance use or hallucinatory experiences belongs to the clinician.
  - Holding the change until #833 lands is sequencing, not a clinical source.
- **Item 2.**
  - Recording every hedge on immunosuppression as "bilmiyorum" is this author's operationalisation, as CD-100 T4(c) is for pregnancy_status.
  - Extending it to an answer still unresolved after the follow-up is also this author's. The patient has not said yes or no, "bilmiyorum" is the authored record of exactly that, and the label keeps the record honest about how it got there.
  - NG253 covers people aged 16 or over. In a child chain the mapping keeps the same direction on the strength of the CKM presence value set alone (Unknown is not No). NICE NG254 (under 16s) was not retrieved, and nothing here extrapolates an adult threshold to a child.
- **Item 3.** As CD-100 T4.
