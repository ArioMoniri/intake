# CD-21 — acknowledgements (rows 14-20)

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026, INT-432). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

INT-414 (#726) adds conversational delivery lines around each question. Do the acknowledgements (thanks, 'don't know' and correction) pass PolicyGuard (no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising), stay B1 plain Turkish in the siz register, ask nothing, carry no numeral, and fit the moment they are said?

## Decision

All seven pass unchanged and stay within the five-word acknowledgement bound (files/03 §4.2). The thanks judge nothing about the answer. The 'don't know' lines accept not knowing, not remembering, not being sure or preferring not to say as an answer, with no pressure and no reassurance, and they do not minimise what was not said. The correction lines credit the patient and never imply they were wrong. Each line below is its own entry with its six checks, the author's verdict and the verifier's verdict; the Turkish is byte-equal to the catalogue key it names.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-21.yaml`.

### What each check means

- **PolicyGuard**: PolicyGuard.filter(text, scripted=False) on the dynamic path: no violation, nothing dropped, no bridge; and by reading, no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising.
- **B1 plain Turkish**: at most two sentences of at most fifteen words, everyday words, no medical jargon (Sağlık Bakanlığı plain-language aim).
- **siz register**: siz throughout; no sen; no doubled-polite imperative; warmth from -elim/-alım and -ebilirsiniz.
- **not a question**: no '?', no mı/mi/mu/mü, no interrogative opening a listener hears as a question.
- **no numerals**: no digit in tr or en (invariant 22).
- **fits its context**: true to what the product does at that moment, and right for the slot, the delivery context and the lane.
