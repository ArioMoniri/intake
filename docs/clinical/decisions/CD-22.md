# CD-22 — instrument and sensitive framing (rows 27-32, 39-40)

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026, INT-432). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

INT-414 (#726) adds conversational delivery lines around each question. Do the instrument introductions, the sensitive-section transitions and cadence lines, and the family-history transitions pass PolicyGuard (no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising), stay B1 plain Turkish in the siz register, ask nothing, carry no numeral, and fit the moment they are said?

## Decision

Seven pass unchanged and one is reworded. sensitiveLead.b opened with the interrogative word 'Nasıl', which only the conditional '-sanız' resolves at the third word, so it began like a question right before the turn's one real question. It becomes 'Size rahat gelen şekilde yanıtlayabilirsiniz.', with the same meaning, the same register and no promise about skipping. 'Anket' is kept over 'ölçek' in the instrument lines because it is the everyday word. These lines are transitions: they are not the files/04 §4.2 pre-brief required before the suicide-pathway items, which is owed separately and is not discharged here. The new line is in the catalogue, the server allow-list and the HB-C1 table on #726 (INT-414). Each line below is its own entry with its six checks, the author's verdict and the verifier's verdict; the Turkish is byte-equal to the catalogue key it names.

Status: `active`. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-22.yaml`.

### What each check means

- **PolicyGuard**: PolicyGuard.filter(text, scripted=False) on the dynamic path: no violation, nothing dropped, no bridge; and by reading, no diagnosis, treatment, prognosis, reassurance or minimising.
- **B1 plain Turkish**: at most two sentences of at most fifteen words, everyday words, no medical jargon (Sağlık Bakanlığı plain-language aim).
- **siz register**: siz throughout; no sen; no doubled-polite imperative; warmth from -elim/-alım and -ebilirsiniz.
- **not a question**: no '?', no mı/mi/mu/mü, no interrogative opening a listener hears as a question.
- **no numerals**: no digit in tr or en (invariant 22).
- **fits its context**: true to what the product does at that moment, and right for the slot, the delivery context and the lane.

### The lines

Every line passed all six checks. *Author* is the medical-author pass and *Verifier* is the independent pass.
