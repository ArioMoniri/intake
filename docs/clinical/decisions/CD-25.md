# CD-25 — Chest-pain RED why lines: the action and the service, never the condition

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-25.yaml`. Status: **active**.

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient. **HB-C12b stays open** for the physician of record.

## Question

The two `why_tr` lines of `rf.chest_pain_cardiac_features` (files/protocols/base.life_threat.yaml) say *"Anlattığınız belirtiler kalple ilgili olabilir"*, which tells the patient something about their own condition. That is diagnosis language: HB-C12b, CLAUDE.md invariant 2, and the pack's own rule that `why_tr` is never a diagnosis. The REMOTE line also uses *"triyaj"*, an unexplained term above B1. What do the two lines say instead, for simulated-patient testing on the synthetic host?

Origin: the INT-465 clinical review (PR #796), blocker 1.

## Decision

Both lines become the service-fact why lines that CD-24 decided, and had independently verified, for the generic RED, sentence for sentence. They hold for any KIRMIZI rule of either EK-4 category, and they name no condition.

| location | why_tr |
|---|---|
| REMOTE | Verilen cevaplara göre beklemeden değerlendirme gerekiyor. 112 ambulansıyla getirilen hastalar, acil serviste ayrıca öncelik sırası için bekletilmez. |
| ON_SITE | Verilen cevaplara göre beklemeden değerlendirme gerekiyor. Acil serviste hastaların önceliği girişte belirlenir ve en öncelikli hastalar bekletilmeden içeri alınır. |

- **First sentence (both).** It states the KIRMIZI outcome as a need for assessment without waiting. EK-4 Kategori 2, this rule's level, takes the patient for assessment and treatment within ten minutes. No time figure is given to the patient: it is a service target, not an instruction, and a numeral would also have to be expanded for speech (invariant 22).
- **REMOTE, second sentence.** This keeps the old line's MADDE 8(5) service fact. *"triyaj"* becomes *"öncelik sırası"*, using the Tebliğ's own definition in MADDE 4(1)(p).
- **ON_SITE, second sentence.** This keeps the old line's door-triage service fact (MADDE 8(3)), with EK-4 Kategori 1 for *"bekletilmeden"*. It does not reuse the ambulance line, because the patient is not travelling.
- **Removed with the condition sentence:** *"durumunuz"* (your condition) and *"Bu belirtilerde"* (with these symptoms).
- **Unchanged:** the gate, level, route, register, `action_tr`, `do_nots_tr` and the UNKNOWN question.
