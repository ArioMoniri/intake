## Question

Which current source states a layperson-answerable stroke trigger and its urgency?

## Decision

Write rf.stroke_fast as KIRMIZI_K2 / EMERGENCY_NOW / ALERT on one probe: face droop, arm weakness or speech difficulty that began suddenly and is still present (NICE NG128 1.1.1, FAST, in people with sudden onset of neurological symptoms). EK-4 prints 'Akut hemiparazi/disfazi' in Kırmızı Kategori 2. The probe opens a dated onset slot, rf_stroke_fast_onset, as the tense anchor, because the HB-C6 slot time_last_known_well is a duration and the engine cannot anchor on it. Tense ever: the probe carries 'şu anda sürüyor', so the anchor only proves the symptom is dated. A resolved deficit is NG128 1.1.5's suspected TIA, to be seen within twenty-four hours, which is a different action and is not written. Vision loss is not in FAST or EK-4's line.

## Sources (verbatim in the committed snapshots)

- NICE-NG128 (1.1.1): "Use a validated tool, such as FAST (Face Arm Speech Test), outside hospital to screen people with sudden onset of neurological symptoms for a diagnosis of stroke or transient ischaemic attack (TIA)." — supports `trigger`
- NICE-NG128 (1.1.5): "Refer immediately people who have had a suspected TIA for specialist assessment and investigation, to be seen within 24 hours of onset of symptoms." — supports `tia_arm`
- TR-EK4-2022 (EK-4 Kırmızı Kategori 2): "Akut hemiparazi/disfazi" — supports `level`
