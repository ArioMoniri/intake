# CD-15 — Neurosurgery pre-visit scope: known cancer, an existing brain shunt and a first seizure

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

V-BVSC-6: should the beyin ve sinir cerrahisi pre-visit ask about a known cancer, an existing brain shunt and a first-ever seizure?

## Decision

### Known cancer: yes

NICE NG234 makes a past or current diagnosis of cancer the first factor in recognising spinal metastases or metastatic spinal cord compression (MSCC). It tells clinicians to contact the MSCC coordinator:

- immediately, as an oncological emergency, when such a person has cord-compression symptoms;
- within 24 hours when they have spinal pain with the listed features.

Without the cancer fact, red-flag row 6 has no input. The item reuses cerrahi onkoloji's id and wording (`onceki_kanser_tanisi`, "Daha önce size kanser tanısı konuldu mu?"), ungated, which also gives one audio clip. The rule it feeds is placed in `base.life_threat` (CD-13), so that rule needs a base-level cancer slot. Until then the neurosurgery item is its gate input.

### First seizure: yes

NICE NG217 1.1.1 refers adults urgently, for an appointment within two weeks, after a first suspected seizure. The item is a clinician-only note item, never a RED rule:

- an ongoing or prolonged seizure is a separate emergency pattern;
- a seizure after a head injury is NG232's own emergency-ambulance criterion (CD-12).

The item is ungated. Its lay follow-up describes a seizure without naming a diagnosis, and it keeps "first" and "recently", so a person with known epilepsy or an old faint does not answer yes to it.

### Existing brain shunt: stays draft_needs_source

No retrieved current guideline says what a pre-visit should ask a person with a cerebrospinal fluid shunt, or which shunt symptoms need which action.

One sourced part is recorded. NICE NG232 lists "any previous brain surgery" as a head-injury referral risk factor, and Q14 already records brain or spine surgery, which includes a shunt insertion. Because Q14 mixes brain and spine surgery, it cannot feed that head-injury factor as it stands. Splitting it belongs to the HB-C2 author together with the base head-injury rule. No shunt item is added.

Status: `active`. The shunt question and the first-seizure route stay draft_needs_source. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-15.yaml`.
