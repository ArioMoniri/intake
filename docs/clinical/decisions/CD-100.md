## Question

For each row: which slot stays, which is dropped or narrowed, with what Turkish text, or which part belongs to the owner?

## Decision

Each row gets one outcome:

- **accept**: the twin is real, but both slots stay unchanged under their own ids, because each records a fact the other does not (the §C "keep under its own id" path).
- **change**: apply the exact slot, text or gate change given in the row. The lead edits the proposal (`docs/evidence/proposals/<slug>.md`) and regenerates the candidate.
- **owner_question**: every decider is the owner or the base owner. D18 does not reach these, so the row is recorded and not decided.

**Count: 10 accept, 32 change, 8 owner_question, 0 reject, 0 draft_needs_source.**

Four rules decide most rows.

1. **A base must_ask slot is the id of record.** A base must_ask slot cannot be suppressed, so a branch item has two paths.
   - **Removed.** A branch item that re-asks it word for word is removed. This covers course since onset, complaint location and what was tried.
   - **Narrowed.** A branch item whose extra half is clinically distinct keeps only that half:
     - The base allergy item is one record for every substance class (openEHR `adverse_reaction_risk`). The dental, anaesthesia and dermatology items keep only the procedure-relevant substances.
     - Drug intolerance is a separate record from drug allergy (NICE CG183 1.2.5). The diabetes, endocrinology and internal-medicine items keep only the non-allergic reaction.
     - The anticoagulant half of a medicine item goes to base `anticoagulant_use`.
2. **A branch umbrella that names a cluster item drops what the cluster asks.**
   - The family-medicine chronic-disease umbrella asks only for other diseases.
   - The pain-clinic impact item keeps work and sleep (NICE NG193 1.1.8).
   - The AMATEM item keeps health harm (NICE CG115 1.2.2.6).
3. **Child hydration and feeding items follow CD-10's `true_is_concern`.**
   - The urine rows are CD-10's change, already applied on this branch.
   - The feeding construct CD-10 left open is decided here. One shared `az_beslenme` ask covers feeding or drinking less than usual (NICE NG143 "poor feeding in infants").
   - Only after a yes, a stopped-completely follow-up asks the IMCI general danger sign (children aged two months up to five years).
   - An answer that the child takes nothing at all ("hiç içmiyor", "hiçbir şey almıyor", "emmiyor") is `az_beslenme = evet`, never "hayır", even when it starts with "hayır". The follow-up is then asked.
   - Under two months, IMCI's young-infant chart lists "not feeding well" as a very-severe-disease sign. A yes to `az_beslenme` already records it.
4. **Kept pairs are distinct constructs on a source:**
   - unexplained fever against recent fever (NG12 1.10.1);
   - vaccine-component allergy, and acute illness on the day of vaccination (Green Book chapter 6);
   - the child allergen list, while base is self-addressed;
   - food intolerance for the dietitian;
   - any vomiting against vomiting everything;
   - a new lump anywhere against a lump in the complaint area;
   - faltering weight against growth concern;
   - prior substance-use treatment against prior mental-health support;
   - breathing effort against chest indrawing.

In women's health, the LMP and the pregnancy gate are each asked once, under the base id.

- The pregnancy-gated items open on `pregnancy_status != 'hayir'`, so "emin değilim" now opens them (NICE NG126 1.4.5).
- On `pregnancy_status`, "bilmiyorum" and every hedge are recorded as the "emin değilim" choice, never UNCLEAR and never "hayır". This is a per-slot entry beside CD-04's `*` rows. An UNCLEAR would make the gate Unknown, and an Unknown gate opens nothing (files/13 §9.2), so an unsure woman would never hear the five items.
- This asks more patients, never fewer.
- The removed slots (`son_adet_tarihi`, `gebelik_durumu`) also leave the order list of the candidate's INT-353 reorder op (ops[0] at `151cf34f`), wherever the candidate carries that op. It is not in this branch's candidate.
- The base-side mechanics of the LMP and the pregnancy gate are owner parts.

**Ids.** A narrowed slot takes a new id whenever its old id names the part removed. A stale reader then fails at compile time instead of misreading. Generated `soru_N` ids are positional (`sid = q.slot or f"soru_{i}"`), so the ids after a removed item are pinned with explicit `slot=` names, and no other id moves.
