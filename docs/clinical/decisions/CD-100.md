## Decision

Each row gets one outcome:

- **accept**: the twin is real, but both slots stay unchanged under their own ids, because each records a fact the other does not (the §C "keep under its own id" path).
- **change**: apply the exact slot, text or gate change given in the row. The lead edits the proposal (`docs/evidence/proposals/<slug>.md`) and regenerates the candidate.
- **owner_question**: every decider is the owner or the base owner. D18 does not reach these, so the row is recorded and not decided.

**Count: 10 accept, 32 change, 8 owner_question, 0 reject, 0 draft_needs_source.**

Four rules decide most rows.

1. **A base must_ask slot is the id of record.** A base must_ask slot cannot be suppressed, so a branch item has two paths.
   - **Removed.** A branch item that re-asks it word for word is removed. This covers course since onset, complaint location and what was tried.
   - **Narrowed.** A branch item whose extra half is clinically distinct keeps only that half:
     - The base allergy item is one record for every substance class (openEHR `adverse_reaction_risk`). The dental, anaesthesia and dermatology items keep only the procedure-relevant substances.
     - Drug intolerance is a separate record from drug allergy (NICE CG183 1.2.5). The diabetes, endocrinology and internal-medicine items keep only the non-allergic reaction.
     - The anticoagulant half of a medicine item goes to base `anticoagulant_use`.
2. **A branch umbrella that names a cluster item drops what the cluster asks.**
   - The family-medicine chronic-disease umbrella asks only for other diseases.
   - The pain-clinic impact item keeps work and sleep (NICE NG193 1.1.8).
   - The AMATEM item keeps health harm (NICE CG115 1.2.2.6).
3. **Child hydration and feeding items follow CD-10's `true_is_concern`.**
   - The urine rows are CD-10's change, already applied on this branch.
   - The feeding construct CD-10 left open is decided here. One shared `az_beslenme` ask covers feeding or drinking less than usual (NICE NG143 "poor feeding in infants").
   - CD-103's `icemez_ememez` ("Çocuğunuz hiçbir şey içemez ya da ememez hâle geldi mi?") asks the IMCI general danger sign directly and ungated, beside `az_beslenme`. Under the verifier's ruling they are different thresholds, not twins, so both are asked. The round-1 `beslenmeyi_birakma` follow-up is withdrawn and superseded by `icemez_ememez`.
   - An answer that the child takes nothing at all ("hiç içmiyor", "hiçbir şey almıyor", "emmiyor") is `az_beslenme = evet`, never "hayır", even when it starts with "hayır".
   - Under two months, IMCI's young-infant chart lists "not feeding well" as a very-severe-disease sign. A yes to `az_beslenme` already records it.
4. **Kept pairs are distinct constructs on a source:**
   - unexplained fever against recent fever (NG12 1.10.1);
   - vaccine-component allergy, and acute illness on the day of vaccination (Green Book chapter 6);
   - the child allergen list, while base is self-addressed;
   - food intolerance for the dietitian;
   - any vomiting against vomiting everything;
   - a new lump anywhere against a lump in the complaint area;
   - faltering weight against growth concern;
   - prior substance-use treatment against prior mental-health support;
   - breathing effort against chest indrawing.

In women's health, the LMP and the pregnancy gate are each asked once, under the base id.

**T1, owner part, re-read on the corrected premise (INT-519 item 4).**

- **Premise corrected.** T1's owner part assumed base `lmp_date` carried `pregnancy_status != 'hayir'` and so closed on a plain "hayır". It never closed. `'hayir'` matched no answer, and since INT-441 the gate names the three authored answers. Base asks the last menstrual period after every answer to `pregnancy_status`, in every chain.
- **What is already met.** The base step T1 asked for is met for every patient who answers `pregnancy_status`. The three-answer gate stays. It is never narrowed to `!= 'hayır'`: a woman who answers "hayır" may still be pregnant (NG126 1.4.5), and a missed period is a common ectopic symptom (NG126 1.4.3).
- **The remaining gap.** A `pregnancy_status` that is unanswered, declined or UNCLEAR, where UNCLEAR means an off-choice answer that T4(c)'s hedge mapping does not reach. Every term of the three-answer gate is then Unknown. An Unknown gate opens nothing (files/13 §9.2), so the LMP is not asked.
- **Decision.** CD-110's population guard (PR #804, INT-470) closes the gap. It is re-spelled as an extra OR term on the three-answer gate, never on `!= 'hayır'` or `'hayir'`:

  ```
  pregnancy_status == 'evet' || pregnancy_status == 'hayır' || pregnancy_status == 'emin değilim'
    || (recorded_male_sex_at_birth == false && recorded_age_outside_bounds == false)
  ```

  - The guard decides only when `pregnancy_status` is Unknown. It only ever adds askers, and never removes the LMP after any of the three answers.
  - This keeps what CD-110's gate does as written: `'hayir'` matches no answer, so its first term is true after every answer.
  - It does not carry CD-110's prose reading that a plain "hayır" from a recorded male sex at birth, or from a recorded age outside the bounds, skips the LMP. Under the corrected premise that would ask fewer patients than base asks today, and CD-110 leaves its age bounds draft_needs_source.
  - While those bounds are draft, the age atom is left out, because it can only narrow. The OR term is then `recorded_male_sex_at_birth == false` alone.
  - The atoms are bound as CD-110 says: two-valued, from recorded demographics, false when nothing is recorded, never through the slot lookup.
- **Applies when.** CD-110 has landed and its guard prerequisites exist (the GuardExpr function `sex_at_birth` and the two-valued atom binding). Until then base keeps the three-answer gate, and the gap stays open.
- **kadın Q2.** `son_adet_tarihi` leaves the kadın chain once the OR term is applied. Until then it stays, because it is the only ask that reaches a woman whose `pregnancy_status` is Unknown.
- **Re-spelling.** Whichever of INT-519 and INT-470 merges second re-spells CD-110's `gate_shape` and `opens_on` to this form. It also re-spells CD-110's and this file's quotes to the authored literals.

- **Red-flag gate input.** `lmp_date` gates the pregnancy family (`rf.pregnancy_bleeding_pain`, HB-C6). Applying the OR term therefore runs the goldens, with near-miss controls, and `make safety` in the same change.
