# CD-11 — Göğüs hastalıkları occupational items: D3 coverage and the current-exposure gate

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

Two questions:

1. Does owner decision D3's occupational-exposure approval, delivered `text_first`, cover göğüs hastalıkları Q10 `mesleki_toz_duman_gaz` and Q10a `is_disinda_azalma` (V-GH-2)?
2. Is a current-exposure gate in front of Q10a clinically supported? Q10 asks about any job ever held, while Q10a assumes the patient still works there (V-GH-1).

## Decision

The two parts have different deciders.

### The D3 reading: recommended to the owner, not decided here

V-GH-2 names the owner as decider. D18 delegates clinical decisions only, and CLAUDE.md sends any decision to persist new categories of personal data to the human. This decision therefore records a recommended reading, not a ruling. V-GH-2 stays open with the owner.

The recommended reading is that D3 approves by data category:

- its object is "specialty questions that would collect new sensitive-data categories";
- its approved list is a list of categories;
- its delivery rule binds "each such item".

On that reading, the departments in parentheses record where a category was found on 26 September and do not limit the approval. Q10 and Q10a both collect "employer / occupational exposure", so both would be covered as review-queue candidates.

Until the owner confirms, the items stay `text_first` and not servable. In any case, the four items D3 lists as not decided close before L1:

- the storage register;
- envelope encryption;
- counsel's basis per category;
- the slot data-category tag.

### The current-exposure gate: decided (pulmonology and occupational medicine)

Q10a must not be asked of a patient who no longer does that work. NICE NG245 1.4.1 looks for an occupational component by asking whether symptoms are the same, better or worse on days away from work and on holiday. Both questions presuppose current work, and NG245 asks that all answers be recorded for later review.

The fix-round wording puts a condition inside a yes/no question ("Hâlâ böyle bir işte çalışıyorsanız, …"). A retired patient's answer cannot be read, and their "hayır" looks like "no occupational component" to red-flag row 4.

The fix has two parts:

1. A separate current-exposure gate item.
2. Q10a, reworded without the condition, asked only after a yes to the gate.

A patient who answers no to the gate is never asked Q10a. `is_disinda_azalma` stays unanswered rather than "no".

### Red-flag row 4

- **Exposure arm** (current exposure, and symptoms easing away from work): follows 1.4.1. Active.
- **Action anchor:** NG245 1.4.2 (refer to an occupational asthma specialist). Active.
- **Population arm:** both recommendations concern people with asthma, so this arm stays draft_needs_source. The candidates are:
  - cluster.medical `astim_koah_tanisi`, which also covers COPD;
  - the pack's own `hisiltili_solunum`, a reported wheeze. NG245 1.1.1 checks for it in suspected asthma, but under 1.1.2 it does not confirm asthma.
- **Turkish referral route:** draft_needs_source.

Status: `active`. The owner question is open; the row-4 population arm and route are draft_needs_source. Machine-readable form: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-11.yaml`.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

- **Row 4's population arm.** NG245 1.4.1 and 1.4.2 apply to people with asthma, and no pack slot captures asthma alone: `astim_koah_tanisi` includes COPD, and a reported wheeze is a symptom, not a diagnosis.
- **Row 4's Turkish referral route.** NG245 1.4.2 names a UK occupational asthma specialist, and no retrieved Turkish regulation or guideline maps that referral or sets its urgency.

The D3 reading is not a draft value but an open owner question (V-GH-2). Independently of it, the items are not servable until the four D3 "not decided" items close (INT-349).
