# CD-09 — Extraction-sensitivity floor and the golden set for lane and model swaps

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-09.yaml`. Status: **draft_needs_source** (the golden-set definition and both gates are active; the floor, the margin and the labelling scheme are open). Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

A floor in this decision is a release gate. It is never a statement about the product (invariant 20). Measured values are recorded in `docs/evidence/` with a date and a named corpus.

## Question

What per-rule extraction sensitivity must a lane or model swap reach before it may carry red-flag extraction, by what non-inferiority margin may it trail the lane it replaces on red-flag false negatives, and what golden set must that be measured on?

Origin: INT-428/INT-430 strict medical review (2 Oct 2026), required design change 12 and physician decision 8; INT-428/INT-430 AI/ML review, must-have evals.

## Decision

1. **Two gates on every lane or model swap.**
   - **Regression gate (every deployment).** One true positive and one near-miss negative per red-flag rule, the Turkish negation adversarial set, and the RED goldens with the council on and off (identical results) must all pass. They are run for the exact provider, model@revision, prompt and decoding parameters being swapped in. A failure stops or reverts the swap.
   - **Evidence gate (PHI path only).** Per-rule extraction sensitivity, measured on the clinician-labelled real-speech golden set, must meet the sensitivity floor, and red-flag false negatives must be non-inferior to the current lane within the margin. Both are judged on a per-rule minimum labelled sample with a stated acceptance statistic, so a floor can never be met on the single true positive per rule that the regression gate requires (GMLP 2021 principles 3 and 8: a sample of adequate size, statistically sound test plans).
2. **The golden set.**
   - Transcripts produced by the pinned ASR from real Turkish clinical speech of the intended population.
   - Each red-flag slot labelled by clinicians as `PRESENT`, `ABSENT` or `UNCLEAR`; that labelling is the reference standard.
   - At least one true positive and one near-miss negative per rule, the files/04 §8.2 negation set, and the RED council-on/off set.
   - Never used to tune a prompt or a model.
   - Every result bound to provider, model@revision, prompt hash and decoding parameters.
3. **Labelling works on transcripts.** Audio is used only from a consented research corpus under its own ethics route (HB-C10). Production audio is never retained (invariant 3), so it is never a labelling source.
4. **Synthetic-patient runs and hosted-lane runs never count as files/04 §8.3 evidence.** They may only serve the regression gate.
5. **Until the floor and margin are set, and until the real-speech golden set exists, no PHI-path swap can pass.** The synthetic deployment may still swap lanes for stress testing on the regression gate alone, and those runs are tagged synthetic.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

- **`sensitivity_floor`** and **`non_inferiority_margin`.** No current guideline or regulation, Turkish or international, sets a numeric extraction-sensitivity floor or a non-inferiority margin for a symptom-extraction component that feeds a deterministic triage engine. The FDA, Health Canada and MHRA principles (GMLP 2021 principle 6; PCCP 2023 principle 3) place the derivation of performance goals and acceptance criteria on the manufacturer's clinical benefit-risk analysis, which is a clinical-owner decision.
- **`swap_gates.evidence_gate.per_rule_minimum_labelled_cases_and_acceptance_statistic`** (how many labelled cases each rule needs, and which statistic decides that the floor and the margin are met). GMLP asks for a sample of adequate size and a statistically sound test plan but gives no size or statistic.
- **`golden_set.labelling.labellers_and_adjudication`** (how many clinicians label each item, and how disagreements are settled). No source sets it.
- The harmonised successor text (IMDRF/AIML WG/N88 FINAL:2025) did not respond from this host at retrieval and is not cited.
- Any number written here would be invented, so the YAML carries the required draft literals.
- The golden-set definition, both gates and the evidence rules are active.
