# CD-03 — Narrative PRESENT arms the scripted probe; it does not fire RED

Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

When the open narrative, the council or a document indicates that a red-flag feature is present, does that fire the RED rule directly, or does it only arm the class-4 scripted probe (StepPriority.RF_PROBE_ARMED)? What does a narrative denial yield?

## Decision

A narrative, council or document reading of PRESENT or UNCLEAR on a rule-gate slot arms the class-4 probe and does not fire RED. The scripted question for that slot is asked at StepPriority.RF_PROBE_ARMED (class 4) at the first turn boundary with no class 0-3 step pending (SAFETY_SCRIPT, GATEKEEPER, OPEN_NARRATIVE, REENTRY), ahead of MUST_ASK and DETAIL; its answer is rendered on screen, and the rule fires only from the directly answered slot. NICE CG95 1.2.1.1 asks for this immediate direct check, and CLAUDE.md invariant 1 already bars council output from producing RED. A narrative denial never yields ASKED_ABSENT: the slot stays NOT_ASKED (rendered 'Sorulmadı') and the scripted question is still asked. The CKM history archetype records positive presence and sends explicit absence to a separate exclusion archetype, so this is the author's conservative choice rather than an openEHR rule (inference_disclosure), resting on the repository's rule that an unanswered slot is never an elicited negative. Arming does nothing for a slot already ASKED_ABSENT, because the frontier only picks NOT_ASKED or UNCLEAR steps; a red-flag value volunteered after its slot closed is the open blocker HB-C21 and is not decided here. Scope: patient-answerable rule slots. A rule whose gate is itself a deterministic transcript predicate (rf.crisis_disclosure_signposting, draft) is outside this decision. An armed probe that is never answered because the session ends is a clinician item; CD-08 (physician decision 7) names its recipient role and channel, and its deadline stays draft there.
