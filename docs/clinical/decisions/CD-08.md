# CD-08 — YELLOW_REVIEW routing: recipient role, channel, SLA and failure path

Machine-readable record: `packages/protocols/decisions/CD-08.yaml`. Status: **draft_needs_source** (the recipient role and channel are active; the SLA, out-of-hours cover and escalation are open). Scope: simulated-patient stress testing only (owner decision D18, 2 Oct 2026). A licensed physician's review is required before any real patient.

## Question

Who receives a YELLOW_REVIEW (an LLM candidate flag, capped at YELLOW_REVIEW and visible to clinicians only), through which channel, within what response time, what happens out of hours, and what happens when nobody responds?

Origin: INT-428/INT-430 strict medical review (2 Oct 2026), physician decision 7, against files/04 §4.2 defect 1 ("notify" has no recipient, no hours, no SLA and no failure path). Also covered: the same review's late-landing addendum (design change 6) and the unanswered armed probe that sibling decision CD-03 defers to physician decision 7.

## Decision

1. **Channel.** Every YELLOW_REVIEW goes, unconditionally, to the booked clinic's named inbox (`clinic_inbox`, files/04 §4.2).
2. **Recipient: a role, never a person.** Role id `tenant_clinic_triage_trained_clinician`: a clinician of the tenant facility, rostered by the tenant to that inbox, who holds the in-service triage training that Tebliğ MADDE 8(2) requires of anyone performing triage. Eligible titles are taken from the Tebliğ's definition of sağlık personeli (MADDE 4(1)(n)): tabip, hemşire, ebe, acil tıp teknikeri, acil tıp teknisyeni. The non-clinical and assistant titles in that list are excluded. That restriction is this author's tightening, not the regulation's text, and is disclosed as such in the YAML.
3. **The reviewer sets the urgency of any follow-up** by clinical judgement. NICE NG143 1.3.4 leaves the urgency of follow-up for an intermediate-risk finding identified remotely to the assessing healthcare professional.
4. **Not the acil servis triage desk.** Regulatory triage is defined for patients presenting to the acil servis (MADDE 4(1)(p)), and a YELLOW_REVIEW is a clinician-only directive, not an EK-4 level (files/12 §2.3). Routing it to the acil triage desk would give it an acuity it does not have.
5. **Never patient-facing.** A YELLOW_REVIEW is never shown to the patient and never stands in for a patient-facing action. Patient-facing urgency comes only from the rules engine (invariant 1).
6. **Never expires, never auto-clears.** It closes only on a clinician's recorded outcome. If the visit starts first, it is shown in the pre-visit note. A failed delivery to the inbox counts as an SLA breach, an SLA breach is an emitted event, and a YELLOW_REVIEW never lowers or replaces a rules-engine level.
7. **Same inbox, more item kinds.** The same inbox, role and constraints carry the other clinician-only items the INT-432 decisions route here:
   - `late_landing_rf_addendum`: a red-flag-relevant result that lands after the session (medical review design change 6);
   - `armed_probe_unanswered_at_session_end`: a scripted probe armed but left unanswered at session end (sibling decision CD-03, which defers its recipient and deadline to this decision);
   - `chest_pain_arms_unresolved`: a CD-01 chest-pain slot still `UNCLEAR` after its follow-up, or left without an answer when the session closes (CD-04 round 4), which can leave both chest-pain arms Unknown (raised under CD-04);
   - `proxy_chest_pain_screen_unasked`: a proxy session leaves the seven chest-pain slots unasked until the DSL can select proxy copy (raised under CD-01). It is raised only when the subject is in the adult age band of the two chest-pain rules or of unknown age, and never for a subject known to be outside that band (for example a child intake), where neither chest-pain rule applies.

   Every kind is pinned to the note and never dropped.

   The proxy item makes the gap visible to a clinician; it does not close it. Until the DSL can select proxy copy, neither chest-pain RED rule can fire in a proxy session (CD-01 finding F13). In simulated stress testing, a proxy persona with chest pain is reported as unscreened, never as negative.

   Each item kind is labelled in the inbox, and its SLA may differ; all stay draft.
8. **While the SLA and escalation stay draft,** every YELLOW_REVIEW is still delivered and pinned to the note. A missing SLA never drops or delays delivery. The tenant configuration reports the inbox as not ready rather than inventing an SLA.

**Why acknowledgement is not an outcome.** The AoMRC's fail-safe notification keeps two records: acknowledgement of receipt, and the subsequent action (or plan to act). Its recommendations ask that every report be "read, acknowledged and acted upon (or plan to act)", and that the organisation identify and follow up the ones that were not. A click that records no decision would close an item nobody has acted on.

**Safety direction.** Nothing here can lower a level or drop an item. D3 can only keep items open longer. D4 adds a readiness condition. D5 keeps every item flowing to the only built queue while it reports itself not ready.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

- **`sla`** (acknowledgement time, per inbox item kind), **`out_of_hours.coverage`**, **`failure_path.escalation_role`** and **`failure_path.escalation_after`.** NICE NG143 1.3.4 leaves the urgency of follow-up for an intermediate-risk finding identified remotely to the clinical judgement of the assessing professional and sets a time only for red features (1.3.3), which a YELLOW_REVIEW can never carry. The Tebliğ's EK-4 states one numeric time target, for KIRMIZI Kategori 2 (files/04 §3), and a YELLOW_REVIEW is not an EK-4 level. No Turkish regulation found sets a response time, out-of-hours cover or an escalation chain for a pre-visit clinic inbox. Any value written here would be invented, so the YAML carries the required draft literals.
- **Round 4 (INT-554 item D4): these four are tenant-agreed values.** Re-searched on 2026-10-17. The AoMRC recommendations, the one current Royal College text found on following up notifications nobody acted on, tie the time frame to the acuity of the finding and leave the escalation interval to the healthcare organisation. A clinic-inbox item has no acuity of its own. What is missing is each tenant's statement of the four values, per item kind, from its clinical governance. The D18 lane cannot supply it, and the register holds no platform-wide value.
- The recipient role, the channel and every constraint listed in the Decision section are active.
