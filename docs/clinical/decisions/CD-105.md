## Question

For each open item that the #642 sign-off packets route from the specialty-wave verification reports (waves 1-3, 4-5, 6, 7, 8 and 9), what is the clinical decision, on what current source, and which part stays with the owner?

## Decision

Thirty-eight items. Accepted (16): the AMATEM ASSIST-shaped items stay as unscored history with no window and no instrument path; the avulsed-tooth item stays as a history input; candidates stay L0 until the physician of record signs; the hematoloji heavy-period item keeps its conditional lead-in with an answer mapping for people who do not menstruate; the mood-elevation, nükleer tıp, getat, halk sağlığı and yaşlı hearing items stand as the verifier left them; the pregnancy fetal-movement split stands, with an answer mapping that keeps the gate open on any report of movement; the antenatal duration lines stand; the incontinence items keep voice delivery; pedodonti keeps cluster.dental as its parent. Changed (8): biomass wood ("odun") returns to the göğüs item once the lexicon false positive is fixed; preparation, counselling and periodic-review visits drop the complaint duration ask; the flu-vaccine ask uses a one-year window; ortodonti gains proxy variants; spor gains the exertional fainting and chest-discomfort items; odyoloji gains the sudden hearing loss item; the iş ve meslek skin item gets dryness back from its own source. Rejected (3): the day-of-surgery fasting times leave the pre-visit pack; the adli tıp safety, companion and strangulation items stay held; the göğüs and hematoloji weight-loss screens are not removed in favour of the base probe. Four placement items stay draft_needs_source where no snapshot states an action, with the sourced parts placed by CD-13. Seven items are owner questions.

**Inference disclosure.** The sources fix the clinical constructs and their timing. Which items leave the pre-visit pack, the answer mappings, the interim neutral duration line, the new slot ids and the Turkish wording of the added and reworded items are this author's choices made on those sources. Placement of any rule follows CD-13; where no snapshot states an action, the placement stays draft_needs_source and nothing is extended.

## What stays draft_needs_source and why

Four placement items stay draft_needs_source, each with its own source_gap in the YAML.

- **wave 6 alarm inputs with no rule** (`docs/evidence/verification/specialty-wave6-internal-mental.md#L77`): No snapshot states the action for suspected giant-cell arteritis: the BSR 2020 guideline returned HTTP 403 at retrieval and is not in PMC. Nefroloji Q5 stays as CD-13 recorded it.
- **wave 7 alarm inputs with no rule** (`docs/evidence/verification/specialty-wave7-onc-women-peds.md#L117`): HB-C4's pregnancy rule set and HB-C3's paediatric rule set do not exist, and no snapshot in the repository states a layperson-reportable trigger with an action for SVC obstruction, hypercalcaemia or ovarian hyperstimulation. Nothing is extended.
- **wave 8 alarm inputs with no rule** (`docs/evidence/verification/specialty-wave8-geriatric-dental.md#L111`): No snapshot in the repository states a layperson-reportable trigger with an action for falls with head injury in older adults, nocturnal breathlessness in a sleep clinic, or the periodontal abscess and necrotising patterns, and no retrieved Turkish source sets a duty to ask about sleepiness while driving.
- **wave 9 alarm inputs with no rule** (`docs/evidence/verification/specialty-wave9-occupational-public.md#L113`): No current snapshot states the action for a rabies-risk animal contact (the HSGM Kuduz Profilaksi Rehberi is not snapshotted), a household TB contact, a fainting history in aviation medicine, or fever before a spa cure.
