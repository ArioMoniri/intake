# LD-04 — Occupational items: work-related sick leave, job tenure and household tuberculosis contact — category and basis

Scope: synthetic and test use only (owner decision D25, 3 Oct 2026). A licensed Turkish lawyer countersigns before any real patient.

## Question

Under which KVKK category and on what ground are three released items processed: iş ve meslek hastalıkları Q17 (isle_ilgili_rapor, a health report taken for a work-related health problem), Q18 (is_suresi, time in the current job) and halk sağlığı Q11 (evde_verem_temasi, someone in the household recently diagnosed with tuberculosis)?

## Decision

Each item's purpose is the patient's own diagnosis and care, a specific, explicit and legitimate purpose limited to what it needs (KVKK m.4(2)(c), (ç)). Q17, work-related sick leave: whether the patient took a health report for a health problem is the patient's own health data, special category under KVKK m.6(1); a sağlık raporu is by definition the record of a person's health status (Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 4(1)(m)). The INT-349 register row is occupational, as D3 and the owner's D24 answer 4Y read it. Ground: the patient's açık rıza under the files/06 §2 consent scope (KVKK m.6(3)(a)). The employment and occupational-safety ground of m.6(3)(f) is not ours: it covers what a legal obligation in those fields makes necessary, and a pre-visit intake discharges no such obligation. Q18, job tenure: how long the patient has worked in the current job is not among the categories m.6(1) lists, so it is ordinary personal data; its ground is the patient's açık rıza (KVKK m.5(1)) under the same consent scope, it sits in the occupational row, and it keeps the protections of the record it is stored in. Q11, household tuberculosis contact: the answer says something about two people. For the patient it is health data about their own exposure (m.6(1)), processed on açık rıza (m.6(3)(a)). For the household member it is that person's health data, a diagnosis, and that person gave no consent; the ground is m.6(3)(e), processing necessary for protecting public health, preventive medicine and medical diagnosis by persons under a duty of secrecy or authorised institutions. As in LD-03, that ground is the health service provider's: it holds in the hospital deployment and is not decided for the consumer deployment, where the item must not be served; nothing enforces that today, so the deployment-role gate of follow-up INT-474 comes before any register row is filled. The INT-349 register row is household (D3's household contacts). The household member's notice follows the Tebliğ timing — within a reasonable time of collection, at the first contact, or at the latest at the first transfer (Aydınlatma Tebliği m.6(1)); the mechanism is not decided. Recipients for all three: the tenant's clinicians giving this visit's care, through the consented share, only as far as that care requires (read from Kişisel Sağlık Verileri Hakkında Yönetmelik m.6(1)); an employer is not a person giving the health service and never receives these answers. Retention and the consent scope each item rides are not decided.

Legal basis: KVKK 6698 m.4(2)(c), (ç); m.5(1); m.6(1); m.6(3)(a), (e), (f); Aydınlatma Tebliği m.6(1); Kişisel Sağlık Verileri Hakkında Yönetmelik m.2(1), m.6(1); Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 4(1)(m).

## What stays draft

Four parts are not answered by any retrieved text. Consumer-deployment ground for the household member's diagnosis: the same open question as LD-03 (whether a controller that is not a health institution may rely on KVKK m.6(3)(e)); a Kurul decision or counsel's opinion would settle it. Notice mechanism for the household member: the Tebliğ m.6(1) gives the timing, not a way to reach someone the controller never contacts; a Kurul decision or counsel would settle it. Retention for all three items: no retrieved text sets it; the hospital medical-record retention rules and the deletion regulation, neither snapshotted here, would settle it. Consent scope for all three: which files/06 §2 purpose the answers ride is a consent-text question on CLAUDE.md's stop-and-ask list; the owner decides on this lane's recommendation.

While draft: No notice text is rendered to a household member; retention stays the slots.value annotation's engineering default, unconfirmed by this lane; and the compiler keeps refusing occupational and household slots because the served register's counsel fields are empty. The consumer-deployment exclusion has no mechanism until follow-up INT-474, so the household register row must not be filled before it lands.

Inference disclosure: That m.6(3)(f) does not cover a pre-visit intake is this author's reading: the ground is tied to 'hukuki yükümlülüklerin yerine getirilmesi', and the intake fulfils none. Kişisel Sağlık Verileri Hakkında Yönetmelik m.2(1) limits that regulation to processes the Ministry of Health runs, so applying its m.6(1) access limit to a vendor's pre-visit record, and reading from it that an employer never receives the answers, is this author's inference; the same result follows from KVKK m.4(2)(ç)'s limitation principle. Treating the household member's diagnosis under m.6(3)(e) and leaving the consumer deployment open follows LD-03's reading of who may use that ground. For counsel, as in LD-03: where m.6(3)(e) applies in the hospital deployment, Kurul practice is reported to disfavour açık rıza as a fallback, and refusing consent must not gate the core intake; no Kurul text is snapshotted here.
