# LD-02 — Minors and proxy answers (HB-L20) in synthetic and test sessions

Scope: synthetic and test use only (owner decision D25, 3 Oct 2026). A licensed Turkish lawyer countersigns before any real patient.

## Question

In synthetic and test sessions, who may answer intake questions for a child or for a dependent adult, at which ages, and on what consent footing under Turkish law (Türk Medeni Kanunu velâyet and vesayet, Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği, KVKK açık rıza)? Which of HB-L20's nine questions can the texts answer?

## Decision

Age: a person is a minor until the eighteenth birthday; marriage makes a person of age, and a court may declare a minor of fifteen or over of age at the minor's own request with the parent's consent (TMK m.11, m.12). For a person born on 29 February, the eighteen years run out on 28 February in a year with no 29 February: TMK m.5 applies the general provisions of the Borçlar Kanunu to all private-law relations as far as they fit, and TBK m.92 ends a period counted in years on the matching day of the last month or, when that month has no such day, on its last day. In law, who may act for whom: for a minor, a parent holding velâyet or a vasi, because the parents are the child's legal representatives toward third parties and a vasi represents the person under vesayet in every legal transaction (TMK m.335, m.342, m.403, m.448); for an adult under kısıtlama, the vasi, or the parents where the court left a restricted adult child under their velâyet (TMK m.335, m.448); an adult with discernment who is not restricted has full capacity and answers for themselves (TMK m.10); an adult without discernment and without a court-appointed representative has nobody who can act in their name, because a person without discernment cannot act with legal effect and has no capacity (TMK m.14, m.15). The Ministry's report procedure follows the same line: for a person under eighteen the veli or vasi completes the personal health form, and a restricted person applies with the vasi (Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 6(1)-(2)). A minor is heard and involved as far as the minor understands, even where the representative's consent suffices (HHY m.24). Carers: the law gives a carer, relative or companion with no legal representation no power to act in the patient's name, and the Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği lets another person be informed in the patient's place only on the patient's signed written request (HHY m.18); it does not bar a clinician in the hospital deployment from taking history from a relative, which KVKK m.6(3)(e) covers. That the carer answers for no one in a synthetic session is the owner's product rule (D24 answer 1Y), not the law, and it differs from files/06's rule that companion or proxy answers are taken and flagged proxy_reported — a point for the files/06 owner. Consent footing: an adult with capacity consents for themselves (KVKK m.6(3)(a)); for a child without discernment the legal representative acts (TMK m.14, m.15, m.342; HHY m.24 for medical interventions); whether a minor with discernment gives açık rıza for their own health data alone, because consent is a strictly personal right (TMK m.16), or needs the representative is not decided. Product consequence inside the synthetic scope: the child path and the carer path stay closed, and proxy items are reworded for the representative or skipped (D24 answer 1Y). Of HB-L20's nine questions, two are answered: the legal effect of emancipation (a married or court-emancipated minor is of age, so the guardian requirement ends in law) and the leap-day birthday; which evidence of emancipation to accept, and the other seven, stay draft. Nothing here relaxes INT-223's fail-closed defaults.

Legal basis: TMK 4721 m.5, m.10, m.11, m.12, m.14, m.15, m.16, m.335, m.342, m.403, m.448; TBK 6098 m.92; Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği m.18, m.24; Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 6(1)-(2); KVKK 6698 m.3(1)(a), m.6(3)(a), m.6(3)(e).

## What stays draft

Seven HB-L20 questions, the evidence of emancipation and the minor's own consent are not answered by any retrieved text. adolescent_self_consent and consent_footing.minor_with_discernment: KVKK names no age, and TMK m.16 exempts strictly personal rights from the representative's consent while HHY m.24 asks the veli for medical interventions; a Kurul decision or settled Yargıtay case law on açık rıza by a minor with discernment would settle it. unverified_dob, dob_collection_mandatory and guardian_relationship_proof: no text says which evidence of age or of velâyet a pre-visit service must hold; a Ministry rule on e-Nabız or MHRS identity, or counsel's opinion, would settle them. emancipation_evidence: TMK m.11 and m.12 give the effect, not the evidence (a court decision or a marriage record, and how it is checked). adolescent_private_guardian_default, guardian_link_basis and jurisdiction_tr_only: no retrieved text addresses them; counsel's opinion under KVKK m.5 and m.6 and the product's market scope would settle them.

While draft: INT-223's defaults hold unchanged: no date of birth is collected, every non-adult path demands the guardian, no guardian link is written, a non-TR subject is refused, and no adolescent self-consent, emancipation path or guardian-disclosure default is opened.
