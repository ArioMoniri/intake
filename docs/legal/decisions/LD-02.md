# LD-02 — Minors and proxy answers (HB-L20) in synthetic and test sessions

Scope: synthetic and test use only (owner decision D25, 3 Oct 2026). A licensed Turkish lawyer countersigns before any real patient.

## Question

In synthetic and test sessions, who may answer intake questions for a child or for a dependent adult, at which ages, and on what consent footing under Turkish law (Türk Medeni Kanunu velâyet and vesayet, Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği, KVKK açık rıza)? Which of HB-L20's nine questions can the texts answer?

## Decision

Age: a person is a minor until the eighteenth birthday; marriage makes a person of age, and a court may declare a minor of fifteen or over of age at the minor's own request with the parent's consent (TMK m.11, m.12). Who may answer: for a minor, only a parent holding velâyet or a vasi, because the parents are the child's legal representatives toward third parties and a vasi represents the person under vesayet in every legal transaction (TMK m.335, m.342, m.403, m.448); for an adult under kısıtlama, the vasi, or the parents where the court left a restricted adult child under their velâyet (TMK m.335, m.448); for an adult who is not under kısıtlama, nobody but the adult, who has full capacity (TMK m.10); a carer, relative or companion with no legal representation answers for no one, and the Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği lets another person be informed in the patient's place only on the patient's signed written request (HHY m.18). The Ministry's own report procedure follows the same line: for a person under eighteen the veli or vasi completes the personal health form, and a restricted person applies with the vasi (Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 6(1)-(2)). A minor is still heard and involved as far as the minor understands, even where the representative's consent suffices (HHY m.24). Consent footing: a child without discernment cannot act with legal effect, so the legal representative acts (TMK m.14, m.15, m.342; HHY m.24 for medical interventions); whether a minor with discernment gives açık rıza for their own health data alone, because consent is a strictly personal right (TMK m.16), or needs the representative is not decided. Product consequence inside the synthetic scope: the child path and the carer path stay closed, and proxy items are reworded for the representative or skipped, as the owner directed (D24 answer 1). Of HB-L20's nine questions, only the legal effect of emancipation is answered: a married or court-emancipated minor is of age, so the guardian requirement ends in law; which evidence of it to accept, and the other eight questions, stay draft. Nothing here relaxes INT-223's fail-closed defaults.

Legal basis: TMK 4721 m.10, m.11, m.12, m.14, m.15, m.16, m.335, m.342, m.403, m.448; Hasta Hakları Yönetmeliği m.18, m.24; Sağlık Raporları Yönetmeliği Madde 6(1)-(2); KVKK 6698 m.3(1)(a), m.6(3)(a).

## What stays draft

Eight HB-L20 questions and one evidence rule are not answered by any retrieved text. adolescent_self_consent: KVKK names no age and TMK m.16 exempts strictly personal rights from the representative's consent while HHY m.24 asks the veli for medical interventions; a Kurul decision or settled Yargıtay case law on açık rıza by a minor with discernment would settle it. unverified_dob, dob_collection_mandatory and guardian_relationship_proof: no text says which evidence of age or of velâyet a pre-visit service must hold; a Ministry rule on e-Nabız or MHRS identity, or counsel's opinion, would settle them. emancipation_evidence: TMK m.11 and m.12 give the effect, not the evidence (a court decision or a marriage record, and how it is checked). leap_day_maturity: TMK m.11 counts years and says nothing about 29 February; a Yargıtay decision or the nüfus practice would settle it. adolescent_private_guardian_default, guardian_link_basis and jurisdiction_tr_only: no retrieved text addresses them; counsel's opinion under KVKK m.5 and m.6 and the product's market scope would settle them.

While draft: INT-223's defaults hold unchanged: no date of birth is collected, every non-adult path demands the guardian, no guardian link is written, a non-TR subject is refused, and no adolescent self-consent, emancipation path or guardian-disclosure default is opened.
