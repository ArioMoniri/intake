1.2.3 Immediate management of a suspected acute coronary syndrome
Management of ACS should start as soon as it is suspected, but should not delay transfer to hospital. The recommendations in this section should be carried out in the order appropriate to the circumstances.
1.2.3.1
Offer pain relief as soon as possible. This may be achieved with GTN (sublingual or buccal), but offer intravenous opioids such as morphine, particularly if an acute myocardial infarction (MI) is suspected. [2010]
1.2.3.2
Offer people a single loading dose of 300 mg aspirin as soon as possible unless there is clear evidence that they are allergic to it.
If aspirin is given before arrival at hospital, send a written record that it has been given with the person.
Only offer other antiplatelet agents in hospital. Follow the NICE guideline on acute coronary syndromes. [2010]
