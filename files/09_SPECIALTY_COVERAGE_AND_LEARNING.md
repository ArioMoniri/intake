# 09 · Specialty coverage (all branches) and the two learning loops (v1.4, 21 Aug 2026)

> This file answers two questions from the product owner: (1) *why only three specialties?* — it is now **every branch that books outpatient visits in Türkiye**, with an honest maturity model so nothing unreviewed ever reaches a patient; (2) *how does the system get better on its own?* — through **two separate loops**: a per-patient loop that adapts deterministically inside one patient's record, and a cross-patient loop that proposes protocol/prompt changes which clinicians approve. Neither loop can weaken safety; both are inspectable in the admin console (`08`).

## 2. Maturity model (what may reach a patient)
| level | meaning | who/what produces it | may be served to patients? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L0 generated** | starter pack drafted by the *Protocol Authoring Agent* from the cluster pack + branch template + cited public guidance (Turkish specialty-society guidelines, MoH clinical protocols); every slot has `ask_tr/follow_up_tr`; red flags only *copied* from base/cluster, never invented | agent, automatically for all branches | **no** — review queue only |
| **L1 reviewed** | a licensed physician of that branch approved the PR (CODEOWNERS) | clinician | yes, in pilot tenants |
| **L2 validated** | ≥ 100 real intakes, note acceptance ≥ 70 %, must-ask coverage ≥ 95 %, zero safety incidents, satisfaction ≥ 4.2/5 | data + clinical lead sign-off | yes, all tenants |
| **L3 published** | open-sourced with an evaluation report | research track | yes |
Rollout plan: P0 generates L0 for **all** branches (taxonomy-driven); P1 ships the 9 cluster packs at L1 and 3 deep packs (İç Hastalıkları, Gastroenteroloji, Romatoloji) at L1→L2; P2 lifts the 10 highest-volume branches (İç Hastalıkları, Çocuk, Üroloji, Göz, KBB, Ortopedi, Kardiyoloji, Nöroloji, Kadın Doğum, Dermatoloji) to L1/L2; P3 reaches L1 for every branch and L3 for the base + clusters. A branch without an L1 pack runs its cluster pack — which is a real, reviewed protocol, not a fallback.

## 3. Loop 1 — per-patient adaptation (online, deterministic, patient-visible)
**Object:** `PatientOverlay` (versioned, patient-owned, exported/deletable), built from three sources only: (a) confirmed extraction from the patient's own intakes, (b) clinician-accepted note content, (c) confirmed imported records. LLM output alone never becomes a learned fact — it must be confirmed by the patient (in-app card) or a clinician (note acceptance).
**What it learns (with provenance and confidence):** chronic conditions and their baselines (e.g., NYHA class, usual pain 3/10), medication list and adherence pattern, allergies, recurring symptoms and what "normal for me" looks like, prior triage outcomes and how they resolved, pending follow-ups a clinician asked for ("BP log"), communication preferences (siz/sen, pace, literacy, prefers chips for numbers, typed vs voice, best time of day), companion relationships, and cross-specialty facts (the rheumatology DMARD informs the infection red flags in a dermatology visit).
**What it changes, deterministically:** confirm-don't-reask; **delta mode** for follow-ups ("Son görüşmeden bu yana ne değişti?" first, full HPI only on change); skip resolved sections; pre-fill with `confirm` steps; activate patient overlays (e.g., `overlay.oncology_patient`, `overlay.immunosuppressed`, `overlay.pregnant`) which may only add/tighten red flags; pacing and modality; brings screening forward when the observer lanes (`03 §4.7`) show sustained distress (never skips it).
**Guardrails:** monotonic red flags; instruments untouched; every learned fact shows "where from" and can be corrected or deleted by the patient in *Me → Hakkımda öğrendikleri*; per-field share scope; a fact older than 12 months is re-confirmed before use; no cross-patient data ever enters a patient's context.

## 4. Loop 2 — cross-patient learning (offline, evidence-gated, clinician-approved)
> v1.4 addition: proposals that touch **device-relevant content** (anything the `device_functions` boundary table in `06 §0.3`/INT-216 marks as MDSW) additionally pass QMS design-change control (ISO 13485) before canary — recorded in the proposal's evidence bundle. The pipeline remains technically unable to modify red-flag levels, instruments, consent texts, Talker tools or PolicyGuard (INT-172).
**Signals (aggregated, de-identified, k ≥ 10 per pattern):** per protocol step — clarification rate, `follow_up` rate, skip rate, time, abandonment, PolicyGuard blocks, ASR confusions per term; per note — clinician edits per section, acceptance, "missing" flags, false-RED reviews, clinician-reported missed flags; per session — satisfaction (CSAT + reason), completion, latency; per branch — coverage gaps (questions clinicians add by hand).
**Agent:** `services/learning` runs weekly as an **Inspector → Architect** pipeline (the evidence-gated update pattern from clinical self-improving-agent work): Inspectors write case-level findings on de-identified traces; the Architect commits only **recurring, cross-patient** patterns that pass thresholds (≥ 10 patients, ≥ 2 clinics, effect size + direction stable over 2 weeks) into **proposals**, never direct changes.
**Proposal types (allowed):** reword/reorder questions; add `follow_up_tr` or `choices_tr`; add `ask_if/skip_if`; add STT context vocabulary; promote pack maturity; add a **candidate** red flag (enters as `YELLOW_REVIEW` only; a clinician may promote it to a rule via ADR); prompt improvements for the *extraction* and *note* modules using **DSPy + GEPA** with clinician-corrected notes as the feedback set (one module at a time, held-out validation, Pareto selection — GEPA becomes unreliable when optimising several interacting modules at once, so the Talker prompt and safety prompts are **out of scope** for automatic optimisation).
**Forbidden for the agent:** relaxing or removing red flags, touching instruments or consent texts, changing Talker tools or PolicyGuard, any change to `residency_tr` routing, any use of identifiable data in a proposal.
**Path to production:** proposal = PR in `packages/protocols` or `packages/llm-evals/prompts` + attached evidence bundle (aggregate stats, de-identified exemplars, simulator run, triage goldens, latency harness, shadow replay on de-identified transcripts, Langfuse experiment link) → CODEOWNER clinician approves → canary 5 % → 25 % → 100 % with automatic rollback if guardrail KPIs move (acceptance −5 pts, safety blocks +50 %, completion −5 pts, CSAT −0.3) → protocol version + hash recorded on every session so any effect is attributable.
**Tooling (all self-hosted):** Langfuse (MIT core; traces, annotation queues for clinician labels, datasets, experiments, CI gating, alerts), DSPy + GEPA (offline optimisation), the existing simulator and golden suites, ClickHouse-backed analytics (Langfuse's store) or Postgres materialised views.

## 5. Legal and ethical frame (see `06 §8`)
Loop 1 operates inside the patient's own record under the core consent. Loop 2 operates on de-identified aggregates under the service-improvement purpose stated in the consent text; anything that resembles research (publishing, model training on voice) requires the separate research consent and ethics approval, and voice-based model training additionally requires the Acıbadem-led study protocol. Patients can opt out of Loop 2 without losing any feature.

## 6. Tests that keep the loops honest
- `overlay_monotonicity`: no patient overlay can lower a red-flag level or remove a rule (property test).
- `learned_fact_provenance`: every `PatientOverlay` field has a confirmed source; unconfirmed LLM facts are rejected at the type level.
- `proposal_scope`: proposals touching forbidden paths fail CI; proposals without an evidence bundle fail CI.
- `k_anonymity`: aggregate tables refuse patterns with n < 10.
- `canary_rollback`: simulated KPI dip triggers rollback within one evaluation window.
- `taxonomy_coverage`: every branch in `branches.yaml` resolves to an L1+ pack or its cluster pack; `protocols coverage` prints the matrix used by the admin console.

## 7. Security & technical monitoring of the learning pipeline (canonical in `10`)
- `services/learning` runs with **read-only** credentials on de-identified views (never on raw tables), in a sandboxed job (no egress except the internal Langfuse and the Git remote), with a PR-bot identity that can open but not merge; proposals and evidence bundles are signed; the k-anonymity helper refuses patterns below threshold and its refusals are metrics.
- The Protocol Authoring Agent (L0 drafts) has no access to patient data at all — it reads the taxonomy, cluster packs and cited guidance only.
- Monitoring: proposal throughput, approval/rejection reasons, canary guardrail hits, rollback latency, GEPA held-out deltas, job duration/failures, and an alert if any proposal touches a forbidden path (the CI `proposal_scope` test is the hard stop; the alert is the early warning).
