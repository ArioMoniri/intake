## KPIs (instrumented from P1)
- Activation: % new users completing one fast intake within 7 days (target 45 %).
- Completion: fast-mode completion ≥ 80 %; median duration ≤ 4 min.
- Quality: clinician acceptance ≥ 70 % (P1) → ≥ 85 % (P2); must-ask coverage ≥ 95 % in simulator; hallucinated-fact rate < 0.5 % (audited sample).
- Safety: RED sensitivity 100 % on goldens; false-RED ≤ 2 %; crisis-path completion 100 %; RED preemption ≤ 300 ms (p99 in production traces); PolicyGuard block rate tracked per profile.
- Speech & feel: Turkish medical WER ≤ 10 % on the internal test set; barge-in ≤ 150 ms; round-trip per profile as in `02 §8`; % scripted turns served from clip cache ≥ 60 % in fast mode; insertion-window wait p50 ≤ 2 s for Work speech.
- Trust: voice consent opt-in ≥ 70 %; wellbeing opt-in ≥ 40 %; share revocations honoured < 1 min; learned-fact corrections by patients tracked (a high rate means Loop 1 is over-eager).
- Experience & waits: CSAT ≥ 4.3, NPS ≥ 40 by P2; time-to-first-question ≤ 3 s; note ready ≤ 60 s after wrap-up; clinician time-to-open median < 2 h; check-in→seen delta measured where connectors exist.
- Coverage & learning: % of booked branches served by L1+ packs (P1 100 % via clusters; P3 100 % deep); proposals approved/rejected per month; canary rollbacks; GEPA deltas on held-out sets; k-anonymity rejections.
- Operability: 100 % of PHI-path components self-hostable and probed; release blocked if not; probe uptime per model; cost per session by profile.
- Reliability: SLO attainment (API 99.9 %, voice session success 99.5 %, note ≤ 60 s 99 %), error-budget burn, MTTR for P1 incidents < 4 h, change-failure rate < 10 %, deploy frequency, restore-drill success.
- Security: MTTD < 15 min for P1 signals; vulnerability SLA adherence (critical 72 h / high 7 d / medium 30 d); secrets and certificate rotation on time; zero PHI in logs (scan pass rate 100 %); pen-test findings closed; tabletop cadence kept.
- Business: notes delivered per clinic/week; visit-time delta; hospital NPS.

## Research tracks (parallel, university-led)
1. **Turkish medical ASR benchmark**: 5 h anonymised consented clinic-style Turkish audio; compare Soniox v5 / ElevenLabs Scribe v2 / Qwen3-ASR / faster-whisper / Inworld-routed; publish; decides STT per profile.
2. **Turkish DAM validation**: ethics-approved study (PHQ-9/GAD-7-labelled Turkish speech, n≈1,500); fine-tune multilingual backbone per Kintsugi methodology; AUROC with indeterminate band.
3. **Pre-visit note usefulness RCT-lite**: clinician time, missed-red-flag rate, patient satisfaction vs usual care.
4. **Protocol library**: clinician-authored packs; open-source the DSL and base protocols (Apache-2.0).
6. **Voice-based psychiatric signal validation (Turkish)**: calibrate affect/linguistic/acoustic lanes against PHQ-9/GAD-7 on the n≈1,500 cohort of track 2, using the Turkish audio-visual bipolar corpus as a secondary reference; pre-registered; lanes stay research-only until AUC and calibration are published.
5. **Conversational feel study (new)**: within-subject comparison of `cascade_fast` vs `s2s_cloud` vs `residency_tr` on completion, perceived naturalness, and must-ask coverage with Turkish patients (n≈60), including low-literacy and elderly cohorts.
