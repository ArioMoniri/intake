## 0. Read this first if you are picking up cold

Six things about this project are counterintuitive and get re-derived wrongly every time:

1. **The observer lanes are RETAINED.** An earlier draft proposed cutting `affect` and `acoustic` on cost and PHI-egress grounds. **That was wrong and is withdrawn** — see §3. Do not re-propose it.
2. **"Clinician-only" is the *unsafe* direction, not the safe one.** For the affect lane, showing output to a clinician is what creates a medical-device function. Not-rendered-at-all is safe. §4.7 of `03` originally had this backwards.
3. **The binding regulator is MDR/TİTCK, not FDA.** FDA's CDS guidance has a statutory device *exclusion* (§520(o)(1)(E)) that has **no EU/TR equivalent** — there is no Criterion 1 to fail because there is no carve-out to qualify for. This is *worse* for us, not better.
4. 🔴 **ATADEK is the WRONG committee, and "afiliye" is a trap.** Acıbadem runs **two** bodies: **KAEK** (Klinik Araştırmalar Etik Kurulu) for studies **within** the klinik araştırmalar yönetmeliği scope — **ours** — and **ATADEK** for studies **outside** it. ATADEK's own form says *"tıbbi cihaz … **(Klinik Etik Kurula başvurunuz)**"*, and **ATADEK is not on TİTCK's approved-committee list at all**. KAEK is: **`2025-KAEK-51`, Aktif**. Mail **kaek@acibadem.edu.tr**, never atadek@. **Separately: Acıbadem's own "afiliye hastane" is NOT the statutory *afiliasyon* of 3359 Ek m.9** — same word, different institutions. **Arguing site eligibility from the word "afiliye" on an Acıbadem page is void, and it is the single most likely way the dossier gets rejected**, because the university's own vocabulary supplies a plausible wrong answer.
5. **"Turkish networks are bad" is FALSE, and any argument leaning on it is withdrawn.** Same dataset, same quarter, same method: Türkiye mobile RTT p50 **22 ms** against Germany 24, UK 31, Italy 32, Spain 29 — **the joint-lowest median of the set, with a tighter tail (p95/p50 = 2.23) than Germany (2.42), Poland (2.43) or Spain (2.69).** The honest statement is *"mobile radio has a 2× tail everywhere, and Türkiye's is at the better end of Europe."* The real risk is **loaded** latency, not idle latency — see §2. `05 §4` carried one such claim ("on 4G, p95 RTT runs 3–4× p50") and it is corrected in place; **`01` carries no such claim**, checked 25 Aug 2026.
6. **Data residency is a latency *win*, not a compliance tax.** Three files treated it as pure cost. It is the single largest transport lever in the design. See §2 and D11.

## 0b. ⚠️ PENDING CONSTRAINT CHANGE — all models self-hosted (25 Aug 2026)

The product owner has imposed a hard constraint: **"all models should be self hostable… I want to have all models on my own server."**

**This invalidates three of the five pins in §1.** Soniox `stt-rt-v5`, Soniox `tts-rt-v2`, GPT-Realtime-2.1, ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 and Inworld profiling are all cloud-only. Under this constraint **`cascade_fast` and `s2s_cloud` collapse into `residency_tr`** — there is effectively one profile plus `typed`.

**What it buys, and it is a lot:** no Art. 9 cross-border transfer *at all*; no vendor DPA chain or sub-processor register; `consent_vendor_parity` (inv. 25) becomes trivially satisfiable; the two-stream defect becomes **structurally impossible** rather than test-enforced; and the consent sentence becomes *"bu analiz Türkiye'deki sunucularımızda yapılır."* It also **saves 156 ms p50 / 188 ms p95** of vendor-hop RTT (measured, §2).

**What it costs, and this is the honest part:**
- **Capabilities that disappear outright.** Soniox's structured `ContextObject` medical-term biasing and its **free bundled realtime diarization** — the latter is load-bearing for the `acoustic` lane's two-estimator gate, so losing it reopens a defect we had closed. GPT-Realtime's **async function calling**, which the six-tool Talker structurally requires.
- **Turkish TTS quality.** Chatterbox is the only self-hostable Turkish option and Turkish is *not* among its six dedicated language packs.
- **The GPU collision.** This is the real tension: **self-hosting everything is the right privacy answer and it runs straight into the finding in §2 that GPU supply in Türkiye is thin.** No hyperscaler GPU in-country; ~120–160 MW national commercial DC capacity.

**Open, decisive:** does **Qwen3.5-Omni** have downloadable open weights? A previous pass concluded API-only and ruled it out on that basis — but it is the best Turkish S2S in existence, and if weights exist it resolves Turkish S2S *and* self-hosting at once. **Under verification by `SelfHostStack`. Treat the §1 "ruled out" row for it as provisional until that lands.**

## 7. Gotchas

- **File is `01_MARKET_AND_STRATEGY.md`**, not `01_VISION_AND_MARKET.md`. Cost an agent a wasted read.
- **Never `git add -A` while an applier agent is mid-edit.** On 25 Aug two state-file commits (`0ebadab`, `f22ce38`) swept the admin-console agent's in-progress work on `08` and `10` into themselves. **Nothing was lost**, but ~240 insertions of security work sit under commit messages about TTS models and GPU sizing, so `git log` no longer describes the tree. **Stage explicit paths** — `git add files/00_STATE.md` — whenever an agent owns other files.
- **The source-hygiene rule extends to MODEL claims.** A GPU-cloud deploy guide published a `huggingface-cli download` command for a repo that does not exist, while hedging the licence as *"expected"*. **Resolve the repo id and read the raw LICENSE blob before trusting any deploy guide, benchmark post or vendor page.**
- **Any claim of a Turkish health API, FHIR endpoint, or Ministry approval process is FABRICATED until confirmed by a `saglik.gov.tr` or `resmigazete.gov.tr` source.** One SEO lead-generation page (`ininia.com`) invented specific endpoints — `GET https://enabiz.gov.tr/fhir/r4/Observation`, sample Patient JSON with a real-looking OID — **all of which 404**. It poisoned **two independent research lanes** in this session and the search summariser relayed its fabrications as fact **five times**. **Probe the endpoint or read the Resmî Gazete.** Same failure class as "badges are not licence grants", arriving from the regulatory side.
- **Do not run more than ~3 agents concurrently.** A 5-agent batch on 25 Aug hit the session limit and **all seven running agents died at once**, losing ~40 minutes of research with no partial output recoverable.
- Agents that yield a one-line non-report need an explicit "deliver the full report" follow-up; this has happened twice.
- A `re.sub` intended to drop a dependency once deleted the whole of INT-187. **Always `git diff` after a scripted edit** and check the issue count.
- Perl one-liners fail on `[]` and `{}` escaping here — use Python `pathlib` + `str.replace` with an assertion on the match count.
